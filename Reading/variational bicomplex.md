consider $\displaystyle{k}$-th order derivative $\displaystyle{\frac{\partial^{k}\phi ^{i}}{\partial x^{\mu_{1}}\dots \partial x^{\mu _{k}}}}$. the corresponding jet coordinate is denoted by $\displaystyle{\phi ^{i}_{\mu_{1}\dots \mu _{k}}}$. 



we define $\displaystyle{\frac{\partial^{S}}{\partial \phi ^{i}_{\mu_{1}\dots \mu _{k}}}}$ as

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
\frac{\partial ^{S}\phi ^{j}_{\nu_{1}\dots \nu _{k}}}{\partial \phi ^{i}_{\mu_{1}\dots \mu _{k}}} & =\delta ^{j}_{i}\delta ^{\mu_{1}}_{(\nu_{1}}\dots \delta ^{\mu _{k}}_{\nu _{k})} \\
\frac{\partial ^{S}\phi ^{j}_{\nu_{1}\dots \nu _{k}}}{\partial \phi ^{i}_{\mu_{1}\dots \mu _{m}}} & =0
\end{align}
$$
