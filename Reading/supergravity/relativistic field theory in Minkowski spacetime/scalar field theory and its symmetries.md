S & =-\frac{1}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{D}x[\eta ^{\mu \nu}\partial _{\mu}\phi ^{i}\partial _{\nu}\phi ^{i}+m^{2}\phi ^{i}\phi ^{i}]
