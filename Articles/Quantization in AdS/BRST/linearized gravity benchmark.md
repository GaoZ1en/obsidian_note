# Linearized Gravity Benchmark

Linearized Einstein gravity in AdS$_3$ tests a tensor gauge system with a non-trivial boundary phase space. Locally every Einstein perturbation is pure gauge, but Brown-Henneaux diffeomorphisms carry charges and generate two physical boundary-graviton towers.

## 1. Linearized BRST complex

Let $g^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}$ be the unit-radius AdS$_3$ metric,

$$
\begin{align}
R^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}=-2g^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}.
\end{align}
$$

For the metric perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$ introduce a vector ghost $c_{\mu}$, antighost $\bar c_{\mu}$, and Nakanishi-Lautrup field $B_{\mu}$. At the linearized level,

$$
\begin{align}
sh_{\mu\nu}&=\nabla ^{(0)}_{\mu}c_{\nu}+\nabla ^{(0)}_{\nu}c_{\mu},
&sc_{\mu}&=0,\\
s\bar c_{\mu}&=B_{\mu},
&sB_{\mu}&=0.
\end{align}
$$

This differential is off-shell nilpotent because the linearized diffeomorphism algebra is Abelian.

## 2. de Donder gauge and ghost operator

Use

$$
\begin{align}
F_{\mu}[h]
=\nabla ^{(0)\nu}h_{\mu\nu}
-\frac{1}{2}\nabla ^{(0)}_{\mu}h,
\qquad
h=g^{(0)\mu\nu}h_{\mu\nu},
\end{align}
$$

and the gauge-fixing fermion

$$
\begin{align}
\Psi=\int _M\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{-g^{(0)}}\,
\bar c^{\mu}\left(F_{\mu}[h]+\frac{\xi}{2}B_{\mu}\right).
\end{align}
$$

The Faddeev-Popov operator follows from

$$
\begin{align}
sF_{\mu}[h]
&=\left(\nabla ^{(0)2}\delta _{\mu}^{\nu}
+R^{(0)}_{\mu}{}^{\nu}\right)c_{\nu}\\
&=(\nabla ^{(0)2}-2)c_{\mu}.
\end{align}
$$

The first equality was checked as a covariant tensor identity and the second uses the AdS$_3$ Einstein condition. The ghost action is therefore governed, up to the overall gauge-fixing sign convention, by

$$
\begin{align}
S_{\mathrm{gh}}
\sim-\int _M\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{-g^{(0)}}\,
\bar c^{\mu}(\nabla ^{(0)2}-2)c_{\mu}.
\end{align}
$$

## 3. Sector decomposition

The local linearized-gravity notes organize an off-shell perturbation as

$$
\begin{align}
h_{\mu\nu}
=h^{TT}_{\mu\nu}
+\Theta _{\mu\nu}\chi
+\mathcal{L}_{V}g^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}.
\end{align}
$$

The scalar trace sector is removed by the linearized Einstein equation on the physical normalizable solution space. The remaining two relevant sectors are:

- $\mathcal{L}_{V}g^{(0)}$ with $V$ a proper diffeomorphism, which belongs to a BRST quartet;
- TT representatives of Brown-Henneaux boundary gravitons, which are locally Lie derivatives but whose generating vectors are not proper because they carry boundary charges.

For every non-zero proper Faddeev-Popov mode, the gauge coordinate $V$, its scalar-polarization or gauge-fixing partner, $c_{\mu}$, and $\bar c_{\mu}$ reduce to the same contractible quartet normal form as in Maxwell theory.

## 4. Proper and Brown-Henneaux modes

The independently reduced AdS$_3$ gravity phase space supplies the cohomology target.

The mixed highest-weight roots $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ have gauge parameters that vanish at the boundary. Their representatives have zero symplectic norm in the allowed Brown-Henneaux phase space and belong to the proper BRST-exact sector.

The chiral roots

$$
\begin{align}
(h,\bar h)=(2,0),
\qquad
(h,\bar h)=(0,2)
\end{align}
$$

are generated by asymptotic diffeomorphisms with non-trivial boundary values and charges. They are locally pure gauge but are not elements of $\mathfrak{g}_{\mathrm{prop}}$. The bulk ghost domain must therefore exclude these asymptotic generators. Equivalently, they survive in boundary BFV cohomology.

## 5. Renormalized symplectic pairing

The physical pairing is not obtained from the gauge-fixed bulk current alone. The GHY term and AdS$_3$ counterterm produce a corner one-form on $\partial\Sigma$, so the relevant object is the renormalized form

$$
\begin{align}
\Omega _{\mathrm{ren}}
=\Omega _{\mathrm{bulk}}+\Omega _{\mathrm{corner}}.
\end{align}
$$

The BRST canonical relation must retain the corresponding boundary term. On proper diffeomorphisms it vanishes and the quartet pairing descends to zero in cohomology. On Brown-Henneaux transformations it gives the physical surface charge and ultimately the Virasoro central extension.

At one-particle level, the ghost-number-zero cohomology is therefore

$$
\begin{align}
H^{0}_{\mathrm{bulk,local;1p}}(Q_{\mathrm{BRST}})&=0,\\
H^{0}_{\mathrm{bdy;1p}}(Q_{\mathrm{BRST}})
&=\mathcal{H}_{(2,0)}\oplus\mathcal{H}_{(0,2)},
\end{align}
$$

where the second line denotes the two Brown-Henneaux descendant towers with the symplectic normalization fixed in the reduced CPS notes. The full state cohomology also contains the vacuum and the Fock space generated by these boundary classes.

## Benchmark verdict

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Linearized nilpotency | Pass off shell. |
| Faddeev-Popov operator | Pass: $(\nabla^{2}-2)\delta_{\mu}^{\nu}$ on AdS$_3$. |
| Symplectic compatibility | Structural pass with $\Omega_{\mathrm{ren}}$: the proper boundary term vanishes and the Brown-Henneaux term is retained as a charge. |
| Proper diffeomorphism quartet | Structural pass modewise for non-zero FP modes; a global mode-by-mode normalization integral was not repeated here. |
| Local bulk cohomology | Pass: no propagating Einstein graviton in three dimensions. |
| Brown-Henneaux modes | Pass: $(2,0)$ and $(0,2)$ survive as boundary cohomology. |
| Proper mixed roots | Pass: $(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ are removed. |
| Induced pairing | Pass only with the renormalized bulk-plus-corner symplectic form. |

This benchmark shows that BRST-CPS reduction must be performed relative to the boundary charge algebra. Treating every locally pure-gauge metric perturbation as BRST exact would incorrectly remove the entire Brown-Henneaux Hilbert space.
