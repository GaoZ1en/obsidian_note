$$\begin{align}
S & =\int \mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ -g }\left(-\frac{1}{4}F_{\mu \nu}F^{\mu \nu}-\frac{\mu ^{2}}{2}(A_{\mu}-\nabla _{\mu}\phi)^{2}\right)

$$\begin{align}
A_{\mu} & \to A_{\mu}+\nabla _{\mu}\Lambda \\

\end{align}$$
