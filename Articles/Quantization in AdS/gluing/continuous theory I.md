consider a circle with cuts of length $\displaystyle{L_{1}}$ and $\displaystyle{L_{2}}$

$$\begin{align}
C & =L_{1}+L_{2} &
\end{align}$$

take $\displaystyle{A}$ and $\displaystyle{B}$ as the endpoints of intervals

$$\begin{align}
I_{1} & =[0,L_{1}], & A\to B \\
I_{2} & =[0,L_{2}], & B\to A
\end{align}$$

in each intervals, the action of a massive scalar is given by

$$\begin{align}
S_{i}[\phi _{i}] & =\dfrac{1}{2}\int \mathrm{d}t\int _{I_{i}}\mathrm{d}x_{i}\left[\dot{\phi}_{i}^{2}-\phi ^{'2}_{i}-m^{2}\phi _{i}^{2}\right]
\end{align}$$

take a variation of the action

$$\begin{align}
\delta S_{i} & = \int \mathrm{d}t\int _{I_{i}}\mathrm{d}x_{i}\left[-\ddot{\phi}_{i}+\phi''_{i}-m^{2}\phi_{i}\right]\delta\phi_{i} \\
 & +\int\mathrm{d}t\left[\phi'_{i}\delta\phi_{i}\right]_{0}^{L_{i}}+\int _{I_{i}} \mathrm{d}x_{i}  [\dot{\phi}_{i}\delta \phi _{i}]^{t_{f}}_{t_{i}}
\end{align}$$

for a Dirichlet theory, we impose the boundary condition as

$$\begin{align}
\phi _{i}|_{0,L_{i}}=0
\end{align}$$
