# Author review for the rewritten regional quantum-gluing draft

This file is not included in the manuscript. The paper has been rebuilt around separately quantized Neumann regions, an interface interaction, cutoff-dependent response matching, and the interval spectrum and correlator tests.

## Claim decisions retained in the draft

1. The penalty/interface coupling is presented as one concrete realization, not a general or intrinsic definition of QFT gluing.
2. Spectral statements are restricted to fixed branches or fixed low-energy windows.
3. Correlator statements concern only the displayed cutoff-dependent smeared equal-time observables.
4. No continuum product-Fock equivalence, arbitrary-state theorem, or interacting-QFT construction is claimed.
5. The four-region appendix tests edgewise assembly and partial gluing, not general associativity.
6. The global $\mathrm{AdS}_2$ appendix is a curvature benchmark and contains no Rindler or wedge-reconstruction claim.

## Numerical reporting decisions

1. The joined interval spectrum table uses the leading matched coupling $\pi^2N/(4L)$; the correlator calculations use the exact trigamma coupling $\pi^2/[4L\psi_1(N+1)]$.
2. Joined correlator references are cross-checked by full-interval mode sums and image-kernel evaluations. No analytic truncation-remainder theorem is claimed.
3. Last digits of errors near $10^{-10}$ are treated as implementation dependent; the manuscript claims the convergence pattern and scale.
