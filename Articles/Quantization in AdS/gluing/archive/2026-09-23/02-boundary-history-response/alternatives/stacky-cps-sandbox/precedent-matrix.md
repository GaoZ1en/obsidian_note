# Precedent Matrix for a Stacky CPS Formulation

## Verdict

There are direct precedents for every classical ingredient separately, and one especially close precedent for scalar cutting and gluing. What is not supplied by these papers is the full combination used in the active project: a massive Lorentzian scalar with a timelike artificial cut, prescribed boundary histories, an action-first derivation of matching, and a canonical comparison of quantize-first with glue-first.

## Three Meanings of “Stack” That Must Not Be Confused

1. **Families and gauge morphisms.** A field object is evaluated on a test parameter space $S$; gauge transformations appear as morphisms or higher morphisms. This is the functor-of-points direction.
2. **Spacetime descent.** The assignment $U\mapsto\mathfrak F(U)$ satisfies local-to-global descent for spacetime open sets $U$. This is locality in the spacetime direction.
3. **Derived equations of motion.** The critical locus is enhanced so that gauge identities, stabilizers, non-transverse intersections, and equation complexes are retained homologically.

For a free scalar there are no gauge morphisms, so the first layer is initially a sheaf of sets, regarded as a discrete stack. The derived layer can still record the linear equation complex and a non-transverse gluing problem. A useful formulation must state which of these layers it is using.

## The Missing Theorems

### Classical Lorentzian Target

Choose explicit Sobolev/energy spaces and compatibility conditions for the massive Klein--Gordon initial-boundary value problem. Prove that the uncut solution space is equivalent to the regional solution object with matching field and oriented flux traces. The comparison must respect the action-derived CPS form, not merely the set of normal-mode frequencies.

### Relative CPS Target

Show that the family $\mathfrak{Sol}_i\to\mathfrak B_\Gamma$ carries the appropriate relative presymplectic structure: its restriction to each fixed-$q_i$ fiber is the conserved regional form, while variation in the base records the boundary symplectic flux.

### Finite-Regulator Canonical Target

At a common cutoff, identify the correctly reduced regional object and construct

$$\begin{align}
S_N:\Gamma_{\mathrm{red},N}\longrightarrow\Gamma_{\mathrm{global},N}, \qquad S_N^{\mathrm T}J S_N=J,
\end{align}$$

together with the equality of the quadratic Hamiltonians. Only then does the metaplectic/CCR comparison follow at fixed $N$.

### Continuum Target

Study the large-mode Bogoliubov coefficients and apply Shale--Stinespring. A classical derived equivalence, a recovered spectrum, and a finite-dimensional metaplectic lift do not prove continuum Fock equivalence.
