in this note we will consider a free scalar in the $\displaystyle{\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}}$ spacetime with metric

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =-(1+r^{2})\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}r^{2}}{1+r^{2}}+r^{2}\mathrm{d}\Omega ^{2}_{d-1}q
\end{align}$$

the eom of a free scalar is

$$\begin{align}
\frac{1}{1+r^{2}}\partial _{t}^{2}\phi+(1+r^{2})\partial _{r}^{2}\phi+\frac{(d-1)+(d+1)r^{2}}{r}\partial _{r}\phi+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\Delta _{S^{d-1}}\phi-\mu ^{2}\phi & =0
\end{align}$$

now we consider the eigenfunction $\displaystyle{\phi _{n}}$ with eigenvalue $\displaystyle{\lambda _{n}}$ of the spatial Laplacian

$$\begin{align}
(1+r^{2})\partial _{r}^{2}\phi _{n}+\frac{(d-1)+(d+1)r^{2}}{r}\partial _{r}\phi _{n}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\Delta _{S^{d-1}}\phi _{n}-\mu ^{2}\phi _{n} & =-\lambda _{n}\phi _{n}
\end{align}$$

define $\displaystyle{\tilde{\lambda}=\lambda-\mu ^{2}}$, make the ansatz $\displaystyle{\phi=Y_{\ell \mathbf{m}}(\Omega _{d-1})R(r)}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
(1+r^{2})\partial _{r}^{2}R+\frac{(d-1)+(d+1)r^{2}}{r}\partial _{r}R+\tilde{\lambda} R-\frac{\ell(\ell+d-2)}{r^{2}}R=0
\end{align}$$
