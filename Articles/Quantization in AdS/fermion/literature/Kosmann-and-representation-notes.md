# Kosmann and Representation-Theory Notes

## Kosmann Lift

Godina--Matteucci provide a geometric treatment of spinor Lie derivatives and recover the Kosmann lift. The only property required in this project is that, for Killing $\xi$, the lift is compatible with the spin connection and commutes with the Dirac operator.

The source-extended treatment is essential: a componentwise ordinary Lie derivative is frame dependent, while the combined diffeomorphism and compensating local Lorentz transformation gives the covariant formula in Section 05.

## $SO(3,2)$ Positive-Energy Modules

Positive-energy spinor modules are labelled $D(E_0,1/2)$, with unitarity bound $E_0\geq1$. The endpoint $D(1,1/2)$ is the Di singleton. In the main interval,

$$\begin{align}
\Delta_+>\frac32, \qquad 1<\Delta_-<\frac32,
\end{align}$$

so both modules lie strictly above the spinor bound and have no singleton quotient.

The representation identification in Section 08 is not based on this classification alone: the project also checks the lowest state, all compact $SO(3)\times SO(2)$ types, their multiplicities, and the Casimir.
