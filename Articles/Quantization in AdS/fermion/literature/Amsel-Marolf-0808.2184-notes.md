# Amsel--Marolf 0808.2184: extraction notes

## Supported statements

For a Dirac field on fixed $AdS_d$, the paper gives

$$
\psi=\alpha\Omega^{(d-1)/2-m}
+\beta\Omega^{(d-1)/2+m}+\cdots
$$

and derives the projected subleading coefficients. It computes the fermionic boundary flux and shows that both branches are normalizable for $|m|<1/2$. Its appendices reduce the global Dirac equation to a coupled first-order radial system, then to partner Pöschl--Teller operators. It also relates the problem to positive self-adjoint extensions.

## Translation used here

For AdS4 their symbol is $d=4$. Their $\widetilde n=d\Omega$ points inward, so

$$
\widetilde\gamma^{\hat n}=-\gamma^{\hat r}_{\rm outward}.
$$

Accordingly, the projector names on their $\alpha,\beta$ are reversed in the project notation, while the physical slow/fast coefficients are unchanged.

## Limits

Finiteness of the standard inner product is not identical to action finiteness. Their extension discussion supports self-adjointness/positivity, but this project does not reproduce the full deficiency-index proof. Their paper is not used to define the boundary-complete Noether charge.
