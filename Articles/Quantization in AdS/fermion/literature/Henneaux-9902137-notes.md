# Henneaux hep-th/9902137: Extraction Notes

## Supported Statements

In Euclidean Poincaré AdS, Henneaux derives the roots $d/2\pm m$ and stresses that the two projected asymptotic coefficients can be varied independently off shell. Interior regularity relates them only on shell.

For a first-order Dirac action, fixing both projected halves would be the analogue of fixing both $q$ and $p$. Fixing one half determines the coefficient of the local boundary term. Henneaux also identifies the cross-pairs as radial canonical variables.

## Translation Used Here

- Euclidean Poincaré radial coordinate is replaced by global $r$.
- The paper's boundary orientation is translated to the outward global normal.
- The overall Lorentzian $i$ is restored.
- The sign of the boundary term is checked again from the finite-cutoff global variation rather than copied.

## Limits

The paper does not derive the alternative Lorentzian theory, the even graded Cauchy symplectic form, CAR, global spectra, or the $SO(2,3)$ charges used here.
