# 05. Noether symmetry and $SO(2,3)$ charges

## 1. Vielbein as a fixed source

The background is fixed, but the variational identity is organized before setting its source variation to zero. Regard $e^{\hat a}{}_{\mu}$ and the spin connection determined by it as external sources. A diffeomorphism generated by $\xi$ is combined with the local Lorentz transformation that returns the vielbein to the chosen frame. On spinors the resulting lift is the Kosmann derivative

$$
\mathcal L^K_\xi\psi
=\xi^\mu\nabla_\mu\psi
+\frac14(\nabla_{\hat a}\xi_{\hat b})\gamma^{\hat a\hat b}\psi,
$$

$$
\mathcal L^K_\xi\bar\psi
=\xi^\mu\nabla_\mu\bar\psi
-\frac14(\nabla_{\hat a}\xi_{\hat b})\bar\psi\gamma^{\hat a\hat b}.
$$

For an exact AdS Killing field $\nabla_{(\mu}\xi_{\nu)}=0$, this is a symmetry of the Dirac operator:

$$
[\mathcal L^K_\xi,\not\!\nabla]=0.
$$

The field-space vector is

$$
X_\xi
=\int_{M}d^4x\left(
\mathcal L^K_\xi\psi\frac{\vec\delta}{\delta\psi}
+\frac{\overleftarrow\delta}{\delta\bar\psi}\mathcal L^K_\xi\bar\psi
\right).
$$

## 2. Tangency to the two configuration spaces

Let $\zeta^a$ be the conformal Killing field induced on the Einstein cylinder $\mathbb R\times S^2$. Including the compensating Lorentz rotation, the leading data transform as three-dimensional conformal spinors:

$$
\delta_\xi\alpha
=\zeta^aD_a\alpha
+\frac14D_{[a}\zeta_{b]}\gamma^{ab}\alpha
+\frac{\Delta_-}{3}(D_a\zeta^a)\alpha,
$$

$$
\delta_\xi\beta
=\zeta^aD_a\beta
+\frac14D_{[a}\zeta_{b]}\gamma^{ab}\beta
+\frac{\Delta_+}{3}(D_a\zeta^a)\beta.
$$

The radial frame rotation is essential: applying an ordinary tensor Lie derivative to the components would miss the spin term and would not preserve the projector statement covariantly. The transformations are homogeneous in $\alpha$ and $\beta$. Therefore

$$
\alpha=0\Longrightarrow\delta_\xi\alpha=0,
\qquad
\beta=0\Longrightarrow\delta_\xi\beta=0,
$$

so every exact $SO(2,3)$ generator is tangent to each pure pre-phase space.

Strictly, spinors furnish a representation of the universal cover $\mathrm{Spin}(2,3)\simeq\mathrm{Sp}(4,\mathbb R)$; “$SO(2,3)$” below refers to its Lie algebra action.

## 3. Off-shell boundary-complete Noether identity

Let $L_D$ denote the bulk four-form and

$$
\ell_s=s\frac{i}{2}\bar\psi\psi\,\mathrm{vol}_{\Gamma_R},
\qquad
s=+1\ \text{(standard)},
\qquad
s=-1\ \text{(alternative)}.
$$

The regulator embedding is included among the external sources. This is needed because a generic AdS Killing field has a nonzero normal component at $r=R$. The source-extended transformation gives, off shell,

$$
X_\xi\mathbin{\cdot}\delta S_R^s
=\alpha_{\xi,R}^s|_{\Sigma_f}
-\alpha_{\xi,R}^s|_{\Sigma_i}
+\beta_{\xi,R}^s|_{\Gamma_R},
$$

with

$$
\boxed{
\alpha_{\xi,R}^s
=\int_{\Sigma_R}\iota_\xi L_D
+\int_{S_R^2}\iota_\xi\ell_s .
}
$$

The second term is a genuine corner term: it is the time-slice endpoint of the timelike polarization action. The finite-$R$ radial term $\beta_{\xi,R}^s$ includes the displacement of $\Gamma_R$. It is retained until the limit. For an exact Killing field and either source-free pure boundary condition,

$$
\lim_{R\to\infty}\beta_{\xi,R}^s=0.
$$

No equation of motion is used to define the symmetry or $\alpha_{\xi,R}^s$.

## 4. Charge read from the action

The regulated charge is defined directly by

$$
\boxed{
Q_{\xi,R}^s
=X_\xi\mathbin{\cdot}\theta_R-\alpha_{\xi,R}^s .
}
$$

Equivalently, its bulk part is the integral of the canonical Kosmann Noether current

$$
J_\xi^\mu
=\frac{i}{2}\left[
\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\mathcal L^K_\xi\psi
-(\mathcal L^K_\xi\bar\psi)\gamma^\mu\psi
\right]-\xi^\mu\mathcal L_D,
$$

supplemented by the explicit corner subtraction from $\ell_s$. Thus

$$
Q_{\xi,R}^s
=\int_{\Sigma_R}\sqrt\sigma\,\tau_\mu J_\xi^\mu
-\int_{S_R^2}\iota_\xi\ell_s.
$$

This is the primary formula. Varying the vielbein source and performing the Belinfante rearrangement rewrites the same on-shell quantity as a Hilbert stress-tensor integral plus the corresponding spin superpotential and the displayed polarization corner. The stress tensor is therefore a derived rewriting, not a guessed definition of the charge.

On shell the symmetric bulk Dirac Lagrangian vanishes. Under the source-free pure boundary conditions the corner density also vanishes in the limit. The surviving expression is finite because $\mathcal L^K_\xi$ maps a normalizable mode to another normalizable solution.

More explicitly, the first nonzero corner/radial bilinear scales as

$$
R^{-1-2m}\quad\text{(standard)},
\qquad
R^{-1+2m}\quad\text{(alternative)},
$$

and both vanish for $0<m<1/2$.

## 5. Conservation

The off-shell Noether identity implies

$$
\nabla_\mu J_\xi^\mu
=-i\left[
\bar\psi(\overleftarrow{\not\!\nabla}+m)\mathcal L^K_\xi\psi
-(\mathcal L^K_\xi\bar\psi)(\not\!\nabla-m)\psi
\right].
$$

It vanishes on the pre-phase space. Stokes' theorem then gives

$$
Q_{\xi,\Sigma_f}^s-Q_{\xi,\Sigma_i}^s
=-\lim_{R\to\infty}\int_{\Gamma_R}\sqrt{-\gamma}\,n_\mu J_\xi^\mu=0,
$$

where the final equality uses the same source-free asymptotic estimates as above. This conservation proof is logically separate from charge finiteness.

## 6. Hamiltonian identity

Varying the complete finite-$R$ Noether current, without dropping the corner, gives

$$
\iota_{X_\xi}\Omega_R+\delta Q_{\xi,R}^s
=-\int_{S_R^2}
\left[
\iota_\xi\theta_D(\delta\psi)
+\delta(\iota_\xi\ell_s)
\right]
+\mathcal R_{\xi,R}^s.
$$

Here $\mathcal R_{\xi,R}^s$ is precisely the contraction of the finite-cutoff radial variational form with the source-extended displacement. The term $\delta(\iota_\xi\ell_s)$ cancels the polarization contribution contained in $\mathcal R_{\xi,R}^s$. What remains is proportional to the free boundary variation:

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
\delta\alpha=\delta\bar\alpha=0,&\text{standard},\\
\delta\beta=\delta\bar\beta=0,&\text{alternative},
\end{array}
$$

plus the decaying $R^{-1\mp2m}$ remainders. Therefore

$$
\boxed{
\iota_{X_\xi}\Omega=-\delta Q_\xi
}
$$

for both source-free theories. The sign agrees with the workspace convention $Q=X\mathbin{\cdot}\theta-\alpha$.

## 7. Charge algebra

For field-independent exact Killing vectors,

$$
\{Q_\xi,Q_\eta\}_{\rm gP}=Q_{[\xi,\eta]}+K(\xi,\eta).
$$

The possible $K$ is configuration-independent. Evaluating on the zero solution gives $K=0$, so the classical charges realize $\mathfrak{so}(2,3)$ without a central extension.

## Verification

- **Verified:** tangency, finite-cutoff corner bookkeeping, asymptotic finiteness, zero radial charge flux, and the limiting Hamiltonian identity for the two pure source-free conditions.
- **Assumptions:** exact AdS Killing fields, source-extended regulator embedding, fixed torsion-free vielbein, and derivative-controlled asymptotics.
- **Not verified:** asymptotic non-Killing transformations, fluctuating gravity, and quantum anomalies are outside scope.
