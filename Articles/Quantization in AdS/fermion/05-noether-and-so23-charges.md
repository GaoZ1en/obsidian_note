# 05. Noether Symmetry and $SO(2,3)$ Charges

## 1. Vielbein as a Fixed Source

A diffeomorphism generated by $\xi$ is combined with the local Lorentz transformation that returns the vielbein to the chosen frame. On spinors the resulting lift is the Kosmann derivative, a generalization of Lie derivative for spinors:

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{\xi}^{K}\psi & =\xi ^{\mu}\nabla _{\mu}\psi+\dfrac{1}{4}(\nabla _{\hat{a}}\xi _{\hat{b}})\gamma ^{\hat{a}\hat{b}}\psi \\
\mathcal{L}_{\xi}^{K}\bar{\psi} & =\xi ^{\mu}\nabla _{\mu}\bar{\psi}-\dfrac{1}{4}(\nabla _{\hat{a}}\xi _{\hat{b}})\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\hat{a}\hat{b}}
\end{align}$$

For an exact AdS Killing field $\nabla_{(\mu}\xi_{\nu)}=0$, this is a symmetry of the Dirac operator:

$$\begin{align}
[\mathcal{L}_{\xi}^{K},\gamma ^{\mu}\nabla _{\mu}] & =0
\end{align}$$

The field-space vector is

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi} & =\int \mathrm{d}^{4}x\left(\mathcal{L}^{K}_{\xi}\psi \overrightarrow{\dfrac{\delta}{\delta \psi}}+\overleftarrow{\dfrac{\delta}{\delta \bar{\psi}}}\mathcal{L}_{\xi}^{K}\bar{\psi}\right)
\end{align}$$

## 3. Off-Shell Boundary-Complete Noether Identity

The total regulated action is

$$\begin{align}
S^{s} & =\int _{M_{R}}\mathrm{d}^{4}x\sqrt{ -g }\mathcal{L}+s \int _{\Gamma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ -\gamma }\ell \\
\mathcal{L} & = \dfrac{i}{2}(\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu}\nabla _{\mu}\psi-\nabla _{\mu}\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu}\psi-2m\bar{\psi}\psi) \\
\ell & =\dfrac{i}{2}\bar{\psi}\psi
\end{align}$$

here $\displaystyle{s=\pm 1}$ and $\displaystyle{+1}$ for standard quantization and $\displaystyle{-1}$ for alternative. Act the diffeomorphism generated by $\displaystyle{\xi}$ on the action, we have

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta S_{R}^{s} & = \alpha ^{s}_{\xi,R}|_{\Sigma _{f}-\Sigma _{i}}+\beta ^{s}_{\xi,R}|_{\Gamma _{R}} \\
\alpha ^{s}_{\xi,R} & = \int _{\Sigma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L}-s \int _{\partial \Sigma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ h }\tau _{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\ell \\
\beta ^{s}_{\xi,R} & =\int _{\Gamma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ -\gamma }n_{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\left(\mathcal{L}+sn^{\nu}\nabla _{\nu}\ell+K\ell _{s}\right)
\end{align}$$

here $\displaystyle{K}$ is the extrinsic curvature scalar

$$\begin{align}
K & =\gamma ^{ab}\nabla _{a}n_{b}
\end{align}$$

by asymptotic analysis, for $\displaystyle{0<m< \dfrac{1}{2}}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
\lim_{ R \to \infty }  \beta ^{s}_{\xi,R} & =0
\end{align}$$

thus $\displaystyle{X_{\xi}}$ is an exact symmetry, and the corresponding Noether charge is given by

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi,R}^{s} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \theta _{R}-\alpha ^{s}_{\xi,R} \\
 & = \int _{\Sigma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}J^{\mu}_{\xi}+s\int _{\partial \Sigma _{R}} \mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ h }\tau _{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\ell
\end{align}$$

here

$$\begin{align}
J^{\mu}_{\xi} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \theta ^{\mu}+\mathcal{L} \\
 & =\xi _{\nu}T^{\mu \nu}+\nabla _{\nu}U_{\xi}^{\mu \nu}+\dfrac{i}{4}\xi _{\rho}\left(\mathcal{E}_{\bar{\psi}}\gamma ^{\mu \rho}\psi+\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu \rho}\mathcal{E}_{\psi}\right)-\dfrac{i}{2}\xi ^{\mu}\left(\bar{\psi}\mathcal{E}_{\psi}-\mathcal{E}_{\bar{\psi}}\psi\right) \\
 & \approx \xi _{\nu}T^{\mu \nu}+\nabla _{\nu}U_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} \\
T^{\mu \nu} & =\dfrac{i}{4}\left(\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu}\nabla ^{\nu}\psi-\nabla ^{\nu}\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu}\psi+\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\nu}\nabla ^{\mu}\psi-\nabla ^{\mu}\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\nu}\psi\right) \\
U_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} & =\dfrac{i}{4}\xi _{\rho}\bar{\psi}\gamma ^{\mu \nu \rho}\psi
\end{align}$$

therefore

$$\begin{align}
H^{s}_{\xi,R} & \approx \int _{\Sigma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}\xi _{\nu}T^{\mu \nu} \\
 & +\int _{\partial \Sigma _{R}}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ h }\tau _{\mu}(n_{\nu}U_{\xi}^{\mu \nu}+s\xi ^{\mu}\ell)
\end{align}$$

by Noether theorem we know

$$\begin{align}
H^{s}_{\xi,R}|_{\Sigma _{f}}-H^{s}_{\xi,R}|_{\Sigma _{i}} & \approx 0
\end{align}$$
