\begin{abstract}

We extend the finite-action covariant phase-space construction of gravitational energy to partial Cauchy surfaces with a bifurcate horizon or an anchored extremal inner boundary.  Working in vacuum Einstein AdS$_3$ with Brown--Henneaux boundary conditions, we retain the timelike brick wall, Gibbons--Hawking--York terms, Harlow--Wu endpoint potential, Hayward joints, and embedding variation in a single regulated action.  This gives an outer-minus-inner Hamiltonian identity and an action-derived moving-boundary phase space in which $(\mathcal L_wg,-w)$ is an exact gauge degeneracy.  For the full linear Brown--Henneaux metric tangent quotient $H^\sigma(S^1)/\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb R)$, $\sigma>5/2$, we prove the slice-covariant classical identity
\[
\delta^2H_\xi^\infty
=\frac{s_\xi}{2\pi}\delta^2\!\left(\frac{A[g,\gamma]}{4G}\right)
+E_{{\rm can,p}}(P_{\rm HW}h)
=\frac{s_\xi}{2\pi}\delta^2\!\left(\frac{A[g,\gamma]}{4G}\right)
+E_{{\rm can,p}}[h]+\int_\gamma\Upsilon_{\rm p}.
\]
An endpoint-fixing PSL section changes the Brown--Henneaux generator only by exact global AdS Killing fields and removes the apparent unprojected anchor pole.  The combined canonical energy is positive and has the manifest Bañados/Schwarzian form
\[
E_{{\rm can,p}}[h[f]]
=\frac1{8G}\int_{\mathbb R}
\big[(F_f')^2+(F_f'')^2\big]\,\mathrm ds .
\]
Its kernel is exactly $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb R)$.  The combined form has a unique continuous closure to the endpoint-fixed $H^2$ section, no continuous extension below $H^2$, and no coercive lower bound in the ordinary $H^2$ norm.  Compact BTZ and AdS-Rindler calculations fix the normalization and control every regulator layer.  The result is classical and does not assume graviton entropy, modular crossed products, or quantum JLMS.

\end{abstract}
