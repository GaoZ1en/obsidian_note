# Smooth BTZ Moving Polarization Family

## Status and result

This note constructs a non-collar, nonzero, finite-amplitude family of exact
BTZ solutions on which the complete moving inner-wall variational problem is
integrable.  It uses the same finite-action Harlow--Wu convention as
`../article/` and the total embedding momentum derived in
`moving embedding and edge phase space.md`.

The main result is an explicit regulator-dependent mixed polarization.  Its
Brown--York flux vanishes identically, its source one-form is exact at every
finite wall, and a uniform normal wall translation has the integrable
Hamiltonian $H_\perp^{\rm edge}=0$ after the mixed boundary functional is
included.  The wall functional tends to the full fixed-boost area density,
so it is accompanied by a matched comparison corner that vanishes in the
horizon limit.  The bulk Noether/area corner is not changed.

## 1. Finite-amplitude exact family

Let $F\in\operatorname{Diff}^+(S^1)$ have degree one,

$$
F(\phi+2\pi)=F(\phi)+2\pi,
\qquad F'(\phi)>0,
\tag{1.1}
$$

and define

$$
R(\phi):=r_+F'(\phi).
\tag{1.2}
$$

In a full Kruskal collar of the bifurcation circle, use fixed boost
normalization $s$ and write

$$
\mathrm ds^2
=\mathrm d\rho^2-s^2\sinh^2\rho\,\mathrm d\lambda^2
+R(\phi)^2\cosh^2\rho\,\mathrm d\phi^2.
\tag{1.3}
$$

Equation (1.3) is the pullback of BTZ by $\phi\mapsto F(\phi)$ and is
therefore an exact vacuum solution.  Direct component evaluation also gives

$$
R_{\mu\nu}+2g_{\mu\nu}=0
\tag{1.4}
$$

for an arbitrary positive function $R(\phi)$.  The family is nonzero on the
horizon whenever $F'\neq1$.  Its infinitesimal directions include
$\cos\phi\,\partial_\phi$ and $\sin2\phi\,\partial_\phi$, the two smooth
representatives constructed previously.

Globally, multiply the two time-independent tangential horizon generators by
a smooth stationary radial bump that equals one on a full Kruskal collar and
zero before the AdS boundary, and exponentiate the resulting proper vector
fields. Their finite flows are Kruskal smooth, preserve the Brown--Henneaux
boundary data, and remain time-symmetric. They are nonzero on every inner
wall inside the collar, so this is not the old collar family in which the
perturbation vanishes near the wall. This controlled family carries
subregion-edge data but no nontrivial outer Virasoro charge.

For a concrete fixed-source finite-dimensional neighborhood, keep $r_+$ and
$s$ fixed, take the local flows generated by the two vector fields with
parameters $(a,b)$, and restrict to a compact parameter set on which

$$
0<c\leq F'_{a,b}(\phi)\leq C<\infty.
\tag{1.5}
$$

Uniformly for $r_+$ in any chosen compact positive calibration interval,
(1.5) supplies the estimates used below. The physical phase-space tangent
directions are $(a,b)$ at fixed $r_+$; varying $r_+$ is a separate
area/transgression calibration family because it changes the fixed-boost
asymptotic source.

## 2. Exact wall canonical pair

At the inner wall $\rho=\epsilon$, with outward normal $-\partial_\rho$,

$$
\gamma_{ij}
=\begin{pmatrix}
-s^2\sinh^2\epsilon&0\\
0&R^2\cosh^2\epsilon
\end{pmatrix}_{(\lambda,\phi)},
\tag{2.1}
$$

and the exact Brown--York momentum density is

$$
\boxed{
\Pi^{ij}
=\frac1{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}
\begin{pmatrix}
-R/s&0\\
0&s/R
\end{pmatrix}.
}
\tag{2.2}
$$

Allow both physical variations $\delta R$ and a uniform moving-wall
displacement $\delta\epsilon$.  The pulled-back induced metric varies as

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_X\gamma_{\lambda\lambda}
&=-2s^2\sinh\epsilon\cosh\epsilon\,\delta\epsilon,\\
\Delta_X\gamma_{\phi\phi}
&=2R\cosh^2\epsilon\,\delta R
+2R^2\sinh\epsilon\cosh\epsilon\,\delta\epsilon.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2.3}
$$

Consequently

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal B_X
=-\frac12\Pi^{ij}\Delta_X\gamma_{ij}
=-\delta_X\left(
\frac{sR\cosh^2\epsilon}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}
\right).
}
\tag{2.4}
$$

More strongly, for arbitrary two tangents in $(R,\epsilon)$,

$$
\boxed{
-\frac12\delta_X\Pi^{ij}\wedge
\delta_X\gamma_{ij}=0.
}
\tag{2.5}
$$

The $\lambda\lambda$ and $\phi\phi$ contributions proportional to
$\delta R\wedge\delta\epsilon$ cancel exactly.  Thus the finite-amplitude
family is a Lagrangian submanifold of the Brown--York boundary phase space.

## 3. Mixed wall functional

The trace Legendre functional is only half of the required primitive in the
horizon limit.  On the present family define instead

$$
\boxed{
w_{\rm sm,\epsilon}
:=\frac12\Pi^{ij}\gamma_{ij}
+\frac{sR}{2\kappa_{\rm p}^2}
=\frac{sR\cosh^2\epsilon}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}.
}
\tag{3.1}
$$

It is a legitimate regulator-dependent mixed functional on the selected
boundary submanifold. Equations (2.4)--(2.5) give, at every finite cutoff,

$$
\boxed{
(\mathcal B_X+\delta_Xw_{\rm sm,\epsilon})
\big|_{\mathscr L_{\rm sm,\epsilon}}=0,
\qquad
\Omega_{\mathcal H_\epsilon}
\big|_{\mathscr L_{\rm sm,\epsilon}}=0.
}
\tag{3.2}
$$

This is a mixed polarization adapted to the smooth family, not a universal
functional on arbitrary wall data.  The fixed parameter $s$ is part of its
boundary ensemble.

## 4. Normal translation and endpoint terms

The on-shell total normal embedding momentum per unit
$\mathrm d\lambda\,\mathrm d\phi$ is

$$
P_\perp^{\rm tot}
=\frac{2sR\sinh\epsilon\cosh\epsilon}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}.
\tag{4.1}
$$

Increasing the coordinate cutoff moves the inner wall opposite to its
outward normal, $\chi_\perp=-\delta\epsilon$.  Hence (4.1) reproduces exactly
the $\delta\epsilon$ part of (2.4).  After adding (3.1), the contraction of the
complete wall two-form with this uniform translation vanishes.

The endpoint slices remain orthogonal to the wall.  For a stationary uniform
radial displacement,

$$
\Delta_X\eta=0,
\qquad
C_\Gamma[\mathcal L_\chi g]=0.
\tag{4.2}
$$

Therefore neither the Hayward pair nor the Harlow--Wu endpoint supplies a
hidden term. Moreover every metric in (1.3) is stationary with zero ADM
extrinsic curvature on a $\lambda={\rm const}$ Cauchy slice,

$$
K^{\Sigma}_{ab}=0,
\qquad
\delta K^{\Sigma}_{ab}=0.
\tag{4.3}
$$

The bulk ADM/Lee--Wald symplectic form therefore vanishes for every pair of
allowed family tangents. On the selected family,

$$
I_{(0,\partial_\epsilon)}\Omega_{{\rm p},X}=0
=\delta H_\perp^{\rm edge},
\qquad
\boxed{H_\perp^{\rm edge}=0}
\tag{4.4}
$$

up to an irrelevant constant.  This proves field-space closure and path
independence for the uniform regulator translation.  It does not prove
integrability for a nonuniform wall displacement or for arbitrary smooth
horizon data.

## 5. Wall-to-corner transgression

Per unit $\lambda\,\mathrm d\phi$, the regulated fixed-boost comparison
density and the new wall functional are

$$
I_{\rm fuse,\epsilon}
=\frac{sR\cosh\epsilon}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2},
\qquad
W_{\rm sm,\epsilon}
=\frac{sR\cosh^2\epsilon}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}.
\tag{5.1}
$$

Keeping the total comparison representative unchanged requires

$$
I_{{\rm corner},\rm sm,\epsilon}
:=I_{\rm fuse,\epsilon}-W_{\rm sm,\epsilon}
=\frac{sR}{\kappa_{\rm p}^2}
(\cosh\epsilon-\cosh^2\epsilon).
\tag{5.2}
$$

For bounded physical tangents and a bounded first regulator displacement,

$$
I_{{\rm corner},\rm sm,\epsilon}=O(\epsilon^2),
\qquad
\delta_XI_{{\rm corner},\rm sm,\epsilon}=O(\epsilon).
\tag{5.3}
$$

Thus the wall functional carries the full limiting fused density while the
matched comparison corner vanishes.  This is a boundary-polarization
redistribution; the oppositely oriented bulk Noether cut still tends to
$sA_\gamma/\kappa_{\rm p}^2$. The quantity in (5.2) is a regulator-removal
comparison representative, not a second joint action added simultaneously
with $w_{{\rm sm},\epsilon}$ at finite cutoff. The actual orthogonal Hayward
joint is zero. Adding (5.2) again to the finite-wall action would spoil the
source cancellation (3.2).

## 6. H0$_X$--H6$_X$ audit on the family

For the compact parameter neighborhood (1.5), define
$\mathcal P_\epsilon^{\rm sm}$ by the exact finite flows above, evaluated at
the wall $\rho=\epsilon$. Then:

- **H0$_X$:** the fixed-$(r_+,s)$ parameter topology $(a,b)$ is independent of
  $\epsilon$. The exact metrics, outer Brown--York Hamiltonians, areas, and finite-action
  surface functionals depend smoothly on these parameters. Compactness of
  the circle and (1.5) give uniform bounds for their first two physical
  variations and regulator limits.
- **H1$_X$:** equations (3.1)--(3.2) give an explicit differentiable Lagrangian
  mixed polarization.
- **H2$_X$:** the finite flows are smooth in a two-sided Kruskal collar and remain
  in exact radial gauge there, so $C_\epsilon=\delta C_\epsilon=0$ uniformly.
- **H3$_X$:** the joins are orthogonal with fixed boost data; (4.2) removes the
  endpoint forms. The perturbations are already extremal representatives, so
  the gauge-completion descent pairs vanish locally.
- **H4$_X$:** equations (5.1)--(5.3) give the transformed wall-to-corner
  transgression for every physical tangent.
- **H5$_X$:** every metric is an exact vacuum solution. Its Taylor coefficients
  therefore satisfy the first- and second-order vacuum equations separately;
  $\partial_\lambda$ is the fixed-normalization background boost Killing
  field and preserves the wall ensemble.
- **H6$_X$:** near the cut the finite diffeomorphism is purely tangential and
  independent of the normal coordinates to first order. Hence the cut stays
  extremal and $h_{kk}=h_{ll}=0$; one may take $v=0$ in the collar.

This proves the moving analogues H0$_X$--H6$_X$ for a concrete non-collar,
nonzero, finite-dimensional vacuum family. It does not prove the original
fixed-embedding hypotheses H0--H6 for general data, nor either set for the
full Brown--Henneaux Hilbert space or the projected AdS-Rindler mode space.

The stationary wall-shape extension is now stronger.  For arbitrary smooth
periodic $R(\phi)>0$ and an arbitrary wavy wall $\rho=E(\phi)>0$,
`intrinsic moving boundary and edge realization.md` derives an exact local
functional $w_{\rm wave}$ such that the wall source is
$-\delta w_{\rm wave}$ up to a periodic $\phi$ derivative.  The integrated
wall flux therefore vanishes on this infinite-dimensional stationary family.
Time-dependent walls and non-orthogonal joints remain separate.

## Verification status

**Verified:** exact cosmological Einstein equation for arbitrary positive
$R(\phi)$; wall momentum, source primitive, Lagrangian flux, total normal
momentum, time-symmetric bulk symplectic zero, endpoint zeros, transgression
limits, and first regulator variation.
`btz_smooth_moving_polarization_check.wl` performs 20 exact tests, including
the degree-one identity $\int_0^{2\pi}R\,\mathrm d\phi=2\pi r_+$ and zero
integrated density for both edge tangents.

**Assumptions:** three-dimensional vacuum Einstein gravity; unit AdS radius;
fixed $r_+>0$ and $s>0$ on the physical edge phase space; $F'>0$; uniform
radial wall translation; compact parameter set (1.5); inner outward normal
$-\partial_\rho$; no inner AdS counterterm.

**Not verified here:** time-dependent wall translations; non-orthogonal
joints; a universal mixed functional outside the stationary compact family;
uniform estimates for the AdS--Rindler projected modes; or a quantum edge
algebra.  The nonuniform stationary compact extension is H55.
