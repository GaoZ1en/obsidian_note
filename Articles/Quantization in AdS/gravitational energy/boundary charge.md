from [[Articles/Noether charge/Poisson bracket of conserved charges|Poisson bracket of conserved charges]] we know the Noether charge of full gravity theory generated by the Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi ^{\rho}}$ is given by

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =\lim_{ \varepsilon \to 0 } \frac{1}{8\pi G}\int_{\partial \Sigma_{\varepsilon}}(-K^{\mu \nu}+K\gamma^{\mu \nu}-\gamma^{\mu \nu})\gamma_{\nu \rho}\xi^{\rho}\varepsilon_{\mu \mu_{1}}^{\Gamma_{\varepsilon}}\mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{1}} \\
 & =-\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{\partial \Sigma}\gamma^{ab}\xi^{c}T_{bc}\varepsilon^{\Gamma}_{aa_{1}}\mathrm{d}x^{a_{1}} \\
T_{ab} & = \lim_{ \varepsilon \to 0 } \frac{1}{4G}(K_{ab}-K\gamma_{ab}+\gamma_{ab})|_{z=\varepsilon}
\end{align}$$

rewritten in the tensor form, we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =-\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau ^{a}\xi ^{b}T_{ab}
\end{align}$$

here $\displaystyle{q}$ is the induced metric on $\displaystyle{\partial \Sigma}$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2}_{\partial \Sigma} & =q_{mn}\mathrm{d}x^{m}\mathrm{d}x^{n} \\
 & =r^{2}\mathrm{d}\phi ^{2}\quad r\to \infty
\end{align}$$

given the perturbation

$$\begin{align}
g_{\mu \nu} & =g^{(0)}_{\mu \nu}+\kappa h_{\mu \nu}+\kappa ^{2}k _{\mu \nu}+\mathcal{O}(\kappa ^{3})
\end{align}$$

use $\kappa^{2}=8\pi G$. in the Fefferman-Graham gauge near $\displaystyle{z=0}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
g_{zz} & =\dfrac{1}{z^{2}}+\kappa h_{zz}+\kappa ^{2}k_{zz}+\mathcal{O}(\kappa ^{3}) \\
g_{za} & =\kappa h_{za}+\kappa ^{2}k _{za}+\mathcal{O}(\kappa ^{3}) \\
g_{ab} & =\dfrac{1}{z^{2}}g^{(0)}_{ab}+\kappa h_{ab}+\kappa ^{2}k _{ab}+\mathcal{O}(\kappa ^{3})
\end{align}$$

with

$$\begin{align}
h_{zz},k _{zz} & =\mathcal{O}(z^{0}), & h_{za},k _{za} & =\mathcal{O}(z^{-1}), & h_{ab},k _{ab} & =\mathcal{O}(z^{0})
\end{align}$$

the boundary stress tensor reads

$$\begin{align}
T_{ab} & =\lim_{ z \to 0 } \dfrac{1}{4G}\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\Delta g_{zz}g^{(0)}_{ab}-\Delta g_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}\Delta g_{ab}+\Delta g\,g^{(0)}_{ab}+\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}\Delta g\,g^{(0)}_{ab}\right. \\
 & \left.+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{a}g_{zb}+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{b}g_{za}-zg^{(0)cd}D^{(0)}_{c}g_{zd}g^{(0)}_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z^{2}g^{(0)cd}g_{zc}g_{zd}g^{(0)}_{ab} \right)
\end{align}$$

here we denote

$$\begin{align}
\Delta g_{zz} & =g_{zz}-\dfrac{1}{z^{2}} \\
\Delta g_{ab} & =g_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{z^{2}}g^{(0)}_{ab} \\
\Delta g & =g^{(0)ab}\Delta g_{ab}
\end{align}$$

the perturbative expansion of $\displaystyle{T_{ab}}$ has the form

$$\begin{align}
T_{ab}[g] & =\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^{2}}T_{ab}^{(-2)}+\dfrac{1}{\kappa}T_{ab}^{(-1)}[h]+T^{(0)}_{ab}[h,k]+\mathcal{O}(\kappa)
\end{align}$$

Since $\kappa^{2}=8\pi G$, one has $1/(4G)=2\pi/\kappa^{2}$. Write

$$\begin{align}
h & =g^{(0)cd}h_{cd}, & k & =g^{(0)cd}k_{cd}.
\end{align}$$

Then the expansion of the displayed boundary stress tensor gives

$$\begin{align}
T^{(-2)}_{ab} & =0 \\
T^{(-1)}_{ab}[h] & =2\pi \lim_{ z \to 0 }\left(\dfrac{1}{2}h_{zz}g^{(0)}_{ab}-h_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}h_{ab}+h\,g^{(0)}_{ab}+\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}h\,g^{(0)}_{ab}\right. \\
 & \left.+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{a}h_{zb}+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{b}h_{za}-zg^{(0)cd}D^{(0)}_{c}h_{zd}g^{(0)}_{ab}\right) \\
T^{(0)}_{ab}[h,k] & =2\pi \lim_{ z \to 0 }\left(\dfrac{1}{2}k_{zz}g^{(0)}_{ab}-k_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}k_{ab}+k\,g^{(0)}_{ab}+\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial _{z}k\,g^{(0)}_{ab}\right. \\
 & \left.+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{a}k_{zb}+\dfrac{1}{2}zD^{(0)}_{b}k_{za}-zg^{(0)cd}D^{(0)}_{c}k_{zd}g^{(0)}_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z^{2}g^{(0)cd}h_{zc}h_{zd}g^{(0)}_{ab}\right)
\end{align}$$

in [[Articles/Quantization in AdS/gravitational energy/scalar charge matching|scalar charge matching]] we have derived the Noether charge corresponding to diffeomorphism generated by Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi}$

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ h }\tau _{\mu}n_{\nu}\left(S_{\xi,h}^{\mu \nu}+S_{\xi,k}^{\mu \nu}\right)
\end{align}$$

where

$$\begin{align}
S_{\xi,h}^{\mu\nu} & =s_{2}\nabla ^{(0)[\mu}\xi ^{\nu]}+\dfrac{1}{2}hD_{1,\xi}^{[\mu \nu]}+D_{2,\xi}^{[\mu \nu]} \\
s_{2} & =-\dfrac{1}{4}h_{\rho \sigma}h^{\rho \sigma}+\dfrac{1}{8}h^{2} \\
D_{1,\xi}^{\mu \nu} & =-h^{\mu \rho}\nabla ^{(0)}_{\rho}\xi ^{\nu}+\Gamma ^{(1)}[h]^{\nu \mu}_{~~~\rho}\xi ^{\rho} \\
D_{2,\xi}^{\mu \nu} & =h^{\mu}_{~\lambda}h^{\lambda \rho}\nabla ^{(0)}_{\rho}\xi ^{\nu}-h^{\mu \rho}\Gamma ^{(1)}[h]^{\nu}_{~\rho \sigma}\xi ^{\sigma}+\Gamma ^{(2)}[h,h]^{\nu \mu}_{~~~\rho}\xi ^{\rho}
\end{align}$$

and

$$\begin{align}
S_{\xi,k}^{\mu\nu} &=\xi_{\rho}\nabla^{(0)[\mu}k^{\nu]\rho} +\frac{1}{2}k\nabla^{(0)[\mu}\xi^{\nu]} -k^{\rho[\mu}\nabla^{(0)}_{\rho}\xi^{\nu]}.
\end{align}$$

write

$$\begin{align}
\xi ^{z} & =\dfrac{z}{2}D^{(0)}_{a}\zeta ^{a} \\
\xi ^{a} & =\zeta ^{a}-\dfrac{z^{2}}{4}D^{(0)a}D^{(0)}_{b}\zeta ^{b}+\mathcal{O}(z^{4})
\end{align}$$

where $\zeta^{a}(t,\phi)$ is the boundary conformal Killing vector,

$$\begin{align}
D^{(0)}_{a}\zeta_{b}+D^{(0)}_{b}\zeta_{a} & =g^{(0)}_{ab}D^{(0)}_{c}\zeta^{c}.
\end{align}$$

In strict FG gauge, $h_{z\mu}=k_{z\mu}=0$. With the outward normal chosen as $n_{z}=-1/z$, the surface measure gives

$$\begin{align}
\sqrt{ h }\tau_{\mu}n_{\nu}S^{\mu\nu} & =-\sqrt{ q^{(0)} }\tau^{(0)}_{a}z^{-3}S^{az}+\mathcal{O}(z).
\end{align}$$

All boundary indices in the following formulas are raised and lowered with $g^{(0)}_{ab}$. Define

$$\begin{align}
h_{\partial} & =g^{(0)ab}h_{ab}, & k_{\partial} & =g^{(0)ab}k_{ab}.
\end{align}$$

The finite FG coefficients of the surface current are

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{S}^{a}_{\xi,k} & :=\lim_{ z \to 0 }z^{-3}S_{\xi,k}^{az} \\
 & =\lim_{ z \to 0 }\left[-\zeta_{b}\left(k^{ab}+\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial_{z}k^{ab}\right)+\dfrac{1}{2}\zeta^{a}k_{\partial}\right], \\
\mathcal{S}^{a}_{\xi,h} & :=\lim_{ z \to 0 }z^{-3}S_{\xi,h}^{az} \\
 & =\lim_{ z \to 0 }\left[\dfrac{1}{4}h^{a}_{~c}h^{c}_{~b}+\dfrac{1}{8}\delta^{a}_{b}\left(h_{cd}h^{cd}-h_{\partial}^{2}\right)\right]\zeta^{b}.
\end{align}$$

Thus the FG expansion of the surface-charge expression gives

$$\begin{align}
H^{(0)}_{\xi,S} & =-\int_{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q^{(0)} }\tau^{(0)}_{a}\left(\mathcal{S}^{a}_{\xi,h}+\mathcal{S}^{a}_{\xi,k}\right).
\end{align}$$

On the other hand, in strict FG gauge the boundary-stress-tensor expression above gives

$$\begin{align}
T^{(0)}_{ab} & =2\pi \lim_{ z \to 0 }\mathcal{B}_{ab}, \\
\mathcal{B}_{ab} & =-k_{ab}-\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial_{z}k_{ab}+\left(k_{\partial}+\dfrac{1}{2}z\partial_{z}k_{\partial}\right)g^{(0)}_{ab}, \\
H^{(0)}_{\xi,T} & =-\int_{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q^{(0)} }\tau^{(0)a}\zeta^{b}\mathcal{B}_{ab}.
\end{align}$$

The two expressions differ by

$$\begin{align}
H^{(0)}_{\xi,S}-H^{(0)}_{\xi,T} & =-\int_{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q^{(0)} }\tau^{(0)a}\Delta_{\xi,a}, \\
\Delta_{\xi,a} & =\lim_{ z \to 0 }\left[\left(\dfrac{1}{4}h_{ac}h^{c}_{~b}+\dfrac{1}{8}g^{(0)}_{ab}\left(h_{cd}h^{cd}-h_{\partial}^{2}\right)\right)\zeta^{b}-\dfrac{1}{2}\zeta_{a}\left(k_{\partial}+z\partial_{z}k_{\partial}\right)\right].
\end{align}$$

Therefore the two charge formulas are not identical off shell. The mismatch is a local boundary improvement term. The boundary-stress-tensor expression is the Brown-York charge of the full action with the Gibbons-Hawking term and counterterm; in strict FG gauge its finite part depends on $k_{ab}$ but the nonlinear $h_{ab}$ terms in $K_{ab}-K\gamma_{ab}+\gamma_{ab}$ are suppressed by extra powers of $z$. The surface current $S_{\xi,h}+S_{\xi,k}$ was derived from the finite bulk action as a CPS superpotential, so it retains the explicit quadratic $h$-sector and a trace-$k$ improvement. They agree only after imposing the additional boundary relation $\Delta_{\xi,a}=0$ for the chosen asymptotic data, or after adding the corresponding boundary improvement to one of the two definitions.
