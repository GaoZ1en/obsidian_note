## derivation from the full theory

this appendix derives the full-theory charge identity underlying Sec. 2 from the unexpanded renormalized action.

### first variation and symplectic form

write the bulk action as

$$\begin{align}
S_{\mathrm{bulk}} & =\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{-g}\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bulk}}, \\
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bulk}}&:=\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^2}(R+2)+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{m}}.
\end{align}$$

take a variation of the bulk action with respect to the metric $\displaystyle{g_{\mu \nu}}$ and matter field $\displaystyle{\Psi^{A}}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
\delta S_{\mathrm{bulk}} & =\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{3}x\sqrt{-g}\left[-\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^2}\left(\mathcal{E}^{\mu \nu}-\kappa ^2T^{\mu \nu}\right)\delta g_{\mu \nu}+E_{A}\delta\Psi ^A-\nabla _{\mu}\vartheta ^{\mu}[\delta g,\delta\Psi]\right], \\
\mathcal{E}^{\mu \nu} & =R^{\mu \nu}-\dfrac{1}{2}Rg^{\mu \nu}-g^{\mu \nu}, \\
\vartheta ^{\mu} & =\vartheta _{\mathrm{EH}}^{\mu}+\theta _{\mathrm{m}}^{\mu}, \\
\vartheta _{\mathrm{EH}}^{\mu}[\delta g] & =-\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^2}\left(g^{\mu\alpha}\nabla ^{\nu}\delta g_{\alpha\nu}-g^{\alpha\beta}\nabla ^{\mu}\delta g_{\alpha\beta}\right).
\end{align}$$

the timelike pullback of the Einstein-Hilbert potential combines with the variation of

$$\begin{align}
S_{\Gamma} & =\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^2}\int _{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{-\gamma}\,(K-1)
\end{align}$$

according to

$$\begin{align}
\left.\delta S_{\mathrm{EH}}\right|_{\Gamma}+\delta S_{\Gamma} & =-C_{\Gamma}[\delta g]\big|_{\partial\Sigma _f}+C_{\Gamma}[\delta g]\big|_{\partial\Sigma _i}-\dfrac{1}{2}\int _{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{-\gamma}\,\mathcal{T}^{ab}\delta\gamma _{ab}, \\
C_{\Gamma}[\delta g] & :=\int _{\partial\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\phi\sqrt{q}\,\tau _{\mu}C_{\Gamma}^{\mu} \\
C_{\Gamma}^{\mu} & =-\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^{2}} \gamma ^{\mu \nu}n^{\rho}\delta g_{\nu \rho}
\end{align}$$

the Brown-Henneaux set the Brown-York source term $\displaystyle{-\dfrac{1}{2}\int _{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{-\gamma}\,\mathcal{T}^{ab}\delta\gamma _{ab}}$ to zero, while the matter condition of Sec. 1 gives $\displaystyle{\left.\delta S_{\mathrm{m}}\right|_{\Gamma}=0}$. the full symplectic potential is therefore

$$\begin{align}
\theta & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{\sigma}\,\tau _{\mu}\vartheta ^{\mu}-C_{\Gamma}.
\end{align}$$

take a further variation, we get the full pre-symplectic form

$$\begin{align}
\Omega & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}\delta \vartheta ^{\mu}-\delta C_{\Gamma}
\end{align}$$

### Noether charge associated with diffeomorphisms

again we summarize the diffeomorphism transformation generated by an arbitrary vector $\displaystyle{\xi _{\mu}}$ as

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi} & =\int \mathrm{d}^{3}x\left(\mathcal{L}_{\xi}g_{\mu \nu} \dfrac{\delta}{\delta g_{\mu \nu}}+ \mathcal{L}_{\xi}\Psi^{A} \dfrac{\delta}{\delta \Psi^{A}}\right)
\end{align}$$

which act on the metric $\displaystyle{g_{\mu \nu}}$ and the matter field $\displaystyle{\Psi^{A}}$ as

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta g_{\mu \nu} & =\mathcal{L}_{\xi}g_{\mu \nu}, & X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Psi^{A} & =\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\Psi^{A}
\end{align}$$

diffeomorphism covariance of the bulk action gives

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta S_{\mathrm{bulk}} & =\alpha _{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}}|_{\Sigma _{f}}-\alpha _{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}}|_{\Sigma _{i}} \\
\alpha _{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}} & =-\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bulk}}
\end{align}$$

on $\displaystyle{\Gamma}$, its pullback combines with the action of $\displaystyle{X_{\xi}}$ on the boundary action as

$$\begin{align}
\int _{\Gamma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ -\gamma }n_{\mu}\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bulk}}+X_{\xi}\cdot \delta S_{\Gamma} & =-\mu _{\xi}|_{\partial \Sigma _{f}}+\mu _{\xi}|_{\partial \Sigma _{i}} \\
\mu _{\xi} & :=\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\mu _{\xi}^{\mu} \\
\mu _{\xi}^{\mu} & =\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^{2}}((K-1)\gamma ^{\mu}_{~\nu}\xi ^{\nu}-D^{\mu}(n_{\nu}\xi ^{\nu}))
\end{align}$$

again the possible matter term on $\displaystyle{\Gamma}$ is absent under the assumptions of Sec. 1. hence

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\mathbin{\cdot}\delta(S_{\mathrm{bulk}}+S_{\Gamma}) & =\alpha _{\xi}\big|_{\Sigma _f}-\alpha _{\xi}\big|_{\Sigma _i}, & \alpha _{\xi}&=\alpha _{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}}-\mu _{\xi}.
\end{align}$$

the corresponding Noether charge is

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & := X_{\xi}\cdot \theta-\alpha _{\xi} \\
 & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}(X_{\xi}\cdot \vartheta ^{\mu}+\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{bulk}})-\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\left(X_{\xi}\cdot C_{\Gamma}^{\mu}-\mu _{\xi}^{\mu}\right)
\end{align}$$

for the gravitation sector, we have

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \vartheta _{\mathrm{EH}}^{\mu}+\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^{2}}\xi ^{\mu}(R+2) & =-\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^{2}}\xi _{\nu}\mathcal{E}^{\mu \nu}+\nabla _{\nu}Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} \\
Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} & := \dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^{2}}(\nabla ^{\mu}\xi ^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}\xi ^{\mu})
\end{align}$$

and Appendix B gives the matter sector result

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \theta _{\mathrm{m}}^{\mu}+\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{m}} & =\xi _{\nu}T^{\mu \nu}+E_{A}\xi ^{\nu}(\Delta ^{\mu}_{~\nu}\Psi)^{A}
\end{align}$$

the matter superpotential $\displaystyle{\nabla _{\nu}U_{\xi}^{\mu \nu}}$ vanishes under the assumptions of Sec. 1. therefore the bulk part of the Noether charge

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}} & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}^{2}x\sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{\mu}\left(-\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^{2}}\xi _{\nu}(\mathcal{E}^{\mu \nu}-\kappa ^{2}T^{\mu \nu})+E_{A}\xi ^{\nu}(\Delta ^{\mu}_{~\nu}\Psi)^{A}+\nabla _{\nu}Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu}\right) \\
 & \approx \int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}n_{\nu}\gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho}Q^{\rho \nu}_{\xi} \\
 & =\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^{2}}\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\left(-K^{\mu}_{~\nu}\xi ^{\nu}+D^{\mu}(n_{\rho}\xi ^{\rho})-\gamma ^{\mu \nu}n^{\rho}\nabla _{\rho}\xi _{\nu}\right)
\end{align}$$

together with the corner contribution

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi,\Gamma} & =-\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\left(X_{\xi}\cdot C_{\Gamma}^{\mu}-\mu _{\xi}^{\mu}\right) \\
 & =\dfrac{1}{2\kappa ^{2}} \int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\left(-K^{\mu}_{~\nu}\xi ^{\nu}+2(K-1)\gamma ^{\mu}_{~\nu}\xi ^{\nu}-D^{\mu}(n_{\rho}\xi ^{\rho})+\gamma ^{\mu \nu}n^{\rho}\nabla _{\rho}\xi _{\nu}\right)
\end{align}$$

we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =H_{\xi,\mathrm{bulk}}+H_{\xi,\Gamma} \\
 & =-\int _{\partial \Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\sqrt{ q }\tau _{\mu}\xi _{\nu}\mathcal{T}^{\mu \nu} \\
\mathcal{T}^{\mu \nu} & =\dfrac{1}{\kappa ^{2}}(K^{\mu \nu}-K\gamma ^{\mu \nu}+\gamma ^{\mu \nu})
\end{align}$$

which is exactly the Brown-York charge defined in other approaches.
