# 3. Quantization

We now use the temporal-gauge modes to reduce the Maxwell phase space to oscillator variables. The dual scalar construction in Section 2 gives the same field-strength spectrum, but the canonical variables used here are the temporal-gauge potential representatives.

## 3.1 Mode expansion and symplectic pairing

The positive-frequency modes are $A_{nm}^{\mu}=D^{\mu}\Psi_{nm}$, with

$$\begin{align}
\omega_{nm}&=2n+2+|m|, &
n&\in\mathbb{N}, &
m&\in\mathbb{Z}.
\end{align}$$

The normalization in Section 2 is chosen so that

$$\begin{align}
\Omega[A_{nm},A_{n'm'}^{*}]&=-i\delta_{nn'}\delta_{mm'}, &
\Omega[A_{nm},A_{n'm'}]&=0.
\end{align}$$

The propagating part of a real solution can be expanded as

$$\begin{align}
A^{\mu}(x)\big|_{\mathrm{osc}}&=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\left(a_{nm}A_{nm}^{\mu}(x)+a_{nm}^{*}A_{nm}^{*\mu}(x)\right).
\end{align}$$

Substitution into the symplectic form gives

$$\begin{align}
\Omega\big|_{\mathrm{osc}}&=-i\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\delta a_{nm}\wedge\delta a_{nm}^{*}.
\end{align}$$

The temporal-gauge modes therefore form a standard positive-norm oscillator basis for the propagating Maxwell sector.

## 3.2 Hamiltonian and angular momentum

The mode $A_{nm}$ has energy $\omega_{nm}$ and angular momentum $m$. With normal ordering, the oscillator Hamiltonian and angular momentum are

$$\begin{align}
H_{\mathrm{osc}}&=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}\omega_{nm}a_{nm}^{*}a_{nm}, \\
J_{\mathrm{osc}}&=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{m\in\mathbb{Z}}m\,a_{nm}^{*}a_{nm}.
\end{align}$$

The static gauge sector is not included in these sums.

## 3.3 Canonical quantization

Promoting the oscillator coefficients to operators gives

$$\begin{align}
a_{nm}&\mapsto \hat{a}_{nm}, &
a_{nm}^{*}&\mapsto \hat{a}_{nm}^{\dagger}.
\end{align}$$

The non-vanishing commutators are

$$\begin{align}
[\hat{a}_{nm},\hat{a}_{n'm'}^{\dagger}]&=\delta_{nn'}\delta_{mm'}.
\end{align}$$

The vacuum is defined by

$$\begin{align}
\hat{a}_{nm}\ket{0}&=0, &
n&\in\mathbb{N}, &
m&\in\mathbb{Z}.
\end{align}$$

The oscillator Hilbert space is generated by acting on $\ket{0}$ with the creation operators $\hat{a}_{nm}^{\dagger}$.

## 3.4 Static and gauge sectors

The zero-energy solutions of the temporal-gauge equations are residual gauge modes under the falloff and regularity assumptions used for the oscillator sector. Proper gauge transformations have vanishing symplectic pairing with the propagating modes and are quotiented out.

For a compact $\mathrm{U}(1)$ theory, large gauge transformations may label distinct winding sectors. These sectors are external labels for the oscillator Hilbert space rather than additional normalizable propagating modes.
