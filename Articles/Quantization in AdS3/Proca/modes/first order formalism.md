we have seen that the eom can be decomposed into two first order equations

$$\begin{align}
\varepsilon _{\mu}^{~\nu \rho}\nabla _{\nu}A_{\rho}\pm \mu A_{\mu} & =0
\end{align}$$

here the volume element $\displaystyle{\varepsilon _{\mu \nu \rho}}$ is normalized as $\displaystyle{\varepsilon _{tr\phi}=\sqrt{ -g }=r}$. for simplicity, we only consider the $\displaystyle{+}$ branch. the eom can be expanded as

$$\begin{align}
r\partial _{r}A^{\phi}+2A^{\phi}-\frac{1}{r(1+r^{2})}\partial _{\phi}A^{r}-\mu A^{t} & =0 \\
\frac{1+r^{2}}{r}\partial _{\phi}A^{t}+r\partial _{t}A^{\phi}+\mu A^{r} & =0 \\
-\frac{1+r^{2}}{r}\partial _{r}A^{t}-\frac{1}{r(1+r^{2})}\partial _{t}A^{r}-2A^{t}+\mu A^{\phi} & =0
\end{align}$$

together with the constraint equation

$$\begin{align}
\nabla _{\mu}A^{\mu} & =0 \\
\implies \partial _{t}A^{t}+\partial _{r}A^{r}+\frac{1}{r}A^{r}+\partial _{\phi}A^{\phi} & =0
\end{align}$$
