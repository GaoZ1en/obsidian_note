# Mixed root solutions

We use the same global AdS$_3$ conventions as [[eom]]:

$$
\mathrm{d}s^2=-(1+r^2)\mathrm{d}t^2+\frac{\mathrm{d}r^2}{1+r^2}+r^2\mathrm{d}\phi^2 .
$$

For a separated contravariant perturbation

$$
h^{\mu\nu}=e^{-i\omega t}e^{im\phi}f^{\mu\nu}(r),
$$

the Cartan eigenvalues are

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\xi_0}h^{\mu\nu}
=-\frac{i}{2}(\omega+m)h^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_0}h^{\mu\nu}
=-\frac{i}{2}(\omega-m)h^{\mu\nu}.
$$

Thus the weights are

$$
h=\frac{\omega+m}{2},\qquad
\bar h=\frac{\omega-m}{2}.
$$

The usual boundary-graviton primaries are

$$
h_{L,0}^{\mu\nu}
=C\frac{e^{-4it}}{(1+r^2)^2}\bar\xi_1^\mu\bar\xi_1^\nu,
\qquad
h_{R,0}^{\mu\nu}
=C\frac{e^{-4it}}{(1+r^2)^2}\xi_1^\mu\xi_1^\nu.
$$

They have weights $(2,0)$ and $(0,2)$ respectively.

## Highest-weight conditions

The mixed roots obtained from the Casimir equations are

$$
(h,\bar h)=(2,1),\qquad (h,\bar h)=(1,2).
$$

With the conventions above, the $(2,1)$ representative is generated from the left boundary graviton by

$$
h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}:=\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_{-1}}h_{L,0}^{\mu\nu}.
$$

It satisfies

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\xi_1}h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_1}h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}=0,
$$

and

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\xi_0}h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}=-2i h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_0}h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}=-i h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}.
$$

Explicitly, in the component order $(t,r,\phi)$,

$$
h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}
=C e^{i(\phi-3t)}
\begin{pmatrix}
\dfrac{i r}{(1+r^2)^{7/2}} &
-\dfrac{r^2-1}{2(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
\dfrac{i}{2r(1+r^2)^{5/2}} \\[6pt]
-\dfrac{r^2-1}{2(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
\dfrac{i r}{(1+r^2)^{3/2}} &
-\dfrac{1}{2(1+r^2)^{3/2}} \\[6pt]
\dfrac{i}{2r(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
-\dfrac{1}{2(1+r^2)^{3/2}} &
0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The $(1,2)$ representative is the right-moving mirror,

$$
h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}:=\mathcal{L}_{\xi_{-1}}h_{R,0}^{\mu\nu}.
$$

It satisfies

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\xi_1}h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_1}h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}=0,
$$

and

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\xi_0}h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}=-i h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_0}h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}=-2i h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}.
$$

Explicitly,

$$
h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}
=C e^{-i(\phi+3t)}
\begin{pmatrix}
-\dfrac{i r}{(1+r^2)^{7/2}} &
-\dfrac{r^2-1}{2(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
-\dfrac{i}{2r(1+r^2)^{5/2}} \\[6pt]
-\dfrac{r^2-1}{2(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
-\dfrac{i r}{(1+r^2)^{3/2}} &
-\dfrac{1}{2(1+r^2)^{3/2}} \\[6pt]
-\dfrac{i}{2r(1+r^2)^{5/2}} &
-\dfrac{1}{2(1+r^2)^{3/2}} &
0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

## Gauge parameters

The boundary graviton representatives in [[eom]] are locally pure gauge:

$$
h_{L,0}^{\mu\nu}
=\nabla^\mu \zeta_L^\nu+\nabla^\nu\zeta_L^\mu,
\qquad
h_{R,0}^{\mu\nu}
=\nabla^\mu \zeta_R^\nu+\nabla^\nu\zeta_R^\mu.
$$

Because $\xi_a$ and $\bar\xi_a$ are Killing vectors, acting with a Killing Lie derivative preserves the pure-gauge form:

$$
\mathcal{L}_K h_{\zeta}^{\mu\nu}
=\nabla^\mu (\mathcal{L}_K\zeta)^\nu
+\nabla^\nu(\mathcal{L}_K\zeta)^\mu .
$$

Therefore the mixed roots are generated by

$$
\zeta_{(2,1)}^\mu=\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_{-1}}\zeta_L^\mu,
\qquad
\zeta_{(1,2)}^\mu=\mathcal{L}_{\xi_{-1}}\zeta_R^\mu .
$$

The explicit results are

$$
\zeta_{(2,1)}^\mu
=C e^{i(\phi-3t)}
\left(
\frac{r}{8(1+r^2)^{5/2}},
-\frac{i}{8(1+r^2)^{3/2}},
\frac{1}{8r(1+r^2)^{3/2}}
\right),
$$

and

$$
\zeta_{(1,2)}^\mu
=C e^{-i(\phi+3t)}
\left(
\frac{r}{8(1+r^2)^{5/2}},
-\frac{i}{8(1+r^2)^{3/2}},
-\frac{1}{8r(1+r^2)^{3/2}}
\right).
$$

Direct substitution gives

$$
h_{(2,1)}^{\mu\nu}
=\nabla^\mu\zeta_{(2,1)}^\nu+\nabla^\nu\zeta_{(2,1)}^\mu,
\qquad
h_{(1,2)}^{\mu\nu}
=\nabla^\mu\zeta_{(1,2)}^\nu+\nabla^\nu\zeta_{(1,2)}^\mu .
$$

## Boundary behavior

At the Brown-Henneaux boundary $\Gamma$,

$$
\zeta_{(2,1)}^t,\zeta_{(1,2)}^t=O(r^{-4}),
\qquad
\zeta_{(2,1)}^r,\zeta_{(1,2)}^r=O(r^{-3}),
\qquad
\zeta_{(2,1)}^\phi,\zeta_{(1,2)}^\phi=O(r^{-4}).
$$

Thus

$$
\zeta_{(2,1)}\big|_{\Gamma}=0,
\qquad
\zeta_{(1,2)}\big|_{\Gamma}=0.
$$

The descendants are generated by

$$
\zeta_{(2,1);n,\bar n}
=\mathcal{L}_{\xi_{-1}}^n\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_{-1}}^{\bar n}
\zeta_{(2,1)},
\qquad
\zeta_{(1,2);n,\bar n}
=\mathcal{L}_{\xi_{-1}}^n\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_{-1}}^{\bar n}
\zeta_{(1,2)}.
$$

Near $r=\infty$, the lowering Killing vectors have coefficients of order

$$
\xi_{-1},\bar\xi_{-1}
\sim O(1)\partial_t+O(r)\partial_r+O(1)\partial_\phi.
$$

If a vector field obeys

$$
\eta^t=O(r^{-4}),\qquad
\eta^r=O(r^{-3}),\qquad
\eta^\phi=O(r^{-4}),
$$

then $\mathcal{L}_{\xi_{-1}}\eta$ and $\mathcal{L}_{\bar\xi_{-1}}\eta$ obey the same type of falloff. Hence, by induction,

$$
\zeta_{(2,1);n,\bar n}\big|_{\Gamma}=0,
\qquad
\zeta_{(1,2);n,\bar n}\big|_{\Gamma}=0,
\qquad n,\bar n\geq0.
$$

The mixed-root towers are therefore proper gauge transformations in the Brown-Henneaux phase space. They are non-zero bulk representatives, but their gauge parameters vanish at the spatial boundary and do not define independent boundary-graviton oscillators.
