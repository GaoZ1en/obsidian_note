the gauge fixed eom is given by

$$\begin{align}
\nabla ^{(0)2}h_{\mu \nu}+2h_{\mu \nu} & =0
\end{align}$$

and we can factorize this second order equation by defining

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{D}^{\pm})_{\mu}^{~\nu} & =\delta _{\mu}^{~\nu}\pm \varepsilon _{\mu}^{~\nu \rho}\nabla _{\rho}
\end{align}$$

therefore

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{D}^{+})_{\mu}^{~\nu}(\mathcal{D}^{-})_{\nu}^{~\rho}h_{\rho \sigma}=0 \\
\implies \mathcal{D}^{+}h=0 \text{ or } \mathcal{D}^{-}h & =0
\end{align}$$
