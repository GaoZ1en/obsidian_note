the gauge fixed eom is given by

$$\begin{align}
\nabla ^{(0)2}h_{\mu \nu}+2h_{\mu \nu} & =0
\end{align}$$

in addition, we impose the TT gauge, i.e.,

$$\begin{align}
h & =g^{\mu \nu}h_{\mu \nu}=0 \\
\nabla ^{(0)} _{\mu}h^{\mu \nu} & =0
\end{align}$$

expand these equations in the coordinate $\displaystyle{(t,r,\phi)}$ with metric

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =-f(r)\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}r^{2}}{f(r)}+r^{2}\mathrm{d}\phi ^{2}
\end{align}$$

with $f(r)=1+r^{2}$,

we have

$$\begin{align}
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{tt}-\frac{2}{f}h_{tt}-2r^{2}f h_{rr}-4r\partial_{r}h_{tt}+4r\partial_{t}h_{tr},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{tr}-\frac{3r^{4}+1}{r^{2}f}h_{tr}+2r\partial_{t}h_{rr}+\frac{2r}{f^{2}}\partial_{t}h_{tt}-\frac{2}{r^{3}}\partial_{\phi}h_{t\phi},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{t\phi}+2r\partial_{t}h_{r\phi}-\frac{2(2r^{2}+1)}{r}\partial_{r}h_{t\phi}+\frac{2f}{r}\partial_{\phi}h_{tr},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{rr}+4r\partial_{r}h_{rr}+\frac{2(3r^{4}+r^{2}-1)}{r^{2}f}h_{rr}+\frac{4r}{f^{2}}\partial_{t}h_{tr}-\frac{2r^{2}}{f^{3}}h_{tt}+\frac{2}{r^{4}}h_{\phi\phi}-\frac{4}{r^{3}}\partial_{\phi}h_{r\phi},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{r\phi}-\frac{2}{r}\partial_{r}h_{r\phi}-\frac{3f}{r^{2}}h_{r\phi}+\frac{2f}{r}\partial_{\phi}h_{rr}+\frac{2r}{f^{2}}\partial_{t}h_{t\phi}-\frac{2}{r^{3}}\partial_{\phi}h_{\phi\phi},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}h_{\phi\phi}+\frac{2}{r^{2}}h_{\phi\phi}+\frac{4f}{r}\left(\partial_{\phi}h_{r\phi}-\partial_{r}h_{\phi\phi}\right)+2f^{2}h_{rr}.
\end{align}$$

here

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}X&=f\partial_{r}^{2}X+\frac{1+3r^{2}}{r}\partial_{r}X-\frac{1}{f}\partial_{t}^{2}X+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\partial_{\phi}^{2}X 
\end{align}$$

and

$$\begin{align}
-\frac{h_{tt}}{f}+f h_{rr}+\frac{h_{\phi\phi}}{r^{2}}&=0 .
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
-\frac{1}{f}\partial _{t}h_{tt}+f\partial _{r}h_{tr}+\frac{1+3r^{2}}{r}h_{tr}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\partial _{\phi}h_{t\phi} & =0 \\
-\frac{1}{f}\partial _{t}h_{tr}+f\partial _{r}h_{rr}+\frac{1+4r^{2}}{r}h_{rr}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\partial _{\phi}h_{r\phi}-\frac{1}{r^{3}}h_{\phi \phi}+\frac{r}{f^{2}}h_{tt} & =0 \\
-\frac{1}{f}\partial _{t}h_{t\phi}+f\partial _{r}h_{r\phi}+\frac{1+3r^{2}}{r}h_{r\phi}+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\partial _{\phi}h_{\phi \phi} & =0
\end{align}$$

---

a pure gauge configuration is generated by a vector $\displaystyle{\zeta ^{\mu}}$ as

$$\begin{align}
h_{\mu \nu} & =\nabla ^{(0)} _{\mu}\zeta _{\nu}+\nabla ^{(0)} _{\nu}\zeta _{\mu}.
\end{align}$$

Writing the lower-index components as
$\zeta_{\mu}=(\zeta_{t},\zeta_{r},\zeta_{\phi})$, this gives

$$\begin{align}
h_{tt}&=2\partial_{t}\zeta_{t}-2rf\zeta_{r},\\
h_{tr}&=\partial_{t}\zeta_{r}+\partial_{r}\zeta_{t}-\frac{2r}{f}\zeta_{t},\\
h_{t\phi}&=\partial_{t}\zeta_{\phi}+\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{t},\\
h_{rr}&=2\partial_{r}\zeta_{r}+\frac{2r}{f}\zeta_{r},\\
h_{r\phi}&=\partial_{r}\zeta_{\phi}+\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{r}-\frac{2}{r}\zeta_{\phi},\\
h_{\phi\phi}&=2\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{\phi}+2rf\zeta_{r}.
\end{align}$$

Substituting this into the trace equation gives

$$\begin{align}
h=2D=0,
\end{align}$$

where

$$\begin{align}
D:=\nabla_{\mu}\zeta^{\mu}
=-\frac{1}{f}\partial_{t}\zeta_{t}
+f\partial_{r}\zeta_{r}
+\frac{1+3r^{2}}{r}\zeta_{r}
+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{\phi}.
\end{align}$$

The transverse equations become

$$\begin{align}
0=\nabla^{\mu}h_{\mu\nu}
=W_{\nu}+\partial_{\nu}D,
\end{align}$$

where

$$\begin{align}
W_{\nu}:=(\nabla^{(0)2}-2)\zeta_{\nu}.
\end{align}$$

In components, $W_{\nu}=0$ is

$$\begin{align}
0={}&\mathcal{L}\zeta_{t}
-4\zeta_{t}
-2r\partial_{r}\zeta_{t}
+2r\partial_{t}\zeta_{r},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}\zeta_{r}
+2r\partial_{r}\zeta_{r}
+\frac{2r}{f^{2}}\partial_{t}\zeta_{t}
-\left(1+\frac{1}{r^{2}}\right)\zeta_{r}
-\frac{2}{r^{3}}\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{\phi},\\
0={}&\mathcal{L}\zeta_{\phi}
-4\zeta_{\phi}
+\frac{2f}{r}\left(\partial_{\phi}\zeta_{r}
-\partial_{r}\zeta_{\phi}\right).
\end{align}$$

Thus the TT condition for the pure gauge representative is equivalently

$$\begin{align}
D=0,\qquad
(\nabla^{(0)2}-2)\zeta_{\nu}=0.
\end{align}$$

Once these vector equations are imposed, the six tensor equations above are
not independent extra equations. They are the gauge-fixed form of the
linearized Einstein equation evaluated on
$h_{\mu\nu}=2\nabla^{(0)}_{(\mu}\zeta_{\nu)}$, so the pure gauge
parametrization reduces the problem from solving six coupled equations for
$h_{\mu\nu}$ to solving the divergence-free vector equation for $\zeta_{\mu}$.

$$\begin{align}
\varepsilon _{\mu}^{~\nu \rho}\nabla _{\nu}h_{\rho \sigma} & =\pm h_{\mu \sigma}
\end{align}$$
