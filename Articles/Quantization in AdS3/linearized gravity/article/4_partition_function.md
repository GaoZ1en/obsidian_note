# Boundary-graviton partition function

The quantization of Section 2 produces one left-moving and one right-moving boundary oscillator at each level $k\geq2$. Their canonical trace gives the linearized partition function.

## Canonical trace over edge oscillators

Let $q=e^{2\pi i\tau}$ and $\bar q=e^{-2\pi i\bar\tau}$. The left-moving excitations are generated by

$$
\hat a_{L,0}^{\dagger},\hat a_{L,1}^{\dagger},\hat a_{L,2}^{\dagger},\ldots,
$$

where $\hat a_{L,n}^{\dagger}$ has oscillator level $k=n+2$. The right-moving oscillators are

$$
\hat a_{R,0}^{\dagger},\hat a_{R,1}^{\dagger},\hat a_{R,2}^{\dagger},\ldots,
$$

with right-moving level $\bar k=\bar n+2$. The canonical trace over the edge-mode Fock space is therefore

$$
Z_{\mathrm{edge}}
=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{edge}}}
\left(q^{N_L}\bar q^{N_R}\right),
$$

where

$$
N_L=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(n+2)\hat a_{L,n}^{\dagger}\hat a_{L,n},\qquad
N_R=\sum_{\bar n=0}^{\infty}(\bar n+2)\hat a_{R,\bar n}^{\dagger}\hat a_{R,\bar n}.
$$

For each bosonic oscillator of level $k$, the trace gives a factor $(1-q^k)^{-1}$. Hence the linearized edge-mode contribution is

$$
Z_{\mathrm{1-loop}}
=\prod_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(1-q^k)(1-\bar q^k)}
=\prod_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{|1-q^k|^2}.
$$

This formula is the canonical partition function of the Brown-Henneaux edge modes in the linearized theory.

## Classical prefactor and zero modes

Including the classical saddle contribution of thermal AdS$_3$ gives the semiclassical expression

$$
Z_{\mathrm{vac}}
=|q|^{-c/12}\prod_{k=2}^{\infty}\frac{1}{|1-q^k|^2},
\qquad c=\frac{3}{2G},
$$

where the prefactor comes from the classical on-shell action and the infinite product is the linearized determinant.

The lower limit $k=2$ is the key feature of pure gravity. Levels $k=0$ and $k=1$ are removed because they correspond to the exact $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})_L\oplus\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})_R$ isometries of the AdS$_3$ background. Counting them as oscillators would incorrectly include gauge redundancies as physical states.

Thus the one-loop answer is determined by the asymptotic edge-mode phase space. The local bulk equation $(\nabla^{(0)2}+2)h_{\mu\nu}=0$ is used to construct convenient representatives of the modes, but the states counted by $Z_{\mathrm{1-loop}}$ are the quantized Brown-Henneaux edge oscillators.
