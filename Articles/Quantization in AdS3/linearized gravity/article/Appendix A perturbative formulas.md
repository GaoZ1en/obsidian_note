# Perturbative formulas

This appendix records the perturbative formulas used in the main text. The full gravitational covariant phase space and Noether-charge formulas are expanded around global AdS$_3$,

$$
g_{\mu\nu}=g^{(0)}_{\mu\nu}+h_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The goal is not to rederive the full gravity formalism, but to check that the linearized quantities used in Sections 1 and 3 are mutually consistent.
