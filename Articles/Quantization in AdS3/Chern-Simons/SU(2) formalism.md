the action for Chern-Simons theory with gauge group $\displaystyle{\mathrm{SU}(2)}$ is given by

$$\begin{align}
S_{\text{bulk}} & =\frac{k}{4\pi}\int _{M} \mathrm{Tr}\left( A\wedge \mathrm{d}A+\frac{2}{3}A\wedge A\wedge A \right)
\end{align}$$

take a variation of the action, we have

$$\begin{align}
\delta S_{\text{bulk}} & =\int _{M}\mathrm{Tr}(\delta A\wedge E)+\theta|_{\Sigma _{f}}-\theta|_{\Sigma _{i}}+\alpha |_{\Gamma} \\
E & =\frac{k}{2\pi} F \\
\theta & =\frac{k}{4\pi}\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{Tr}(A\wedge \delta A) \\
\alpha & =\frac{k}{4\pi}\int _{\Gamma}\mathrm{Tr}(A\wedge \delta A) \\
\implies \omega & =\frac{k}{4\pi}\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{Tr}(\delta A\wedge \delta A)
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{F=\mathrm{d}A+A\wedge A}$ is the curvature of the gauge field $A$. here we choose the basis of $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ as $\displaystyle{T_{a}}$ with $a=1,2,3$, which satisfy the normalization

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{Tr}(T_{a}T_{b}) & =\frac{1}{2}\delta _{ab}
\end{align}$$

and commutation relation

$$\begin{align}
[T_{a},T_{b}] & =i\varepsilon _{abc}T_{c}
\end{align}$$

## linear approximation

in the linear approximation, the eom is given by

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}A & =0 \\
\implies \mathcal{D}_{\mu}^{~\nu}A_{\nu}^{a} & =0, a=1,2,3
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{\mathcal{D}_{\mu}^{~\nu}=\varepsilon _{\mu}^{~\alpha \nu}\partial _{\alpha}}$ is the linearized eom operator. similar to the $\mathrm{U}(1)$ case, the solutions of the eom can be classified into three branches, which are generated by the gauge parameters

1. $\displaystyle{(0,0)}$ branch: $\displaystyle{\Lambda _{(0,0)}^{a}=0}$, which gives the trivial vacuum solution $\displaystyle{\psi _{(0,0)}^{a}=0}$.
2. $\displaystyle{(1,0)}$ branch: $\displaystyle{\Lambda _{(1,0)}^{a}= \frac{-i}{\sqrt{ k }}\frac{e^{-i(t-\phi)}r}{(1+r^{2})^{1/2}}\delta ^{a 3}}$, which gives the topologically non-trivial vacuum solution $\displaystyle{\psi _{(1,0)}^{a}=\mathcal{L}_{\xi _{1}}\Lambda _{(1,0)}^{a}}$ and its descendants $\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}_{\xi _{-1}}^{n}\psi _{(1,0)}^{a}}$. only for $\displaystyle{k> 0}$
3. $\displaystyle{(0,1)}$ branch: $\displaystyle{\Lambda _{(0,1)}^{a}=\frac{-i}{\sqrt{ -k }}\frac{e^{-i(t+\phi)}r}{(1+r^{2})^{1/2}}\delta ^{a 3}}$, which gives the topologically non-trivial vacuum solution $\displaystyle{\psi _{(0,1)}^{a}=\mathcal{L}_{\bar{\xi} _{1}}\Lambda _{(0,1)}^{a}}$ and its descendants $\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}_{\bar{\xi} _{-1}}^{n}\psi _{(0,1)}^{a}}$. only for $\displaystyle{k< 0}$
