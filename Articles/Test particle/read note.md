reference: Gedanken experiments to destroy a black hole. II. Kerr-Newman black holes cannot be overcharged or overspun

---

for a Kerr-Newman black hole, there are only three independent parameters, mass $\displaystyle{M}$, charge $\displaystyle{Q}$ and angular momentum $\displaystyle{J}$. however due to some physical considerations (weak cosmic censorship conjecture), they are bound by

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
M^{2}\geqslant \frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}}+Q^{2}
\end{align}
$$

when this inequality is not satisfied, the spacetime contains a naked singularity, which is visible from infinity.
