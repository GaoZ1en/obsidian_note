$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
\delta S & =\int \mathrm{d}^{2}x\left(-F^{\mu \nu}\partial _{\mu}\delta A_{\nu}\right) \\

\end{align}
$$

symplectic form

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
\omega =\delta \theta & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}x\delta F^{01}\wedge \delta A_{1}
\end{align}
$$

$$\tag{1.3}
\begin{align}

\end{align}
