## classical system

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
L & =\frac{1}{2}A_{ab}\dot{x}^{a}\dot{x}^{b}, a,b=1,\dots,n
\end{align}
$$

assume that $\displaystyle{A_{ab}}$ is positive definite, then by redefine $\displaystyle{x^{a}\to \xi ^{a} }$, we can diagonalized $\displaystyle{A_{ab}}$ into $\displaystyle{\mathbb{I}_{n}}$. then the Lagrangian becomes

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
L & =\frac{1}{2}\delta _{ab}
\end{align}
$$
