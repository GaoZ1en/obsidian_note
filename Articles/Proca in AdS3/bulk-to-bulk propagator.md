in this file we will consider the bulk-to-bulk propagator of Proca field in Euclidean $\displaystyle{\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}}$ under Poincare coordinate

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =\frac{1}{z_{0}}^{2}\left( \mathrm{d}z_{0}^{2}+\sum ^{d}_{i=1}\mathrm{d}z_{i}^{2} \right)
\end{align}
$$

the eom is

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
D^{\alpha}\partial _{[\alpha}A_{\mu]}-m^{2}A_{\mu} & =J_{\mu}
\end{align}
$$

we seek a solution of the form

$$\tag{1.3}
\begin{align}
A_{\mu}(z) & =\int \mathrm{d}^{d+1}w\sqrt{ g }G_{\mu}^{~\nu}(z,w)J_{\nu}(w)
\end{align}
$$

with propagator

$$\tag{1.4}
\begin{align}
D^{\alpha}\partial _{[\alpha}G_{\mu]}^{~\nu}(z,w)-m^{2}G_{\mu}^{~\nu}(z,w)=-\delta _{\mu}^{~\nu}\delta(z-w)
\end{align}
$$

take the ansatz

$$\tag{1.5}
\begin{align}
G_{\mu}^{~\nu} & =F(u)T_{\mu}^{~\nu}
\end{align}
$$
