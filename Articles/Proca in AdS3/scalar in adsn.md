the metric in global coordiante for $\mathrm{AdS}_{n}$ is

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =\frac{1}{\cos ^{2}\rho}(-\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\mathrm{d}\rho ^{2}+\sin ^{2}\rho \mathrm{d}\Omega ^{2}_{n-2})
\end{align}
$$

where $\displaystyle{t\in(-\infty,+\infty)}$, $\displaystyle{\rho \in \left[ 0, \frac{\pi}{2} \right)}$, and $\displaystyle{\mathrm{d}\Omega ^{2}_{n-2}}$ is the metric for unit sphere $\displaystyle{S^{n-2}}$. the Laplacian is given by

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
\sqrt{ -g } & =\frac{\sin ^{n-2} \rho}{\cos ^{n}\rho}\sqrt{ g_{S^{n-2}} } \\
g^{tt} & =-\cos ^{2}\rho \\
g^{\rho \rho} & =\cos ^{2}\rho \\
g^{\mu \nu} & =\mathrm{diag}\left( -\cos ^{2}\rho,\cos ^{2}\rho,\frac{\cos ^{2}\rho}{\sin ^{2}\rho}g^{-1}_{S^{n-2}} \right)
\end{align}
$$

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
\Delta\phi & =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ -g }}\partial _{\mu}(\sqrt{ -g }g^{\mu \nu}\partial _{\nu}\phi) \\
 & =-\cos ^{2}\rho \partial _{t}^{2}\phi+\frac{\cos ^{n}\rho}{\sin ^{n-2}\rho}\partial _{\rho}\left( \frac{\sin ^{n-2}\rho}{\cos ^{n-2}\rho}\partial _{\rho}\phi \right)+\frac{\cos ^{2}\rho}{\sin ^{2}\rho} \Delta _{S^{n-2}}\phi
\end{align}
$$
