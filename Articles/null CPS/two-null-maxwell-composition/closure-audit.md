## Endpoint and gauge verdict

The fixed outer exact dressed-connection coefficient is an endpoint section /
boundary-polarization choice, not ordinary bulk gauge fixing. It selects the
intrinsic field-strength presentation at finite cuts. With variable anchors,
$\delta E_0\wedge\delta\lambda_{\rm rel}$ is not a kernel direction and the
unanchored charged-frame theory is a different phase space.

## Distributional interface verdict

Equality of the complete traces $i^*F$ and $i^*(*F)$ in the matched-$H^1$
graph is sufficient for the union field to solve source-free Maxwell in the
distributional sense: the jump terms in $dF$ and $d{*F}$ vanish. Matching only
one master/news profile would not establish this.

Goursat existence; those are the analytic arguments in calculations 05--09.
