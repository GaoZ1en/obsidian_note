## Claims that are not novel

- Maxwell equations or canonical brackets on one null plane;
- edge frames and electric-flux moment maps;
- reduction by stages in gauge theory with corners;
- scalar/vector harmonic reduction on $S^2$;
- a modewise Bessel Goursat solution;
- Peierls equality or Weyl functoriality in isolation;
- gravitational corner gluing in general terms.
