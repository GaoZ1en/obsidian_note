# Scope, Spaces, and Theorem Ledger

## 1. Convention manifest

Start from the unfixed Maxwell action

$$
S[A]=-\frac12\int_DF\wedge *F,
\qquad F=dA,
$$

on

$$
D=[0,U]_u\times[0,V]_v\times S^2_R,
\qquad ds^2=-2\,du\,dv+q_{AB}dx^Adx^B,
$$

with signature $(-+++)$,

$$
\operatorname{vol}_4=du\wedge dv\wedge\operatorname{vol}_{S^2},
\qquad q_{AB}=R^2\gamma_{AB}.
$$

The field-space differential is $\delta$.  The Lee--Wald representatives are

$$
\theta[A;\delta A]=-\delta A\wedge *F,
$$

$$
\omega(\delta_1,\delta_2)
=\delta_1A\wedge *\delta_2F-\delta_2A\wedge *\delta_1F.
\tag{1.1}
$$

No Lorenz, radial, light-cone, or generator gauge is used to obtain (1.1).
Potential representatives may be chosen only after gauge-invariant statements
have been derived.

The initial cross is

$$
X=N_+\cup_{S_0}N_-,
\qquad N_+=\{u=0\},\quad N_-=\{v=0\},\quad S_0=\{u=v=0\}.
$$

The incoming characteristic symplectic form is defined so both generator
parameters (u,v) carry positive measures.  The conversion from induced
boundary orientations is recorded in calculation 01.

## 2. Fixed topological and radiative sector

The main theorem fixes a trivial bundle and

$$
Q_E=0,\qquad n=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{S^2}F=0,
\qquad \ell\geq1.
\tag{2.1}
$$

Because $H^1(S^2)=0$, every $\ell\geq1$ cut one-form has a unique exact plus
coexact decomposition.  The $\ell=0$ electric flux and Chern number are not
radiative modes and are excluded from the main theorem.  A variable
$\ell=0$ flux-frame extension is optional.

## 3. Gauge-invariant characteristic data

Write

$$
r_A(v,x)=F_{vA}|_{N_+},\qquad
p_A(u,x)=F_{uA}|_{N_-},
$$

$$
E_0(x)=F_{uv}|_{S_0},\qquad
b^0_{AB}(x)=F_{AB}|_{S_0}.
$$

The complete sheet-constraint datum is

$$
\boxed{\mathcal X_F=(r_A,p_A;E_0,b^0_{AB}).}
\tag{3.1}
$$

It reconstructs the constrained sheet fields through

$$
E_+(v)=E_0-\int_0^vD^Ar_A(v')\,dv',
\qquad
b^+_{AB}(v)=b^0_{AB}+2\int_0^vD_{[A}r_{B]}(v')\,dv',
\tag{3.2}
$$

$$
E_-(u)=E_0+\int_0^uD^Ap_A(u')\,du',
\qquad
b^-_{AB}(u)=b^0_{AB}+2\int_0^uD_{[A}p_{B]}(u')\,du'.
\tag{3.3}
$$

Equations (3.2)--(3.3) do not solve the transverse bulk evolution equation.

## 4. Gauge and frame ledger

Four operations are kept distinct.

1. **Bulk proper gauge.** In the anchored bulk theorem, a smooth $\epsilon$
   vanishes on both outer cuts.  Its common value at $S_0$ is allowed because
   the two sheet charges cancel there; in the split presentation this common
   value is the diagonal direction.
2. **Sheet-proper gauge.** In the present fixed-endpoint policy,
   $\epsilon_+|_{S_0}=\epsilon_+|_{S_+}=0$ and
   $\epsilon_-|_{S_0}=\epsilon_-|_{S_-}=0$.
3. **Diagonal cut gauge.** A common $\eta(x)$ acts on the two corner frames as
   $\chi_\pm\mapsto\chi_\pm+\eta$.  On the electric matching locus it is a
   presymplectic kernel direction and is quotiented.
4. **Relative frame displacement.** It changes
   $\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}=\chi_--\chi_+$.  When the outer exact anchors are
   variable, it is charged and generated by $E_0$; it is not proper gauge.
   In the main fixed-anchor policy it is not an independent variation because
   connection compatibility determines it from $r^E,p^E$.

Calling all four operations “edge gauge” would erase the theorem's decisive
endpoint condition.

## 5. Endpoint policy

Let $S_+=\{u=0,v=V\}$ and $S_-=\{v=0,u=U\}$.  The main first-round theorem
uses:

- fixed exact parts of $\bar a_+(V)$ and $\bar a_-(U)$, chosen to vanish;
- fixed outer generator frames, or equivalently gauge parameters that vanish at
  the outer cuts;
- freely transported coexact/curvature data, so the anchor does not set
  $F_{AB}$ to zero;
- retained physical outer fluxes.

For one electric vector harmonic with
$D_AY_{\ell m}=\sqrt{\lambda_\ell}\,\mathbb E_A^{\ell m}$, write

$$
r_A^E=r^E\mathbb E_A,\qquad p_A^E=p^E\mathbb E_A,
\qquad s_\ell=\sqrt{\lambda_\ell}.
$$

Since $\partial_v\bar a_+=r$ and $\partial_u\bar a_-=p$, the anchors give

$$
q^E_+(0)=-\int_0^V r^E(v)\,dv,
\qquad
q^E_-(0)=-\int_0^U p^E(u)\,du.
\tag{5.1}
$$

Connection compatibility therefore fixes

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{rel},\ell m}
=\frac1{s_\ell}
\left(\int_0^U p^E_{\ell m}(u)\,du
-\int_0^V r^E_{\ell m}(v)\,dv\right).}
\tag{5.2}
$$

If the two outer exact anchors are promoted to variables, (5.2) acquires their
difference and $\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}}$ is no longer fixed by
$\mathcal X_F$.  That alternative space is not the main theorem's space.

## 6. Function spaces

### 6.1 Finite harmonic and generator-basis cutoffs

For $1\leq\ell\leq L$, use a real harmonic basis.  Its number of scalar
harmonics is

$$
N_L=\sum_{\ell=1}^L(2\ell+1)=L(L+2).
\tag{6.1}
$$

For the symplectic rank test, choose $K$ Darboux/on-shell pairs in each master
polarization.  Then

$$
d_{\mathrm{master},h}^{\mathrm{sympl}}=2K,
\qquad
d_{E\oplus B,h}^{\mathrm{sympl}}=4K.
\tag{6.2}
$$

Separately, a generator-jet dictionary of order $J$ has $2J+1$ coordinates
per master: one common corner value and $J$ one-sided jets on each sheet.
That odd-dimensional space is used only to test invertibility of the
field-strength/master data dictionary; it is not called a symplectic block.
Neither cutoff is evidence for a continuum completion theorem.

### 6.2 Rapid-angular matched-$H^1$ core and smooth subcore

Let $\mathscr S_{\mathrm{rap}}^1(X)$ consist of matched-$H^1$ generator
profiles whose real spherical-harmonic $H^1$ norms decrease faster than every
power of $1+\lambda_\ell$.  Requiring the same property after every generator
derivative defines the dense smooth-generator subcore.  On that subcore, a
convenient family of seminorms is

$$
p_{j,N}(r)=\sup_{0\leq v\leq V}
\|(1-\Delta_S)^{N/2}\partial_v^jr(v)\|_{L^2(S^2)},
\tag{6.3}
$$

and similarly for $p$.  The transport estimate has the honest shifted form

$$
p_{j,N}(E_+-E_0)\leq
V\,p_{j,N+1}(r),
\qquad
p_{j,N}(b^+-b^0)\leq C V\,p_{j,N+1}(r).
\tag{6.4}
$$

The matched-$H^1$ version uses the corresponding $L^2$ generator norms.
Neighboring angular seminorms make the transport map continuous on both
cores; there is no same-order Sobolev claim hidden in (6.4).

### 6.3 Shifted Sobolev/graph candidate

The natural first candidate places (r,p) at angular order $H^s$ and the
transported normal electric/magnetic fields at $H^{s-1}$.  Corner data are
placed at the transported order, while division by
$s_\ell=\sqrt{\lambda_\ell}$ restores one order for master corner values.
Closedness of compatibility, Hausdorffness of the quotient, and bounded bulk
reconstruction in this completion remain `conditional`.

## 7. Theorem ledger

### T-A — sheet constraint theorem

**Status: proved.** Equations (3.2)--(3.3) define a continuous bijection between
$\mathcal X_F$ and completed sheet fields satisfying the four transport
constraints, on finite harmonic spaces, the rapid matched-$H^1$ core, and its
smooth subcore.  This is not bulk reconstruction.

### T-Ba — anchored corner gauge reduction

**Status: proved.** At finite harmonic/generator cutoff and with (5.1), the
compatible split-frame locus modulo sheet-proper and diagonal gauge is
canonically isomorphic to the intrinsic field-strength characteristic data.
The kernel is exactly the declared proper plus diagonal algebra.  Calculation
05 supplies the closed compatibility map, continuous canonical slice, closed
gauge image, and Hausdorff quotient on the rapid matched-$H^1$ core.

### T-Bb — unanchored equivalence

**Status: false.** If outer exact dressed-connection anchors vary independently,
diagonal reduction leaves relative/outer charged data.  Forgetting them is not
injective and not a symplectic quotient on variable-flux sectors.

### T-C — Maxwell/master commuting diagram

**Status: proved.** For every finite $\ell\geq1$ sector, the exact (E/B)
dictionary identifies Maxwell corner compatibility with matched master corner
values and is invertible.  On the rapid matched-$H^1$ core and its smooth
subcore, continuity follows from the harmonic formulas and neighboring
seminorms.  Bulk Goursat reconstruction is imported only as a read-only lemma
for the next theorem.

### T-D — bulk Maxwell Goursat reconstruction

**Status: proved.** Calculation 05 constructs both inverse composites and a
continuous canonical potential lift on the anchored rapid matched-$H^1$ core.
The shifted Sobolev/graph completion remains `conditional`.

### T-E — Lee--Wald symplectomorphism

**Status: proved.** Calculation 06 proves the finite Stokes identity, direct
Maxwell/master pullback, endpoint ledger, and ambiguity audit on the anchored
rapid matched-$H^1$ core.  The shifted completion remains `conditional`.

### T-F — two-cell full Maxwell composition

**Status: proved.** Calculation 07 defines the complete $F,*F$, endpoint,
connection, and frame trace; proves the distributional gluing criterion;
performs diagonal reduction; and proves internal Lee--Wald cancellation on the
anchored rapid matched-$H^1$ core.

### T-G — three-cell full Maxwell associativity

**Status: proved.** Calculation 08 combines Goursat uniqueness, reduction by
stages, two internal cancellations, and compatibility transitivity.  The new
full-Maxwell regression retains independent E/B and frame/connection variables
on both interfaces.

### T-H — Hamiltonian test algebra

**Status: false.** On the finite anchored cross with variable physical outer
data, $\partial_t$ and $\partial_z$ have nonzero endpoint symplectic anomalies.
$SO(3)$ and cut-gauge subtests pass, but the complete requested algebra does
not.  Calculation 09 records the required endpoint-policy repairs.

### T-I — Peierls/Weyl composition

**Status: conditional.** T-F supplies the symplectic relation, but the declared
gate order stops after the failed finite-cross T-H translation tests.  B1 only;
no Hilbert factorization claim.
