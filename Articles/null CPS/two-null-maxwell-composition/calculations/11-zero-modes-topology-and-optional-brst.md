# Zero Modes, Topology, and Optional BRST

## Status

**Status: conditional.** None of the following is a blocker for the main
$\ell\geq1$, $Q_E=n=0$, trivial-bundle Layer-L or Layer-G theorem. They are
not included in the global energy, Hamiltonian, Peierls, or Weyl sums.

## Separated sectors

- $\ell=0$ electric flux $Q_E$;
- magnetic Chern number
  $n=(2\pi)^{-1}\int_{S^2}F$;
- large-gauge periodicity of an optional flux-frame pair;
- harmonic one-forms and flat holonomies when $H^1(S)\neq0$;
- nontrivial transition cocycles.

The integer $n$ is a sector label, not a tangent oscillator.  A variable
electric flux can be paired with a frame only after deciding whether that frame
is $\mathbb R$- or $U(1)$-valued and which large gauge transformations are
proper, charged, or quotiented.

BRST/BV--BFV language is optional and may be added only after the ordinary
Abelian kernel, quotient, endpoints, and composition theorem are closed.  It
must not be used to hide a failed classical rank or Hausdorff test.
