# Classical product novelty audit

## Verdict

**No standalone novelty claim is justified by the present search.**  The
classical product is a technically coherent, gravity-specific integration of
known null-data, null-action, corner, and characteristic-phase-space
ingredients.  The inspected literature did not reveal the entire exact
combination below in one construction, but a negative focused search is not a
proof of novelty.

The appropriate status is **project-specific integration, with a potentially
new combination**.  It should be frozen as methodology/background unless a
concrete closing wall, a functional theorem, or a new obstruction supplies a
sharply stated gravity result.

## Product audited

The comparison target is not any individual formula.  It is the combined
classical product

> EH/HF action-derived finite double-null characteristic reduction + explicit
> affine Raychaudhuri Green pullback + corner area/boost + endpoint-map/twist
> moment-map reduction + independent first-jet composition + explicit
> affine-to-area canonical map with endpoint terms.

## Source-by-source comparison

### Reisenberger

- [gr-qc/0703134](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0703134) develops free initial
  data on two intersecting null hypersurfaces and their symplectic structure.
- [arXiv:1211.3880](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.3880) gives the double-null
  symplectic form with endpoint maps and their momenta.  Its endpoint momentum
  is the coefficient of $\delta s_A^i$ integrated over the common initial
  parameter domain.  This supports the corrected source-density reading
  $\widetilde\tau_{Ai}^{\mathrm{Reis}}=\pi_{Ai}$.

These papers are the closest antecedent for the double-null and endpoint
sector.  The present project does not treat their formulas as an action-first
derivation of the whole audited product.

### Hopfm\"uller--Freidel and null actions

- [Hopfm\"uller--Freidel 1611.03096](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03096)
  identifies spin-$0/1/2$ canonical pairs on a null hypersurface and supplies
  the bulk-plus-cut representative used here.
- [Lehner--Myers--Poisson--Sorkin 1609.00207](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.00207),
  [Parattu et al. 1501.01053](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.01053), and
  [Jubb et al. 1612.00149](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00149) establish the
  surrounding null-boundary/joint action technology.

They establish action and corner ingredients, not the full finite
double-null reduction-and-composition chain audited here.

### Recent characteristic phase-space work

- [Chandrasekaran--Flanagan 2309.03871](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03871)
  treats the phase space associated with two intersecting null hypersurfaces,
  including corner area/relative-boost structure and symmetry reduction.
- [Ciambelli--Klinger 2607.07785](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07785) gives
  double-null characteristic data, null constraints, and corner gluing
  structure.
- [CFL 2608.14449](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14449) treats the phase space on
  a caustic-free null segment, including its spin sectors and Green-kernel
  structure, while distinguishing its single-segment scope from a complete
  double-null corner construction.

These sources substantially narrow any novelty claim.  None should be read as
evidence that the project's assembled product is new; they show that most of
its gravity ingredients already have close antecedents.

## Gravity-specific content beyond the Maxwell benchmark

There is real gravity-specific structure:

- nonlinear shear focusing in Raychaudhuri and its nonlocal variation map;
- Damour transport of the normal connection/twist;
- the shared area/relative-boost corner pair and normal-bundle gauge;
- field-dependent affine-to-area charts and first-jet matching of
  $(q_{AB},B_{AB})$.

This is enough to make the calculation useful as a gravity methodology and a
controlled benchmark.  It is not yet enough to justify reopening the gravity
project as a research direction: the general $f,g$ law is generic cotangent
geometry, the moment-map structure has strong antecedents, and the physical
closing-wall/functional theorems remain absent.

## Decision rule

Freeze the chain now.  Reopen only if at least one of the following occurs:

1. an action-derived closing wall produces a new obstruction or a new
   compositional charge;
2. the full functional quotient/nondegeneracy theorem closes in a natural
   topology;
3. a source is found that changes the current classification; or
4. a manuscript comparison identifies a precise theorem that is absent from
   the antecedents and is not merely their juxtaposition.
