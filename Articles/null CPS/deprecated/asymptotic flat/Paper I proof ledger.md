# Paper I proof ledger

## Frozen scope

Paper I is restricted to pure Maxwell theory on the exterior null collar

$$
\mathcal M_{R_0,R}^{I}
=
\left\{
(u,r,\phi)
\ \middle|\
u\in I=[u_-,u_+],\ R_0\leq r\leq R,\ \phi\in S^1
\right\}
$$

of fixed three-dimensional Minkowski spacetime in a fixed Bondi frame。The defining function、null normalization、inner pullback and finite log-cap sequence $\mathbf K=(K_m)$ are fixed。The gauge-fixed history space is the radial-gauge slice of the gauge-saturated parent space defined in `3d Maxwell.md` sections 4--5。

The counterterm comparison uses exactly two categories：

1. the **strict intrinsic gauge-invariant category** generated by the induced background geometry、$F_{ij}$ and finitely many tangential derivatives；
2. the **extended normal-field category** which additionally admits the cutoff fields

$$
\mathcal E_R=-RF_{ur},
\qquad
\mathcal C_R=2\sqrt R\,F_{r\phi}.
$$

No Maxwell equation is used in the main action theorem。

## Frozen central theorem

### Paper-I Main Theorem

For every fixed finite nondecreasing log-cap sequence $\mathbf K$ and bounded coefficient set in the Fréchet topology of `definitions.md`：

1. no counterterm in the strict intrinsic gauge-invariant category cancels the constant-Coulomb divergence

$$
\frac{\log(R/r_0)}2\int_Idu\int_{S^1}d\phi\,E^2；
$$

2. in the extended normal-field category，the two explicit Coulomb and radiative counterterms of `3d Maxwell.md` section 9 make the regulated relative action converge locally in $C^2$ as $R\to\infty$；
3. with arbitrary variations at $u=u_\pm$，the same relative action has finite null-lid potentials and corner terms and hence a finite first variation；
4. these statements hold on the off-shell history space and therefore do not select the Maxwell-EOM log cap $n\leq\lceil m\rceil$。

The theorem is category-relative。It does not claim uniqueness of the extended scheme、a maximum history space、or a preferred boundary polarization。
