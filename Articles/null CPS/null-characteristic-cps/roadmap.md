## Stopping Rule for a First Paper

The stopping rule has been met for the declared zero-flux/zero-Chern product
benchmark: Phase II closes for $S^2_R$, the half-cross and closed intrinsic
profile theorems are explicit, the global gauge policy is fixed, and the
genuine trace-map composition regression passes.  The benchmark should now be
frozen and moved into manuscript distillation and bibliography polishing, not
extended to curved backgrounds, BRST, Yang--Mills, gravity or
regional-factorization claims.
