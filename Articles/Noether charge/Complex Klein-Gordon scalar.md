## Klein-Gordon

The action of a complex Klein-Gordon field is

$$\begin{align}
S=-\int{\rm d}^{d+1}x\sqrt{-g}(g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi^*+m^2\phi\phi^*)
\end{align}$$

The variation is

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta S&=-\int_M{\rm d}^{d+1}x\sqrt{-g}(g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\delta\phi^*+g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi^*\partial_\nu\delta\phi+m^2\phi\delta\phi^*+m^2\phi^*\delta\phi)\\
&=\int_M{\rm d}^{d+1}x\sqrt{-g}[(\nabla^2\phi-m^2\phi)\delta\phi^*+(\nabla^2\phi^*-m^2\phi^*)\delta\phi]\\
&+\int_{\partial M}{\rm d}^dx\sqrt{\sigma}\tau^\mu(\partial_\mu\phi\delta\phi^*+\partial_\mu\phi^*\delta\phi)
\end{aligned}$$

The (pre)symplectic form is

Here $\tau^\mu$ is the future-directed unit normal to the Cauchy slice.

$$\begin{align}
\theta=\int_{\Sigma}{\rm d}^dx\sqrt{\sigma}\tau^\mu(\partial_\mu\phi\delta\phi^*+\partial_\mu\phi^*\delta\phi)
\end{align}$$

This is related to inner product. We will begin the discussion later.
