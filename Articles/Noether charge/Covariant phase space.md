# Covariant Phase Space Formalism and Noëther theorem

## Ordinary Approach

$$\begin{align}
S=\int_ML+\int_{\partial M}\ell
\end{align}$$

suppose dynamical fields are denoted as $\phi^a$. Then the variation of the bulk Lagrangian always have the form

$$\begin{align}
\delta L=E_a\delta\phi^a+{\rm d}\Theta
\end{align}$$

Where $\Theta$ is called the symplectic potential. The variation of the action is thus

$$\begin{align}
\delta S=\int_ME_a\delta\phi^a+\int_{\partial M}(\delta\ell+\Theta)
\end{align}$$

For $\delta S$ to be zero, we make the ansatz that

$$\begin{align}
(\Theta+\delta\ell)|_{\Gamma}={\rm d}C
\end{align}$$

Then

$$\begin{aligned}
\delta S&=\int_{M}E_a\delta\phi^a+\int_{\Sigma_+-\Sigma_-}(\Theta+\delta\ell)+\int_{\Gamma}{\rm d}C\\
&=\int_{M}E_a\delta\phi^a+\int_{\Sigma_+-\Sigma_-}(\Theta+\delta\ell)+\int_{\partial\Gamma}C\\
\\
&=\int_{M}E_a\delta\phi^a+\int_{\Sigma_+-\Sigma_-}(\Theta+\delta\ell-{\rm d}C)
\end{aligned}$$

Then we have the equation of motions and the generalized ansatz

$$
\tag{}
\begin{align}
E_a=0\\
\Theta+\delta\ell={\rm d}C
\end{align}
$$

The (pre)symplectic current is defined as

$$\begin{align}
\omega=\delta(\Theta+\delta\ell-{\rm d}C)=\delta(\Theta-{\rm d}C)
\end{align}$$

On the one hand, $\omega$ is closed as a two-form on the (pre)phase space. On the other hand, $\omega$ is also closed as a $(d-1)$-form on spacetime

$$\begin{align}
{\rm d}\omega={\rm d}\delta(\Theta-{\rm d}C)=\delta{\rm d}\Theta=\delta(\delta L-E^a\delta\phi_a)=-\delta E^a\wedge\delta\phi_a\approx0
\end{align}$$

The last equality holds on the solution space, with tangent variations satisfying $\delta E^a=0$.

Finally we define the (pre)symplectic form $\tilde\Omega$ on $\tilde{\cal P}$ as

$$\begin{align}
\tilde\Omega=\int_{\Sigma}\omega
\end{align}$$

By definition, the variation of any dynamical field $\phi$ under the infinitesimal diffeomorphism generated by a vector field $\xi^\mu$ is

$$\begin{align}
\delta_\xi={\cal L}_\xi\phi
\end{align}$$

Define vector field $X_\xi$ on configuration space

$$\begin{align}
X_\xi=\int{\rm d}^dx{\cal L}_\xi\phi^a\frac{\delta}{\delta\phi^a}
\end{align}$$

Then the infinitesimal diffeomorphism transformation of any configuration space tensor $\sigma$ is given by

$$\begin{align}
\delta_\xi\sigma={\cal L}_{X_\xi}\sigma
\end{align}$$

A configuration space tensor $\sigma$ which is also a spacetime tensor locally constructed out of the dynamical and background fields is ***covariant*** under the infinitesimal diffeomorphism generated by a vector field $\xi^\mu$ if

$$\begin{align}
\delta_\xi\sigma={\cal L}_\xi\sigma
\end{align}$$

\tag{2.2}

\delta \mathbf{L} & =\mathbf{E}^{\mu \nu}\delta g_{\mu \nu}+\mathrm{d}\Theta \\
\mathbf{E}^{\mu \nu} & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}\left( -R^{\mu \nu}+\frac{1}{2}Rg^{\mu \nu}+g^{\mu \nu} \right)\cdot \frac{1}{3!}\varepsilon_{\mu_{0}\mu_{1}\mu_{2}}\mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{0}}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{1}}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{2}} \\
 \Theta & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}(g^{\rho \mu}\nabla^{\nu}\delta g_{\mu \nu}-g^{\mu \nu}\nabla^{\rho}\delta g_{\mu \nu})\cdot \frac{1}{2!}\varepsilon_{\rho \mu_{1}\mu_{2}}\mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{1}}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{2}}
\end{align}
$$

then (this is the most important part)

$$
\tag{2.3}
\begin{align}
-X_{\xi}\cdot \Omega & =\delta Q_{\xi}=-X_{\xi}\cdot \int_{\Sigma}\delta \Theta \\
 X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta & =\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\Theta-\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta) \\
 & =\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\Theta+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta-\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta) \\
 & =\xi \cdot \mathrm{d}\Theta+\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \Theta)+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta-\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta) \\
 & =\xi \cdot(\delta \mathbf{L}-\mathbf{E}^{\mu \nu}\delta g_{\mu \nu})+\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \Theta)+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta-\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta) \\

 & \approx \delta(\xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta)-(\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta)+\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \Theta) \\
-X_{\xi}\cdot \Omega & = \int_{\Sigma}\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})+\int_{\Sigma}(\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta)-\int_{\partial \Sigma}\xi \cdot \Theta
\end{align}
$$

For the three-dimensional Einstein-Hilbert Lagrangian with cosmological constant, the Noether charge form is the same Komar form as in the zero-cosmological-constant case:

$$
\tag{2.4}
\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L} & =\mathrm{d}Q_{\xi}-2\mathbf{E}^{\mu\nu}\xi_{\nu}\cdot\frac{1}{2!}\varepsilon_{\mu\mu_{1}\mu_{2}}\mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{1}}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\mu_{2}} \\
Q_{\xi} & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}(\nabla^{\nu}\xi^{\mu}-\nabla^{\mu}\xi^{\nu})\varepsilon_{\mu\nu\rho}\mathrm{d}x^{\rho}.
\end{align}
$$

On shell this gives

$$
\tag{2.5}
\begin{align}
-X_{\xi}\cdot \Omega & \approx \delta\int_{\partial\Sigma}Q_{\xi}+\int_{\Sigma}(\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta)-\int_{\partial\Sigma}\xi\cdot\Theta .
\end{align}
$$
