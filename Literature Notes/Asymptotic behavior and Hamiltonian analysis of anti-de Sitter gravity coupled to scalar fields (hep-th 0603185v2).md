abstract: "We examine anti-de Sitter gravity minimally coupled to a self-interacting scalar field in $D\\geq 4$ dimensions when the mass of the scalar field is in the range $m_{\\ast}^{2}\\leq m^{2}<m_{\\ast} ^{2}+l^{-2}$. Here, $l$ is the AdS radius, and $m_{\\ast}^{2}$ is the Breitenlohner-Freedman mass. We show that even though the scalar field generically has a slow fall-off at infinity which back reacts on the metric so as to modify its standard asymptotic behavior, one can still formulate asymptotic conditions (i) that are anti-de Sitter invariant; and (ii) that allows the construction of well-defined and finite Hamiltonian generators for all elements of the anti-de Sitter algebra. This requires imposing a functional relationship on the coefficients $a$, $b$ that control the two independent terms in the asymptotic expansion of the scalar field. The anti-de Sitter charges are found to involve a scalar field contribution. Subtleties associated with the self-interactions of the scalar field as well as its gravitational back reaction, not discussed in previous treatments, are explicitly analyzed. In particular, it is shown that the fields develop extra logarithmic branches for specific values of the scalar field mass (in addition to the known logarithmic branch at the B-F bound)."

In this paper, Henneaux, Martínez, Troncoso and Zanelli study Einstein gravity on asymptotically $\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}$ spacetimes, with $d+1\geqslant 4$, minimally coupled to a self-interacting scalar. In addition to the mass term, consider the potential terms

$$\begin{align}
V_{\mathrm{int}}(\phi)=\frac{a}{3}\phi^3+\frac{b}{4}\phi^4+\frac{c}{5}\phi^5.
\end{align}$$

On substituting the slow branch $r^{-\Delta_-}$ into the scalar equation, $V_{\mathrm{int}}'(\phi)$ produces sources at orders $r^{-2\Delta_-}$, $r^{-3\Delta_-}$, and $r^{-4\Delta_-}$. Lower-order corrections and gravitational backreaction can also contribute to the coefficients at these orders. A source at order $r^{-k\Delta_-}$ resonates with the fast branch when $k\Delta_-=\Delta_+$. Since $\Delta_-+\Delta_+=d$, the three resonant values of $\Delta_+$ are $2d/3$, $3d/4$, and $4d/5$, when they lie in the allowed mass window. If the total source at that order is nonzero, a pure $r^{-\Delta_+}$ correction cannot solve the inhomogeneous equation, and one must include a term proportional to $r^{-\Delta_+}\log r$. The authors relax the scalar and metric falloffs to include the resulting terms and show that the gravitational and scalar contributions to the charge variation cancel their divergences. They then relate the fast and slow coefficients to make the charges integrable and preserve the asymptotic AdS symmetry. [§§4.6 and 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0603185#page=24)

$$\begin{align}
\phi & =r^{-\Delta}\psi+\beta_{1}\psi ^{2}r^{-2\Delta}+\beta_{2}\psi ^{3}r^{-3\Delta}+\beta_{3}\psi ^{4}r^{-4\Delta}+r^{\Delta-d}\chi+\dots \\
h_{rr} & =\dfrac{\kappa}{r^{2}}(\alpha_{1}\psi ^{2}r^{-2\Delta}+\alpha _{2}\psi ^{3}r^{-3\Delta}+\alpha _{3}\psi ^{4}r^{-4\Delta}+\alpha _{4}\psi ^{5}r^{-5\Delta})+\dfrac{f_{rr}}{r^{5}}+\dots \\
h_{ra} & =\dfrac{f_{ra}}{r^{2}}+\dots,\quad h_{ab}=\dfrac{f_{ab}}{r}+\dots
\end{align}$$

The logarithmic branches are not needed to see the effect relevant to our problem. We set $V_{\mathrm{int}}=0$ and ask whether the metric backreaction of a nonzero slow branch fits our chosen gravitational falloffs. In $\mathrm{AdS}_3$, writing $\Delta=\Delta_-$, the scalar stress tensor is already quadratic in the slow coefficient. In Fefferman–Graham gauge the resulting correction to $g_{ab}$ is of order $r^{2-2\Delta}$. It exceeds the standard $O(1)$ falloff for any $0<\Delta<1$, but it fits our weaker condition $g_{ab}=r^2g^{(0)}_{ab}+o(r)$ for $\Delta>1/2$. At $\Delta=1/2$ it is already of order $r$, regardless of the separate resonance in the scalar equation. Our question is which falloffs admit this backreaction while keeping the action finite off shell.
