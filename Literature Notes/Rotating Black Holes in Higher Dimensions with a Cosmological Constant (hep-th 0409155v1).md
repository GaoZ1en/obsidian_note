publication date: 2004-09-15T11:14:09Z
abstract: "We present the metric for a rotating black hole with a cosmological constant and with arbitrary angular momenta in all higher dimensions. The metric is given in both Kerr-Schild and Boyer-Lindquist form. In the Euclidean-signature case, we also obtain smooth compact Einstein spaces on associated S^{D-2} bundles over S^2, infinitely many for each odd D\ge 5. Applications to string theory and M-theory are indicated."
