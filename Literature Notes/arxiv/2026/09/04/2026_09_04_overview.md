### 2. [[Boundary-Value Problem in Type IIB Supergravity and Holography (2609.03126v1)]]

- **Why high:** `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`. It derives the boundary polarization and ensemble from the action variation rather than adding a free-energy sign rule after the fact.
- **Source-derived:** the field-by-field canonical momenta, Legendre boundary term, PST comparison, D1–D5 calculation, warped-$\mathrm{EAdS}_6$ example, and stated global assumptions were reconstructed from the complete PDF/TeX source.
- **Checked:** the ensemble variation, D1–D5 3d/10d coefficient map, $c=6N_1N_5$, and the $\mathrm{EAdS}_3$/$\mathrm{EAdS}_6$ extrinsic curvatures reproduce exactly.
- **Boundary:** the action fixes bare canonical momentum. Its identification with shifted quantized Page momentum is proved only when the shift is inert, as in the examples; the general fixed-Page-charge problem remains open.

### 3. [[Dynamical Selection of Horizon-BMS Goldstone Modes in Evaporating Black Holes (2609.03477v1)]]

- **Why high:** `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`. It explicitly attempts to turn a horizon large diffeomorphism into a dynamical Goldstone sector coupled to evaporation, making it a direct stress test of action-first boundary reasoning.
- **Source-derived:** the 29-page PDF and complete TeX source were mapped through the quadratic action, separated angular problem, mass ODE, Padé profile, and claimed filtering mechanism.
- **Checked:** xAct reproduces the stated $G_{vv}|_{r=0}$, and Mathematica reproduces $f^2=Cm^3/m'$ from $f_3=0$. Mathematica also gives a smooth $k=0$ constant counterexample to the blanket rejection of Ansatz I and shows that the printed supertranslation vector misses the angular derivative required by its gauge condition.
- **Verdict:** the central coupled-evaporation claim is not proved. The mass ODE comes only from the Goldstone equation and the final fields are never checked against $G_{vv}=T_{vv}$; moreover, $N$ is constant within Ansatz II, so variation across an $m(N)$ family cannot be interpreted as time-dependent filtering along one black-hole history.

### 4. [[Quasi-local form for alpha-z Renyi QNEC from fixed-ray escorts (2609.04016v1)]]

- **Why high:** `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`. Its core mechanism is the failure of nonlinear escort formation to commute with restriction to a moving region, isolated as an explicit transport response.
- **Source-derived:** the 46-page generated PDF and complete TeX source were mapped through the fixed-ray and Holevo decompositions, perturbative BKM metric, ball bound, full bilocal null Hessian, conjectural diagonal QNEC, and coherent free-scalar test.
- **Checked:** a noncommuting $2\times2$ Mathematica test reproduces the fixed-ray integral and Holevo identities to below $6\times10^{-17}$. The coherent-state $\beta$ integral, sandwiched coefficient, and cut-Hessian derivative also reproduce.
- **Boundary:** the regulated decompositions and Hessian identity are exact under the paper's support, smoothness, and common-regulator assumptions. General $\alpha$--$z$ QNEC positivity remains explicitly conjectural; it is verified only for the stated coherent family, and the separate local-energy/entropy/transport terms need not have regulator-independent continuum limits.

### 5. [[Boundary conditions for axionic wormholes, imaginary distance bound and KSW allowability (2609.04137v1)]]

- **Why high:** `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`. It separates the metric boundary polarization, gravitational contour, saddle relevance, flux-sector convergence, and KSW allowability in an explicit wormhole model.
- **Source-derived:** the 43-page PDF and complete TeX source were mapped through the axion/three-form Fourier duality, exact Dirichlet fixed-flux kernel, Dirichlet and Neumann saddle geometries, PL contour analysis, interpolating IDB, and weak-KSW exclusion test.
- **Checked:** Mathematica reproduces the mixed Neumann boundary conditions, GS Hamiltonian constraint, flux-sum phase and strict convergence strip, and the generic weak-KSW boundary curve with zero residual.
- **Boundary:** the Dirichlet kernel is exact only for the selected non-self-adjoint $q>0$ Feynman prescription; the Neumann flux sum is semiclassical; PL irrelevance is tied to the positive-real lapse contour and physical lift. The KSW calculation proves compatibility through a necessary spatial condition plus duality/sheet inputs, not full equivalence between KSW and IDB.
