1. [[The Kerr-CFT energy gap (2609.17656v1)|The Kerr/CFT energy gap]] — Robert Penna. `[T1-charge; T1-Wald-CPS; T1-boundary; T1-symmetry; T2-dS-BH-holography]`
   - **Verdict:** correct as an exhibition of a reference-metric/order-of-limits ambiguity, not as a derivation of a unique Kerr/CFT energy gap.    - **Verdict:** correct under the identity-component, regular-dual, nowhere-vanishing-orbit, formal continuum-measure, Shavgulidze-completion, ergodicity, and strong-continuity assumptions stated in the note. It is not a classification of every singular orbit or representation.

The source's displayed `QCS_3` direct product is a typo: its own action and summary require a semidirect product.

3. [[Canonical and symplectic analysis of the Holst action in the G to 0 limit (2609.17833v1)|Canonical and symplectic analysis of the Holst action in the G→0 limit]] — Victor Julián Pérez-Aquino, Alberto Escalante. `[T1-symplectic; T2-model]`

# Why these match my current projects

`2609.17666` is the strongest direct bridge to the regional-algebra programme. It supplies a nontrivial corner symmetry group, central extensions, coadjoint data, and induced representations; the note records exactly where regular-orbit and continuum-measure assumptions enter, so these structures can be reused without pretending that singular sectors are classified.

`2609.17656` isolates a problem that recurs in charge sewing: subtraction/reference data are part of the definition of a Hamiltonian observable. Agreement with Cardy scaling does not by itself choose the reference prescription.

`2609.17582` is an especially clean action-first model. The equation removed by radial gauge fixing survives as a constraint, and the constrained Noether charge agrees with a covariant trace-current flux. This is the same methodological discipline needed before quotienting regional boundary-history brackets.

`2609.18028` demonstrates the failure mode on the other side. A metric, near-horizon falloff, and familiar charge formula do not define a covariant phase space without an action and complete field content. Its xAct obstruction makes the gap explicit rather than philosophical.

`2609.17833` is relevant to first-order and Holst testbeds, but mainly as a warning that a degenerate presymplectic matrix must be resolved before first-/second-class counting or gauge fixing.
