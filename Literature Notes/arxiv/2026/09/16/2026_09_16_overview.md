# High-priority recommendations

1. [[Perturbations of a Schwarzschild black hole and Newman-Unti gauge (2609.15730v1)|Perturbations of a Schwarzschild black hole and Newman--Unti gauge]] — Pujian Mao, Baijun Zeng, Kai-Yu Zhang. `[T1-boundary; T1-symmetry; T1-charge; T2-spectral; T2-dS-BH-holography]`
   - **Verdict:** correct under the paper's linearized Schwarzschild, NU-gauge, falloff, and quasinormal-mode assumptions. The work extracts Bondi/NU data and constraint evolution, but does not furnish a nonlinear phase space or an independently derived finite Hamiltonian charge algebra.
   - **Independent evidence:** Mathematica reproduced the radial recurrence through $a_5$ and the retarded-time primitive. Explicit unit-sphere component calculations checked the even/odd tensor-harmonic divergence identities for $\ell=2,3$. A general xAct proof was attempted but blocked by `ToCanonical::noident`, so the angular verification remains finite-mode rather than general.

2. [[The Conformal w-Algebra (2609.13381v1)|The Conformal w-Algebra]] — Simon Heuveline, Nia Robles Del Pino, Andrew Strominger. `[T1-symmetry; T1-charge nearby; T2-celestial-carrollian; T2-dS-BH-holography; A-big-name]`
   - **Verdict:** correct as an algebraic and twistor-action construction. The conformal $w$-algebra unifies flat and cosmological gravitational soft algebras, but the paper does not establish finite integrable surface charges, flux laws, central extensions, or a specified covariant phase space realizing the algebra.
   - **Independent evidence:** Sage checked the defining vector-field bracket, the homogeneity ideal, all 256 index choices of the global $\mathfrak{sl}(4)$ bracket, a nontrivial $\Lambda$-deformed soft bracket, recovery of its two components, and one exact Jacobiator.

3. [[An entropic characterization of Haag duality (2609.13550v1)|An entropic characterization of Haag duality]] — Ruizhi Liu, Lauritz van Luijk. `[T1-boundary; T2-spectral; T3-math]`
   - **Verdict:** correct under the paper's factor, finite-dimensional exhaustion, tomographic-completeness, purity, and normality assumptions. The theorem equates Haag duality with asymptotic vanishing of a shell conditional mutual information; importing it to continuum type-III QFT or gauge algebras requires additional split/regulator and centre analysis.
   - **Independent evidence:** Mathematica checked on a deterministic four-qubit pure state that the ordinary conditional mutual information, its conditioning-side exchange, and the paper's finite-factor entropy rewrite agree to 48 decimal digits. The multiple-cone and recovery conclusions remain source-derived theorems, not independently re-proved here.

4. [[Hadamard states for spacetimes with timelike boundaries (2609.13358v1)|Hadamard states for spacetimes with timelike boundaries]] — Alessandro Pietro Contini, Alexander Strohmaier. `[T1-boundary; T1-symplectic; T2-spectral; T2-model]`
   - **Verdict:** correct under scalar formal self-adjointness, normal hyperbolicity, global hyperbolicity with timelike boundary, positivity, and homogeneous Dirichlet assumptions. It proves existence and smooth universality of quasifree Dirichlet--Hadamard states, not the corresponding result for open sewing ports, gauge constraints, or non-Dirichlet boundary conditions.
   - **Independent evidence:** Mathematica checked exactly, mode by mode, the complex-structure identities, positivity of the one-particle norm, and the spectral causal-propagator initial conditions. The $b$-microlocal, Lopatinskij--Shapiro, and deformation arguments were inspected in full but not machine-verified.
