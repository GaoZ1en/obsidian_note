# Result and claim boundary

The paper proves an equivalence between an operator-algebraic completeness condition and an asymptotic entropic condition. Let $A,B\subset B(\mathcal H)$ be commuting von Neumann factors, generated by increasing sequences of finite-dimensional matrix algebras $A_n,B_n$, and assume

$$
A\vee B=B(\mathcal H).
$$

With

$$
D_n=A_n\vee B_n,
\qquad
A_{m,n}=A_m'\cap A_n,
\qquad
B_{m,n}=B_m'\cap B_n,
$$

the following are equivalent:

1. Haag duality, $A=B'$;
2. for every unit vector $\Omega$ and every $m$,
   $$
   \lim_{n\to\infty}I(D_m:D_n'\mid A_{m,n})_\Omega=0;
   $$
3. for some unit vector $\Omega$, the same outer-radius limit tends to zero as $m\to\infty$.

This is a theorem under the paper's stated algebraic hypotheses. It is not a general theorem for arbitrary continuum QFT nets: the finite-dimensional exhaustion and tomographic completeness assumptions do real work.

# Why this is high priority

This paper hits the vault's regional/global observable-algebra programme directly. It turns the desired sewing statement

$$
\text{all operators compatible with the complement already belong to the region}
$$

into the vanishing of an information-theoretic obstruction across a separating shell. In the notation above, the inclusion $A\subseteq B'$ is automatic from locality; the conditional mutual information measures the residual failure of the reverse inclusion.

For the current gluing programme, the useful conceptual point is not merely that conditional mutual information appears. It is that the proof isolates a recovery map

$$
\alpha_m:\widehat A_{m,\infty}\longrightarrow A_{m,\infty},
\qquad
\widehat A:=B',
$$

and uses asymptotically perfect recovery to approximate every $x\in B'$ weakly by elements of $A$. This is close in spirit to reconstructing a regional observable from finite interior data plus controlled collar data, but here the control is entropic rather than action-first or symplectic.

# Finite-shell entropy formula

For a pure global vector $\Omega$, the four factors

$$
D_m,quad A_{m,n},quad B_{m,n},quad D_n'
$$

form a finite/type-I four-partite decomposition. Purity lets the authors rewrite the conditional mutual information using only the finite-dimensional factors:

$$
\begin{aligned}
I(D_m:D_n'\mid A_{m,n})_\Omega
={}&S(D_m\vee A_{m,n})_\Omega
+S(D_m\vee B_{m,n})_\Omega\\
&-S(A_{m,n})_\Omega-S(B_{m,n})_\Omega.
\end{aligned}
$$

The same purity argument gives the exchange symmetry

$$
I(D_m:D_n'\mid A_{m,n})_\Omega
=I(D_m:D_n'\mid B_{m,n})_\Omega.
$$

Thus the condition does not privilege which member of the complementary pair is called the region.

# Conditional-entropy duality

The technical bridge is a duality formula valid when a finite-dimensional factor $F=B(\mathcal F)$ is coupled to a von Neumann algebra $M$. With conditional entropy defined by Araki relative entropy,

$$
H(F\mid M)_\Omega
:=-D(\omega_{F\otimes M}\Vert \operatorname{Tr}\otimes\omega_M),
$$

the authors prove

$$
H(F\mid M)_\Omega+H(F\mid M')_\Omega=0.
$$

They obtain this from Connes spatial-derivative duality and the dual operator-valued weight of the normalized partial trace. The finite factor is essential here: it makes the trace normalization and the relevant index finite.

For fixed $m$, exhaustion and continuity of relative entropy give

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}I(D_m:D_n'\mid A_{m,n})_\Omega
=H(D_m\mid A_{m,\infty})_\Omega
+H(D_m\mid B_{m,\infty})_\Omega.
$$

Writing

$$
A_{m,\infty}=A_m'\cap A,
\qquad
\widehat A_{m,\infty}=A_m'\cap B',
$$

conditional-entropy duality turns the right-hand side into

$$
H(D_m\mid A_{m,\infty})_\Omega
-H(D_m\mid\widehat A_{m,\infty})_\Omega.
$$

Equivalently, it is the data-processing defect

$$
I(D_m:\widehat A_{m,\infty})_\Omega
-I(D_m:A_{m,\infty})_\Omega\geq0.
$$

# Recovery-map proof of the converse

Vanishing of the data-processing defect activates an approximate Petz-recovery theorem. Because the marginal state need not be faithful, the appendix first cuts down to support projections and constructs a normal completely positive subunital recovery map. The resulting estimate is

$$
\begin{aligned}
&I(D_m:\widehat A_{m,\infty})_\Omega
-I(D_m:A_{m,\infty})_\Omega\\
&\qquad\geq \frac14
\left\|
\omega_{D_m\vee\widehat A_{m,\infty}}
-\omega_{D_m\vee A_{m,\infty}}
\circ(\operatorname{id}_{D_m}\otimes\alpha_m)
\right\|^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

For $x\in\widehat A=B'$, define

$$
x_m=(\operatorname{id}_{A_m}\otimes\alpha_m)(x)\in A.
$$

The recovery estimate makes matrix elements of $x_m-x$ vanish on the dense subspace $\bigcup_kD_k\Omega$. Uniform boundedness then gives weak convergence $x_m\to x$. Since $A$ is weakly closed, $x\in A$, so $B'\subseteq A$ and hence $A=B'$.

The proof therefore supplies more structure than a numerical diagnostic: it identifies the channel that removes the apparent excess algebra and shows how zero defect promotes approximate reconstruction to equality of von Neumann algebras.

# Spin-system geometry

For a pure state on a quasi-local spin algebra, let $A$ be a spatial region, $B=A^c$, $D_r$ an inner ball, and

$$
A_{r,R}=A\cap(D_R\setminus D_r),
\qquad
B_{r,R}=B\cap(D_R\setminus D_r).
$$

The criterion becomes

$$
\lim_{R\to\infty}I(D_r:D_R^c\mid A_{r,R})_\omega=0.
$$

Haag duality holds precisely when this vanishes for every fixed $r$, or equivalently when the resulting limit tends to zero as $r\to\infty$ for one pure GNS vector.

The shell matters conceptually. Conditioning on only the $A$-part of the annulus tests whether the complementary $B$-part and the exterior retain information about the inner region that cannot be mediated by $A$. A nonzero limit is therefore an obstruction to assigning the full commutant to the regional algebra.

# Area law and multiple cones

Assume the strict entropy law

$$
S(X)=\alpha|\partial X|-\gamma,b_0(\partial X)+o(1),
$$

with the error tending to zero for all four regions entering the conditional mutual information. If $A$ is a union of $q$ disjoint cones, boundary terms cancel and the authors obtain

$$
\lim_{R\to\infty}I(D_r:D_R^c\mid A_{r,R})
=2(q-1)\gamma.
$$

Consequently:

- a single cone satisfies Haag duality under this entropy law;
- a union of at least two cones satisfies it exactly when $\gamma=0$;
- for a non-abelian anyon of quantum dimension $d_a$, the single-cone conditional mutual information is shifted by $2\log d_a$, reproducing failure of cone duality when $d_a>1$.

These conclusions are conditional on the stated entropy law and its uniform error control. The paper explicitly notes that the constant called $\gamma$ is not invariant under arbitrary finite-depth circuits, so the associated Haag-duality property is not a phase invariant in this generality.

# Translation-invariant spin chains

For a translation-invariant pure state on a one-dimensional spin chain, set

$$
S_n=S([0,n)),
\qquad
s=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{S_n}{n}.
$$

For the two half-chains and finite intervals chosen in the paper,

$$
I(D_m:D_n^c\mid A_{m,n})
=2\bigl(S_{n+m}-S_{n-m}\bigr).
$$

Using

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}(S_{n+1}-S_n)=s,
$$

the outer limit is

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}I(D_m:D_n^c\mid A_{m,n})=4ms.
$$

Thus half-chain Haag duality is equivalent to $s=0$. The authors emphasize that both the general zero-entropy-density conjecture for translation-invariant pure states and the corresponding half-chain Haag-duality conjecture remain open; earlier claimed proofs contain gaps.

# Relation to action-first sewing

The paper gives a strong algebraic target for the vault's sewing programme, but not a replacement for the covariant-phase-space construction.

The compatible pieces are:

1. **Regional completeness:** $A=B'$ is exactly the statement that no extra operator commutes with the complementary region.
2. **Finite collar:** the annulus $D_R\setminus D_r$ acts as a controllable interface separating interior and exterior.
3. **Recovery:** the Petz map provides an explicit approximation mechanism from the enlarged commutant algebra back to the regional algebra.
4. **Continuum warning:** the theorem assumes finite-dimensional local exhaustions, while continuum QFT local algebras are normally type III and need a split/nuclearity or regulated approximation before the criterion can be imported.

For scalar response and gauge sewing, a concrete next question is whether the finite-port/truncated regional algebras already used in the vault admit the four-factor decomposition required here, and whether the response kernel controls the same data-processing defect. For gauge theory, Gauss-law centres and edge sectors obstruct the factor assumption and should be expected to modify the criterion rather than disappear.

# Verification log

## Checked

- The source was read through the abstract theorem, its conditional-entropy and recovery proof, the spin-system applications, the translation-invariant chain argument, and the non-faithful recovery appendix.
- Mathematica evaluated a deterministic pure state on four qubits, identified as $(D,A,B,C)$, and checked numerically to 48 decimal digits that
  $$
  I(D:C\mid A)=I(D:C\mid B)
  $$
  and that the ordinary definition equals the finite-factor rewrite
  $$
  I(D:C\mid A)=S(DA)+S(DB)-S(A)-S(B).
  $$
  This is a finite-dimensional consistency check of the purity identities, not a proof of the asymptotic theorem.
- The spin-chain factor is internally consistent: from
  $$
  I=2(S_{n+m}-S_{n-m})
  $$
  and a limiting one-site entropy increment $s$, the telescoping sum gives $S_{n+m}-S_{n-m}\to2ms$, hence $I\to4ms$.

## Assumptions

- $A$ and $B$ are commuting factors with $A\vee B=B(\mathcal H)$.
- Both factors admit increasing finite-dimensional matrix-algebra exhaustions.
- The global state used in the entropy rewrite is pure.
- Normality, $\sigma$-finiteness, and support conditions required by the relative-entropy and recovery results.
- The strict area law and decay of all four $o(1)$ terms for the multiple-cone application.

## Not independently verified

- The cited Connes spatial-derivative duality and the operator-valued-weight normalization underlying the conditional-entropy lemma.
- The full von Neumann-algebra approximate-recovery theorem and the appendix's reduction from non-faithful to faithful states.
- Exhaustion continuity of Araki relative entropy in the precise generality used.
- The proposed applicability to arbitrary continuum QFT local nets; the paper does not claim this without additional structure.
- The strict entropy law, the non-abelian-anyon entropy shift, and the physical assumptions entering the topological applications.

# Bottom line

This is a direct structural result for regional observable algebras. It proves that, in a controlled quasi-local setting, Haag duality is exactly equivalent to the disappearance of an entropic obstruction across arbitrarily large shells, and it promotes that disappearance to actual algebra equality through recovery and weak closure. For the vault, it supplies a sharp algebraic benchmark for finite-port sewing and a clear list of what must be repaired before passing to gauge constraints or type-III continuum algebras.
