# Verdict

Not proven by the current paper in the complete sense advertised in the abstract. The paper gives a concrete relaxed Bondi ansatz, identifies the new boundary canonical pair, and displays charges whose Barnich--Troessaert bracket is the parity-restricted log-BMS algebra. However, the source explicitly says that the Einstein-equation reduction, the complete residual-symmetry and charge derivations, the antipodal corner matching, and the timelike-infinity check will appear in work in preparation.

The strongest statement supported by the present source is therefore:

> Under the stated relaxed Bondi falloffs, parity restriction, harmonic exclusions, and the displayed but incompletely derived CPS formulas, logarithmic supertranslations act at null infinity and pair centrally with ordinary higher-harmonic supertranslations. The source indicates, rather than proves within this paper, the full matching to spatial infinity.

This remains high-priority for the vault because it puts a nontrivial extra boundary pair directly into the null-infinity presymplectic current. It is not yet an arbitrary radiative-interface completion theorem.

# Source map

The paper is a seven-page letter without section headings. Its logical blocks are:

1. **Motivation and scope.** Reviews the parity-restricted log-BMS algebra at spatial infinity and states the goal of realizing it autonomously at null infinity.
2. **Relaxed Bondi gauge.** Replaces $g_{rr}=0$ by a logarithmic $r^{-3}$ falloff, records the compatible metric expansion, and explains why the transformation restoring strict Bondi gauge is improper.
3. **Parity branches and physical field.** Relates the $P/Q$ branches at spatial and null infinity and extracts one physical scalar $\widetilde C$ from the two relaxed-gauge coefficients.
4. **Evolution and residual symmetries.** Displays the Bondi mass and angular-momentum equations, the residual vector field, and the logarithmic-supertranslation parameter $\widetilde T$.
5. **CPS charges and algebra.** Gives the radial symplectic current, the charges and flux, the transformation of $\widetilde C$, and the centrally extended Barnich--Troessaert bracket.
6. **Corner claim and outlook.** Argues that matching of transformations implies charge matching, but sends the explicit antipodal and timelike-infinity calculations to a work in preparation.

# Boundary conditions and conventions

The discussion is in four-dimensional asymptotically flat gravity near future null infinity in retarded coordinates $(u,r,x^A)$. The unit-sphere metric and derivative are $q_{AB}$ and $\nabla_A$. The Bondi--Sachs determinant condition and $g_{rA}=0$ are retained, while strict $g_{rr}=0$ is relaxed to

$$
g_{rr}=\frac{1}{r^3}\left(\widetilde R(u,x^A)+\log r\,\widetilde{\widetilde R}(u,x^A)\right)+o(r^{-3}).
$$

The angular metric is

$$
g_{AB}=r^2q_{AB}+rC_{AB}+D_{AB}
+\frac14q_{AB}C_{CD}C^{CD}+o(r^0),
$$

where $C_{AB}$ and $D_{AB}$ are tracefree. The news is

$$
N_{AB}:=\partial_uC_{AB}.
$$

The leading radial equations give

$$
\beta=\frac{\log r}{8r^2}\partial_u\widetilde{\widetilde R}
-\frac{1}{8r^2}\left(\frac14C^{AB}C_{AB}
-\frac12\partial_u\widetilde{\widetilde R}
-\partial_u\widetilde R\right)+o(r^{-2}),
$$

$$
V=-r+2M+o(r^0),
$$

$$
U^A=-\frac{1}{2r^2}\nabla_BC^{AB}
+\frac{\log r}{r^3}\left(-\frac23\nabla_BD^{AB}
+\frac1{12}\partial^A\partial_u\widetilde{\widetilde R}\right)
+\frac{1}{r^3}U^A_{3,0}+o(r^{-2}).
$$

The paper stresses that the relaxed terms are generated by diffeomorphisms, but that these diffeomorphisms are improper because their charges need not vanish. Thus imposing strict Bondi gauge would remove physical boundary information in this phase-space choice.

# The extra null-infinity field

The electric part of the shear is parametrized by the Goldstone scalar $C$,

$$
C^{(E)}_{AB}=-2\nabla_{\langle A}\nabla_{B\rangle}C,
$$

with the $\ell=0,1$ harmonics removed. From the two relaxed-gauge coefficients the paper defines

$$
\widetilde C
:=\frac18\left(u\partial_u^3\widetilde R
+\partial_u^2\widetilde{\widetilde R}\right),
$$

also without $\ell=0,1$ components, and chooses

$$
\partial_u\widetilde C=0.
$$

The source treats the orthogonal combination of $\widetilde R$ and $\widetilde{\widetilde R}$ as gauge redundancy. The condition $\partial_u\widetilde C=0$ is an explicit phase-space restriction, not a consequence independently derived in the letter.

The displayed evolution equations are

$$
\partial_uM=-\frac18N_{AB}N^{AB}
+\frac14\nabla_A\nabla_BN^{AB},
$$

$$
\partial_u\mathcal P_A^L
=\nabla^B\mathcal M_{AB}+C_{AB}\mathcal J^B.
$$

Their detailed derivation is explicitly assigned to the work in preparation.

# Residual symmetries

The preserving vector fields have

$$
\xi^u=f(u,x^A)
+\frac1{r^2}\left(\log r\,\xi^u_{2,1}
+\xi^u_{2,0}\right)+o(r^{-2}),
$$

$$
\xi^r=rh(x^A)+O(r^0),
\qquad
\xi^A=Y^A(x^A)+O(r^{-1}),
$$

where $Y^A$ is a conformal Killing field of the sphere and

$$
f=T-uh,
\qquad
h=-\frac12\nabla_AY^A.
$$

An involved combination of the subleading coefficients and of $\widetilde R,\widetilde{\widetilde R}$ defines the independent logarithmic-supertranslation parameter $\widetilde T(x^A)$. Both $T$ and $\widetilde T$ contain physical $\ell\ge2$ modes; the lower harmonics of $\widetilde T$ are gauge redundancies.

For a test term $\xi^u=a\log r/r^2$ on the flat retarded background with $g_{ur}=-1$,

$$
(\mathcal L_\xi g)_{rr}
=-2\partial_r\xi^u
=\frac{a(4\log r-2)}{r^3}.
$$

This independently confirms the precise scaling that motivates the $r^{-3}(1+\log r)$ relaxation. It does not verify preservation of the full nonlinear falloff system.

# Presymplectic current, charges, and flux

The relevant radial current is displayed as

$$
\omega^r
=\frac{\sin\theta}{32\pi G}\,\delta N_{AB}\,\delta C^{AB}
+\frac{\sin\theta}{32\pi G}\,\partial_u
\left(8\,\delta C\,\delta\widetilde C\right).
$$

Because $\omega$ is a field-space two-form, the products of variations must be read as antisymmetrized wedges; the letter suppresses this notation. The second term is a $u$-boundary contribution and identifies $C$ and $\widetilde C$ as the new canonical pair. It vanishes when there is no radiation and $\partial_u\widetilde C=0$.

After contraction with a residual symmetry and use of the equations of motion, the source states

$$
I_\xi\omega^r=\partial_u(\delta q_\xi+f_\xi)
$$

up to sphere total derivatives. The integrated charge is

$$
Q_\xi
=\oint\frac{\sin\theta}{16\pi G}
\left(Tq_T+\widetilde Tq_{\widetilde T}
+Y^A(q_Y)_A\right),
$$

with

$$
q_T=4(M+\widetilde C),
\qquad
q_{\widetilde T}=-4C,
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
(q_Y)_A={}&2\mathcal P_A^L-2u\partial_AM
-\frac12C_{AC}\nabla_BC^{BC}
-\frac18\partial_A(C_{BC}C^{BC})\\
&+2\left(3\widetilde C\,\partial_AC
+C\,\partial_A\widetilde C\right).
\end{aligned}
$$

The nonintegrable flux is

$$
f_\xi[\delta]
=\frac{\sin\theta}{32\pi G}
fN^{AB}\delta(C_{AB}-4\widetilde C_{AB}).
$$

Since $\widetilde C$ has no $\ell=0,1$ modes, its shift of $q_T$ leaves total energy-momentum unchanged. This is a harmonic-orthogonality statement, not a statement that the higher charges are unchanged.

# Log-BMS algebra

The new field transforms as

$$
\delta_\xi\widetilde C
=(\mathcal L_Y-3h)\widetilde C+\widetilde T.
$$

Using the Barnich--Troessaert bracket, the source obtains

$$
\{Q_{\xi_1},Q_{\xi_2}\}_{\mathrm{BT}}
=Q_{[\xi_1,\xi_2]}
+\oint\frac{\sin\theta}{4\pi G}
\left(T_1\widetilde T_2-\widetilde T_1T_2\right).
$$

The central bilinear is exactly antisymmetric. Restricted to the abelian higher-harmonic translation sector it satisfies the cocycle/Jacobi condition, yielding

$$
\mathfrak{logBMS}
=\mathfrak{iso}(3,1)\ltimes
\mathfrak h\!\left(T^{\ell\ge2},\widetilde T^{\ell\ge2}\right),
$$

an infinite direct sum of Heisenberg pairs. This algebraic check verifies the displayed central term; it does not reconstruct the charge from the Einstein--Hilbert presymplectic potential.

# Corner matching and soft statements

The paper argues that the spatial- and null-infinity transformations are the same in their overlap and therefore that the charges match at the corner. It immediately states that the antipodal matching relations will be explicitly derived in the work in preparation, where timelike infinity will also be checked. The conclusion that existing soft theorems are unchanged similarly depends on that not-yet-present matching analysis.

For vault use, keep three claims separate:

1. the null-infinity relaxed falloff and displayed log-BMS charge algebra;
2. the conservation/matching of those charges through the spatial/null corner;
3. the implication for the scattering Ward identities and soft sector.

The current letter provides direct formulas for the first, but only an argument and deferred calculations for the second and third.

# Relation to the gluing programme

The useful structural lesson is that an apparently subleading gauge relaxation can contribute an actual corner canonical pair:

$$
(C,\widetilde C)
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\partial_u\left(\delta C\wedge\delta\widetilde C\right)
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\text{Heisenberg central term}.
$$

This is directly relevant to regional observable-algebra sewing: declaring the relaxing diffeomorphism to be pure gauge would erase a charged boundary direction. But the paper neither constructs a regional/global Peierls comparison nor proves that these data are sufficient for a general radiative null cut. The antipodal and timelike matching needed for global scattering remain outside the presented derivation.
