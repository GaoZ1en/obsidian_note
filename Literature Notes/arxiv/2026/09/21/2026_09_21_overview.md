# Why these match my current projects

`2609.21691` directly supports the exact AdS$_3$ orbit and regional-algebra work. It makes the quotient by the stabilizer operational at the level of the symplectic form and path-integral measure. The $k=1$ Virasoro vacuum orbit removes the $L_{0,\pm1}$ directions before quantization, while the exceptional BMS$_3$ cases show that a little group can be larger than the actual orbit stabilizer. This is precisely the distinction that must remain visible when sewing boundary symmetry data.

`2609.21398` is useful for the action-first CPS programme because it tests a concrete current rather than inferring a phase-space origin from an attractive geometric picture. Its failure is informative: a current representative, its exact-form ambiguity, the field dependence of its generator, the Hodge map, and the path through background space are separate inputs. A finite family integral is not automatically a canonically transported observable.

Together the two papers give complementary controls. The orbit paper shows how a correct symplectic quotient creates the right state space; the PEE paper shows how a plausible local form can fail to arise from a restricted Iyer--Wald construction even when its flux property is correct.
