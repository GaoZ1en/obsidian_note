### 2610.02306v1 — Temperature and Moduli Independence of the Supersymmetric Index from Gravity

**Source-derived; medium.** The question is whether temperature and asymptotic scalar moduli can change the gravitational contribution to a supersymmetric index. ### 2610.02656v1 — Photon Quantization and Propagator in Rotating Coordinates
