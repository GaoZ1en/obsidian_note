### M2. Dynamics of Regge calculus with torsion — 2610.00593v1

**Source-derived.** [Official abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00593v1), gr-qc; no page count in comments. Edge vectors and interface holonomies define a simplicial Einstein–Cartan system, with torsion measured by the mismatch between an edge vector and its transport from the neighboring simplex. The authors construct three- and four-dimensional actions, recover the torsion-free Regge limit, and compare the independent edge/holonomy variations with continuum equations. The interface data offer a concrete discrete model for testing first-order regional matching. It remains medium because the abstract supplies dynamics and a continuum comparison, not a boundary symplectic form or charge algebra. Codes: T2-model, T1-boundary. The claimed continuum and torsion-free reductions were not independently checked.

### M3. Gravity and generalised geometry from a Lie 2-algebroid perspective — 2610.01292v1

**Source-derived.** [Official abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01292v1), primary hep-th, cross-list math.DG; 14 pages. Starting from a Courant algebroid as a differential graded symplectic manifold, the authors construct a Lie 2-algebroid bracket that defines torsion, curvature and Bianchi identities. They claim uniqueness of a generalized metric-compatible connection after fixing torsion and the ordinary splitting connection, equivalence of different splittings up to quasi-isomorphism, and recovery of the closed-string effective action. This is a compact reference for organizing homological gauge data and convention-dependent curvature constructions. Medium placement is deliberate: the link to current CPS/sewing work is methodological, with no charge or boundary construction in the abstract. Codes: T3-math, T2-model; separate A-big-name signal for Chris Hull. No uniqueness or action-reduction claim was independently verified.
