### M2. Hamiltonian framework for Chiral Gauge Theories on a Disk Boundary

### M3. Carroll-Weyl gauged null strings in AdS

### M8. Polynomial and Eigenspace Factorisations for the One-loop Dilatation Operator at Finite N

### M9. Beyond the Dolgov-Kawasaki instability

### M10. Probability measures for de Sitter JT gravity

The authors compare mixed density matrices built from double-trumpet Wheeler–DeWitt solutions with no-boundary wavefunctions in positive-curvature JT gravity. Medium placement: the result depends on the selected amplitudes and probability prescription; neither a normalizable distribution on an unlimited size range nor a universal cosmological prediction has been established by this sketch.
