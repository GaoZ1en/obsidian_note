### M2. Disformal Closure Selects 3D Scalar-Tensor Gravity without Local Bulk Modes

The potential use is a constrained three-dimensional gravity testbed whose boundary/global sector may remain nontrivial. Medium placement reflects that the boundary phase space and charges are proposed directions, not supplied results in the inspected abstract. The branch restrictions are essential; no universal degree-of-freedom statement or derivation is independently verified here.

### M3. Proving the OSV conjecture with the gravitational path integral

[2610.12550v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12550v1) — `T2-dS-BH-holography; T2-model`. **Source-derived.** The paper addresses the OSV relation between the black-hole helicity supertrace and the squared topological-string partition function in four-dimensional N=2 supergravity from Type IIA Calabi–Yau compactification. Its abstract reports Euclidean saddles valid to all orders in the supergravity derivative expansion and equivariant localization of their on-shell action to north/south pole contributions given by holomorphic/antiholomorphic free energies at attractor values. The claimed single-center proof is scoped to the topological-string coupling expansion; applying the formula to other saddles motivates a nonperturbative refinement. This may inform higher-derivative saddle/action calculations, but does not directly supply the current regional CPS or AdS-mode objects, so it remains medium. The proof, measure and saddle-completeness claims have not been independently assessed; official abstract only.

### M4. Dirac-Born-Infeld action from inequivalent representations of open strings

[2610.13191v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13191v1) — `T2-spectral; T1-boundary`. **Source-derived.** For open strings in a constant magnetic field, the question is how background dependence enters when canonical variables are held universal. This offers a comparison for background-dependent Fock representations and boundary normalization, but its string-specific construction has no immediate demonstrated translation to the current AdS modes. Medium, official abstract only; implementability criteria and normalization regularization remain unverified.
