### 1. Conserved currents associated with conformal symmetry in linearized gravity (2609.39476v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The question is which local currents accompany Lorentz, dilation and special conformal transformations of linearized gravity on Minkowski space. The source constructs distinguished currents and parameter families involving at most first derivatives of the metric perturbation, and gives matter balance equations. A generalized Noether argument handles additional conditions; specifically, the claimed special conformal symmetry is in generalized harmonic gauges. This offers a catalogue for comparing gauge-fixed currents with CPS generators, but neither gauge independence nor boundary integrability follows from the abstract. Medium placement reflects that the concrete regional charge construction still needs to be supplied; a 62-page derivation was not added to today's deep queue.

Reason codes: `T1-charge; T1-symmetry; T2-model`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39476v1). Primary: `gr-qc`; categories: General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc).

### 2. Light-Ray Supersymmetry, BMS Algebras and the Averaged Null Energy Condition (2609.39691v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The paper asks how supersymmetry acts on parallel light-ray operators supported on a null hypersurface. It constructs operators from the supersymmetry current, R current and stress tensor, and claims their graded commutators contain a universal supersymmetric generalized BMS algebra in unitary Lorentzian CFTs in three and four dimensions. Positivity of the averaged null energy operator follows from the proposed local supersymmetry algebra, with free massless examples as source checks. This is useful background for null-surface observable algebras and positivity constraints. It stays medium because the current vault application would require an explicit identification with bulk boundary data; the abstract's extension to general supersymmetric QFT is presented as supporting arguments, not the same established CFT result.

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry; T1-boundary; T3-math`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39691v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th).

### 3. Dear Quantizers, Stochastic $\approx$ Brane (2609.38209v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The question is whether the extra Langevin dimension of stochastic quantization has a useful bulk/boundary interpretation. Using the supersymmetric stochastic formulation and Morse-theoretic methods, the source describes equilibrium quantization as turning a relative QFT into an absolute one through a cohomological equilibrium TFT, and relates enriched Neumann boundary data to the lower-dimensional BV/BRST complex. Analytic continuation supplies proposed links to the A-model and nonrelativistic/Carrollian limits. The boundary-complex dictionary is potentially useful for comparing BV boundary data with regional constructions. It remains medium: the abstract does not provide the action variation, response map or sewing statement needed for a direct application, and no equivalence of quantum constructions was independently checked.

Reason codes: `T1-boundary; T3-math`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38209v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th) ; Mathematical Physics (math-ph).

### 4. Energy Transmission Across Holographic Conformal Interfaces in General Dimensions (2609.38318v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** This studies energy transport through holographic conformal interfaces beyond two dimensions. For a specified class of AdS-sliced domain walls, the authors derive a transmission coefficient from the warp factor, claim independence of incidence angle and incident profile within that class, and bound transmission using central-charge-like data on the two sides. The concrete use is as a model of interface response and transmitted flux when testing a regional boundary prescription. Medium placement reflects the extra work needed to translate the holographic observable into the vault's causal response and symplectic data. The reported universality is restricted to the chosen geometries; it is not a general assertion about all higher-dimensional interfaces.

Reason codes: `T1-boundary; T2-dS-BH-holography`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38318v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th) ; Statistical Mechanics (cond-mat.stat-mech); Strongly Correlated Electrons (cond-mat.str-el); General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc).

### 5. Projective Symmetry and Its Breaking in Quadratic Metric-Affine Gravity (2609.39988v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** For parity-even four-dimensional metric-affine actions linear in curvature and quadratic at most in torsion/nonmetricity, the source classifies generalized projective invariance and its breaking. It reports a reduction from twelve gravitational coefficients to five, four vectorial Noether identities, and a method for solving the singular connection equations in a basis adapted to the symmetry. Matter couplings distinguish decoupled Maxwell theory, classically chiral massless fermions, scalar U(1) data and compensating Stückelberg extensions. The useful lesson is a concrete gauge-zero-mode versus auxiliary-field testbed: exact invariance constrains the source currents, while breaking can lift the zero modes without making them propagating. It is medium because no boundary symplectic reduction or charge calculation is supplied by the abstract, and quantum anomalies are outside its explicitly classical fermion statement.

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry; T2-model`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39988v1). Primary: `gr-qc`; categories: General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc).

### 6. A New Approach to Black Hole Thermodynamics in General EMD-like Theories (2609.38311v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The paper asks whether thermodynamic data of charged black holes in general Einstein–Maxwell–dilaton-like systems can be found without first constructing the complete metric. With multiple Maxwell fields and dilatonic scalars, algebraic and differential relations among thermodynamic quantities, scalar charges and a nonextremality parameter lead to two choices of master equations. The authors solve in a charge expansion and report agreement with perturbative black-hole solutions through quartic order. This may provide benchmarks for first-law and scalar-charge calculations. It remains medium because the direct connection to a chosen CPS boundary condition and normalization has not been established here; the reported quartic comparison is a source check, not independent verification or an all-orders existence result.

Reason codes: `T1-charge; T2-dS-BH-holography; T2-model`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38311v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th) ; General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc).

### 7. Large gaps and BTZ entropy in modular spectra with positive integer degeneracies (2609.39930v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The source constructs modular-invariant torus spectra with a unique vacuum, discrete levels and positive integer degeneracies by recursive repairs of the Maloney–Witten–Keller completion and finite moment matching. For sufficiently large central charge it claims controlled large gaps; in one family, suitably smoothed densities reproduce a perturbative BTZ saddle through every fixed finite order in the inverse central charge, including positive energies below the canonical thermal-AdS transition. This is useful spectral context for AdS3 partition functions and for distinguishing canonical dominance from microcanonical counts. It is medium because modular spectra alone do not establish a complete local CFT or identify the vault's canonical Hamiltonian spectrum, and the smoothing and convergence arguments were not reconstructed here.

Reason codes: `T2-spectral; T2-dS-BH-holography`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39930v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th).

### 8. Light non-vacuum BMS$_3$ blocks at order $1/c_{M}$ (2609.39893v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** This derives the first inverse-central-charge correction to highest-weight BMS3 blocks with light external primaries and nonvacuum exchange, holding the other central charge and operator weights fixed. The source reduces the calculation to the global sector plus descendants with exactly one nonglobal generator, then resums derivatives of global blocks for nonzero exchanged boost weight. For pairwise identical external operators it reports checks from boundary gravitons in a shadow representation and a formal Virasoro contraction, with commutation of contraction and coefficient extraction proved at each fixed level. This offers a useful asymptotic-symmetry representation benchmark. Medium placement reflects its indirect relation to the current bulk spectrum; zero boost weight requires a separate quotient and is explicitly not a smooth limit of the generic answer.

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry; T2-spectral; T2-celestial-carrollian`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39893v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th).

### 9. Reconsidering the Vacuum Energy of a Massive Scalar Field in a Spherical Cavity (2609.39540v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The paper revisits the renormalized vacuum energy of a massive scalar inside a spherical Dirichlet cavity. It uses zeta regularization and uniform asymptotic Bessel expansions, reports a sign correction to an older analytic calculation, and finds vanishing energy at large mass together with several sign changes as the radius varies. The likely use is a controlled boundary-spectrum benchmark for subtraction conventions and heavy-mass decoupling in mode-sum work. It remains medium because the geometry is a cavity rather than the current AdS problem; the correction is reported from the official abstract and was not independently recalculated in this run.

Reason codes: `T2-spectral; T1-boundary`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39540v1). Primary: `math-ph`; categories: Mathematical Physics (math-ph).

### 10. Leading Logarithms in the Effective Theory of Gravity: corrections to the scalar mass (2609.38496v1)

**Source-derived; abstract-level sketch.** The question is how much of gravitational EFT loop corrections can be generated recursively from the leading Lagrangian. Adapting leading-log methods from chiral perturbation theory, the authors compute scalar pole-mass leading logarithms through three loops and describe a recursion to arbitrary loop order. They report cancellation of a general gauge-fixing parameter in the pole mass. This is useful for separating physical mass renormalization from gauge-dependent intermediate quantities in spectral calculations. It stays medium because leading logs do not supply the full finite mass correction or the boundary-dependent AdS spectrum, and the recursion and gauge cancellation were not independently checked here.

Reason codes: `T2-spectral; T2-model`. Official source: [v1 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38496v1). Primary: `hep-th`; categories: High Energy Physics - Theory (hep-th) ; General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology (gr-qc).
