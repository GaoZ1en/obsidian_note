### 2610.05460v1 — Extremal black hole throats from non extremal near horizon expansions

**Source-derived — official abstract only.** For smooth stationary black-hole families with a smooth extremal limit in common Gaussian-null coordinates, the authors combine surface-gravity, radial and Killing-time scalings to recover extremal throats from nonextremal expansions. ### 2610.06470v1 — A local second law for black hole dynamics in the charged large-$D$ membrane paradigm

**Source-derived — official abstract only.** The authors derive entropy production for charged large-D membranes by evaluating an Einstein–Maxwell horizon constraint through the non-affine Raychaudhuri equation and translating it to auxiliary membrane kinematics. **Source-derived — official abstract only.** This long paper classifies complex worldline saddles for massive charged particles in two-dimensional maximally symmetric spaces with homogeneous electric fields. It complexifies target space and proper time, organizes sectors by winding/puncture data, and expresses actions through momentum residues; the authors also distinguish local-patch solutions from saddles admitting a global lift. This is a useful prospective reference for AdS2 one-loop calculations and for keeping global saddle admissibility separate from a local solution. It remains medium because a concrete determinant or contour prescription for the vault has not been chosen; neither the 74-page-plus-appendix classification nor which saddles actually contribute has been verified here.

Official [abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03862v1). Categories: hep-th, gr-qc (primary first). Reasons: `T2-spectral; T2-dS-BH-holography`.

### 2610.05528v1 — The pseudo-Riemannian spectral zeta function density on scattering manifolds

**Source-derived — official abstract only.** Sadat Kyaee studies diagonal trace densities of Feynman-prescribed complex powers of principally scalar Laplace-type operators on pseudo-Riemannian scattering spaces. The abstract claims meromorphic continuation, relates poles to local curvature invariants, and combines uniform resolvent estimates with a Hadamard-parametrix analysis; a geometric no-return argument replaces causal convexity outside Lorentzian signature. This may be a useful analytic reference for spectral regularization and separating local residues from global spectral data. It remains medium because scattering-space hypotheses and the prescription must be checked before transferring the result to AdS boundary problems; no determinant formula or AdS application is inferred here.
