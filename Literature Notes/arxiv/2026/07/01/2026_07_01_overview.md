## Instantaneous boundary sectors and Yang-Mills boundary charges

- arXiv: [2606.31265v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31265v1)
- Categories: primary `math-ph`; cross-listed `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-symplectic`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Boundaries in the Instantaneous Formulation of Field Theories (2606.31265v1)]]

Source claim: fixed spatial Dirichlet data give an instantaneous tangent bundle $TQ^b$, while a boundary-velocity condition gives a disjoint union of fixed-boundary sectors,

$$\begin{align}
D=\ker(Tr)=\bigsqcup_{b\in Q^\partial}TQ^b.
\end{align}$$

Main mechanism: the Legendre transform is defined on $D$ and lands in $D^*$, with no boundary-conjugate momentum. The total phase space is Poisson and leafwise symplectic; in Yang-Mills,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal P_{\rm YM}=\bigsqcup_{b\in Q^\partial}\mathcal P_b.
\end{align}$$

Sector-preserving transformations can carry the electric-flux momentum

$$\begin{align}
J_b^\xi=-\int_{\partial\Sigma}\operatorname{Tr}(E\xi_\partial),
\end{align}$$

while transformations changing $b$ move Poisson leaves and are not Hamiltonian in this phase space.

Transfer to current projects: this is a clean Hamiltonian-side model for why boundary-moving transformations may require edge-mode enlargement before they admit Hamiltonian charges.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the formal Yang-Mills covariance identity $D_{A^g}E^g=g^{-1}(D_AE)g$. The infinite-dimensional smoothness assumptions, global gauge-group topology, imported falloff results, affine non-temporal-gauge extension, and CPS/null-boundary extension remain source-derived or explicitly open.

## Dynamical completion of coupling-charge thermodynamics

- arXiv: [2606.30707v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30707v1)
- Categories: primary `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Dynamical Completion of Coupling-Charge Thermodynamics (2606.30707v1)]]

Source claim: a gravitational coupling promoted to a scalar-gauge pair can be given local dynamics without turning it into a spacetime-dependent coupling in the original field equations.

Main mechanism: use the defect

$$\begin{align}
\Delta_i=F_i-\mathcal L_i
\end{align}$$

and the completed Lagrangian

$$\begin{align}
\widetilde{\mathbf L}_{\rm dyn} =\mathbf L_0+\sum_i\alpha_i\boldsymbol\Delta_i -\frac12\sum_i\kappa_i\,d\alpha_i\wedge *d\alpha_i -\frac12\sum_i\hat\kappa_i\,\boldsymbol\Delta_i\wedge *\boldsymbol\Delta_i .
\end{align}$$

The top-form equation gives

$$\begin{align}
\alpha_i+\hat\kappa_i\Delta_i=\bar\alpha_i,
\end{align}$$

and hence

$$\begin{align}
(\Box-m_i^2)(\alpha_i-\bar\alpha_i)=0, \qquad m_i^2=\frac{1}{\kappa_i\hat\kappa_i}.
\end{align}$$

Transfer to current projects: this is a direct coupling-as-charge and CPS-adjacent construction. It separates the thermodynamic charge $\bar\alpha_i$ from the local scalar excitation $\alpha_i(x)-\bar\alpha_i$, which is useful for avoiding false local-coupling interpretations in extended first-law notes.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the top-form elimination and massive-scalar algebra. The full black-hole first law, full surface-charge derivation, admissible coupling split, and Gauss-Bonnet convention-dependent formula remain source-derived or blocked.
