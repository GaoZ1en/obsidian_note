# High-priority recommendations

## Supertranslations in the bulk of spacetime

**Paper:** [2512.20331v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20331v3), Pujian Mao. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `gr-qc`; 11 pages. This is a replacement, not a new submission.

**Codes:** `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-charge`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `V-update`.

**Technical relevance:** A new null foliation is written as $\bar u=u+f(r,x^a)$, with $d\bar u$ required to remain null. The nonlinear eikonal equation is propagated from boundary data $f_0$ by characteristic flow. In Minkowski space the construction turns a boundary supertranslation into a bulk congruence with shifted expansion and shear. In Schwarzschild space the characteristic potential

$$
D(r)=1-\frac{L^2}{r^2}\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)
$$

allows a transition between null generators without and with an exterior turning point. At linear order on a fixed null hypersurface, the proposed operator is

$$
\bar P-P_-
=2\epsilon\int_\Sigma dx^-d^2x^\perp
\,\partial_i f,T^{+i}+O(\epsilon^2).
$$

**Deep note:** [[Supertranslations in the bulk of spacetime (2512.20331v3)]]. It reconstructs the NU/eikonal definition, plane and spherical Minkowski characteristic flows, optical memory, the Schwarzschild turning-point mechanism, the End Matter, and the linearized light-ray proposal. Mathematica reproduced the inverse-metric equations, characteristic solutions, optical tensor, coordinate Jacobian, Schwarzschild flow, and linearized pullback.

**Verification verdict:** The geometric flow is substantially checkable, but the printed spherical characteristic Eq. (23) omits $r^{-2}$ in $dp_a/ds$. The Schwarzschild exterior turning point requires $L^2\geq27M^2$, with the critical point at $r=3M$; nonzero $L$ alone is insufficient. The full light-ray algebra and commutation with the Hamiltonian are not proven because the necessary equal-null-time stress-tensor algebra, contact terms, boundary terms, and nonlinear gravitational CPS generator are absent.

**Author signal:** None. Placement is entirely technical.

## Factorization Algebras and Quantum Groups from Generalized Poisson Sigma Models

**Paper:** [2607.09486v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09486v1), Keyou Zeng. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `math-ph`, `math.QA`; 132 pages. Codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-model`, `T3-math`.

- **Source claim:** Higher-dimensional topological and holomorphic-topological Poisson sigma models encode shifted Poisson data, enriched boundaries, defects, boundary factorization algebras, and Koszul-dual quantum groups.
- **Mechanism:** An enriched boundary is a $P_{d-1}$ algebra $C$ equipped with a coisotropic lift $A\to\mathcal Z(C)$ from the bulk $P_d$ algebra. The mixed boundary-corner operation of Eq. (3.35) realizes that lift on observables. Boundary tree diagrams recover classical $P_d$ brackets; universal-defect fusion produces coproducts, crossings produce $R$-data, and three-defect configurations produce associators.
- **Central constructions:**

  $$
  \begin{array}{ccc}
  &\mathcal Z(C)&\\
  &\nearrow\scriptstyle{\widetilde\varphi}\downarrow&\\
  A&\xrightarrow{\varphi}&C,
  \end{array}
  $$

  $$
  \Delta(\rho_c)
  =\rho_c\otimes1+1\otimes\rho_c
  +\frac{\hbar}{2}\delta_c{}^{ab}\rho_a\otimes\rho_b.
  $$

- **Likely use:** A reusable algebraic language for bulk-to-corner actions, BV anomaly inflow, BF/Chern-Simons defects, and boundary quantum-group data; the universal heat-kernel identities may shorten half-space and corner graph calculations.
- **Why not high:** It does not construct Wald/CPS currents, surface-charge integrability, or a gravitational boundary phase space. Several all-orders statements remain conjectural or heuristic.
- **Detailed note:** [[Factorization Algebras and Quantum Groups from Generalized Poisson Sigma Models (2607.09486v1)]]. The note is selective rather than a 132-page monograph and preserves the tree-level/conjectural boundary.
- **Author signal:** None.

## Monodromy and geometry of heavy-light Virasoro blocks

**Paper:** [2607.09500v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09500v1), Mikhail Belakovskiy, Vladimir Belavin. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `math-ph`; 15 pages. Codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`.

- **Source claim:** In the heavy-background uniformizing coordinate $\mathfrak w=\psi_1^{(0)}/\psi_2^{(0)}$, the eigenvectors of a light-operator monodromy matrix encode the two boundary endpoints of the corresponding bulk geodesic.
- **Mechanism:** Flatness at each cubic vertex gives background-independent cross-ratio equations. The heavy geometry enters only through $z\mapsto\mathfrak w(z)$, after which internal endpoints are solved algebraically and elementary geodesic actions are added.
- **Central formula:**

  $$
  \bigl(
  \mathfrak w(z_i),\mathfrak w(z_{i+1});
  \mathfrak w(y_{i+2}),\mathfrak w(z_{i+2})
  \bigr)
  =-
  \frac{-\epsilon_i+\epsilon_{i+1}+\epsilon_{i+2}}
  {\epsilon_i-\epsilon_{i+1}+\epsilon_{i+2}}.
  $$

- **Likely use:** An algorithmic bridge among Virasoro monodromy, accessory parameters, AdS$_3$ geodesic networks, and multi-point semiclassical correlators. The non-vacuum HHLLL block reduces to one quartic endpoint equation.
- **Why not high:** It is a correlator/geodesic-network method without CPS, charges, edge modes, or boundary symmetry algebra.
- **Detailed note:** [[Monodromy and geometry of heavy-light Virasoro blocks (2607.09500v1)]]. The formulas are source-derived; the paper's Euclidean rederivation is not counted as this run's independent check.
- **Author signal:** None.

## Massless fermionic current of Schwinger pairs in 3D de Sitter spacetime

**Paper:** [2607.09594v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09594v1), Manizheh Botshekananfard, Clément Stahl. Primary `hep-th`; 25 pages. Codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`.

- **Source claim:** Whittaker in-modes and adiabatic subtraction give a finite induced current for a massless charged fermion in a constant electric field on the expanding dS$_3$ patch, with linear weak-field response and $E^{3/2}$ strong-field scaling.
- **Mechanism:** Solve the Dirac modes, form the in-vacuum mode sum, subtract the adiabatic UV term, and analyze the physical orthonormal-frame current $j^{\hat x}=\Omega J^1$.
- **Central limits:**

  $$
  \frac{j^{\hat x}}{eH^2}
  =-\frac{\lambda}{16}+O(\lambda^3),
  \qquad
  \frac{|j^{\hat x}|}{eH^2}
  \sim\frac{\lambda^{3/2}}{4\pi^2}.
  $$

- **Likely use:** A $2+1$-dimensional spinor-mode and composite-current benchmark for comparison with heat-kernel, point-splitting, or spectral methods.
- **Why not high:** It is a curved-QFT mode-sum calculation, not a charge/CPS construction. More importantly, the printed component ratio and current definition imply the opposite sign from the counterterm in Eq. (4.14).
- **Detailed note:** [[Massless fermionic current of Schwinger pairs in 3D de Sitter spacetime (2607.09594v1)]]. Mathematica reproduced the weak/strong asymptotics of the final source formula and exposed the subtraction-sign failure; the complete renormalization chain is not verified.
- **Author signal:** None.

## A Small-Throat Boundary Condition for the Tunneling Wave Function of the Universe

**Paper:** [2607.08815v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08815v1), Masaki Yamada. Primary `gr-qc`; cross-list `hep-th`; 10 pages. Codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

- **Source claim:** A finite small-throat problem can select the tunneling wave function before the zero-size limit is taken.
- **Mechanism:** In $q=a^2$ minisuperspace, radiation produces two turning points for

  $$
  U(q)=1-H^2q-\frac{\epsilon}{q},
  \qquad
  q_\pm=\frac{1\pm\sqrt{1-4H^2\epsilon}}{2H^2}.
  $$

  Neumann data $\dot q(0)=0$ alone allow both saddles. Restricting the initial value to a relative cycle inside

  $$
  0<|q_i|<R_\epsilon,
  \qquad
  R_\epsilon=\sqrt{q_-q_+}=\frac{\sqrt\epsilon}{H},
  $$

  includes the inner saddle $q_-$ and excludes the outer saddle $q_+$. The tunneling exponent is controlled by

  $$
  B_\epsilon=6\pi^2\int_{q_-}^{q_+}\sqrt{U(q)},dq,
  $$

  followed by $\epsilon\to0$.
- **Likely use:** A concrete model for jointly choosing endpoint conditions, Riemann sheet, and Picard-Lefschetz cycle before a degenerate gravitational limit.
- **Why not high:** The construction is restricted to closed-universe minisuperspace and contains no field-theoretic CPS, edge mode, charge, or local constraint algebra. Its global sheet coverage is supported numerically rather than by a global injectivity proof.
- **Verification boundary:** Source-derived; no independent saddle or elliptic-integral reproduction was run.
- **Author signal:** None.

## A note on the stability of surfaces along null cones under area-preserving variations

**Paper:** [2607.09325v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09325v1), Markus Wolff. Primary `math.DG`; cross-list `gr-qc`. Codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

- **Source claim:** Stable spacelike cross-sections of expanding null cones have a Hawking-energy lower bound under the dominant energy condition; stable cross-sections of the standard Minkowski lightcone are round spheres.
- **Mechanism:** For $\partial_t x=f\theta^{-1}L$, define the gauge-invariant Jacobi operator by $\partial_tH^2=J(f)$ and impose

  $$
  \int_\Sigma fJ(f),d\mu
  \geq c\int_\Sigma f^2,d\mu,
  \qquad
  \int_\Sigma f,d\mu=0.
  $$

  Hersch test functions, the Gauss equation, DEC, and Gauss-Bonnet yield

  $$
  m_H(\Sigma)
  \geq\frac23c
  \left(\frac{|\Sigma|}{16\pi}\right)^{3/2}.
  $$

- **Likely use:** Null-cross-section geometry, Hawking-energy positivity, STCMC foliations, and rigidity arguments in gravitational-energy work.
- **Why not high:** The paper concerns geometric stability, not null-boundary data, CPS, charges, symmetry generators, or quantization. It is intentionally not labeled `T1-boundary`.
- **Verification boundary:** Source-derived; the elliptic stability proof and Hersch balancing argument were not independently reproduced.
- **Author signal:** None.
