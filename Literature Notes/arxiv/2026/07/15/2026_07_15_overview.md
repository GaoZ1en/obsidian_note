## 1. The entropy of dynamical black holes deviating from electrovacuum in second order — `2607.12794v1`

Detailed note: [[The entropy of dynamical black holes deviating from electrovacuum in second order (2607.12794v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

The paper starts from a stationary Einstein--Maxwell black hole in affine Gaussian null coordinates, places the nearby marginally trapped tube at

$$\begin{align}
u=U(s;v,x),
\end{align}$$

and computes its area through second order. Its formalism-side contribution is the constraint-subtracted current

$$\begin{align}
\bm\omega' =\bm\omega -\xi\cdot(\delta\bm E\,\delta\phi) -\xi\cdot(\bm E\,\delta^2\phi) -\xi^a\delta^2\bm C_a,
\end{align}$$

followed by null-boundary exact-form improvements and the entropy

$$\begin{align}
\bm S =\frac{2\pi}{\kappa_3} (\bm Q_\xi-\xi\cdot\bm B_{\mathcal H^+}).
\end{align}$$

Under total NEC, compact horizon sections, and late-time stationarity, the first-order expansion and apparent-horizon displacement vanish and the source obtains

$$\begin{align}
\delta^2S=\frac{1}{4G}\delta^2A_{\mathcal T}.
\end{align}$$

The detailed note maps the full equations (1)--(98) chain, separates event-horizon and apparent-horizon data, tracks the varying generator, and translates the null-boundary representative into the vault's AdS gravitational-energy vocabulary. Mathematica/xAct checks reproduce the GNC lowering/rescaling, the Maxwell null-stress coefficient, the quotient variation in the entropy correction, and the final numerical factors.

The principal boundary is explicit: the area formula, pure-gravity horizon current, and Raychaudhuri reduction rely on arXiv:2606.16757; equation (9) is schematic; and the general residual-GNC covariance is not proved. These steps are recorded under `Blocked`, not treated as checked.

## 2. Timelike Entanglement First Law and Linearized Field Equations in Higher Curvature Gravity — `2607.12834v1`

Detailed note: [[Timelike Entanglement First Law and Linearized Field Equations in Higher Curvature Gravity (2607.12834v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

The paper completes the Jacobson--Myers entropy functional with the required surface term, fixes the non-normalizable FG source, and shows that

$$\begin{align}
C_L =1-\sum_{m=2}m\lambda_m f_\infty^{m-1} =-P'(f_\infty)
\end{align}$$

multiplies all three linearized objects:

$$\begin{align}
C_L\,\delta G_{AB},\qquad \Delta T_{\mu\nu},\qquad \Delta S.
\end{align}$$

For a non-critical AdS root $C_L\ne0$, the timelike first law is therefore equivalent to the linearized Lovelock equation in the hyperbolic, perturbative, normalizable sector considered. The completed note follows the cubic calculation, arbitrary-order generalized-delta reduction, Myers action boundary term, canonical momentum, complex spherical extremal surface, Palatini boundary flux, and entropy surface variation.

This is not tagged `T1-Wald-CPS`: the source compares Wald and Jacobson--Myers entropy on a special totally geodesic surface but does not construct $\Theta$, $\omega$, $Q_\xi$, or an Iyer--Wald Hamiltonian. The most useful local follow-up is to derive whether those representatives carry the same $C_L$ only modulo exact and boundary terms.

**Author signal:** none used. Placement is entirely technical.
