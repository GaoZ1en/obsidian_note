## Quantization of Gravity on Null Hypersurfaces

- arXiv: [2607.07785v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07785v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-10.
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-symplectic`.
- Technical relevance: high as a research architecture. It separates branch and corner data, organizes the null presymplectic form into Darboux sectors, and treats cut-moving supertranslations as completely positive maps before a crossed-product extension.
