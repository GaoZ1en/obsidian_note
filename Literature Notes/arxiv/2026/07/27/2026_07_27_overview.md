## Local Geometric Bounds on Generalized Entropy Evolution along Null Horizons — `2607.21770v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.21770v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21770v1). Categories/status: `gr-qc:new; hep-th:cross`. Technical reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`. Author-signal relevance: none.

Detailed note: [[Local Geometric Bounds on Generalized Entropy Evolution along Null Horizons (2607.21770v1)]].

The source combines affine twist-free Raychaudhuri evolution, a generator-wise ultralocal QNEC approximation, and \(R_{kk}=8\pi T_{kk}\). The checked core is

$$
\frac1{dA}\mathcal L_k^2dS_{\mathrm{gen}}
\le
\frac14\left(\frac12\theta^2-\sigma^2\right),
$$

$$
\mathcal L_k\Theta
\le
-\theta\Theta+\frac12\theta^2-\sigma^2,
\qquad
\Theta=\frac4{dA}\mathcal L_kdS_{\mathrm{gen}}.
$$

Mathematica gives zero residual for the area, Einstein/QNEC cancellation, quotient, inverse-bound, general-dimension, and affine-rescaling chains. The inverse form

$$
8\pi T_{kk}
\ge
\mathcal L_k\Theta_q+\theta\Theta_q
$$

is exactly QNEC rewritten, not a new energy condition.

The detailed audit also finds decisive failures. The condition

$$
\frac12\theta^2-\theta\Theta\le\sigma^2
$$

is sufficient for the upper bound itself to be nonpositive, not necessary for QFC. Its alleged QES consequence is therefore not a stability theorem. The Jacobi derivation double counts the optical deformation; \(3H\) is a timelike FLRW expansion rather than the de Sitter null-horizon expansion; the Vaidya generator has inaffinity \(M(v)/r^2\); \(r=2M(v)\) is spacelike for \(\dot M>0\); and the source replaces an inequality by a damping equation without a saturation assumption. The appendices add further sign, perturbative-order, affine/Killing-time, dimension, and factor-two errors.

High placement is retained because the conditional core directly concerns null-horizon generalized entropy and is a useful repair benchmark for the null-boundary project. Reuse only the checked core and the corrected comparison solution, not the downstream stability or scrambling claims.

## Static Black Holes and Iyer--Wald Entropy in \(f(\mathcal R,\mathcal K)\) Gravity — `2607.21786v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.21786v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21786v1). Categories/status: `gr-qc:new`. Technical reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`. Author-signal relevance: none.

Detailed note: [[Static Black Holes and Iyer--Wald Entropy in f(R,K) Gravity (2607.21786v1)]].

$$

\right],

$$

The source prints the order-\(\lambda\) equation as

$$
\delta G_{\mu\nu}
=-\ell^{-2n}\Sigma_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}.
$$

The decisive audit result is that Eq. (4)'s last term has the wrong sign. In the curvature convention used by the paper's own Schwarzschild and entropy components, direct xAct variation gives

$$
+4\nabla^\alpha\nabla^\beta
\left(
f_{\mathcal K}R_{\mu\alpha\nu\beta}
\right),
$$

not the printed \(-4\). The printed tensor fails the diffeomorphism Noether identity:

$$
\nabla_\mu\Sigma^\mu{}_r
=
2^{\frac32-\frac{5n}{2}}
3^{2-n}
M^{1-n}
(n^2-1)
r^{3n-4}.
$$

The displayed \(A(r),B(r)\) solve the two printed radial ODEs, but their omitted angular equation has residual

$$
-2^{\frac12-\frac{5n}{2}}
3^{2-n}
\ell^{-2n}
M^{1-n}
(n^2-1)
r^{3n-3}.
$$

Exact substitution into the correctly varied action equations likewise leaves generic residuals proportional to \(n^2-1\). The claimed metric is therefore on shell only at \(n=\pm1\), not for generic \(n\); the separate \(n=0\) branch also fails its angular equation.

Independently of that failure, the local bifurcation-surface contraction is algebraically correct:

$$
S_W
=
\frac{\mathcal A_H}{4G}
+
\frac{\lambda}{\ell^{2n}}
\frac{(n-1)(1+\sqrt2)G^n}
{(6\sqrt2\pi)^n}
\left(
\frac{\mathcal A_H}{4G}
\right)^{n+1}.
$$

Mathematica proves the equivalence of the paper's three entropy formulas, while xAct reproduces \(\mathcal K=48M^2/r^6\), \(\gamma_H=-1/M^2\), and the Riemann derivatives in the Wald tensor. For generic \(n\), however, this establishes a local functional \(S_{\mathrm{Wald}}[g]|_{\mathcal H}\), not the entropy of the paper's failed black-hole metric.

Conditionally within the printed metric, the \(P=0\) and \(g^{rr}=0\) roots co-move and Eq. (17) also drops \(\ell^{-2n}\). The algebraically corrected shift is

$$
\delta r
=
-c_1
+
\frac{2^{n/2}M^{2n+1}}{3^n\ell^{2n}}
\left[
1+\sqrt2+(\sqrt2-1)n
\right].
$$

At \(n=1\),

$$
A=B=-\frac{r^2}{6\ell^2},
\qquad
\Lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}
=\frac{\lambda}{2\ell^2},
\qquad
r_H
=2M+\frac43\frac{\lambda M^3}{\ell^2}.
$$

The source prints the \(\ell=1\) forms without declaring that choice. Its \(n=-1\) constant correction is real, but evaluating \(f=-6\mathcal K\) after imposing Ricci flatness does not make its Wald functional equal to pure Gauss--Bonnet: \(f_{\mathcal R}^{(0)}=-2X_0\ne0\). The near-\(-1\) logarithm also omits the \(n\)-derivative of the coefficient.

High placement is retained as an unusually useful Iyer--Wald repair benchmark, not as a reusable black-hole solution. The paper has no symplectic potential, \(k_\xi=\delta Q_\xi-\xi\cdot\theta\), outer Hamiltonian, integrability analysis, or first law; a CPS completion must begin by repairing and resolving the full conserved field equations.

## AdS Black Holes Are Short-Lived inside the Spectral Form Factor — `2607.21704v1`

Source claim: naive continuation of the large AdS black-hole saddle can make \(e^{-I(\beta+it)}\) grow for \(d\equiv5\pmod4\), contradicting \(|Z(\beta+it)|\le Z(\beta)\).

Mechanism: keep an off-shell conical black-hole modulus,

$$
Z(\beta+it)
\approx
\int_0^\infty dx\,
\exp\left\{
-N_*\left[
(\beta+it)x^{d-2}(1+x^2)
-\frac{4\pi}{d-1}x^{d-1}
\right]
\right\},
$$

and determine the contributing relative-homology class by Picard--Lefschetz theory. The large black hole loses dominance at

$$
t_D^{(B)}
=\beta\tan\frac{\pi}{2(d-1)}+\cdots,
$$

then leaves the contour at

$$
t_S^{(B)}
=\beta\tan\frac{\pi}{d-1}+\cdots
\qquad(d>3).
$$

For \(d=3\), \(t_D=O(\beta)\) but \(t_S=2\pi\ell+O(\beta^2/\ell)\). A graviton-gas boundary layer turns the late-time answer into a \(1/(\beta+it)\) endpoint tail.

Likely use: complex-temperature AdS partition functions, inverse Laplace transforms, and the distinction between saddle dominance and contour membership. It is not high because it contains no CPS, Noether charge, or spacetime boundary construction; its “endpoint” is the low-energy spectral endpoint.
