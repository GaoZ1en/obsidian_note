### [[Gravitational Memory Beyond Null Infinity through Finite-Distance Carrollian Screens (2607.18675v1)]]

[arXiv:2607.18675v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18675v1) — Felipe Diaz, Sercan Hüsnügil, Oriana Labrin, Leonardo Sanhueza. `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`; `T1-charge`-adjacent only for the separate AdS work-law branch.

The source starts from the exact Robinson--Trautman metric and parabolic equation, maps it to Bondi gauge, reconstructs \(C_{AB}\), \(N_{AB}\), the mass and angular aspects, and shows that a linear harmonic produces pure electric-parity displacement memory. It then embeds a finite null graph \(r=\rho(u,x^A)\), computes its Carrollian geometry and null Brown--York tensor, and identifies

\[
D_bT_a{}^b=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Delta_R^2\Phi+3m\partial_u\Phi=0
\]

after nullity is imposed. The central finite-screen observable is

\[
\Delta_\ell Q_{AB}
=\int du\,X_u^*(\theta q_{AB}+2\sigma_{AB}),
\]

with separate fixed-frame scalar, shape, and vector transitions. Its leading tracefree large-radius projection is exactly

\[
\lim_{r_{\cal N}\to\infty}
\frac{(\Delta_{\cal N}q_{AB})^{\rm TF}}{r_{\cal N}}
=\Delta C_{AB}.
\]

The detailed monograph note covers every section and appendix, a global convention dictionary, the RT/Bondi/screen derivation tree, and the CPS translation. Independent xCoba/Mathematica checks reproduced screen nullity, the exact leading time map, the harmonic decay and norm identities, the late-screen ODE, and the quadratic RT mode/resonance chain. The finite-\(\Lambda\) charge substitution fails as printed by a factor proportional to \(\Lambda+3\), so the note does not rely on the resulting monotonicity formula. The paper also explicitly leaves a genuine finite-screen Hamiltonian charge-memory relation for future work.

#### [[Vacuum Gravity from Entropy Stability, Spectra, and Exact Waves (2607.18518v1)]]

[arXiv:2607.18518v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18518v1) — David S. Pereira. `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`.

Source claim: a logarithmic curvature operator acting on \(\Lambda^0\oplus\Lambda^1\oplus\Lambda^2\) has, around Minkowski, exactly the Hessian of

\[
AR+BR_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
A=\frac{3\beta}{\ell_{\rm P}^4},
\quad
B=\frac{5\beta^2}{2\ell_{\rm P}^4}.
\]

Mechanism: \(\mathbb R=O(h)\), so logarithmic powers \(\operatorname{Tr}\mathbb R^n\) with \(n\ge3\) cannot enter the Minkowski Hessian. The locked spectrum contains the massless graviton, \(m_0^2=3/(5\beta)\), and an opposite-residue spin-two pole \(m_2^2=-6/(5\beta)=-2m_0^2\). The note also reconstructs the distinction between diagonalizable and diagonal-restricted curvature, and the exact square-zero pp-wave mechanism \(\mathbb R^2=0\). Likely use: `linearize.md`, higher-derivative stability, gravitational energy on a selected eigenspace, and the warning that analytic traces miss nilpotent Jordan data. It is not high because it supplies no new charge, boundary, or CPS construction, and its central pole analysis is a model-specific quadratic-gravity benchmark.

#### [[(De)Coherence of a quantum system in an anti-de Sitter spacetime (2607.18407v1)]]

[arXiv:2607.18407v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18407v1) — Mandas Biswas, Anupam Mazumdar. `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

Source claim: a trapped oscillator coupled through the Fermi tidal vertex \(H_{\rm int}=m\ddot h_{11}x^2/4\) to discrete global-AdS graviton modes has a finite-time bath kernel

\[
\alpha_R(t)=\frac{\sin[(\omega_{n\ell}-2\omega_m)t]}
{\omega_{n\ell}-2\omega_m},
\]

whose sign oscillations are interpreted as decoherence and recoherence. The transferable mechanism is boundary condition \(\to\) symplectic normalization \(\to\) discrete modes \(\to\) spectral kernel \(\to\) flat continuum limit. This is directly useful for alternative-quantization work because a boundary branch changes the entire bath measure. It is not high because the source contradicts itself about scalar falloffs, omits daggers in the Lindblad operator, miscomputes the sinc limit and a worked detuning, and does not prove full recoherence or complete positivity. The detailed note preserves the mechanism while treating the numerical rate as unverified.

#### [[Hawking emission of massive vector fields by Kerr black holes (2607.18395v1)]]

[arXiv:2607.18395v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18395v1) — Marco Calzà, Miguel Faria, Yuber F. Perez-Gonzalez, João G. Rosa. `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

Source claim: FKKS/Dolan separation plus the full Proca stress tensor yields Kerr greybody factors for two vector branches and the physical longitudinal branch, followed by Hawking mass/spin loss functions. The central reusable construction is the nonlinear polarization eigenvalue \(\nu\), branchwise horizon/infinity boundary conditions, and

\[
\Gamma_{lmS}=1-\frac{|R_{\rm out}^{lm}|^2}{|R_{\rm in}^{lm}|^2},
\qquad
\mu\le\omega<m\Omega_H
\]

for superradiant propagation. The small-mass longitudinal limit requires \(\mu,\nu\to0\) together; it is scalar-like for \(\mu>0\) but becomes pure gauge at exactly \(\mu=0\). Likely use: a stringent benchmark for the vault's Proca bulk-helicity versus boundary-condition distinction. It is not high because the paper is numerical black-hole phenomenology rather than an active CPS/boundary calculation, and its transformed radial equation, flux appendix, entropy formula, and numerical convergence reporting contain unresolved problems.

#### [[Wave propagation and hybridization of plasmonic modes in Maxwell-Chern-Simons pseudo-electrodynamics (2607.18520v1)]]

[arXiv:2607.18520v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18520v1) — S. Duque Cesar, M. J. Neves. `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`.

Source claim: a nonlocal Chern--Simons term gives \(\omega^2=k^2+\theta^2\) and mixes longitudinal and transverse planar electric amplitudes. Adding \(\mathbf j=\sigma_c\mathbf E\) gives a \(2\times2\) response matrix; with \(z=\sqrt{1-n^2}\), its determinant becomes

\[
i\frac{\sigma_c}{2}z^3
+\left(1-\frac{\sigma_c^2}{4}\right)z^2
+i\frac{\sigma_c}{2}z

The matrix/cubic reduction is a useful Maxwell--Chern--Simons testbed. It is not high because the source imposes a bulk planar Ohm law rather than an actual interface problem, gives no decay/jump conditions proving a surface plasmon, and its stated reactive prescription leaves an uncancelled imaginary factor. Topological protection and high-conductivity energy claims are therefore unsupported.

\[

Likely use: a careful terminology reference for distinguishing internal principal connections from soldered frame gravity. It is not high because the decisive specialization trivializes the proposed Yang--Mills curvature and inserts the Einstein term by hand; the source itself presents this as an initial, conference-scale construction.
