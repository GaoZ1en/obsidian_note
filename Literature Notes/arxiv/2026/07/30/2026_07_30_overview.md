## Resumming Kerr Quasinormal-Mode Frequencies — 2607.27043v1

- **Technical relevance:** `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.
- **Author signal:** none.
- **Source claim:** divergent WKB expansions around the Chandrasekhar--Detweiler potential peak can be made accurate for damped Kerr modes by Padé and Borel--Padé resummation.
- **Main mechanism:** the local peak expansion is pushed to 21st WKB order in slow rotation and 41st order at fixed spin, then resummed and solved iteratively. For the fundamental $m=0$ damped mode at $a=0.99$, the reported real-frequency error against Leaver is below $10^{-7}$.

- **Likely use:** a high-order benchmark for spectral approximations and a clean warning about the change of scaling region near extremality.
- **Why not high:** it is a spectral-numerical method paper rather than a charge, boundary, or CPS construction.
