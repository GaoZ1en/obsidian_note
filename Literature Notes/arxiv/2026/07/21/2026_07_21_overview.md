## [[The de Sitter Scalar Discrete Series: Gupta-Bleuler Structure and Holography (2607.17124v1)]]

Maryam Bajalan, Jean-Pierre Gazeau, Hamed Pejhan — [arXiv:2607.17124v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17124v1), 32 pages. Primary/new status: math-ph:new; cross-list: hep-th.

**Technical relevance:** T1-boundary, T1-symplectic, T2-dS-BH-holography, T3-math.
**Author-signal relevance:** none used.

The paper starts from

$$\begin{align}
\left(Q_0+(p-1)(p+2)\right)\phi=0
\end{align}$$

on global $\mathrm{dS}_4$, constructs two hypergeometric radial branches, evaluates their Klein--Gordon Gram matrix, and obtains the invariant filtration

$$\begin{align}
V_g\subset V=V'\oplus V_g\subset V_{\mathrm{tot}}, \qquad V_{\mathrm{phys}}=V/V_g.
\end{align}$$

The low-$L$ first and second branches are separately null but have unit cross-pairing; their positive/negative combinations complete a Krein carrier. Rescaled limits at $\mathcal I^\pm$ map the physical $L\ge p$ modes to a nonlocal-kernel Hilbert carrier on $S^3$, and a mode kernel implements the coefficientwise isometry $V/V_g\simeq H_{p-1}$.

Independent Mathematica, Sage, and xAct checks reproduced the Casimir reduction, both radial ODE branches on their stated domain, source-ordered Wronskians and normalizations, the low-mode Gram diagonalization, gauge-space dimension, high-$L$ boundary isometry, and constant-curvature geometry. Two failures materially change how the paper should be read:

1. with the displayed actions, $\overline{V'}$ is a proper invariant subspace of $V_{\mathrm{tot}}/V$, so that quotient is reducible and its KG form cannot be inherited through quotienting by the positive-norm space $V$;
2. the printed low-$L$ boundary coefficient misses a factor two:

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_\pm\phi_{L<p,lm} =2\frac{\Gamma(L+2)}{\Gamma(L+p+2)}Y_{Llm}.
\end{align}$$

The positive physical quotient and high-$L$ Fourier kernel do not depend on either failed claim. This remains high priority because it supplies a complete quotient/boundary benchmark and a concrete repair target for the vault's CPS and dS work.
