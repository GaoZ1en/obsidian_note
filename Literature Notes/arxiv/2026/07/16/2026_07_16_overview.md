## 1. Perturbative algebraic quantum field theory with smoothened boundary — `2607.13765v1`

Detailed note: [[Perturbative algebraic quantum field theory with smoothened boundary (2607.13765v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T3-math`, `T2-model`.

The paper replaces a sharp compact boundary by a cutoff collar

$$\begin{align}
(C,N,f), \qquad N=(\operatorname{supp}f)^\circ\setminus C',
\end{align}$$

and keeps the $df$-supported term in

$$\begin{align}
\iota_{Q[f]}\Omega=\delta L[f]-\theta[df].
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\kappa(V):=Q_0V+\frac12\{V,V\}^{\rm std}-i\hbar A(V)
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

Retarded and advanced Møller transport then gives

$$\begin{align}
Q_V^R-Q_V^A =\frac{i}{\hbar}[J,\cdot]_{\star_V},
\end{align}$$

and on a causal cylinder the corrections reduce to past/future charge commutators,

$$\begin{align}
\overrightarrow\Omega_JF=[J^-,F]_{\star_V}, \qquad \overleftarrow\Omega_JF=[F,J^+]_{\star_V}.
\end{align}$$

The monograph note maps every main section and Appendix A, supplies a global notation dictionary and derivation map, reconstructs the free/interacting pAQFT and smoothened BV--BFV chain, and translates the current into the vault's off-shell CPS language. ## 1. Negative shocks versus static patch holography — `2607.14042v1`

Detailed note: [[Negative shocks versus static patch holography (2607.14042v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`. **Author signal:** `A-big-name` for Douglas Stanford; separated from the technical reason.

**Source claim:** the Euclidean de Sitter path integral with an observer worldline cannot equal a positive cyclic Hilbert-space trace once gravitational OTOCs are included.

**Mechanism:** the dS$_3$ shock kernel combines observer recoil and gravitational backreaction. Its lifted $\ell=1$ mode carries recoil, while its negative angular mean is a time-advance shock. The wrong-sign eikonal series has two lateral resummations $\mathcal F_{12}$ and $\mathcal F_{14}$, exchanged by cyclicity. $$\begin{align}
\mathrm{OTOC}\sim1+Ge^t+\left(G^2+\frac{G}{m}+\frac1{m^2}\right)e^{2t}+\cdots,

with same-sign coefficients. Averaging the two lateral resummations restores cyclicity but violates both positivity inequalities.

**Use case:** a precise diagnostic for any static-patch observer algebra and a concrete example of a would-be gauge shock mode made physical by an observer defect. Mathematica reproduced the shock equation, derivative jump, first two moments, small-$Gm$ expansion, and the two-point Fourier kernel.

**Why not high:** it has no direct CPS, charge, or boundary symplectic construction; its value is a dS holography stress test.

## 2. A de Sitter Anti-Scrambling Algebra — `2607.13665v1`

Detailed note: [[A de Sitter Anti-Scrambling Algebra (2607.13665v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`. **Author signal:** none used.

**Source claim:** a negative de Sitter eikonal phase moves half-horizon support through the bifurcation surface, breaks exact KMS, and makes the Hartle--Hawking state nontracial on the observer's crossed-product algebra.

**Mechanism:** early and late operators are dressed by

$$\begin{align}
S_{\rm grav}=e^{ixP_AP_B}, \qquad x<0,
\end{align}$$

so $S_{\rm grav}a(U)S_{\rm grav}^{-1}=a(U-xP_B)$. For the de Sitter sign, this translation can intersect the original commutant. Tomita--Takesaki theory reduces the KMS defect to the commutator of an undressed commutant operator with the translated observable. The defect is nonperturbative in $x$: compact smearing makes it smaller than every power even though the exact continuation is nonzero.

**Central construction:** quantizing the observer clock gives

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal A_{\rm cr}=\mathcal A\rtimes\mathbb R.
\end{align}$$

Exact energy-space OTOCs violate cyclicity for $x<0$; no clock density $\rho(q)$ repairs it for arbitrary intermediate energies. At $x\to-\infty$, clustering instead yields the type III$_1$ free product $\mathcal A_{0,A}*\mathcal A_{0,B}$.

**Use case:** an explicit bridge from horizon scattering to modular/KMS data, crossed products, and commutants. The detailed note distinguishes proved results (cyclic but nonseparating vacuum, nontracial exact crossed product) from the conjecture that the finite-$x$ commutant is trivial.

**Why not high:** the algebraic structure is valuable but does not directly construct the user's CPS/boundary charges. A Mathematica check also found a sign typo in the printed trace-defect prefactor; the corrected factor is recorded in the note.

## 3. Out-of-time-ordered Correlators in de Sitter Revisited — `2607.13137v1`

**Technical relevance:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T1-symmetry`, `T2-spectral`. **Author signal:** none used.

**Source claim:** observer-dressed scalar OTOCs in de Sitter have a robust tree-level time-advance contribution, but their loop-level eikonal completion encounters a vector-sector IR obstruction sourced by large diffeomorphisms.

**Mechanism:** in dS$_3$, a localized shock would require

$$\begin{align}
T_{uu}=\frac{f(\theta)+f''(\theta)}{16\pi G_N}\,\delta(u),
\end{align}$$

whose operator has an $\ell=1$ zero mode. The ladder derivation avoids assuming a localized shock and expresses the phase as a propagator contraction,

$$\begin{align}
i\delta=-\frac14\int du\,d\bar v\, D_{uu,vv}\,p^u p^v.
\end{align}$$

The full graviton decomposition cancels the worst physical-sector divergence but leaves a vector $\ell=1$ logarithm. External constrained sources may project it out at tree level; internal loop propagators cannot. A separate $\ell=0$ large-diffeomorphism contribution gives the positive $G_Ne^{t/\ell_{dS}}$ tree growth.

**Central result:** mass regularization isolates the $\ell=1$ pole and makes all odd Newton powers vanish in symmetric configurations, producing an apparent doubled exponent $4\pi/\beta_{dS}$; the unregulated pole--antipode tree result projects to $\ell=0$ and retains $2\pi/\beta_{dS}$.

**Use case:** tells the local dS/Killing-horizon project which OTOC statements are tree-robust and which are gauge/IR-obstructed.

**Why not high and why overview-only:** it is a diagnostic calculation rather than a reusable boundary/CPS formalism, and the authors themselves leave the complete external gravitational dressing and loop resolution open. A source typo drops one $L$ factor in an AdS harmonic denominator; simple angular projections were checked, but the full propagator was not independently reproduced.

## 7. An exact algorithm for $U(N)$ matrix models in the gauge-invariant singlet sector — `2607.13725v1`

**Technical relevance:** `T2-model`, `T3-math`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`. **Author signal:** none used.

**Source claim:** finite-$N$, finite-coupling singlet-sector observables can be computed exactly in a Schur/restricted-Schur basis with a reusable symmetric-group reduction.

**Mechanism:** ordinary Schur polynomials diagonalize the one-matrix free Hamiltonian; restricted Schurs extend the basis to multiple matrices. Interaction matrix elements reduce to cosets/double cosets and symmetric-group character sums, producing closed polynomials in $N$. The one-matrix implementation is checked against the exact map to $N$ free fermions.

**Central construction:** truncate by excitation number in an orthogonal gauge-invariant basis, precompute group-theoretic data once, and evaluate any gauge-invariant observable as a finite character sum. The multi-matrix bottleneck is the absence of an efficient general algorithm for restricted characters.

**Use case:** relevant to finite-$N$ matrix-horizon toy models and possible exact diagonalization of singlet sectors.

**Why not high and why overview-only:** it is a computational model tool with no present boundary/CPS object, and the 60-page implementation would be best revisited when a concrete matrix-model calculation is active.
