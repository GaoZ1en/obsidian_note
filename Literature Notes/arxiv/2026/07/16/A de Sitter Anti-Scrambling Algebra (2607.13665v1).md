The paper is the operator-algebra companion to the negative-shock calculation. Its main contribution is to turn the sign of an eikonal phase into a statement about modular analyticity, commutants, crossed products, and free products. The finite-coupling claim that the commutant is trivial remains a conjecture; the KMS and nontracial mechanisms are the technically established parts.

## Observer-dressed early and late algebras

The four-dimensional starting point is a Schwarzschild--de Sitter exterior terminated at an observer boundary $r=r_{\rm Obs}$. Early and late matter operators are separated by a scrambling-scale time $T$. The double scaling limit is

$$
G_N\to0,
\qquad
T\to\infty,
\qquad
G_Ne^{2\pi T/\beta_c}\ \text{fixed}.
$$

Highly boosted early and late insertions create null stress tensors on opposite cosmological horizons. Their angular ANEC operators $P_U(\Omega)$ and $P_V(\Omega)$ enter the eikonal action

$$
I=16\pi G_N\int d\Omega\,
P_V(\Omega)
\frac1{-\nabla_{S^2}^2+1-\Lambda r_c^2}
P_U(\Omega).
$$

With $\mathcal S=e^{iI}$, gravitationally corrected correlators can be represented on a doubled QFT Hilbert space by dressed operators

$$
\widetilde a=\mathcal S^{1/2}a\mathcal S^{-1/2},
\qquad
\widetilde b=\mathcal S^{-1/2}b\mathcal S^{1/2}.
$$

The observer algebra $\mathcal A$ is generated by all such early $a$- and late $b$-operators. This construction keeps the shock scattering in operator multiplication rather than treating it as an external correction to expectation values.

## Near-Nariai reduction and the sign $x<0$

Near the Nariai limit, angular dependence decouples and the eikonal phase reduces to a two-dimensional model,

$$
S_{\rm grav}=e^{ixP_AP_B},
$$

where the de Sitter case has $x<0$. The generators $P_A,P_B$ translate chiral fields along half horizons. Dressing acts as a state-dependent translation, for example

$$
S_{\rm grav}\,a(U)\,S_{\rm grav}^{-1}
=a(U-xP_B).
$$

For the AdS sign $x>0$, this is a half-sided translation that preserves the appropriate half-horizon algebra. For $x<0$, it shifts support in the opposite direction and can carry an observable into causal contact with an operator in the original commutant. This support crossing is the operator-algebra meaning of anti-scrambling.

## Exact OTOCs and their perturbative boundary

The four-point functions

$$
G_1=\langle\widetilde a_1\widetilde b_2\widetilde a_3\widetilde b_4\rangle,
\qquad
G_2=\langle\widetilde b_2\widetilde a_3\widetilde b_4\widetilde a_1\rangle
$$

are evaluated exactly in the two-dimensional model using confluent hypergeometric functions $U(a,b,z)$, with a sheet label determined by the signs of $x$ and the Kruskal separations. Their common formal expansion is a ${}_2F_0$-type asymptotic series.

At every fixed order in $x$, suitably smeared correlators retain the ordinary KMS/detailed-balance identities. The exact function does not: the analytic continuation crosses a nonperturbative singularity. This is why an order-by-order check cannot establish the exact observer algebra.

## Tomita--Takesaki reduction of the KMS defect

Let $J_{\Omega_A}$ be the modular conjugation of the undressed $A$-algebra. The difference between the two KMS boundary values reduces to a commutator,

$$
\Delta_{\rm KMS}
=\langle b_2^\dagger|
[J_{\Omega_A}a_1(s)^\dagger J_{\Omega_A},
S_{\rm grav}a_3S_{\rm grav}^{-1}]
|b_4\rangle.
$$

The first operator lies in $\mathcal A_{0,A}'$. Therefore KMS holds when gravitational dressing preserves the original half-horizon algebra. For $x<0$, the translated support crosses the bifurcation surface and the commutator acquires a contact term.

In the chiral model, the field commutator is distributional,

$$
\langle[\phi(U),\phi(U')]\rangle
=-\frac{i(-1)^h}{\Gamma(2h)}
\delta^{(2h-1)}(U-U').
$$

Substitution gives an exact KMS defect proportional to

$$
\theta(-x)
e^{-\frac{i}{x}(U_{13}+i\epsilon)(V_{24}-i\epsilon)}
x^{-2h_+}
\times\text{a finite derivative polynomial}.
$$

Thus the defect vanishes for the AdS sign and is nonzero for the de Sitter sign. Its oscillatory $e^{-iUV/x}$ behavior also explains why compact smearing makes it smaller than every power as $x\to0^-$: it is nonperturbative in the eikonal coupling.

## Quantizing the clock: the crossed product

A perfect clock has Hamiltonian $q$, conjugate $p$, and

$$
[q,p]=i.
$$

Imposing the total Hamiltonian constraint produces the crossed product

$$
\mathcal A_{\rm cr}=\mathcal A\rtimes\mathbb R,
$$

generated by $e^{ikq}$ and clock-dressed matter operators

$$
\widetilde{\mathcal O}(p)
=e^{iH_{\rm QFT}p}\widetilde{\mathcal O}e^{-iH_{\rm QFT}p}.
$$

The Hartle--Hawking vector is formally

$$
|\Psi_{\rm HH}\rangle=|\Omega\rangle\otimes e^{-\pi q}.
$$

Crossed-product correlators are energy- or time-smeared QFT correlators. For an ordinary KMS matter state, moving one operator around the trace is a contour deformation by $2\pi i$. The exact $x<0$ KMS defect obstructs that deformation.

For four energy projections, write

$$
k=E_1-E_2+E_3-E_4.
$$

Directly from the paper's preceding $G_1^{\rm cr}$ and $G_2^{\rm cr}$ formulas, the $x<0$ cyclic difference is

$$
\Delta_{\rm Tr}
=C_{E_1E_2E_3E_4}
e^{-\pi(E_2+E_4)}
(1-e^{-2\pi k})F_x(k).
$$

It is generically nonzero. More generally, no clock density $\rho(q)$ can restore cyclicity for all independent $E_i$, because the required relation makes $\rho(E_4)$ depend on the unrelated intermediate energies $E_2,E_3$.

## What is proved about the commutant

At finite $x<0$, the authors prove that the vacuum is cyclic for the dressed algebra but not separating. They constrain broad classes of candidate commutant operators and then conjecture

$$
\mathcal A'=\mathbb C,
\qquad
\mathcal A=\mathcal B(\mathcal H).
$$

This type-I/trivial-commutant statement is not a theorem of the paper. It should be cited as the proposed algebraic endpoint of the support-crossing mechanism.

## Large negative coupling and the free product

When $x\to-\infty$, clustering kills crossing partitions in alternating early/late correlators. The surviving mutually noncrossing partitions define the free-product state on

$$
\mathcal A_{0,A}*\mathcal A_{0,B}.
$$

The GNS space is

$$
\mathcal H_F
=|\Omega_F\rangle
\oplus\mathcal H_A^*
\oplus\mathcal H_B^*
\oplus(\mathcal H_A^*\otimes\mathcal H_B^*)
\oplus(\mathcal H_B^*\otimes\mathcal H_A^*)
\oplus\cdots.
$$

This free product is type III$_1$ and has a nontrivial commutant generated by right-acting operators. Multi-shock dS JT geometries give a semiclassical picture of each tensor word: alternating shocks separate earlier wavepackets, and infinite separation restores the right action.

The appearance of a nontrivial commutant at $x=-\infty$ does not contradict the finite-$x$ conjecture; it is an emergent algebra obtained after changing the scale separation and retaining only extremely early and late operators.

## Verification note and source corrections

The main operator-algebra and hypergeometric-monodromy arguments are source-derived. One exact energy-algebra step was independently checked with Mathematica:

- subtracting the stated $G_2^{\rm cr}$ from $G_1^{\rm cr}$ at $x<0$ gives the factor $e^{-\pi(E_2+E_4)}$, not the printed $e^{-\pi(E_2-E_4)}$ in `eq:2D-Deltatr-exact`. The printed expression has a nonzero residual for generic energies.

Other source issues relevant to reuse:

- an early two-point normalization misses a factor $1/(2\pi)$ relative to the completeness relation and later four-point formulas;
- one perturbative denominator uses $2h_A+n$ where factorization requires $2h_B+n$;
- substituting the stated time settings into one anti-scrambling expansion leaves an additional $e^{2t}$ unless $t=0$ or it is absorbed into $T$;
- the stated KMS-strip intervals put an extra $i$ inside $\operatorname{Im}t$ and $\arg U$.

The confluent-hypergeometric sheet continuation, Tomita--Takesaki domain assumptions, Morera/contour estimates, and the free-product clustering theorem were not independently reproduced. The trivial-commutant/type-I claim is explicitly retained as conjectural.

## Local use

- Treat the sign of the horizon eikonal phase as an algebra-morphism question: does dressing preserve a half-sided algebra or move it through its commutant?
- A perturbative BRST/KMS identity can hold to all orders while the exact resummation violates it; keep nonperturbative sheet data explicit.
- Crossed-product traces inherit the matter KMS property. If the underlying exact OTOC is not KMS, quantizing the observer clock does not repair cyclicity.
- Separate proved modular properties (cyclic, not separating) from the conjectured finite-coupling algebra type.
