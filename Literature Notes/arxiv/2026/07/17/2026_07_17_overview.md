## 1. Dynamical Entropy Is a Noether Charge — `2607.14289v1`

Detailed note: [[Dynamical Entropy Is a Noether Charge (2607.14289v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Author-signal relevance:** none used for placement.

The paper fixes an affine null generator \(l^\mu\), an auxiliary null normal \(n^\mu\), and the cut metric \(q_{\mu\nu}\), then adds the affine null GHY term

$$
I_{\mathcal N}
=
\frac{1}{8\pi G}
\int_{\mathcal N}\sqrt q\,\theta_l.
$$

Strict null Dirichlet data \(\delta q_{\mu\nu}=0\) and \(\delta l^\mu=0\) select a boundary-potential representative. Four geometric conditions choose an entropy flow \(\xi^\mu=\kappa\mathscr B l^\mu\) and imply the generalized zeroth law

$$
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\ln\frac{\kappa}{\sqrt q}=0,
\qquad
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\ln\kappa=\theta_l.
$$

The null-GHY-improved Noether heat is then intended to give the entropy density

$$
\mathcal S
=
\frac{\sqrt q}{4G}
\left(1-\mathscr B\theta_l\right).
$$

Differentiating with the correctly contracted Eq. (20), and only then using the zeroth-law condition, gives

$$
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\mathcal S
=
-\frac{\sqrt q}{4G}\mathscr B\,
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\theta_l.
$$

Affine, twist-free Raychaudhuri converts this into

$$
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\mathcal S
=
\frac{\sqrt q}{4G}\mathscr B
\left(
N_l^2+
\frac{\theta_l^2}{D-2}
+R_{ll}
\right)\ge0
$$

when the positive-temperature branch \(\kappa>0\) is chosen, future orientation gives \(\mathscr B\ge0\), the cut metric is positive, Einstein's equations hold, and \(T_{ll}\ge0\) for the chosen generator. The full null energy condition is a sufficient stronger assumption. Future orientation alone fixes only \(\kappa\mathscr B\ge0\). Equality remains possible; “strictly at each instant” means a local-in-time law, not strict positivity.

The detailed note keeps three claims separate: the null-boundary variational representative, the on-shell improved Noether potential, and the stronger CPS-integrability statement. The first is not established under the phase space printed in v1: \(\delta q=0\) and \(\delta l^\mu=0\) allow \(\delta\kappa_l\ne0\), although Eq. (11) has already dropped \(\sqrt q\,\kappa_l\). One must additionally impose \(\delta\kappa_l=0\), or vary the full \(\sqrt q(\kappa_l+\theta_l)\) term before restricting to affine configurations. The Letter also does not display the last step \(k_\xi=\delta Q_\xi-i_\xi\Theta\), and its claim of agreement with an integrable CPS charge is deferred to an unpublished reference.

Two further source issues must be carried with the conditional formula. Contracting Eq. (20) gives

$$
l\!\cdot\!\nabla\mathscr B
=
1-\mathscr B\,l\!\cdot\!\nabla\ln\kappa,
\qquad
l\!\cdot\!\nabla(\kappa\mathscr B)=\kappa,
$$

not the prose statement \(l\!\cdot\!\nabla(\kappa\mathscr B)=1\). The dimensionless identity that is true is \(\kappa^{-1}l\!\cdot\!\nabla(\kappa\mathscr B)=1\). The subsequent entropy derivative uses the correct first relation, so that typo does not break the second-law chain. More seriously, Eq. (23) defines \(\mathcal W^\mu=-n^\mu\sqrt q\,\theta_l\) inside a cut integral that already contains \(\sqrt q\); Eq. (24) follows only with the tensor-normalized \(\mathcal W^\mu=-n^\mu\theta_l\), or with an unstated compensating density convention.

## 1. The variational approach to infrared divergencies — `2607.14330v1`

Detailed note: [[The variational approach to infrared divergencies: Applications to black hole action integrals and holographic Wilson loops (2607.14330v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Author-signal relevance:** `A-big-name` for M. Bañados; the medium placement is technical, not author-driven.

**Source claim and mechanism.** Renormalize the on-shell action variation rather than the action. The bulk Euler--Lagrange term vanishes, and the boundary one-form separates as

$$
\int_{\partial M}\theta
=
q_n\delta\mu_n
+
\delta(\Theta_0+\Theta_\infty).
$$

The exact piece integrates to the boundary subtraction; the finite response is \(\delta I_{\rm ren}=q_n(\mu)\delta\mu_n\), with integrability condition \(\partial_mq_n=\partial_nq_m\). Black-hole regularity relations, such as \(\beta=8\pi n\) for Taub--NUT/Bolt, must be imposed before testing closure.

**Likely use.** This is a clean boundary-first comparison for the vault's off-shell/action-to-CPS workflow and for distinguishing counterterm ambiguities from physical response one-forms.

**Why not high today.** It organizes on-shell Euclidean action variations rather than directly deriving Lorentzian Hamiltonian surface charges or their algebra.

## 2. Birman--Schwinger Formulation of the Faddeev--Popov Zero-Mode Problem — `2607.14425v1`

Detailed note: [[Birman-Schwinger Formulation of the Faddeev--Popov Zero-Mode Problem (2607.14425v1)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T2-spectral`, `T3-math`, `T2-model`.

**Author-signal relevance:** none.

**Source claim and mechanism.** After removing constant gauge modes,

$$
\mathcal M_A
=
H_0^{1/2}(1+K_A)H_0^{1/2},
\qquad
K_A=H_0^{-1/2}V_AH_0^{-1/2}.
$$

The Gribov horizon is exactly \(-1\in\operatorname{spec}K_A\). The ghost propagator is the corresponding resolvent, and Gribov's no-pole criterion is its Born truncation. For the periodic transverse background \(A_y^3=a\cos Qx\), the problem reduces to a Mathieu/Jacobi chain; at \(Q=m=1\), the exact threshold is \(a_{\rm crit}\simeq1.9053\), while three- and five-mode truncations give \(2\) and \(1.907\).

**Likely use.** It is a solvable operator benchmark for the BRST/Maxwell work and directly complements the July 16 Gribov-horizon spectral note.

**Why not high today.** It does not construct BRST cohomology or a boundary/CPS structure, and its generic-Yang--Mills extension remains prospective.

## 4. JT gravity on the worldline — `2607.14213v1`

**Technical relevance:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`.

**Author-signal relevance:** none used for placement.

**Source claim.** Coupling a one-dimensional quantum system on a bulk worldline to Euclidean AdS JT gravity replaces fixed Euclidean evolution by an average over gravitationally fluctuating proper times.

**Main mechanism.** On the disk, the effective evolution operator has the form

$$
U_{\rm QG}
=
\int d\beta_{\rm obs}\,
\mu(\beta_{\rm obs})e^{-\beta_{\rm obs}H_{\rm QM}},
$$

where the measure is built from JT Wheeler--DeWitt wavefunctions and is semiclassically peaked at the geodesic separation. The worldline system therefore sees fluctuations of its total Euclidean time, or inverse temperature for a closed path. The double-trumpet sector instead has broad fluctuations because it lacks a smooth semiclassical saddle.

**Likely use.** This is a concrete observer/dressing model and a spectral kernel for studying how gravitational fluctuations smear ordinary quantum mechanics.

**Why not high today.** It is an exact low-dimensional model but does not directly touch the active charge, symplectic, or boundary-algebra derivations.

## 5. Anti-scrambling and euclidean folds from observer correlators in de Sitter space — `2607.14215v1`

**Likely use.** It connects the active de Sitter observer-algebra thread to explicit gravitational correlators and supplies a sharp diagnostic that a candidate finite-dimensional dual must reproduce.
