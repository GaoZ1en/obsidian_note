## Dissipative hydrodynamic actions and horizon symmetries in gravity

- arXiv: [2607.00866v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00866v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Dissipative hydrodynamic actions and horizon symmetries in gravity (2607.00866v1)]]

Source claim: a dissipative stress-tensor Schwinger-Keldysh action for the CFT dual to AdS4-Schwarzschild can be computed by solving linearized gravity on the CGL contour partially on shell, with hydrodynamic fields realized as relative diffeomorphisms between the horizon and asymptotic boundaries.

Main technical mechanism: in the transverse channel,

$$\begin{align}
\xi_y^\sigma=[\zeta^y]_{r_0}^{\infty},\qquad
B_{ty}^\sigma=\delta g_{ty}^{\sigma(s)}-\partial_t\xi_y^\sigma,\qquad
B_{xy}^\sigma=\delta g_{xy}^{\sigma(s)}-\partial_x\xi_y^\sigma,
\end{align}$$

and the unimposed Einstein constraint becomes the $\xi_y^\sigma$ equation of motion. In the full action the nonlinear invariant is the diffeomorphism-dressed source

$$\begin{align}
{\mathcal B}_{\mu\nu}
=\frac{\partial y^\alpha}{\partial x^\mu}
\frac{\partial y^\beta}{\partial x^\nu}g_{\alpha\beta}^{(s)}(y(x))-\eta_{\mu\nu}.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: a concrete horizon-boundary edge-mode analogue for comparing SK hydrodynamic symmetries, horizon boundary conditions, and finite-boundary/CPS constraint reduction.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the displayed fluctuation-dissipation matching for the $T^{ty}$ and $T^{tx}$ momentum-density correlators under the paper's convention $i\Delta=G^R-G^A$. Full xAct reproduction of the radial Einstein reduction was not completed.

## Soft Algebras via Bulk Double Soft Limits

- arXiv: [2607.00864v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00864v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Soft Algebras via Bulk Double Soft Limits (2607.00864v1)]]

Source claim: the celestial soft algebra does not directly imply a bulk operation where the first three universal gravitational soft factors generate the generic higher soft expansion.

Main technical mechanism: the consecutive double-soft expansion is organized as

$$\begin{align}
\mathtt{CSL}_{12}\mathcal M_n
=\frac{1}{\epsilon^2}\mathbb S_{12}^{(0,0),-2}\mathcal M_n
+\frac{1}{\epsilon}\left(\mathbb S_{12}^{(0,0),-1}+\mathbb S_{12}^{(0,1),-1}+\mathbb S_{12}^{(1,0),-1}+\mathbb S_{12}^{(2,0),-1}\right)\mathcal M_n+\cdots.
\end{align}$$

Its leading collinear singularity forms a single-soft-like expansion in the collinear momentum,

$$\begin{align}
\lim_{1\parallel2}\mathtt{CSL}_{12}
=\frac{1}{\epsilon}\frac{4t-2}{t(1-t)}\frac{\bar z_{12}}{z_{12}}S_P^{(0)}
-\frac{2(2t-1)^2}{t(1-t)}\frac{\bar z_{12}}{z_{12}}S_P^{(1)}
-\epsilon\frac{14t^2-10t+2}{t}\frac{\bar z_{12}}{z_{12}}S_P^{(2)}+\cdots,
\end{align}$$

but this does not generate sub-cubically soft terms from the first three soft factors.

Likely use case: a cautionary reference for celestial soft-charge notes: boundary OPE recursion should not be used as a direct bulk soft-expansion generator without specifying the limiting prescription.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the algebraic recombination of the full consecutive-soft coefficient under $t\mapsto1-t$ into the displayed commutator and anticommutator coefficients. The full appendix table was not rederived from angular-momentum differential operators.

## Toward Hamiltonian simulations of Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory

- arXiv: [2607.00082v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00082v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `cond-mat.str-el`, `hep-lat`, `quant-ph`
- Reason codes: `T2-model`, `T1-symmetry` nearby
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Toward Hamiltonian simulations of Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory: constant modes and gauge field truncation (2607.00082v1)]]

Source claim: the flat sector of compact Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory on a torus is a Landau problem on the compact zero-mode torus and provides an analytically controlled benchmark for finite Hamiltonian truncations.

Main technical mechanism: the flat-sector Hamiltonian is

$$\begin{align}
H=\frac{Se^2}{2}\left[\left(p_1-\frac{k}{4\pi}a_2\right)^2+\left(p_2+\frac{k}{4\pi}a_1\right)^2\right],
\end{align}$$

with magnetic-translation algebra

$$\begin{align}
UV=e^{2\pi i/k}VU.
\end{align}$$

The finite truncation uses $\alpha=k/(N_xN_y)$ and lattice translations

$$\begin{align}
T_xT_y=e^{2\pi i\alpha}T_yT_x,
\end{align}$$

so exact global periodic translations are arithmetically obstructed unless the lattice sizes are commensurate with $k$.

In the commensurate case $k\mid N_x$ and $k\mid N_y$, reduced magnetic translations reproduce the $k$-dimensional topological multiplets exactly in the finite model; otherwise the finite clusters are approximation artifacts rather than literal continuum degeneracies.

Likely use case: useful for Maxwell-Chern-Simons quantization notes where global holonomy sectors, topological degeneracy, and finite truncation must be kept distinct from local propagating modes.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a compact-model and Hamiltonian-simulation benchmark, not a direct CPS/charge/boundary-symmetry paper.

## Madelung hydrodynamics and Poisson geometry of wave functions

- arXiv: [2607.01024v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01024v1)
- Categories: primary `math.DG`; cross-listed `math-ph`, `math.SG`
- Reason codes: `T1-symplectic` nearby, `T3-math`
- Technical relevance: medium-low. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: the Madelung transform between quantum mechanics and hydrodynamics can be treated as a momentum map for arbitrary oriented manifolds, and in that role it defines prequantum bundles over coadjoint orbits of semidirect extensions of diffeomorphism groups.

Main technical mechanism: for wave functions without zeros, the transform is a Kahler map between infinite-dimensional Fubini-Study and Fisher-Rao geometries. With noncritical zeros, the transform becomes a symplectomorphism onto coadjoint orbits with Morse-Bott densities. The same momentum-map language reframes the Wallstrom quantization condition and connects the Madelung setting to Marsden-Weinstein symplectic structures on knots and membranes.

Central construction: the transferable object is not a field-theory charge but a symplectic/momentum-map dictionary:

$$\begin{align}
\text{wave function phase-density data}
\longrightarrow
\text{hydrodynamic density/momentum data on a coadjoint orbit}.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: background reference for symplectic/momentum-map geometry, but not directly field-theory CPS.

Reason it is not high-priority today: the hydrodynamic/Poisson geometry is mathematical and not attached to boundary charges, gauge symmetries, or current AdS/CPS calculations.

## Post-Carroll Algebra, Conformal Extensions, and Field Theories

- arXiv: [2606.19112v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19112v2)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `math-ph`
- Reason codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T3-math`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: adding leading $c$-dependent corrections to Carroll transformations defines post-Carroll transformations and a post-Carroll algebra; unlike the ordinary Carroll algebra, the corrected structure admits a central charge in higher dimensions.

Main technical mechanism: the paper constructs the conformal extension of the post-Carroll algebra and field theories invariant under it, then constructs the conformal extension of the Carroll-Bargmann algebra, called the Carroll-Schrodinger algebra. The resulting algebra matches the symmetry algebra of higher-dimensional Carroll-Schrodinger theory.

Central construction: the relevant chain is

$$\begin{align}
\text{Carroll transformations}
\xrightarrow{\;c\text{-dependent corrections}\;}
\text{post-Carroll algebra}
\xrightarrow{\;\text{central extension}\;}
\text{Carroll-Bargmann algebra}
\xrightarrow{\;\text{conformal extension}\;}
\text{Carroll-Schrodinger algebra}.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: useful background for null-boundary and Carrollian symmetry notes, especially when distinguishing strict Carroll limits from first post-Carroll corrections and central extensions.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a replacement-only algebra/field-theory structure paper, not a new charge, flux, CPS, or boundary-condition construction.

- Reason codes: `T2-model`, `T1-symmetry` nearby
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: lattice Maxwell theory in the modified Villain formulation with an ultra-local theta-term action has an exact $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ duality structure once the $S$-transformation is defined with a nonlocal transformation step.

Main technical mechanism: naive Poisson resummation makes the action non-ultralocal. The paper removes that obstruction by folding a nonlocal procedure into the definition of the $S$ transformation. Wilson and 't Hooft loops then transform properly under $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ up to a phase from nontrivial loop self-linking.

Central construction: the reusable mechanism is

$$\begin{align}
\text{modified Villain Maxwell}+\theta
\xrightarrow{\;\text{nonlocal }S\text{-map}\;}
\text{exact lattice }SL(2,\mathbb Z)\text{ action on couplings and loop operators}.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: relevant to Maxwell duality and compact gauge-field notes, especially when comparing continuum electric-magnetic duality with lattice/global loop-operator phases.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is replacement-only and the update is minor language correction; technically useful, but not tied to the immediate CPS/horizon/soft-charge queue.
