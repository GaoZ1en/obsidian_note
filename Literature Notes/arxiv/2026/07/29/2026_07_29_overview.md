## [[Conserved Gravitational Charges as Curvature Fluxes (2607.25646v1)|Conserved Gravitational Charges as Curvature Fluxes — 2607.25646v1]]

- **Technical relevance:** `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`.
- **Author signal:** none used.
- **Source claim:** a divergence-free rank-four tensor and an antisymmetric Killing potential give one curvature-flux construction for energy, momentum, rotation, and boost/center-of-mass charges.
- **Central mechanism:** for flat Poincaré
  \[
  \xi^\mu=a^\mu+\omega^\mu{}_\nu x^\nu,
  \qquad
  F^{\mu\nu}
  =
  \frac{x^\mu\xi^\nu-x^\nu\xi^\mu}{n-1}
  +\frac{x^2\omega^{\mu\nu}}{2(n-1)},
  \]
  and the exact linearized identity is
  \[
  \xi_\lambda(G^{\mu\lambda})^{(1)}
  =
  -\frac{n-1}{2(n-3)}
  \partial_\nu
  \left[
  F_{\beta\sigma}(P^{\nu\mu\beta\sigma})^{(1)}
  \right].
  \]
  Weighted vacuum falloffs then permit the conditional replacement \(P^{(1)}\to R^{(1)}\).
- **Independent result:** the flat potential, master identity, ADM energy/momentum, Schwarzschild and boosted-Schwarzschild normalizations, RT rotation/boost reductions, displaced-Schwarzschild center of mass, Kerr spin, gauge covariance, and generic-Einstein Weyl current were machine checked.
- **Decisive failure:** the AdS expansion (IV.14) omits
  \[
  \frac{2\Lambda}{(n-1)(n-2)}
  \left(
  \delta^\nu_\sigma h^\mu{}_\beta-\delta^\nu_\beta h^\mu{}_\sigma
  +\delta^\mu_\beta h^\nu{}_\sigma-\delta^\mu_\sigma h^\nu{}_\beta
  \right)
  \]
  under Section II's stated “linearize all-down, then raise with \(\bar g\)” convention. The exact \(\mathcal P\)-flux (IV.13) is safe; the Riemann-only AdS representative must first repair the convention and then impose falloffs.
- **Project use:** this should be the next reference for comparing exact \(\mathcal P\) forms with local Iyer--Wald/Barnich--Brandt representatives and for testing weighted Ricci falloffs in AdS and null-boundary work.

- **Technical relevance:** `T2-spectral`.
- **Author signal:** none.
- **Source claim:** the short-time thermal OPE and the long-time quasinormal-mode expansion are two analytic representations of the same retarded correlator with an overlap in complex time.
- **Mechanism and formula:** Mellin transforming the QNM sum gives
  \[
  \mathcal M(s,k)
