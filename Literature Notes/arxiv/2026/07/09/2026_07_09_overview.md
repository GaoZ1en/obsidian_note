- Detailed note: [[BMS3 invariant field theories (2607.06826v1)]]

Source claim: the paper constructs and organizes BMS$_3$ invariant two-dimensional field theories, with electric, magnetic, canonical, coupled, sigma-model, Toda, and symplectically deformed examples. The crucial boundary result is that BMS invariance requires controlling corner terms at $u\to\pm\infty$ by boundary matching conditions and explicit counterterms.

Main technical mechanism: for the free electric scalar,

$$\begin{align}
{\cal L}=\frac12\dot\psi^2,\qquad
\psi=\psi_0(\sigma)+u\psi_1(\sigma),
\end{align}$$

the BMS variation produces endpoint terms proportional to ${\cal L}(\Lambda)\pm{\cal L}(-\Lambda)$ and a divergent superrotation term proportional to $\Lambda b'$. The paper cancels the latter with a corner counterterm and then builds Noether charges

$$\begin{align}
Q=i\int d\sigma\,(f{\cal H}+b{\cal P})
\end{align}$$

whose improved currents realize central extensions

$$\begin{align}
c_M=\frac{96\pi}{\gamma^2},\qquad
c_L=\lambda\frac{96\pi}{\gamma}.
\end{align}$$

The monodromy sector follows from the Riccati-to-linear map

$$\begin{align}
\frac12\psi_1^2-\frac{2}{\gamma}\psi_1'={\cal H}_0,\qquad
\psi_1=-\frac{4}{\gamma}\frac{Y'}{Y},\qquad
Y''-\kappa Y=0,
\end{align}$$

and reproduces the flat 3D gravity conical-defect window $-1/(8G)<E_0<0$.

Likely use case: a direct boundary-model laboratory for BMS charges, corner counterterms, matter-induced flux-balance laws, and the relation between BMS stress-tensor data and 3D gravity monodromy classes.

## On Integrable Structures on Non-compact Boundaries in Three-Dimensional Gravity

- arXiv: [2607.06867v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06867v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T1-charge`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none from the explicit profile watchlist.
- Detailed note: [[On Integrable Structures on Non-compact Boundaries in Three-Dimensional Gravity (2607.06867v1)]]

Source claim: the paper studies AdS$_3$ Einstein gravity in Chern-Simons form on non-compact spatial boundaries. In a diagonal boundary-current sector, the reduced phase space has affine $\hat{\mathfrak u}(1)$ current algebra, finite surface charges, exact Brown-York variables, a closed radial flow equation, and a Lax/inverse-scattering description of integrable boundary dynamics.

Main technical mechanism: radial gauge leaves boundary currents ${\cal J}_\pm$ and chemical potentials $\mu_\pm$ satisfying

$$\begin{align}
\partial_t{\cal J}_\pm=\pm\partial_x\mu_\pm.
\end{align}$$

The reduced surface-charge variation is

$$\begin{align}
\delta Q(\epsilon)=\int_\Sigma dx\,
(\epsilon_+\delta{\cal J}_+ + \epsilon_-\delta{\cal J}_-),
\end{align}$$

and the current algebra is

$$\begin{align}
\{{\cal J}_\pm(x),{\cal J}_\pm(y)\}
=\pm\frac{k}{\pi}\partial_x\delta(x-y),\qquad
\{{\cal J}_+(x),{\cal J}_-(y)\}=0.
\end{align}$$

The exact fluid/gravity map gives

$$\begin{align}
\rho
=\frac{\pi}{2k}\left[
\frac{r^4+\ell^4}{r^4-\ell^4}({\cal J}_+^2+{\cal J}_-^2)
+\frac{4\ell^2r^2}{r^4-\ell^4}{\cal J}_+{\cal J}_-
\right],
\qquad
J=-\frac{\pi}{2k}({\cal J}_+^2-{\cal J}_-^2),
\end{align}$$

and hence

$$\begin{align}
\partial_r\rho
=-\frac{4\ell^2r}{r^4-\ell^4}\sqrt{\rho^2-J^2},\qquad
\partial_rJ=0.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: finite-boundary AdS$_3$ charge and CPS bookkeeping, especially the separation between Hamiltonian boundary time flow and non-Hamiltonian radial cutoff flow.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the algebraic perfect-square relation behind $\rho^2-J^2$ and the radial derivative formula from the source expressions for $\rho$ and $J$. The Chern-Simons symplectic reduction and inverse-scattering sector remain source-derived.

- Detailed note: [[Horizon-Restricted Leading Soft QED as Open Quantum System (2607.07342v1)]]

Source claim: black-hole-horizon-induced charged-branch decoherence can be formulated as leading-soft QED restricted to an exterior algebra. The fixed-history Feynman-Vernon identity ${\cal F}[J,J]=1$ remains exact; the effect comes from unequal-history soft records projected into the horizon complementary output.

Main technical mechanism: the leading soft factor

$$\begin{align}
S^{(0)}_\lambda(q)=e\sum_i\eta_iQ_i
\frac{p_i\cdot\varepsilon_\lambda(q)}{p_i\cdot q}
\end{align}$$

is packaged as a branch-current displacement. The horizon projection is

$$\begin{align}
\alpha_a^{H,(0)}=P_H{\cal K}_\Omega J_a,\qquad
\Delta\alpha_{ab}^{H,(0)}
=P_H{\cal K}_\Omega(J_a-J_b),
\end{align}$$

and the coherent horizon Gram entry is

$$\begin{align}
G_{ab}^{H,(0)}
=\exp\left[
-\frac12\|\Delta\alpha_{ab}^{H,(0)}\|^2
+i\,{\rm Im}\langle\alpha_b^{H,(0)},\alpha_a^{H,(0)}\rangle
\right].
\end{align}$$

The horizon channel is the Schur multiplier

$$\begin{align}
({\cal E}_H\rho)_{ab}=G^H_{ab}\rho_{ab}.
\end{align}$$

Three branches define the Bargmann invariant

$$\begin{align}
{\cal B}^H_{123}=G^H_{12}G^H_{23}G^H_{31},
\end{align}$$

with a phase equal to an oriented symplectic area in horizon soft phase space.

Likely use case: a precise language for soft records, horizon complementary algebras, memory-like displacement data, and finite-dimensional CP constraints without overstating this as a Wald charge or full asymptotic-symmetry algebra.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the $3\times3$ Gram determinant/CP constraint and the spectral Kraus form of a Schur multiplier. The explicit black-hole projection kernel $P_H{\cal K}_\Omega$ remains source-derived.

Source claim: in Chern-Simons theories with charge-conjugation symmetry, codimension-two monodromy defects are new observables around which fields return to themselves up to charge conjugation. Wilson loops are labeled by untwisted affine-algebra representations; monodromy defects are labeled by the corresponding twisted affine algebra.

Main technical mechanism: modular and fusion data of untwisted and twisted affine algebras determine exact correlators of Wilson lines and monodromy defects, forming a $\mathbb Z_2$-crossed braided tensor category. Likely use case: Chern-Simons defect/monodromy reference if current 3D gravity or edge-mode work needs twisted sectors, charge-conjugation defects, or categorical defect bookkeeping.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is very strong mathematically, but the defect is topological/CFT/string-theoretic rather than a gravitational surface-charge or CPS boundary generator construction.
