## Relative entropy for $\lambda\phi^4$ in the Rindler wedge

**Paper:** [2607.07810v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07810v1), Markus B. Fröb, Albert Much, Kyriakos Papadopoulos. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `math-ph`; 38 pages. Codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T3-math`.

- **Source claim:** To first order in $\lambda$, the relative entropy between the vacuum and an interacting coherent state in the right Rindler wedge equals the classical interacting boost Noether charge and obeys a Bekenstein bound.
- **Mechanism:** The authors construct the perturbatively interacting wedge Weyl algebra, use Tomita--Takesaki theory and the Araki--Uhlmann formula, and invoke the Bisognano--Wichmann modular generator $\log\Delta_\Omega=2\pi\mathcal M^{01}$.
- **Central formula:** For the causal interacting solution $\varphi_f^{\mathrm{int}}$,

  $$
  S_{\mathrm{rel}}(\Omega\Vert W(f)\Omega)
  =2\pi\int_{x^0=0}x^1
  T_{00}(\varphi_f^{\mathrm{int}})\,d^3\vec x
  +O(\lambda^2).
  $$

- **Likely use:** A clean algebraic-QFT bridge from modular data to a Noether charge, with a future path to bifurcate horizons, relative entropy, and semiclassical area laws.
- **Why not high today:** The charge is a fixed-background Rindler boost charge in interacting scalar theory; there is no new gravitational boundary phase space, charge algebra, or edge-mode construction.

## Carroll supergravities

**Paper:** [2607.08329v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08329v1), Marc Henneaux. Primary `hep-th`. Codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`, `A-big-name`.

- **Source claim:** Electric and magnetic Carroll limits of $N=1$, $D=4$ supergravity retain first-class constraints and a common local supersymmetry algebra.
- **Mechanism:** The contraction is taken in Hamiltonian form by scaling the lapse and supersymmetry multiplier rather than the canonical fields. The electric limit preserves momentum-quadratic and quartic-fermion terms; the magnetic limit preserves spatial curvature and gravitino gradients.
- **Central construction:** Both sectors obey

  $$
  \{\mathcal S_A(x),\mathcal S_B(y)\}
  =-i\delta_{AB}\mathcal H(x)\delta(x-y),
  \qquad
  \{\mathcal H(x),\mathcal H(y)\}=0,
  $$

  while the two Hamiltonian constraints have complementary electric and magnetic content.
- **Likely use:** A controlled model for Carroll contractions of constrained gravity and for comparing how equal constraint algebras can support inequivalent surface-charge sectors.
- **Why not high today:** The paper does not develop a CPS or null-boundary charge algebra. Its strongest immediate value is as a concrete supergravity testbed.
- **Detailed note:** [[Carroll supergravities (2607.08329v1)]]. The electric and magnetic scalings were independently checked in Mathematica.

## Quantum corrections to the near-extremal thermodynamics of (warped) BTZ black holes

**Paper:** [2607.08482v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08482v1), Emilie Despontin, Stéphane Detournay, Robinson Mancilla, Chiara Toldo. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `gr-qc`; 50 pages. Codes: `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T1-boundary`.

- **Source claim:** Schwarzian and rotational throat zero modes are both lifted at small temperature in topologically massive gravity; the rotational sector cannot be discarded before fixing boundary conditions and ensemble.
- **Mechanism:** For Schwarzian modes, ordinary first-order perturbation theory closes within the throat. The rotational correction is non-normalizable, so $\langle h|\bar{\mathcal L}\,\delta h\rangle$ survives and records gluing to the far region.
- **Central formulas:** With $\ell_2=\ell/2$,

  $$
  \delta\lambda_{\mathrm{Schw}}^{(n)}
  =\frac{|n|T}{32r_0}
  \left(1+\frac{1}{2\mu\ell_2}\right),
  $$

  $$
  \delta\lambda_{\mathrm{Rot}}^{(n)}
  =-\frac{|n|T}{32r_0}
  \left(1-\frac{1}{2\mu\ell_2}\right).
  $$

- **Likely use:** A transferable warning for one-loop throat calculations: mode normalizability, asymptotic boundary conditions, and the order of the $T\to0$ and $r\to\infty$ limits determine the logarithmic partition function.
- **Why not high today:** This is a spectral/thermodynamic calculation, not a new charge or CPS construction.
- **Detailed note:** [[Quantum corrections to the near-extremal thermodynamics of (warped) BTZ black holes (2607.08482v1)]]. Mathematica reproduced the small-$T$ limits of the exact BTZ eigenvalues and the $\nu\to1$ WBTZ-to-BTZ coefficients.

## A symmetry-based resolution of pseudo-gauge ambiguities in local equilibrium

**Paper:** [2607.08569v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08569v1), Carlos Hoyos. Primary `nucl-th`; cross-list `hep-ph`, `hep-th`; 5 pages. Codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-charge`, `T3-math`.

- **Source claim:** Pseudo-gauge transformations and local-current improvements can be organized as additions of conserved spurious currents whose total charges vanish; local equilibrium should depend only on currents of genuine symmetries.
- **Mechanism:** A global density operator

  $$
  \widehat\rho=Z^{-1}e^{-\widehat W},
  \qquad
  \widehat W=\beta_\mu\widehat P^\mu+\zeta\widehat Q,
  $$

  is unchanged by constant chemical potentials for zero-charge spurious currents. At local equilibrium, derivative terms in the intensive variables must be included so that improvement dependence remains a redundancy. In a CFT, quasi-primary currents are proposed as genuine and descendants as spurious.
- **Central construction:** The prescription selects a genuine symmetric stress tensor first, then treats canonical/pseudo-gauge representatives as equivalent additions of total-divergence currents rather than independent thermodynamic charges.
- **Likely use:** A conceptual tool for separating physical charge generators from improvement ambiguities in boundary stress tensors and current algebras.
- **Why not high today:** The application is local-equilibrium spin hydrodynamics; it does not compute gravitational surface charges, symplectic forms, or boundary algebras.

## Entanglement Wedge Reconstruction without Holographic Quantum Error Correction

**Paper:** [2607.08684v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08684v1), Seiji Terashima. Primary `hep-th`; cross-list `quant-ph`; 18 pages. Codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T1-boundary`, `T3-math`.

- **Source claim:** Finite-$N$ entanglement-wedge reconstruction should be separated from the stronger claim that several boundary regions contain code-preserving representatives of one region-independent logical bulk operator.
- **Mechanism:** A common reconstruction for region $R$ and another region must commute, on the code, with the code-preserving local algebra of the erased complements. HaPPY-like codes realize this through a protected invisible logical sector; ordinary finite-$N$ holographic CFT local observables, including smeared stress tensors, detect the support and gravitational dressing of ordinary bulk operators.
- **Central construction:** Replace a shared logical algebra by region-dependent low-energy algebras and region-adapted bulk interpretations: “subregion complementarity” rather than exact holographic quantum error correction.
- **Likely use:** A sharp algebraic caution for gravitational subregion algebras, centers, dressings, and claims of region-independent bulk operators.
- **Why not high today:** The paper is an interpretive algebraic argument; it does not construct a new gravitational CPS, charge algebra, or explicit edge-mode phase space.
