# High-priority recommendations

## 1. Dressed States Call for Logarithmic Asymptotic Symmetries — 2607.16179v1

Detailed note: [[Dressed States Call for Logarithmic Asymptotic Symmetries (2607.16179v1)]]. Official source: [arXiv:2607.16179v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16179v1).

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T3-math`.

**Source claim.** Ordinary Poincaré--Maxwell and BMS representations are said to be inadequate for infrared-finite charged/gravitating states. Adding logarithmic large-gauge or supertranslation partners makes the soft charges a Heisenberg normal subgroup and is claimed to produce Lorentz-compatible dressed representations.

**Technical mechanism.** In electromagnetism, the ordinary and logarithmic charge densities are canonically paired on $S^2$; gravity has the analogous supertranslation/log-supertranslation pair. Harmonic decomposition gives independent finite-dimensional Heisenberg blocks, after which the enlarged asymptotic group is treated as a semidirect product and Mackey induction is invoked.

**Verdict.** The central brackets, kernels, finite-mode ranks, and Jacobi identities check. The representation conclusion does not: irreducibility does not survive restriction to the normal subgroup in general; Stone--von Neumann uniqueness fails for the full infinite-dimensional soft phase space; Lorentz invariance of the cocycle does not supply a unitary Lorentz implementation; and the hard/soft tensor factorization is not derived. This remains high priority because it exposes exactly where a boundary-charge algebra stops short of a quantum dressing theorem.

**Technical versus author signal.** Placement is entirely technical. Blagoje Oblak and Marios Petropoulos are strong domain signals, but neither is one of the profile's coded rising-star names, so no `A-*` code was used.

## 2. Gauge-Unfixed Hamiltonian Casimir and Static Torsionful Sector in the Katanaev--Volovich Model — 2607.15496v1

Detailed note: [[Gauge-Unfixed Hamiltonian Casimir and Static Torsionful Sector in the Katanaev--Volovich Model (2607.15496v1)]]. Official source: [arXiv:2607.15496v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15496v1).

Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T1-charge`, `T2-model`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Source claim.** The first-order Katanaev--Volovich model is reduced both by Dirac--Bergmann and Faddeev--Jackiw methods; a gauge-unfixed Poisson--Sigma Casimir is derived from the reduced first-class ideal and used to normalize a static torsionful black-hole-like branch.

**Technical mechanism.** The natural pairs are $(\omega_x,\varphi)$ and $(e_x^I,\varphi_I)$. After eliminating an auxiliary second-class sector, the two reduced constraints imply

$$
\partial_x\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DB}}
=2e^{2\beta\varphi}
\left(E_{\mathrm{KV}}\Psi+\varphi^I\Psi_I\right),
$$

with

$$
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DB}}
=e^{2\beta\varphi}\varphi_I\varphi^I
+2\int^\varphi ds\,(\alpha s^2+\Lambda)e^{2\beta s}.
$$

On the static branch, $\mathcal C_{\mathrm{DB}}=-2M_{\mathrm{Cas}}$ fixes a radial field $\xi$, while the true Killing norm is $N=e^{4\beta r}\xi$.

**Verdict.** The constraint count, reduced brackets, Casimir PDE and ideal identity, static residuals, surface gravity, and inner-branch algebraic first law check. Four local canonical formulas need correction; Appendix B's advertised full Faddeev--Jackiw inverse is not an inverse on a generic constant background; and for $\beta<0$ the claimed two-horizon range needs the extra lower bound $M_{\mathrm{Cas}}>m_\beta(\infty)$. Moreover, $M_{\mathrm{Cas}}$ is not yet a CPS/ADM charge because the paper supplies no boundary phase space or differentiable boundary generator.

**Technical versus author signal.** High placement is technical. There is no profile-coded author match.

## Scalar quasinormal modes of Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black holes — 2607.15731v1

Detailed note: [[Scalar quasinormal modes of Schwarzschild--anti-de Sitter black holes: spectral analysis and generalized boundary conditions (2607.15731v1)]]. Official source: [arXiv:2607.15731v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15731v1).

Reason codes: `T2-spectral`, `T1-boundary`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** For a massless scalar on SAdS$_4$, the two large-radius coefficients are mixed by a one-parameter condition rather than by imposing only Dirichlet data. Factoring the ingoing horizon branch and the AdS falloff reduces the radial equation to a Chebyshev collocation problem; the coefficient condition becomes one boundary row, and a nullspace projection gives a generalized matrix eigenvalue problem.

**Central construction.** If $A(\omega)$ and $B(\omega)$ are the two asymptotic coefficients, the family is $\cos\theta\,A+\sin\theta\,B=0$. The source numerically finds unstable modes for sampled positive $\theta$ at $x_+=0.01,1,50$ and $\ell=0$; it does not prove instability for every $\theta$ or angular sector.

**Use and boundary.** The coefficient-extraction row is directly reusable for finite-wall and AdS spectral experiments. It is not a paper about scalar alternative quantization: for a massless AdS$_4$ scalar the two exponents do not both lie in the BF normalizability window. The same dimensionless $\theta$ at different horizon radii also need not represent a fixed physical boundary coupling. Medium rather than high because it provides spectral technology, not a phase-space derivation of admissible boundary data.

## Gravitational index of the D1-D5-P black string — 2607.16134v1

Detailed note: [[Gravitational index of the D1-D5-P black string (2607.16134v1)]]. Official source: [arXiv:2607.16134v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16134v1).

Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** The condition $\beta\Omega_R=-2\pi i$ implements $(-1)^F$ on the non-extremal rotating D1--D5--P family. A complex double scaling keeps the BPS charges, $J_L$, and finite $\beta$ while promoting $J_R$ to an infrared regulator. The Euclidean action must first be identified as a mixed-ensemble action and then Legendre transformed to $I_{\mathrm{gc}}=\beta\mathcal F$.

**Central formula.** With $P=Q_1Q_5Q_n$ and $A=\sqrt{P-J_L^2}$,

$$
I_{\mathrm{gc}}
=\frac{\pi P}{A}
=-\frac{4\varphi_1\varphi_5\varphi_n}
{\omega_L^2-\omega_R^2},
$$

which is independent of $J_R$ and $\beta$. A finite-temperature decoupling gives an $S^3$ fibration over complex BTZ and the holomorphic elliptic-genus Cardy action.

**Use and boundary.** This is a clean example of a temperature-dependent complex geometry whose protected action loses regulator dependence only after the correct ensemble transform. Two equations contain factor errors and one explanatory formula omits $\ell_3^4$; the detailed note records the corrections. Medium rather than high because the reusable object is an index saddle, not a charge/CPS or boundary-phase-space construction.

## Quantum Mechanics on Lie Groups: II. Path Integrals — 2607.16029v1

Detailed note: [[Quantum Mechanics on Lie Groups: II. Path Integrals (2607.16029v1)]]. Official source: [arXiv:2607.16029v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16029v1).

Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T2-spectral`, `T3-math`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** A noncommutative Fourier/Trotter construction turns the $L^2(G)$ propagator into decompactified path integrals over $\mathfrak g$, summed over $Y_f\in\operatorname{Logs}(g_f)$. A coadjoint change of momentum converts the left-Haar symbol into a right-Maurer--Cartan action and leaves an endpoint modular factor $1/\Delta(g_i)$ for non-unimodular groups. Gaussian momentum integration yields the Euler--Arnold geodesic action.

**Central construction.** Global topology remains in the logarithm/winding sum, while local fluctuations and their Haar Jacobian live on the full Lie algebra. Measure ghosts cancel the two-loop $\delta(0)$ contacts. This is useful for separating trace, global sectors, measure, and local heat-kernel coefficients.

**Use and boundary.** The stated high-temperature term $1+\beta R/12$ is standard, but the paper's algebraic expression for $R$ has the opposite sign in isotropic $SU(2)$: its exact spectrum gives $1+\beta/8$, while Eqs. (6.8)--(6.9) give $1-\beta/8$. Medium rather than high because it is a powerful mathematical/spectral tool, not directly a field-theory boundary problem.

## Planck mass gravitinos in Einstein--Maxwell backgrounds — 2606.17139v2

Medium rather than high because it does not develop charges, asymptotic data, or a boundary symplectic form. ## Conservation laws in classical Poisson field theories — 2604.09500v2

Official source: [arXiv:2604.09500v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09500v2). Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T1-charge`, `T3-math`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** For gauge theories built from a spacetime-dependent Lie--Poisson bracket, ordinary translation invariance is obstructed by the explicit coordinate dependence of the Poisson tensor. The construction uses a field-dependent matrix $M_A=\exp(C_\mu{}^{\mu\nu}A_\nu)$ to organize the deformed gauge transformations and currents. In the constant canonical-Poisson case the stress tensor obeys a covariant conservation law, and its spatial integral reduces to a boundary term under suitable falloff.

**Central construction and use.** This is a possible algebraic testbed for distinguishing Noether identities, covariant conservation, and genuine finite charges in deformed gauge theories. It remains overview-only and medium because the paper does not supply a boundary phase space, an integrable surface generator, or a CPS derivation; the v2 comments report only references and minor corrections. No author signal affected placement.
