## Thermodynamics of boosted Schwarzschild black holes — `2607.20746v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.20746v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20746v1). Categories/status: `gr-qc:new; hep-th:cross`. Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `A-big-name`.

Detailed note: [[Thermodynamics of boosted Schwarzschild black holes (2607.20746v1)]].

Technical result:
\[
P^a=Mu^a,\qquad
\delta S=\beta^a\delta P_a,\qquad
\beta^a=8\pi G P^a.
\]
A Lorentz boost is improper at \(\mathscr I^+\), so it creates Bondi momentum while leaving
\[
\mathcal A=16\pi M^2,\qquad
\kappa_{\mathcal N}=-\frac1{4M}
\]
unchanged. The horizon generator becomes the BMS supertranslation \(T=w_g=u_0-u_in^i\), whose charge is \(H_{w_g}=u\cdot P=M\). Matching its integrable BMS variation to the horizon Iyer--Wald variation gives the covariant first law.

Independent checks reproduce the sphere integrals, momentum signs, area, surface gravity, first-law coefficients, and Massieu transform. The source nevertheless has three concrete errors: an inconsistent surface-gravity formula in prose, missing \(G\)-factors in \(\beta^a\) and \(\Phi\), and a likely wrong sign in the proposed partition function. The remaining structural gap is the adjusted CPS prescription for the field-dependent generator \(w_g(u[g])\).

Technical relevance is decisive; the Barnich author signal supports but does not cause the high placement.

## The leading-soft cubic graviton self-interaction on the black-hole horizon — `2607.21066v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.21066v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21066v1). Categories/status: `gr-qc:new; hep-th:cross; quant-ph`. Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

\[
W(\lambda,\lambda,\lambda)
=-\frac{3\lambda(2\lambda^2+\lambda+3)}{(\lambda+1)^2},
\qquad
W(7,7,7)=-35.4375.
\]

The paper then postulates

=\sum V_{123}
(b^\dagger_1b^\dagger_2b_3+\mathrm{h.c.})

### Uniqueness of null-local modular flow — `2607.20678v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.20678v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20678v1). Categories/status: `hep-th:new; gr-qc:cross; math-ph:cross`. Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T2-model`.

Detailed note: [[Uniqueness of null-local modular flow (2607.20678v1)]].

Characteristic scalar data on a diamond boundary give a generator-ultralocal covariance. Straightening an angle-preserving flow \(\zeta=\chi\partial_u\) by
\[
\rho=\int^u\frac{du'}{\chi}
\]
produces the universal KMS kernel
\[
\omega_\psi\sim
-\int\frac{F_1(\rho_1,\Omega)F_2(\rho_2,\Omega)}
{\sinh^2\pi(\rho_1-\rho_2-i0)}.
\]
Any nonzero flow change remains proportional to an angular delta function, hence identity-like on the infinite-dimensional \(L^2(S^{D-2})\) factor and not Hilbert--Schmidt for \(D\ge3\). This gives a reusable null-CPS obstruction to local quasi-equivalence.

It stays medium because the final vacuum-weight theorem depends on an asserted trivial modular centralizer, while the Maxwell/graviton extensions leave residual gauge, edge, dressing, and positivity questions open.

### A First-Order Gauge Approach to de Sitter General Relativity — `2607.20687v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.20687v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20687v1). Categories/status: `gr-qc:new`. Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

Detailed note: [[A First-Order Gauge Approach to de Sitter General Relativity (2607.20687v1)]].

The useful Cartan split is
\[
A=\frac12\omega^{ab}L_{ab}+\frac1l e^a\pi_a,
\]
\[
F^{ab}=R^{ab}-l^{-2}e^a\wedge e^b,
\qquad
F^a=l^{-1}T^a-l^{-2}dl\wedge e^a.
\]
The \(dl\)-term is exactly the Jacobian of a local frame rescaling. However, the source never varies its purportedly dynamical \(l(x)\). Its connection equation sets torsion algebraically by spin and gives \(T^a=0\) in spinless matter, contradicting the later claim that \(d\Lambda\ne0\) forces torsion. Two later “spin substitutions” are five-forms in four dimensions. This is retained as a technically instructive negative example, not promoted as a consistent cosmological theory.

### Wilson Towers as Local Bulk Fields — `2607.21397v1`

Official source: [arXiv:2607.21397v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21397v1). Categories/status: `hep-th:new`. Reason codes: `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`.

Detailed note: [[Wilson Towers as Local Bulk Fields (2607.21397v1)]].

The paper resolves
\[
\exp\!\left[
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{t^n}{n}\chi_1(q^n,\bar q^n)
\right]
=\sum_{N\ge0}t^N\chi_N
\]
by \(S_N\) cycle type, then strips the overall descendants:
\[
P_N=(1-q)(1-\bar q)\chi_N

Each \(d^{(N)}_{m,\bar m}\) counts a global multi-trace primary and hence one single-winding Wilson loop. Sage checks reproduce the \(N=2,3\) multiplicities and cycle normalization. This is especially reusable for `Articles/Quantization in AdS/perturbation/multi-particle primaries.md` and the July 3 conical-defect/Wilson-spool note. It remains medium because the BTZ ensemble interpretation and finite-\(c\) Virasoro-primary claims require additional holographic input.
