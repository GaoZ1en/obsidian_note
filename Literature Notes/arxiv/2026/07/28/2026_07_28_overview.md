- **Author signal:** none.
- **Source claim:** if the first asymptotic correction is \(f(\rho)-1\sim c_n\rho^n\), the light-like boundary layer has

  \rho_b\sim\omega^{-2/(n+2)},

  \omega^{\frac{2}{n+2}(2\Delta-d)}.

## [[Canonical Clock Sectors and Relational Frame Equivalence in Brans--Dicke Theory (2607.23852v1)|Canonical Clock Sectors and Relational Frame Equivalence in Brans--Dicke Theory — 2607.23852v1]]

- **Technical relevance:** `T1-symplectic`, `T2-model`.
- **Author signal:** Jerzy Lewandowski gives a separate `A-big-name` signal.
- **Source claim:** the Jordan--Einstein map is canonical on standard ADM phase space, but naive scalar-clock reduction gives
  \[
  \widetilde H_{\rm phys}
  =
  H_{\rm phys}+\frac{p_h}{\phi}.
  \]
- **Mechanism:** although \(\widetilde\phi=\phi\),
  \[
  \widetilde p_\phi
  =
  p_\phi-\frac{p_h}{\phi}.
  \]

  \[
  Q_{ab}=\phi h_{ab},\quad
  P^{ab}=p^{ab}/\phi,\quad
  P_\Phi=p_\phi-p_h/\phi
  \]
  restores a canonical, frame-adapted reduction.
- **Likely use:** a clean warning that canonical transformations and gauge/reduction choices need not commute unless the full canonical sector is moved.
- **Why not high:** it is an ADM relational-time result, not a covariant phase space or boundary-charge construction.

  \[
  f=\frac{x(x+\delta)}{(r_++x)^2}.
  \]
- **Mechanism:** under \(x=\epsilon R\), \(\delta=\epsilon a\), \(v=V/\epsilon\), both \(x\delta\) and \(x^2\) are \(O(\epsilon^2)\). Retaining second order in ingoing coordinates gives

- **Likely use:** bookkeeping for noncommuting extremal and null/near-horizon limits.
- **Why not high:** it supplies an expansion lesson but no null-boundary phase space, symplectic structure, or charge.
