The strongest reusable mechanism is the conjugate pair formed by a corner length and kink angle. The paper embeds this pair in open Virasoro TQFT and hyperbolic tetrahedral gluing. For the current finite-boundary work, the variational ensemble is more immediately transferable than the full TQFT normalization.

# EOW branes, pleated boundaries, and corners

The Euclidean AdS$_3$ region has three kinds of boundary data:

- $Q$: end-of-the-world branes with a Neumann-type condition;
- $P$: the pleated compact boundary assembled from hyperbolic faces;
- $C\subset P$: codimension-two kink curves with length $\ell_i$ and interior angle $\theta_i$.

# Fixed-angle and fixed-length actions

The pleated contribution and Hayward corner term are

$$
I_{\mathrm{pleated}}
=-\frac1{8\pi G_N}
\int_{P\setminus C}\sqrt h,K
+I_{\mathrm{Hayward}},
\tag{2.32}
$$

$$
I_{\mathrm{Hayward}}
=-\frac1{8\pi G_N}
\int_C\sqrt\gamma,(\pi-\theta).
\tag{2.33}
$$

Subtracting the Hayward term gives the fixed-angle action

$$
I_A=I_0-I_{\mathrm{Hayward}},
\tag{2.34}
$$

while adding the corner Legendre term gives

$$
I_L
=I_0+\frac1{8\pi G_N}\int_C\sqrt\gamma,\pi
=I_A+\frac{c}{12\pi}\sum_i\ell_i\theta_i.
\tag{2.35}
$$

Using the Brown--Henneaux normalization with AdS radius set to one,

$$
c=\frac{3}{2G_N},
$$

the coefficient satisfies $c/(12\pi)=1/(8\pi G_N)$. Thus $\ell_i$ and $\theta_i$ are the boundary conjugate data exchanged by the Legendre transform.

Above the black-hole threshold, an open-channel momentum $P_I$ is identified with a geodesic length,

$$
\ell_I=2\pi bP_I.
\tag{2.41}
$$

The normalized gravitational path integral is identified with the open Virasoro TQFT amplitude,

$$
Z_L[M,\gamma(\mathbf P)]
=\widehat Z_{\mathrm{oVir}}[M_E,\Gamma(\mathbf P)].
\tag{2.49}
$$

# Truncated hyperbolic tetrahedra

The classical building block is a generalized hyperbolic tetrahedron whose four vertices are truncated:

- triangular truncation faces lie on EOW branes and remain unglued;
- hexagonal faces carry OPE-channel data and may be glued pairwise;
- an internal edge is smooth when its incident dihedral angles sum to $2\pi$;
- unglued hexagons assemble the pleated boundary $P$.

Length and angle data are organized by Gram matrices

$$
\widetilde G_{ij}=-\cosh\ell_{ij},
\tag{3.5}
$$

$$
G_{ij}=-\cos\psi_{ij},
\tag{3.6}
$$

related by

$$
\widetilde G=MG^{-1}M,
\qquad
M_{ij}=\frac{\delta_{ij}}{\sqrt{G^{-1}_{ii}}}.
\tag{3.7}
$$

In the four-tetrahedron example, the internal-edge equation is

$$
\psi_{14}^{(1)}+\psi_{14}^{(2)}
+\psi_{14}^{(3)}+\psi_{14}^{(4)}=2\pi.
\tag{3.14}
$$

For unit external lengths the source reports $\ell_M=4.086$ and total volume $9.276$. These numerical values were not independently recomputed.

# Quantum face gluing

Open $6j$ manifolds are glued along OPE faces. Closed Wilson loops generated by the gluing are removed through annular surgery, producing the same combinatorics as the tetrahedral decomposition. The amplitude has the form

$$
\widehat Z_{\mathrm{oVir}}[M_E,\Gamma(\mathbf P)]
=\prod_I\left(
\int_0^\infty d\mathring P_I;
g_ag_b\rho_0(\mathring P_I)
\right)
\prod_{\widehat\Delta\in\widehat T}
\widehat W(\widehat\Delta).
\tag{3.20}
$$

The threshold dictionary is

$$
\begin{cases}
\ell_I=2\pi bP_I,&\text{above threshold},\\
\psi_I=2\pi ibP_I,&\text{below threshold}.
\end{cases}
\tag{3.22}
$$

Above threshold, the channel label is a real length; below threshold, imaginary momentum becomes a real kink angle. This is the source's geometric explanation for switching between fixed-length and fixed-angle ensembles.

# Open--closed transform

For the special manifolds built from boundary Wilson loops, the Conformal Turaev--Viro amplitude is obtained by an open--closed transform of two closed Virasoro TQFT copies:

$$
Z_{\mathrm{CTV}}[\widetilde M_E,\widetilde\Gamma(\mathbf P)]
=\prod_i\left(
\int_0^\infty dP_i'\,
\mathbb S_{P_iP_i'}[\mathbf1]
\right)
\left|
\widehat Z_{\mathrm{Vir}}
[\widetilde M_E,\widetilde\Gamma(\mathbf P')]
\right|^2.
\tag{4.1}
$$

The special open sector first obeys

$$
\widetilde Z_A[\widetilde M,\widetilde\gamma(\mathbf P)]
=\widehat Z_{\mathrm{oVir}}[M_E,\Gamma(\mathbf P)],
\tag{4.6}
$$

and the annulus kernel is

$$
\mathbb S_{P_1P_2}[\mathbf1]
=2\sqrt2\cos(2\pi P_1P_2).
\tag{4.9}
$$

After applying this transform to each loop, EOW surfaces are replaced by Neumann punctured spheres and a bulk Wilson graph. Semiclassically the transform exchanges fixed angle and fixed length, leading to

$$
\widetilde Z_L[\widetilde M,\widetilde\gamma(\mathbf P)]
=\left|
\widehat Z_{\mathrm{Vir}}
[\widetilde M_E,\widetilde\Gamma(\mathbf P)]
\right|^2.
\tag{4.13}
$$
