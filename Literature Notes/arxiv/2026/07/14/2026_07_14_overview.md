Detailed note: [[Global Gauge Symmetries and Spatial Asymptotic Boundary Conditions in Yang-Mills Theory (2502.16151v4)]].

**Technical relevance:** `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T1-charge`. The paper derives the numerator and denominator of

$$
\mathcal G_{\mathrm{phys}}
=\mathcal G^I/\mathcal G_0^\infty
$$

in the correct order. A fixed asymptotic Dirichlet sector determines the allowed group $\mathcal G^I$; the canonical symplectic form and Gauss momentum map produce the electric-flux boundary term; localizability identifies $\mathcal G_0^\infty$ as the constraint-generated subgroup. The detailed note reconstructs the compactification, function spaces, momentum-map calculation, quotient, and Higgs stabilizer.

The accepted v4 body is unchanged from v3 apart from publication/bibliography metadata. Independent checks confirm the falloff integral, trace identities, and the correct pullback components. The source itself reverses two radial pullback formulas, writes one bundle automorphism with the wrong type, and overstates the generic broken-Higgs stabilizer; the note records and repairs these points before using downstream conclusions.

**Author signal:** none used. The placement is entirely technical.

## Carrollian limit of NS-NS and Heterotic Supergravity — `2607.09847v1`

Detailed note: [[Carrollian limit of NS-NS and Heterotic Supergravity (2607.09847v1)]]. Codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`.

- **Source claim:** a dilaton rescaling compensates the ultra-relativistic NS--NS divergences and extends to a Carrollian heterotic action with Green--Schwarz data.
- **Mechanism:** decompose $(\hat g,\hat B,\hat\phi,\hat A)$ into $(\tau,h)$, spatial and longitudinal two-form components, and non-Abelian gauge/scalar data; then compare expanded relativistic equations with constrained variation of the limiting action.
- **Central formula:** the stated metric gives $\sqrt{-\hat g}=w^{-1}\Omega_c$, so the full measure scales as $w^{-2\alpha-1}$, not the source's $w^{-2\alpha+1}$. Mathematica therefore obtains $\alpha=1/2$, not $3/2$, for a $w^{-2}$ measure.
- **Use case:** the field decomposition and Green--Schwarz redefinitions are useful for Carrollian/null-boundary dictionaries.
- **Why not high:** the central finite-action order counting is inconsistent, and the paper contains no CPS or charge construction.

## Belinfante Symmetrization from Metric-Affine Conservation Laws — `2607.11322v1`

Detailed note: [[Belinfante Symmetrization from Metric-Affine Conservation Laws: Hypermomentum as the Improvement Term -- The Cases of QED and QCD (2607.11322v1)]]. Codes: `T1-charge`, `T2-model`.

- **Source claim:** the Belinfante improvement is the flat remnant of the metric-affine current identity relating canonical stress, metrical stress, and hypermomentum.
- **Mechanism:** affinely lift a flat theory, vary the independent connection to obtain $\Delta_\lambda{}^{\mu\nu}$, return to flat space, and use $T^{\mu\nu}=t^{\mu\nu}-\partial_\alpha\Delta^{\nu\mu\alpha}/2$.
- **Central criterion:** $\partial_\mu\partial_\nu\Delta_\lambda{}^{\mu\nu}\equiv0$ makes the shift an identically conserved improvement. Mathematica checks the scalar example and the CCJ coefficient $\lambda=(n-2)/(2(n-1))$.
- **Use case:** a clean current-improvement dictionary for the gravitational-energy/diffeomorphism-current notes.
- **Why not high:** it does not construct a boundary charge or CPS form, and the QCD chain has sign/reference gaps; a printed scalar product-rule identity is incorrect.

## $p$-Form Gauge Dynamics and Digital Quantum Simulation — `2607.10950v1`

Detailed note: [[$p$-Form Gauge Dynamics and Digital Quantum Simulation -- Flux and Cosmological Constant Neutralization (2607.10950v1)]]. Codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-charge`, `T2-model`.

- **Source claim:** on an oriented cell complex, the same boundary map determines higher-form Gauss checks, charged boundaries, and dressed Wilson surfaces.
- **Mechanism:** for $p=d=k=2$, solve $\mathcal G_\ell=1$ exactly so $\tau_\ell^x=\sigma_{p_L}^x\sigma_{p_R}^x$ and reduce the plaquette-plus-link encoding to a physical plaquette Hamiltonian.
- **Central construction:** $\mathcal W[R,\partial R]=\prod_{p\in R}\sigma_p^z\prod_{\ell\in\partial R}\tau_\ell^z$ makes the flux region and charged boundary one gauge-invariant operator. A three-qubit Mathematica check reproduces its local Gauss commutation and reduction identity.
- **Use case:** a discrete edge/bulk testbed in which boundary variables are either redundant hardware degrees of freedom or reconstructed domain walls.
- **Why not high:** the paper's main contribution is a lattice Hamiltonian and quantum-simulation benchmark, not a continuum CPS or asymptotic charge algebra.

## The many facets of a hyperbolic tetrahedron — `2604.09396v2`

Detailed note: [[The many facets of a hyperbolic tetrahedron: open and closed triangulations of 3d gravity (2604.09396v2)]]. Codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-model`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

- **Source claim:** open Virasoro TQFT computes compact 3d-gravity regions assembled from truncated hyperbolic tetrahedra, with above-threshold fixed lengths and below-threshold fixed angles.
- **Mechanism:** the Hayward corner term and its Legendre transform exchange the conjugate pair $(\ell_i,\theta_i)$; tetrahedral gluing imposes a $2\pi$ internal-edge angle; an open--closed $\mathbb S$ transform relates the construction to CTV/closed Virasoro TQFT.
- **Central formula:** $I_L=I_A+(c/12\pi)\sum_i\ell_i\theta_i$, with Mathematica confirming $c/(12\pi)=1/(8\pi G_N)$ for $c=3/(2G_N)$.
- **Use case:** finite-boundary variational ensembles and corner Legendre transforms in AdS$_3$ gravity.
- **Why not high:** the paper does not provide CPS charges or a charge algebra; the Virasoro $6j$/TQFT normalization and tetrahedron numerics remain source-derived.

## SymTFT for Continuous Symmetries — `2509.10343v2`

Detailed note: [[SymTFT for Continuous Symmetries: Non-linear Realizations and Spontaneous Breaking (2509.10343v2)]]. Codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`, `T3-math`.

- **Source claim:** continuous Goldstone and CCWZ theories arise by closing a BF-theory sandwich, while lateral boundaries and corners distinguish broken from unbroken phases.
- **Mechanism:** boundary equations turn bulk BF fields into $d\varphi+\mathcal A$; for $G/H$, the split $\mathcal V^{-1}(d+\mathcal A)\mathcal V=P+Q$ produces the CCWZ data; defect sweeping across a corner yields the Ward identity.
- **Central construction:** the unbroken phase uses a $\mathcal D_A$--$\mathcal D_B$ corner and forces charged one-point functions to vanish, whereas the broken $\mathcal D_A$--$\mathcal D_A$ corner maps one vacuum boundary condition to another.
- **Use case:** a topological boundary-condition language for symmetry action, vacuum selection, and edge/interface data.
- **Why not high:** it is an 86-page framework whose direct output is not the gravitational/gauge CPS charge formalism; fusion, higher-linking, higher-group, and appendix variations were not independently reproduced.
