## Einstein-aether elliptic charges and the AdS universal-horizon first law

- arXiv: [2606.27437v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27437v1)
- Categories: primary `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Einstein-aether Elliptic Charges and the First Law of Asymptotically AdS Black Holes (2606.27437v1)]]

Source claim: in the static, spherically symmetric, asymptotically AdS sector of Einstein-aether theory with $c_{14}=0$, the known extra term in the universal-horizon first law is tied to a reduced-action aether symmetry

$$\begin{align}
\delta_f u_a=f a_a,\qquad \nabla_a(fa^a)=0.
\end{align}$$

Main mechanism: after rewriting the aether Lagrangian as $\mathcal L_{\ae}=\mathcal L_0+\nabla_aA^a$, the transformation preserves the reduced action when $f$ obeys the elliptic constraint. The associated current is

$$\begin{align}
J_f^a=-2c_{123}\,\vartheta\,f a^a.
\end{align}$$

The charge vanishes on aether-orthogonal slices because $u\cdot a=0$, but the radial flux is nontrivial:

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal F_{\ae}=-8\pi c_{123}(u\cdot\chi)r^2f\vartheta(a\cdot s).
\end{align}$$

The spherical aether equation gives

$$\begin{align}
(s\cdot\chi)=\alpha r+\frac{\beta}{r^2},\qquad \alpha=\frac1{\ell_s},\qquad \vartheta=-3\alpha=-\frac3{\ell_s}.
\end{align}$$

Thus the aligned limit $\ell_s\to\infty$ is the zero-flux, $Q_f=\delta Q_f=0$ ensemble, and the extra first-law term switches off because it is proportional to $1/\ell_s$.

Transfer to current projects: this is a direct model for how a weakly vanishing constraint-type charge can still control boundary flux in a first-law problem. It is especially useful for finite-boundary, universal-horizon, and CPS notes where the physically relevant contribution lives at the boundary rather than in a bulk charge integral.

Verification status: Mathematica independently checked the radial mode equation, the expansion identity $\vartheta=-3\alpha$, the coupling reduction $c_\vartheta+\frac23c_\sigma=c_{123}$, and the algebraic flux variation. The Raychaudhuri rewrite, CPS sign conventions, boundary Hamiltonian, and full finite-$Q_f$ first law are source-derived or explicitly left incomplete by the paper.

## Observing Massive Scattering from Null Infinity

- arXiv: [2606.27421v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27421v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`
- Technical relevance: medium-high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Observing Massive Scattering from Null Infinity (2606.27421v1)]]

Source claim: massive external states, which naturally reach timelike rather than null infinity, can still leave late-time information at null infinity through soft graviton radiation. The late-time Bondi mass aspect acts as a detector operator for massive outgoing radiation.

Main technical mechanism: continuity between the boundaries of timelike and null infinity relates the late-time limit of the Bondi mass aspect at $\mathscr I^+_+$ to weighted sums of massive scattering cross sections. The same Bondi mass aspect is interpreted as a Carrollian stress-tensor component, with Ward identities constraining its two-point functions.

Likely use case: useful for null-infinity and Carrollian notes because it gives a concrete observable bridge from massive scattering to null-boundary data.

Reason it is not high-priority today: the paper is about detector operators and scattering data rather than a new charge algebra, CPS generator, or boundary symplectic construction.

## Quantum Fluctuations of the Black Hole Horizon

- arXiv: [2606.28243v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28243v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T1-boundary` nearby, `A-rising-star`
- Technical relevance: medium-high. Author-signal relevance: strong because Antony Speranza is on the profile watchlist.
- Detailed note: [[Quantum Fluctuations of the Black Hole Horizon (2606.28243v1)]]

Source claim: the event horizon has a gauge-invariant quantum uncertainty, called a quantum width, definable through a physical experiment measuring the last escape time of a signal emitted from an ingoing light ray.

Main technical mechanism: in perturbative quantum gravity for spherically symmetric black holes, the quantum width depends on probe resolution and can exceed the Planck scale. For a Schwarzschild horizon patch of transverse size $\sigma_\perp$, the abstract reports a scaling of order $\sqrt{l_P r_s^2/\sigma_\perp}$ in a particular parameter regime.

Likely use case: useful for horizon-local observables and boundary/corner interpretability of black-hole fluctuations.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a horizon-observable paper, not a CPS or charge-construction paper. The Speranza author signal justifies watchlist attention but does not override the technical tier.

## Soft QED as Open Quantum System

- arXiv: [2606.27498v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27498v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `cond-mat.stat-mech`, `hep-ph`, `quant-ph`
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry` nearby, `T3-math`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Soft QED as Open Quantum System Infrared Cancellation and Soft-Shell Coarse Graining (2606.27498v1)]]

Source claim: unresolved soft photons can be treated as an open-quantum-system environment, with resolved hard charged particles and photons forming the system. Inclusive probabilities are encoded by Kraus operators and effects.

Main technical mechanism: the Schwinger-Keldysh formulation organizes real and virtual soft contributions on a doubled contour. At leading soft order, tracing a soft shell produces diagonal jump operators fixed by eikonal amplitudes, giving a completely positive unital Schur channel and a scale-flow dephasing semigroup.

Likely use case: useful as a soft-sector/decoherence reference for soft charges, memory, and inclusive observables.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is not a gravitational charge/CPS construction, and its immediate transfer is conceptual rather than formula-level for the current notes.

## Classification of Killing Horizons in D=11 Supergravity

- arXiv: [2606.27843v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27843v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-model`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Classification of Killing Horizons in D=11 Supergravity (2606.27843v1)]]

Source claim: supersymmetric degenerate Killing horizons with closed spatial cross section in D=11 supergravity fall into two classes based on lightcone chirality in Gaussian null coordinates.

Main technical mechanism: if the negative lightcone chirality part of the Killing spinor is nonzero on the horizon, the solution is isometric to a supersymmetric near-horizon geometry. If it vanishes, the spinorial Lie derivative along the horizon-generating Killing vector vanishes, and solutions with more than 13 supersymmetries are pp-waves.

Likely use case: reference for horizon spinor classifications and supergravity near-horizon rigidity.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is classification geometry rather than a boundary charge or CPS paper.

Main technical mechanism: Hollands' exact result for chiral relative entropy is adapted to a diamond region. Likely use case: useful operator-algebraic comparison for AdS3 boundary data, relative entropy, and geometric variations.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is an RT/relative-entropy construction, not a charge, flux, or symplectic-generator result.
