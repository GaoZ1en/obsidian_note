# High-priority recommendations

## Holonomies and Boundary Symmetries in the Discrete BF Formulation of Carroll Dilaton Gravity

- arXiv: [2606.25499v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25499v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`, `math-ph`
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-charge`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Holonomies and Boundary Symmetries in the Discrete BF Formulation of Carroll Dilaton Gravity (2606.25499v1)]]

Source claim: the paper constructs a lattice/holonomy realization of two-dimensional Carroll dilaton gravity as a BF-type gauge theory. The bulk remains topological through flat plaquette holonomies, while the boundary phase space is carried by boundary holonomies and lattice currents. The least restrictive boundary conditions yield a discrete affine Carroll current algebra; imposing a conformal reduction leaves a single current whose improved quadratic composite obeys a Virasoro-type algebra.

Transferable mechanism: the useful object is a discrete BF boundary-current pipeline:

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal A=\tau {\tt H}+e{\tt P}+\omega {\tt B},\qquad \mathcal F=d\mathcal A+\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A,
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal U_\ell=\mathcal P\exp\!\int_\ell\mathcal A,\qquad \mathcal W_f=\prod_{\ell\in\partial f}\mathcal U_\ell,\qquad \mathcal S_{\rm disc}=\sum_f\langle \mathcal X_f,\log\mathcal W_f\rangle+\mathcal S_{\rm boundary}.
\end{align}$$

This is directly useful for comparing continuum null-boundary/Carroll charge language with a lattice holonomy model where residual gauge transformations at boundary links become physical symmetries.

Use case: read this next to the 2026-06-24 discrete warped Chern-Simons holonomy paper. Together they form a compact pair of examples where boundary phase-space labels are holonomies, monodromies, boundary currents, and reduced symmetry algebras rather than local smooth metric data.

Verification status: Sage verified the finite Carroll algebra used by the source, with bracket `[B,P]=H`, central `H`, no Jacobi failures, and a two-step nilpotent lower central series. The lattice affine/Virasoro brackets are source-derived from the TeX source; no independent distributional Poisson-bracket reproduction was completed.

# Medium-priority technical candidates

## Massive fields in 3D Minkowski space and boundary correlators

- arXiv: [2606.25803v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25803v1)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T1-symmetry`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: medium-high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Massive fields in 3D Minkowski space and boundary correlators (2606.25803v1)]]

Source claim: a two-dimensional Carrollian CFT at null infinity can encode massive three-dimensional Minkowski scattering amplitudes through a broader class of Carrollian correlators and a massive bulk-to-boundary propagator.

Main technical mechanism: the paper reviews BMS3/Carrollian primary data, identifies an electric-branch class of Carrollian two- and three-point functions with nonzero boost charge, constructs a massive scalar bulk-to-boundary propagator by taking one point of the massive Feynman propagator to null infinity, and checks that the resulting Carrollian primary wavefunction solves the massive Klein-Gordon equation.

Likely use case: useful for null-boundary/Carrollian holography notes when massive bulk fields must be matched to boundary operators at null infinity, especially as a contrast to the Maxwell/Carroll Hamiltonian and BF-boundary-current papers.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a correlator/S-matrix construction rather than a boundary charge, symplectic form, or edge-mode algebra paper.

## Diffeomorphic Scalar Duality

- arXiv: [2606.25154v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25154v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-model`, `T3-math`, weak `A-big-name`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: weak field-author signal through Claudia de Rham and Andrew J. Tolley.
- Detailed note: [[Diffeomorphic Scalar Duality (2606.25154v1)]]

Source claim: every local scalar EFT has infinitely many dual formulations related by a field-dependent diffeomorphism rather than an ordinary purely local field redefinition; when gravity is coupled, preserving the duality requires Einstein-Cartan variables with independent vielbein and spin connection.

Main technical mechanism: the scalar duality acts by a field/coordinate transformation constrained by integrability conditions and a nonvanishing Jacobian. Coupling to massless gravity makes the transformation local in first-order variables: the vielbein transforms while the spin connection is held fixed, so a torsion-free configuration is generally mapped to a nonzero-torsion configuration.

Likely use case: a reference for first-order gravity and torsion bookkeeping in EFT duality problems, especially when distinguishing local field redefinitions, diffeomorphism gauge choices, and genuine changes of first-order geometric variables.

Reason it is not high-priority today: the paper is broad and long, but its main payoff for this vault is a first-order-gravity convention warning rather than a CPS/charge/boundary construction.

## From Cosmological Cuts to Yang--Mills Wavefunctions in de Sitter Space

- arXiv: [2606.25878v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25878v1)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[From Cosmological Cuts to Yang--Mills Wavefunctions in de Sitter Space (2606.25878v1)]]

Source claim: Yang-Mills wavefunctions in four-dimensional de Sitter can be reconstructed from cosmological cuts, with low-point gluon data fixed by factorization, transverse projectors, current conservation, and the flat-space limit.

Main technical mechanism: the cut discontinuities factorize into lower-point wavefunctions glued by cut propagators; the reconstruction separates cut-detectable terms from cut-invisible contact completions.

Likely use case: a reference for de Sitter wavefunction/correlator reconstruction, not for the immediate AdS/CPS boundary-charge queue.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is amplitude/wavefunction technology rather than a boundary phase-space or charge-algebra construction.

- Technical relevance: medium-low. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Boundary Rigidity in a fixed conformal class for Asymptotically Hyperbolic Manifolds (2606.25940v1)]]

Source claim: for two conformal asymptotically hyperbolic metrics satisfying simplicity or negative-curvature assumptions, equality of marked renormalized boundary distances for chosen conformal representatives forces equality of the metrics.

Main technical mechanism: the transferable object is an inverse-boundary-data rigidity statement for conformally compact/asymptotically hyperbolic geometry.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a mathematical rigidity theorem and does not directly construct phase space, charges, or field-theory boundary conditions.
