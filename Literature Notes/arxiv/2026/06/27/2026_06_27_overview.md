## Conserved charges and $L_\infty$ algebras

- arXiv: [2606.26224v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26224v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T3-math`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none.
- Detailed note: [[Conserved charges and L-infinity algebras (2606.26224v1)]]

Source claim: a cyclic $L_\infty$ Lagrangian field theory carries a direct conserved-charge formula in terms of the equation state $q_\Phi$, the linearized operator $Q_\Phi$, the symmetry data $\xi_\Phi,\Xi_\Phi$, the cyclic pairing $\omega$, and a sigmoid cut operator $\sigma$.

Main mechanism:

$$\begin{align}
\Omega=\frac12\omega(\delta\Phi,[Q_\Phi,\sigma]\delta\Phi),
\end{align}$$

and an action symmetry satisfies

$$\begin{align}
Q_\Phi\xi_\Phi-\Xi_\Phi q_\Phi=0.
\end{align}$$

The Hamiltonian charge obeying $\iota_\xi\Omega+\delta F_\xi=0$ can be written as

$$\begin{align}
F_\xi=-\int_0^1ds\, \omega\left(\frac{d\Phi(s)}{ds}, [Q_{\Phi(s)},\sigma]\xi_{\Phi(s)}
-[\Xi_{\Phi(s)},\sigma]q_{\Phi(s)}\right),
\end{align}$$

or in total-derivative form as

$$\begin{align}
F_\xi=-\omega(\sigma\xi_\Phi,q_\Phi)\big|_\tau.
\end{align}$$

Transfer to current projects: $[Q_\Phi,\sigma]$ is an algebraic replacement for a cut-supported symplectic current. The paper is especially useful for finite-boundary/null-boundary charge work because it keeps the cut regulator explicit and recovers Yang--Mills surface charges and Brown--York charges as examples.

Verification status: Mathematica checked the scalar-field stress-tensor conservation identity in the detailed note inherited from the same official release. The $L_\infty$ coalgebra derivation, $p$-adic string example, Yang--Mills boundary charge, and GR Brown--York reduction are source-derived, not independently verified.

## Conservation law of super-Lorentz charges

- arXiv: [2606.26848v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26848v1)
- Categories: primary `gr-qc`; cross-listed `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`
- Technical relevance: high. Author-signal relevance: none from the exact profile watchlist.
- Detailed note: [[Conservation law of super-Lorentz charges (2606.26848v1)]]

Source claim: under assumptions compatible with generic gravitational scattering, super-Lorentz, leading tail, and leading peeling-breaking charges can be defined invariantly in both Bondi-Sachs and Beig-Schmidt languages, and the super-Lorentz charge obeys an antipodal conservation law between the future and past of spatial infinity.

Main mechanism: Bondi-Sachs data at $\mathcal I^\pm$ are matched to Beig-Schmidt data on the $dS_3$ boundary of spatial infinity. The raw second-order field $J_a$ is split as

$$\begin{align}
J_a=J_a^{(S)}+\widehat J_a,
\end{align}$$

where the source-determined primitive part is subtracted and the homogeneous part carries the charge. The spatial-infinity Wronskian charge $Q^V_\chi[\widehat J]$ matches the Bondi super-Lorentz aspect antipodally.

Transfer to current projects: this is a concrete example where a gravitational charge is only well defined after dressing, source subtraction, and corner matching. It is not a finite-boundary Wald generator, but it is a valuable asymptotic charge dictionary for null-boundary and BMS-like charge notes.

Verification status: source-derived only. No xAct/Mathematica verification of the Bondi-Sachs expansions, Beig-Schmidt equations, Wronskian conservation, or soft-theorem algebra was completed.

## From Holst to Carroll Gravity, a Hamiltonian point of view

- arXiv: [2606.26799v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26799v1)
- Categories: `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`, `T1-symplectic` nearby
- Technical relevance: medium-high. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: the Holst action has a Carroll-invariant magnetic-regime descendant whose Hamiltonian analysis can be organized in Cartan variables $(A,\Omega,e,\tau)$.

Main technical mechanism: the field equations split into torsion-free structure equations

$$\begin{align}
d_Ae=0,\qquad d\tau+\langle\Omega\wedge e\rangle=0,
\end{align}$$

and curvature dynamics

$$\begin{align}
F_A\wedge\tau+[e\wedge d_A\Omega]=0,\qquad \langle e\wedge F_A\rangle=0.
\end{align}$$

The Hamiltonian vector field separates diffeomorphism, rotation, Carroll boost, and a true evolution term

$$\begin{align}
Z_\Omega=-N(\star_eF_A).
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: a compact first-order Carroll gravity model for separating structure equations, gauge directions, and curvature-driven evolution in null-boundary/CPS notes.

Reason it is not high-priority today: the paper assumes a closed spatial slice and does not construct boundary charges, corner symplectic forms, or flux algebras.

## Universal Lichnerowicz Lifting of Near-Horizon Soft Modes

- arXiv: [2606.27308v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27308v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`
- Technical relevance: medium-high. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: near-extremal black-hole one-loop universality can be traced to the finite-temperature lifting of normalizable transverse-traceless tensor zero modes by the Lichnerowicz operator.

Main technical mechanism: scalar reparametrization modes generate TT tensor modes,

$$\begin{align}
\zeta_n^a=\epsilon^{ab}\bar\nabla_b\Phi_n,\qquad h_{ab}^{(n)}=\bar\nabla_a\zeta_b+\bar\nabla_b\zeta_a,
\end{align}$$

with $|n|\ge2$. Small temperature shifts the eigenvalue as

$$\begin{align}
\delta\lambda_n=\frac{\epsilon}{8G}\frac{|n|T}{r_i},
\end{align}$$

up to angular projection factors in rotating throats.

Likely use case: heat-kernel and one-loop determinant notes around near-horizon soft sectors, especially the dictionary from large diffeomorphism to TT zero mode to Schwarzian kernel.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is spectral/thermodynamic machinery rather than a charge, boundary-CPS, or symmetry-algebra construction.

## Large-$N$ Carrollian Thermodynamics from AdS Black-Hole Phase-Space Contractions

- arXiv: [2606.26163v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26163v1)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- Reason codes: `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T1-charge` nearby
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: none.

Source claim: the finite Carrollian AdS black-hole first law is a low-temperature large-$N$ double scaling limit where entropy and the number of boundary degrees of freedom diverge while thermodynamic products remain finite.

Main technical mechanism: the finite line is

$$\begin{align}
\alpha+\gamma=1,\qquad G_{d+1}=c^\gamma G_C,
\end{align}$$

with

$$\begin{align}
T_\lambda\sim c^{1-\alpha},\qquad S\sim c^{-\gamma},\qquad T_\lambda\delta S=O(1).
\end{align}$$

The Carrollian Brown--York energy is the global supertranslation zero mode

$$\begin{align}
Q_C[1]=E_C.
\end{align}$$

Likely use case: comparison point for stationary Carrollian boundary charges and AdS thermodynamics; useful to distinguish conserved zero-mode sectors from generic null-boundary flux-balance laws.

Reason it is not high-priority today: it is a stationary thermodynamic/Brown--York construction, not a new boundary charge algebra or covariant symplectic derivation.

## An observer's quantization of 3d de Sitter

- arXiv: [2606.26241v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26241v1)
- Categories: primary `hep-th`; cross-listed `gr-qc`
- Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`, weak `A-big-name`
- Technical relevance: medium. Author-signal relevance: weak broader field-author signal through Herman Verlinde.

Source claim: the paper defines and computes a de Sitter static-patch density of states in 3d using an $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ sum over Euclidean no-boundary Kerr-lens spacetimes sourced by an observer line defect, with a dual description in complex Liouville string and a further link to double-scaled SYK effective theory.

Main technical mechanism: the gravitational saddle family maps to generalized crosscap geometries in two copies of complex Liouville string. The crosscap amplitudes reproduce semiclassical gravity predictions, and the simplest Kerr-lens sector is related to two copies of the $G\Sigma$ effective theory on the observer worldline.

Likely use case: useful for de Sitter observer/holography background and static-patch density-of-states questions.

Reason it is not high-priority today: the technical chain is long and holography/statistical-mechanics oriented, with no direct CPS charge, boundary symplectic, or flux algebra construction for the current notes.
