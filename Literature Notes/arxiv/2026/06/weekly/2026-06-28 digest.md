# Top papers worth actually reading

## Conserved charges and $L_\infty$ algebras

- arXiv: [2606.26224v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26224v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-06-26.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T3-math`.
- Technical relevance: high. It gives a portable Hamiltonian charge formula using cyclic $L_\infty$ data, the cut operator $[Q_\Phi,\sigma]$, and examples including Yang-Mills surface charges and Brown-York charges.
- Author-signal relevance: none.
- Judgment: read first. This is the week's most transferable formalism paper for charge/CPS work when local variational-current methods are awkward.

## Conservation law of super-Lorentz charges

- arXiv: [2606.26848v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26848v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-06-26.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.
- Technical relevance: high. It gives an asymptotic gravitational charge dictionary using Bondi-Sachs and Beig-Schmidt data, source subtraction, and antipodal matching through spatial infinity.

- Judgment: read after `2606.26224`. It is less finite-boundary than the current AdS/CPS notes, but very useful for null-infinity charge bookkeeping.

## Holonomies and Boundary Symmetries in the Discrete BF Formulation of Carroll Dilaton Gravity
