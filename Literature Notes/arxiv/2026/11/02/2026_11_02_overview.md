### M1. All Symmetries and Boundaries of the Two-Dimensional Free Scalar from Continuous Flat-Gauging

[2610.40818v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40818v1) — **T1-boundary; T1-symmetry; T2-model; T3-math. Source-derived.** The question is how to classify unitary conformal boundaries and topological lines of the noncompact free boson. The authors use a proposed remote-detectability criterion, the SU(2)1 theory and continuous flat-gauging to argue for completeness, then give Lagrangian boundary/defect descriptions, interval Hilbert spaces and annulus open/closed checks. This is a useful exact laboratory for how boundary choices change a scalar Hilbert space and interface composition. Medium placement: completeness depends on the stated criterion, and the CFT defect classification is less immediately usable than today's explicit gravitational boundary equations; it does not itself establish the vault's canonical regional sewing construction.

### M2. Separation of variables for massive spin-2 fields on the Kerr black hole

[2610.40780v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40780v1) — **T2-spectral; T2-model. Source-derived.** The authors address the coupled massive-spin-2 Kerr equations using two commuting angular operators acting on the field, its curl and its linearized Weyl tensor. Their claimed joint eigenfunctions fix relative polarization profiles and leave one second-order radial equation; a transport identity and first integral remove an extra radial solution. This offers a concrete comparison for constrained mode reconstruction and for checking that scalar master reductions retain all physical polarizations. Medium placement: the immediate background is Kerr rather than the current AdS scalar/Dirac/Proca programme. Neither separation completeness nor the ancillary reconstruction operator was independently checked in this sketch.

### M3. Hilbert space in the flat limit of AdS/CFT. II. Massless particles

[2610.40878v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40878v1) — **T2-spectral; T2-celestial-carrollian; T3-math. Source-derived.** The problem is how fixed-dimension AdS lowest-weight representations yield massless flat-space scattering states. The source combines algebra contraction with an infinite boost into a Carrollian basis. It claims that generic representations retain a sum of lower-spin massless sectors, whereas shortening at the unitarity bound removes null lower-spin states, with a common inner-product rescaling. This is useful for distinguishing convergence of mode equations from convergence of the physical Hilbert norm. Medium placement: it concerns a flat-space limit of free representation data, not the current interacting AdS spectrum or finite-region canonical map; the contraction and norm theorem were not reproduced here.

### M4. Gravitational Memory from AdS States in the Flat-Space Limit

Medium placement: the main application is an ordered AdS4 flat-space memory limit, rather than the current AdS3 spectral recovery. The source's reconstruction and numerical memory checks remain unverified here.

### M5. Exponential stability of port-Hamiltonian systems with unstructured, measurable Hamiltonian densities

### M6. Homogeneous Spectral Freedom and Hadamard Consistency of Perturbative Green Functions

[2610.41777v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41777v1) — **T2-spectral. Source-derived.** The question is whether different perturbative particular solutions represent the same quantum state. The source uses the smooth homogeneous-bisolution difference criterion for Hadamard two-point functions of a fixed normally hyperbolic operator, then recomputes two impulsive-wave examples from their metrics and compares mode matching with the geometric parametrix. Its claimed removal of spurious momentum poles suggests a useful diagnostic for reorganized AdS response sums: first hold the operator and admissible homogeneous data fixed. Medium placement: the worked backgrounds are plane waves, with impulsive and zero-mode qualifications; their metric corrections, stress tensors and mode sums have not been checked here.

### M7. Teleparallel Renormalization of Gravitational Action

[2609.31444v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31444v2) — **T1-boundary; T2-model. They report agreement of two tetrad frames for the Euclidean action but a difference on a Lorentzian Wheeler–DeWitt patch, and claim uniqueness after imposing appropriate torsion falloff. This is useful when comparing first-order action representatives and boundary contributions before constructing charges. Medium placement: it is a newly cross-listed, untracked v2 rather than a tracked-version audit, and the immediate calculation is action regularization rather than the current CPS/AdS spectral chain. The uniqueness claim and Lorentzian frame comparison remain source claims.

### M8. Giant Gravitons in AdS5 × SE5 and the Superconformal Index of Toric Quiver Gauge Theories

Medium placement: the brane/worldvolume setup is less immediate than the active AdS field spectra, and index agreement is not a reconstruction of the full interacting Hilbert space. No fluctuation or counting derivation was independently verified.
