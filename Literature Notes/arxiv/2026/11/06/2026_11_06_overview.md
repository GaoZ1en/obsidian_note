### M1 — [Chern-Simons Interfaces and Minimal Models from Quantum Groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07012v1)

Primary `hep-th`; all categories `hep-th, cond-mat.str-el, math-ph`. `T1-boundary; T2-model; T3-math`. **Source-derived; official listing abstract; no independent derivation/CAS verification.**

The source asks how non-chiral interfaces coupled to SU(N) Chern–Simons theory can be represented microscopically. It proposes a continuous topological gauge field coupled to a chain of fundamental complex fermions, producing a q-deformed chain whose operator algebra is controlled by quantum groups. The claimed infrared sectors are a compact boson and a W_N minimal model; a baryon-number/translation Lieb–Schultz–Mattis constraint obstructs a unique symmetric gapped state. At unit filling a Mott term removes the compact-boson sector and leads to familiar anyon chains. Useful for interface Hilbert spaces, braiding and anomaly bookkeeping; medium because this does not yet provide a regional gravitational CPS or a sewing map for the current continuum fields.
