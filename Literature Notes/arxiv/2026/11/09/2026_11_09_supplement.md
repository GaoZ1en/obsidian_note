## Null Strings Gauged, Reloaded and Quantized, II: Null Strings on Flat Backgorund Have Discrete Spectrum

[2611.09420v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09420v1), I. Rasulian, M. M. Sheikh-Jabbari and H. Yavartanoo; hep-th; 20 pages. This continues Schrödinger-representation light-cone quantization of null strings with Carrollian worldsheets and diffeomorphism/Weyl/Carroll–Weyl gauge symmetries. The source claims no critical dimension and discrete nonnegative mass-squared levels specifically in the regular separated Schrödinger sector. Useful as an example of how regularity and representation/domain selection can change a constrained system's apparent spectrum. Medium because it is a string-worldsheet quantization problem, not the current AdS field phase space; the restriction to that sector must not be promoted to a complete spectrum/uniqueness theorem for every null-string quantization.
