### M2 — [Schwinger Effect with a Transversal Boundary](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05530v1)

T1-boundary; T2-spectral; T2-model.

**Source-derived — saved official listing abstract only.** The question is pair production when an electric pulse has a component normal to a boundary, so produced modes reflect. For an instantaneous spatially uniform pulse normal to a plane, the source gives an explicit solution and separates reflected-mode interference in the bulk from a purely boundary contribution; it also states an arbitrary-boundary expression for irrotational pulses. The likely use is a scattering example where boundary conditions change the bulk particle interpretation. Medium placement: useful comparison, but no direct regional symplectic or AdS spectral construction is identified. The production formula is not independently verified.

### M3 — [Static and Dynamical Casimir Effects in a Wedge Within the Worldline Formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05768v1)

T1-boundary; T2-spectral.

**Source-derived — saved official listing abstract only.** For a real massless scalar in a wedge with a semitransparent moving circular arc, the source expands the worldline effective action in arc displacement, retaining arbitrary delta-potential strength. Static energy uses Dirichlet/Neumann/mixed walls and dimensional regularization; quadratic displacement gives pair creation through angular-sector radial Green functions. It identifies the worldline density with a stress-tensor density at curvature coupling xi=1/4, and extends via TE/TM scalars to electromagnetism. This is useful for boundary spectral/regulator bookkeeping. Medium placement: a specific wedge detector/mirror calculation, not today's CPS reduction. The density identity and loop calculation remain source claims.

### M4 — [Partially Massless Condensates and Reentrant NUT/Bolt Phase Transitions in Conformal Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05787v1)
