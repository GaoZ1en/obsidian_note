### M1 — [Weyl Rescalings: A First Order Approach](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04978v1)

T2-model; T1-symmetry.

**Source-derived — official listing abstract only.** The question is how a Weyl rescaling in first-order gravity, with an independent Lorentz connection taken to be Weyl inert, reconciles with the inhomogeneous curvature transformation of the torsion-free metric description. The source resolves this by tracking torsion and discusses conformal matter coupling. The useful lesson is to distinguish the independent-connection field space from the torsion-free subspace before comparing transformations or boundary variations. Medium placement: a useful convention check for Palatini/Cartan calculations, but the abstract does not supply a boundary charge or symplectic construction for the current projects. No claim that commutation with Lorentz symmetry uniquely fixes every possible connection transformation is independently established here.

### M2 — [Hamiltonian of Open String Field Theory for Time-Dependent Solutions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03941v1)

T1-charge; T3-math.

**Source-derived — official listing abstract only.** This paper applies recently constructed conserved charges for L-infinity theories to Witten’s bosonic open string field theory, producing an explicit Hamiltonian for time-dependent solutions. It evaluates the expression on Erler–Maccaferri solutions and on the rolling tachyon, claiming agreement of the latter energy with Sen’s boundary-state result. Its likely use is a concrete example of deriving a time-translation charge in a nonlocal/homotopy-algebraic theory, rather than inferring energy from a static action. Medium placement: the charge construction is relevant, but the OSFT products and analytic solutions are not a current regional-CPS testbed. The stated agreement and charge derivation were not independently checked.

### M3 — [Inner Product of Closed Universe from Gravitational Path-Integral](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03789v1)

T2-model; T3-math; T1-boundary.

**Source-derived — official listing abstract only.** The question is the physical inner product for fixed-topology closed universes in AdS and dS JT gravity. The source constructs it from a gravitational path integral with explicit choices of lapse contour, measure, ordering, global gauge group and endpoint gauge fixing, and relates invariant/co-invariant states in refined algebraic quantization to a BFV–BRST description. It reports exact inner products and resolutions of identity for fixed topology and contour, while the global Hilbert space depends on a big-bang/crunch prescription and differs from BF theory. This can inform sewing measures and constraint implementation. Medium placement: these prescription-dependent closed-universe results do not directly construct the current finite-region boundary phase space; exactness of the path-integral formulas was not reproduced.

### M4 — [Gauge-Invariant Thermodynamic Description of Rotating Black Strings in 5D Minimal Supergravity via Kaluza-Klein Reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04373v1)

T1-charge; T1-boundary; T2-model.

Medium placement: useful comparison material, but a specific KK black-string ensemble is less immediate than today’s general cut/factorization questions. ### M5 — [Crossover from Schwarzian Dynamics to Rotational Soft Modes in Near-Extremal Lovelock Black Holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04726v1)

T2-spectral; T2-dS-BH-holography.

**Source-derived — official listing abstract only.** The question is whether cubic Lovelock interactions change the low-temperature soft-mode count of near-extremal seven-dimensional black holes. At fixed electric charge the source freezes the Maxwell mode and treats the fifteen S5 rotational modes as a quantum rotor: the claimed coefficient of log T crosses from 3/2 to 9 around the inverse moment-of-inertia scale, instead of reaching the naive Gaussian 19/2. A charged slow-rotation solution sets that scale; a probe charged scalar supplies a near-horizon BF/Schwinger threshold. This is useful for distinguishing ensemble constraints from Gaussian mode counting in partition functions. Medium placement: the seven-dimensional near-extremal spectrum is not the current AdS3 normal-mode problem, and the rotor crossover/threshold calculations remain source claims.

Medium placement: that mechanism could be useful, but a precise map to the vault’s interacting AdS Hamiltonian has not been identified. ### M7 — [An Exact QFT/Lattice Map for Fermions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05329v1)

T1-boundary; T2-spectral; T2-model.

**Source-derived — official listing abstract only.** The source constructs an exact map from continuum 1+1-dimensional fermion QFT to a finite lattice with nearest-neighbor couplings and an infinite-dimensional local Hilbert space. It claims exact energy spectrum, global operator algebra, chiral symmetries and anomalies before local truncation; truncation introduces no extra massless modes, and interval entropy converges as it is removed. This is relevant to finite regulators that preserve spectra while making local factorization explicit. Medium placement: a promising structural comparison, but no AdS boundary-domain implementation is given by the abstract; exact untruncated equivalence must not be confused with finite local-dimension truncation.
