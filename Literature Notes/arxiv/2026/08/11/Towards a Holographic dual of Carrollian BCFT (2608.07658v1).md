## Why this is high priority

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

The paper places a genuine boundary inside the bulk of three-dimensional flat spacetime and asks which null-infinity symmetries remain. Its concrete outputs are an EOW-brane profile, a boundary-compatible subalgebra of BMS$_3$/CCA$_2$, a flat-limit map from AdS$_3$/BCFT$_2$, and a representation-theoretic contraction. It does not yet construct charges, correlators, or a complete holographic dictionary, so the result should be read as a kinematic/symmetry foundation rather than a finished duality.

## Source map

1. **Introduction.** Motivates Carrollian BCFT and the search for a flat-space version of AdS$_3$/BCFT$_2$.
2. **BCCA: a brief recap.** Reviews CCA$_2$, introduces spatial boundaries at $\phi=0,\pi$, and selects the boundary-compatible combinations $\mathcal O_n$ and $\mathcal P_n$.
3. **EOW brane in flat space.** Identifies the plane $x^2=r\sin\phi=-a$, derives its tensionlessness intrinsically, and obtains it from the $\ell\to\infty$ limit of an AdS$_3$ EOW brane with $T\sim a/\ell^2$.
4. **Global symmetries.** Shows $\mathfrak{iso}(2,1)\to\mathfrak{iso}(1,1)$, matches the global BCCA generators, contracts the AdS$_3$ Casimir, and discusses induced/highest-weight representations.
5. **Asymptotic symmetries.** Restricts BMS$_3$ vector fields by the EOW brane and recovers the full spatial-boundary BCCA; then repeats the construction by contracting Brown--Henneaux generators.
6. **Other versions.** A temporal boundary $u=0$ corresponds to the null brane $t=r$ and leaves one Virasoro algebra.
7. **Conclusion and future directions.** Emphasizes that representations, correlators, boundary entropy, and the holographic dictionary remain to be built.

There are no appendices. Several long `comment` environments in the TeX source contain discarded draft calculations and author annotations; they are not part of the compiled paper and are excluded here.

## Boundary Carrollian conformal algebra

On the null cylinder, CCA$_2$ is generated by

$$
L_n=i e^{in\phi}(\partial_\phi+inu\,\partial_u),
\qquad
M_n=i e^{in\phi}\partial_u.
$$

The spatial boundaries are placed at $\phi=0,\pi$. The surviving combinations are

$$
\mathcal O_n
=-2\sin(n\phi)\partial_\phi-2nu\cos(n\phi)\partial_u,
\qquad
\mathcal P_n=2i\cos(n\phi)\partial_u.
$$

They obey

$$
\begin{aligned}
[\mathcal O_m,\mathcal O_n]
&=(m-n)\mathcal O_{m+n}-(m+n)\mathcal O_{m-n},\\
[\mathcal O_m,\mathcal P_n]
&=(m-n)\mathcal P_{m+n}+(m+n)\mathcal P_{m-n}
+\text{central term},\\
[\mathcal P_m,\mathcal P_n]&=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

The subtle step is the rejection of $\mathcal R_n=M_n-M_{-n}$. At strict leading order on $\mathscr I^+$, every $M_n$ is tangent to $\phi=0,\pi$. The paper retains the $O(r^{-1})\partial_\phi$ term and imposes the stronger condition that the brane not be displaced even subleadingly. Then

$$
\mathcal R_n\big|_{\phi=0,\pi}
=\frac{2n}{r}\cos(n\phi)\partial_\phi+\cdots
$$

is excluded, whereas the corresponding term in $\mathcal P_n$ vanishes at the boundary. The resulting algebra therefore depends on the boundary-preservation criterion beyond leading asymptotic order.

## The flat EOW brane

### Intrinsic construction

In Cartesian coordinates,

$$
ds^2=-(dx^0)^2+(dx^1)^2+(dx^2)^2,
\qquad
x^2=r\sin\phi.
$$

The simplest brane intersecting $\mathscr I^+$ at $\phi=0,\pi$ is

$$
Q:\quad x^2=0,
$$

or more generally $x^2=-a$ for finite $a$. The action is

$$
I=\frac1{16\pi G}\int_V\!\sqrt{-g}\,R
+\frac1{8\pi G}\int_Q\!\sqrt{-h}\,(K-T).
$$

The Neumann condition gives

$$
K_{ab}=(K-T)h_{ab}.
$$

Tracing in two brane dimensions yields $K=2T$. A constant Cartesian plane has constant normal and $K_{ab}=0$, hence

$$
T=0.
$$

The plane is therefore a tensionless timelike EOW brane. The shifted plane $x^2=-a$ has the same extrinsic geometry.

### Flat limit from AdS$_3$

For global AdS$_3$,

$$
ds^2=-\left(1+\frac{r^2}{\ell^2}\right)dt^2
+\left(1+\frac{r^2}{\ell^2}\right)^{-1}dr^2+r^2d\phi^2,
$$

the strip-BCFT brane is

$$
r\sin\phi=-\frac{T\ell^2}{\sqrt{1-T^2\ell^2}}.
$$

A finite nonzero $T$ does not enter the Minkowski diamond $r\ll\ell$. The controlled scaling

$$
T=\frac{a}{\ell^2}
$$

instead gives

$$
r\sin\phi\xrightarrow[\ell\to\infty]{}-a.
$$

Thus the flat brane is the limit of a family whose tension vanishes as $\ell^{-2}$, rather than the naive flat limit at fixed tension.

## Global symmetry breaking

The plane removes transformations with a normal $\partial_2$ component. From the six generators of $\mathfrak{iso}(2,1)$, the survivors are

$$
P_0=i\partial_0,
\qquad P_1=i\partial_1,
\qquad J_{01}=i(x_0\partial_1-x_1\partial_0),
$$

which form $\mathfrak{iso}(1,1)$. In the BCCA basis,

$$
\mathcal O_1=2iJ_{01},
\qquad
\mathcal P_1=-2P_1,
\qquad
\mathcal P_0=2P_0,
$$

and

$$
[\mathcal O_1,\mathcal P_0]=2\mathcal P_1,
\qquad
[\mathcal O_1,\mathcal P_1]=2\mathcal P_0.
$$

The quadratic Casimir is

$$
\mathcal M_{\mathcal B}^2=\mathcal P_0^2-\mathcal P_1^2.
$$

On the AdS side, the tensionless brane preserves one $\mathfrak{so}(2,1)$ generated by $\mathbb L_{0,\pm1}$. The contraction is

$$
\mathcal O_1=\mathbb L_1-\mathbb L_{-1},
\qquad
\mathcal P_1=\frac1\ell(\mathbb L_1+\mathbb L_{-1}),
\qquad
\mathcal P_0=\frac2\ell\mathbb L_0.
$$

Inverting these relations gives

$$
\mathbb L_0=\frac\ell2\mathcal P_0,
\qquad
\mathbb L_{\pm1}=\frac12(\ell\mathcal P_1\pm\mathcal O_1).
$$

Therefore the corrected Casimir identity is

$$
\mathbb C
=\mathbb L_0^2-\frac12(\mathbb L_1\mathbb L_{-1}+\mathbb L_{-1}\mathbb L_1)
=\frac{\ell^2}{4}(\mathcal P_0^2-\mathcal P_1^2)
+\frac14\mathcal O_1^2.
$$

Consequently $4\mathbb C/\ell^2\to\mathcal M_{\mathcal B}^2$.

## Representation contraction

Starting from a rest-frame induced representation of $\mathfrak{iso}(2,1)$, the boundary-incompatible generators are imposed to annihilate the state. This removes spin and leaves

$$
\mathcal P_0|\mathcal M\rangle=\mathcal M|\mathcal M\rangle,
\qquad
\mathcal P_1|\mathcal M\rangle=0,
\qquad
|\ell\rangle=(\mathcal O_1)^\ell|\mathcal M\rangle.
$$

A finite boost produces

$$
|\mathcal M,\eta\rangle=e^{\eta\mathcal O_1/2}|\mathcal M\rangle,
$$

with energy-momentum eigenvalues $\mathcal M(\cosh\eta,\sinh\eta)$. The same module is obtained from an $\mathfrak{so}(2,1)$ highest-weight state if

$$
\mathcal M=\lim_{\ell\to\infty}\frac{2\Delta}{\ell}.
$$

For a massive AdS$_3$ scalar, $\Delta=1+\sqrt{1+M^2\ell^2}$ realizes this scaling. This part establishes a plausible representation limit, not yet a state/operator dictionary for BCCFT.

## Asymptotic symmetry and central charge

The BMS$_3$ asymptotic vector field is organized by periodic functions $X(\phi)$ and $Y(\phi)$. Imposing the brane tangency condition, including the chosen $O(r^{-1})$ criterion, retains precisely $\mathcal O_n$ and $\mathcal P_n$. The same result follows from the boundary-preserving Brown--Henneaux combination

$$
\mathbb L_n=\mathcal L_n^++\mathcal L_n^-
$$

through

$$
\mathcal O_n=\mathbb L_n-\mathbb L_{-n},
\qquad
\mathcal P_n=\frac1\ell(\mathbb L_n+\mathbb L_{-n}).
$$

With $c=3\ell/(2G)$ for the single BCFT Virasoro algebra,

$$
c_M=\lim_{\ell\to\infty}\frac{2c}{\ell}=\frac3G.
$$

The paper obtains this central term algebraically from contraction. It does not compute covariant phase-space surface charges, prove integrability, or analyze EOW-brane corner terms.

## Alternative temporal boundary

For a Carrollian boundary at $u=0$, every $M_n$ is normal to the boundary and is discarded, while $L_n$ remains tangent. The surviving algebra is one Virasoro copy. In the bulk, the proposed EOW hypersurface is the light cone

$$
t=r.
$$

This is a null brane, unlike the timelike plane used in the main construction. The paper checks only boundary compatibility of asymptotic vector fields; it does not supply a null-boundary action or variational principle for this second setup.

## Derivation map

$$
\begin{gathered}
\text{spatial boundary }\phi=0,\pi
\Longrightarrow \{\mathcal O_n,\mathcal P_n\}
\Longrightarrow \mathrm{BCCA}_2,\\
x^2=-a
\Longrightarrow K_{ab}=0,\ T=0
\Longrightarrow \mathfrak{iso}(2,1)\to\mathfrak{iso}(1,1),\\
T_{\rm AdS}=a/\ell^2
\Longrightarrow Q_{\rm AdS}\to Q_{\rm flat}
\Longrightarrow \mathfrak{so}(2,1)\to\mathfrak{iso}(1,1),\\
\text{Brown--Henneaux}+Q
\Longrightarrow \mathbb L_n
\xrightarrow{\ell\to\infty}
(\mathcal O_n,\mathcal P_n).
\end{gathered}
$$

The four arrows are mutually consistent at the level of geometry, vector fields, Lie algebras, and representation scaling. The missing layer is a dynamical holographic dictionary.

## Translation into the vault's boundary/CPS language

1. The planar EOW brane is a finite timelike boundary meeting null infinity at two codimension-two endpoints. A complete CPS treatment should include the GHY presymplectic potential, allowed boundary variations, and possible corner contributions at $Q\cap\mathscr I^+$.
2. “Boundary compatible” is used here as tangency of vector fields, strengthened at subleading order. This is necessary but not sufficient for a Hamiltonian asymptotic symmetry: one must also preserve the full falloffs, obtain finite integrable charges, and quotient trivial transformations.
3. The central charge $c_M=3/G$ is inherited by contraction. A direct check would evaluate the Barnich--Brandt/Iyer--Wald bracket on the restricted phase space and determine whether the EOW brane modifies the central term.
4. The $T\sim\ell^{-2}$ scaling is a useful finite-cutoff lesson: the brane survives inside the flat diamond only after correlating the source/tension with the regulator/curvature scale.

## Verification log

### Checked

- **PDF/source structure.** Rendered pages 1, 7, 12, 14, 24, and 27. The BCCA equations, EOW construction, global generators, Casimir line, conclusion, figures, and equation labels agree with the extracted source.
- **Classical BCCA brackets.** A fresh Mathematica kernel evaluated the Lie brackets of the two-component vector fields for the nontrivial sample $(m,n)=(3,2)$. Residuals for $[\mathcal O_m,\mathcal O_n]$, $[\mathcal O_m,\mathcal P_n]$, and $[\mathcal P_m,\mathcal P_n]$ all vanished. This checks the centerless vector-field algebra; it does not check the quantum central extension.
- **EOW flat limit.** Mathematica independently evaluated
  $$
  -\frac{(a/\ell^2)\ell^2}{\sqrt{1-(a/\ell^2)^2\ell^2}}
  =-a-\frac{a^3}{2\ell^2}+O(\ell^{-4}),
  $$
  confirming the shifted flat plane.
- **Casimir contraction.** Direct substitution of the inverted generators gave
  $$
  \mathbb C=\frac14\mathcal O_1^2+\frac{\ell^2}{4}\mathcal P_0^2-\frac{\ell^2}{4}\mathcal P_1^2.
  $$
  This confirms the final limit in Eq. (4.19b) and identifies a typo in the preceding printed expression.

### Source-derived

- The Neumann boundary equation and the Brown--Henneaux asymptotic generators were followed from the paper and standard source conventions but were not independently rederived from the full variational problem.
- The exclusion of $\mathcal R_n$ is conditional on the paper's strong subleading tangency requirement. No independent phase-space analysis establishes that this is the unique admissible boundary condition.
- The representation reduction assumes boundary-incompatible generators annihilate physical states; this is a proposal, not a consequence proved from a quantized BCCFT.

### Blocked

- Direct CPS verification of the asymptotic charges and central extension is blocked because the paper does not specify a complete set of metric falloffs at the EOW brane, the associated boundary/corner presymplectic structure, or charge integrability conditions.
- The AFS$_3$/BCCFT$_2$ duality itself cannot be checked: boundary correlators, Witten-diagram limits, boundary states, entropy, and a bulk reconstruction map are explicitly future work.
- The null-brane $t=r$ proposal lacks a null-boundary action and variational principle, so only its vector-field tangency is presently testable.

### Failed

- **Eq. (4.19a), intermediate printed equality.** The PDF and TeX source print $\ell^2\mathcal P_1-\mathcal O_1^2$ where algebraic inversion requires $\ell^2\mathcal P_1^2-\mathcal O_1^2$. The missing square is a source typo. The final equality and Eq. (4.19b) use $\mathcal P_1^2$ and remain correct after this repair.
- **Section 4.4 prose.** One sentence says the flat-space limit is $\ell\to0$. All surrounding equations, the mass-dimension scaling, and the rest of the paper require $\ell\to\infty$; this is also a source typo and is not used downstream.

## Bottom line

Correct under precise conditions: the plane $x^2=-a$ with $T\sim a/\ell^2$ gives a consistent flat limit, and the stated boundary-tangent vector fields realize BCCA. What is not yet proven is that these transformations possess finite integrable gravitational charges on an EOW-brane phase space or that the proposed pair of theories forms a holographic duality.
