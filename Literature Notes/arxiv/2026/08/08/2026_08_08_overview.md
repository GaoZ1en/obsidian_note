### 1. Boundary structure of gauge fields on asymptotically AdS spaces — 2512.06576v2

**Codes:** `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`, `T3-math`

**Technical relevance:** high. **Author signal:** none used. **Listing status:** replacement-only, newly entering local tracking rather than an update of an existing note.

Detailed note: [[Boundary structure of gauge fields on asymptotically AdS spaces (2512.06576v2)]]

The paper treats the boundary-defining function \(\Omega\) as a field of a gauge PDE and defines the boundary simultaneously in spacetime and field space by

\[
\Omega=0,
\qquad
Q\Omega=0,
\qquad
\nabla_a\Omega\neq0.
\]

Normal target-space derivatives then produce a finite recursive boundary calculus. The gravity resonance at normal order \(D-3\) separates the conformal source from the obstruction/response sector; the scalar resonance reproduces the two AdS weights; and Yang--Mills yields a leading boundary connection plus a subleading current-like one-form obeying a generalized conservation equation. This is a strong organizing language for alternative quantization, boundary data, BRST, and the source/response split.

The 62-page detailed note reconstructs the gauge-PDE definitions, almost-Einstein system, curvature decomposition, recursive gravity equations, odd/even boundary split, scalar and Yang--Mills probes, and all six appendices. Mathematica reproduced the binomial/recursion identities and scalar rescaling coefficients; xAct checked the Weyl symmetries and an Abelian divergence identity; Sage checked a finite SU(2) Chevalley--Eilenberg nilpotency model. The full \(d=8\) non-Abelian conformal Yang--Mills coefficient chain remains blocked by its size. The PDF also loses a \(\Box\) glyph in a displayed equation and Appendix B cites a nonexistent “Theorem 4.4”; both are recorded under `Failed`.

The claim boundary matters: this is an equations-of-motion/BRST construction. It does not derive a renormalized presymplectic current, boundary flux, Hamiltonians, or a charge algebra, so its “stress tensor” and “current” data are not yet canonically normalized holographic one-point functions.

### 2. Holographic entanglement entropy with conformal boundary conditions — 2608.06277v1

**Codes:** `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`

**Technical relevance:** high. **Author signal:** none used.

Detailed note: [[Holographic entanglement entropy with conformal boundary conditions (2608.06277v1)]]

For AdS\(_3\) conformal boundary conditions, the fixed data are

\[
\delta(h^{-1/2}h_{ab})=0,
\qquad
\delta K=0,
\]

while the boundary Weyl mode remains dynamical. The clean classical result is that this changes the finite-wall variational polarization, wall position, conformal thermal variables, and interval anchoring, but not the local replica-tip functional:

\[
S_{\rm full}=\frac{A_H}{4G_N},
\qquad
S_A=\frac{A_{\min}}{4G_N}.
\]

The 47-page note covers the CBC action, sphere and torus saddles, full-boundary replicas, Dong's finite-wall argument, global AdS and BTZ geodesics, rotating intervals, the timelike-Liouville/\(T\bar T\)-dressed no-insertion state, and all appendices. Mathematica and xAct independently reproduced the radial Einstein equations, the conical/thermodynamic reductions, the CHM pullback, Burgers solution, saddle series, and the on-shell collapse.

The verification log is unusually important. Eq. (4.6) is missing \(1/n^2\); \(\Delta_K\) is undefined and should be \(\Delta=\sqrt{k^2-4}\); the stated constant-wall scaling \(K\ell-2\propto r_c^{-1}\) is false, with the global-AdS expansion beginning at \(r_c^{-4}\); and several rotating/appendix formulas use the dimensionally inconsistent \(K-\Delta\) where \(K\ell-\Delta\) is required. The “large-\(\widetilde\beta\)” label is the opposite of the derived high-temperature/large-interval regime, the rotating physical branch needs \(2|\widetilde\Omega|<k-\Delta\), and Appendix C's frame-invariance argument omits regulator-boundary terms. Section 4.2 is therefore an area/geodesic cross-check, not an independent global replica-action proof.

The boundary-theory saddle describes a no-insertion state distinct from the state dual to global AdS. Neither the RT result nor the effective \(c_{\rm eff}\) establishes equivalence of the CBC renormalized symplectic structure or a Brown--Henneaux charge algebra.

**Codes:** `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`

**Technical relevance:** medium. **Author signal:** none.

Detailed note: [[Emergent gravitational action from non-local $T\bar T$-like deformations (2608.05913v1)]]

**Source claim and mechanism.** At first order in the bilocal deformation,

\[
W^{(1)}[g]=\lambda\int d\mu(x)d\mu(y)\,
H^{\mu\nu,\rho\sigma}(x,y)
\langle T_{\mu\nu}(x)T_{\rho\sigma}(y)\rangle_0.
\]

One heat kernel supplies the seed-theory contact/short-distance \(TT\) data, while a second represents the prescribed inverse kernel \(F^{-1}\). Their coincident coefficients produce local curvature terms, schematically

\[
\text{seed UV coefficient}\times
\text{coincidence coefficient of }F^{-1}
\longrightarrow \text{local invariant}.
\]

Free Dirac, Proca, and quadratic Yang--Mills examples are model dependent; the leading CFT identity OPE and trace-anomaly contact sector are controlled by \(C_T\) and the type-A anomaly coefficient. **Use:** a practical route from nonlocal deformations to log-controlled curvature actions. **Why not high:** the metric is a background source, the inverse kernel is deformation data, and the calculation does not produce a complete dynamical gravity theory or CPS structure. The detailed note records a missing overall \(\lambda\) in two generic printed formulas and keeps the source-derived determinant chain separate from the independently checked dimensions and special cases.

### 2. Partition Functions of Hermitian and PT-Symmetric Oscillators from Integrable Models — 2608.06047v1

**Codes:** `T2-spectral`, `T3-math`

**Technical relevance:** medium. **Author signal:** none.

Detailed note: [[Partition Functions of Hermitian and PT-Symmetric Oscillators from Integrable Models (2608.06047v1)]]

**Source claim and mechanism.** A radial ODE boundary condition is encoded by a zero of an ODE/IM \(Q\)- or \(T\)-function, converted to \(1+a(\pm E)=0\), and solved through a Destri--de Vega equation. The exact spectral density is the lateral discontinuity of

\[
\partial_E\log(1+a(E)),
\]

so contour integrals give \(Z(\beta)\) and \(\zeta_H(s)\). For the Hermitian full line, \(l=-1\) and \(l=0\) assemble the even/odd sectors. The PT problem is a \(T\)-zero at negative ODE energy and requires the second determination \(a(\theta^{\rm PT}+\mu\pi i)\). Large-energy integrals of motion determine high-temperature coefficients and zeta residues or special values; substituting \(M=3\) reproduces \(\zeta_H(-2)=5/16\). **Use:** a spectral-determinant/counting-function template for future radial boundary operators. **Why not high:** it is one-dimensional and does not yet control AdS degeneracies, zero modes, boundary counterterms, or field-theory determinants. The note also records a missing \(1/(2\pi i)\) in the introduction and incomplete global logarithm-sheet data.
