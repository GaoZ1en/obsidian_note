## 4. A geodesic distance interpretation of Lanczos coefficients — `2608.16982v1`

- Categories: `hep-th`, `math-ph`, `cond-mat.stat-mech`.
- Reason codes: `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`.
- **Source claim:** for the orthogonal-polynomial ensemble associated with a random-matrix density of states, the squared Lanczos coefficients generate rooted planar maps at exact graph distance, and the Krylov-complexity acceleration is their expectation value for an even potential.
- **Mechanism and construction:** writing the recurrence data as `S_n=a_n` and `R_n=b_n^2`, the discrete string equations are
  \[
  \langle n|V'(\lambda)|n\rangle=0,\qquad
  \sqrt{R_n}\langle n-1|V'(\lambda)|n\rangle=1.
  \]
  The exact-distance generating function is `G_n=R_{n+1}-R_n=b_{n+1}^2-b_n^2`. In double-scaled SYK, `b_n^2=(1-q^n)/(1-q)` gives `G_n=q^n`.
- **Likely use:** a clean dictionary among Lanczos growth, orthogonal-polynomial string equations, planar-map distance, and late-time linear Krylov complexity.

- **Audit:** `Checked` for the algebraic acceleration identity and the double-scaled-SYK difference formula: a symbolic seven-site Jacobi-matrix computation reproduced both diagonal and off-diagonal terms with zero residual, and independently reduced `b_{n+1}^2-b_n^2` to `q^n`. `Source-derived` for the planar-map enumeration and Coulomb-gas/string-equation derivation, which were inspected in the 21-page source but not rederived combinatorially.
