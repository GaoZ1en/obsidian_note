## Medium-priority technical candidates

### 1. Quantum de Sitter and Analytically Continued Chern Simons Theory — `2608.07467v1`

**Codes:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`. **Technical relevance:** direct de Sitter and Chern--Simons testbed. **Author signal:** none used for placement.

**Source claim.** A Lefschetz thimble through the self-dual de Sitter saddle is proposed as a definition of the Chern--Simons/Kodama transform. In flat FRW the transform is Airy; in diagonal Bianchi IX the trace mode remains Airy while shear modes are locally Gaussian-damped.

**Mechanism and formula.** For $\mathcal A=\Gamma+iK$ and $I=\kappa Y_{\rm CS}+\int J\cdot\mathcal A$, the Bianchi IX reduction

$$
Y_{\rm CS}^{\rm IX}=V_c\left(\sum_i f_i^2+2f_1f_2f_3\right),
\qquad f_i=\frac{-\Gamma_i+ib_i}{2},
$$

splits at the symmetric saddle into a purely oscillatory trace eigenvalue and two damped shear eigenvalues. The source's local result is

$$
\Psi^{\rm IX}\sim\operatorname{Ai}\!\left[-\left(\frac{3\kappa V_c}{4}\right)^{2/3}\bar P\right]
\exp\left[-\frac{3\kappa V_c|\mathcal L_{\rm sh}|^2}{8(9+b_*^2)}\right].
$$

**Use.** It is a concrete cubic-contour benchmark for the Chern--Simons/gluing line and for separating a physical cycle from a formal holomorphic state.

**Why not high.** There are no boundary charges, CPS structure, or edge modes. The actual Lorentzian-fibre intersection numbers are not computed; the global Bianchi IX flow is unfinished; a single thimble does not yield the real Airy combination as claimed; and the displayed leading wavefunction drops a $p$-dependent Gaussian determinant.

Detailed note: [[Quantum de Sitter and Analytically Continued Chern Simons Theory (2608.07467v1)]].

### 2. Spectral Topology and Universal Krylov Dynamics — `2608.07258v1`

**Codes:** `T2-spectral`, `T3-math`. **Technical relevance:** spectral-measure and orthogonal-polynomial machinery transferable to AdS quantization. **Author signal:** none used for placement.

**Source claim.** The paper organizes Lanczos asymptotics by the spectral tail, support connectivity, endpoint type, and critical gap closing, using Riemann--Hilbert asymptotics.

**Mechanism and formula.** The cyclic Liouvillian measure fixes orthogonal polynomials and hence the Jacobi coefficients,

$$
C(t)=\int e^{i\omega t}d\mu(\omega),
\qquad
\omega p_n=b_{n+1}p_{n+1}+a_np_n+b_np_{n-1}.
$$

The exact Meixner--Pollaczek family gives

$$
b_n=\frac{\Lambda}{2}\sqrt{n(n+2\lambda-1)},
$$

while a two-band support produces theta-functional dependence on the harmonic measure $n\Omega$. The general result is quasiperiodic; it is not generically the single cosine asserted in the paper.

**Use.** This gives a clean route from an AdS two-point spectral density to finite-cutoff Jacobi data, leading slope, offset, and possible multi-band modulation, provided the order of continuum and large-$n$ limits is kept explicit.

**Why not high.** There is no charge, boundary, or CPS construction. The source overstates the general one-cosine form and a universal complexity velocity; its Painleve-II amplitude is not computed; the critical analysis covers only a symmetric varying-weight quartic model; and several printed equations contain checkable errors.

Detailed note: [[Spectral Topology and Universal Krylov Dynamics (2608.07258v1)]].

### 3. Holographic RG flows and wormholes from sinusoidal scalars — `2608.06465v1`

**Codes:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`. **Technical relevance:** homogeneous holographic wormhole with a directly reusable cross-boundary spectral sum. **Author signal:** none.

**Source claim.** Identical sinusoidal scalar sources on two asymptotic AdS boundaries support a connected Euclidean wormhole above $(J/k)_{\rm crit}\simeq22.5$, with the large connected branch thermodynamically preferred wherever it exists.

**Mechanism.** In $d=3$ with $\Delta=2$, the ansatz $\Phi^I=\phi(r)e^{ikx^I}$ makes the stress tensor homogeneous even though the sources are spatially modulated. The radial scalar problem supplies a Sturm--Liouville spectrum $\{\lambda_n,u_n\}$ and the cross-boundary correlator

$$
\langle\mathcal O_+(x)\mathcal O_-(y)\rangle
=\frac{1}{4\pi|x-y|}
\sum_n(-1)^n u_n^2e^{-\sqrt{\lambda_n}|x-y|},
\qquad
\xi=\lambda_0^{-1/2}.
$$

**Use.** The spectral representation is a useful benchmark for finite-depth/gapped holographic geometries and source backreaction.

**Why not high.** The effective beta function is defined only along the homogeneous radial solution; the paper supplies no boundary phase space or charges, no microscopic dual, and no complete stability analysis. The “gap” is Euclidean spectral decay rather than a demonstrated Lorentzian pole gap.

### 4. Magnetic Catalysis and Fermion Mass Generation in de Sitter Spacetime — `2608.07270v1`

**Codes:** `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`. **Technical relevance:** explicit de Sitter mode functions, Landau levels, and a proper-time gap equation. **Author signal:** none.

**Source claim.** A magnetic field enhances a four-fermion instability in de Sitter and can generate a dynamical mass.

**Mechanism.** Landau-level mode functions satisfy

$$
\left[\partial_\eta^2+
\frac{im}{H\eta^2}\left(1-\frac{im}{H}\right)
+\Omega_{N,s}^2\right]f=0,
\qquad
\Omega_{N,s}^2=k_z^2+(2N+1-s)eB,
$$

and the mean-field gap equation is $\Delta_*/G=-\langle\bar\psi\psi\rangle$. In the large-field regime the source finds the instability criterion

$$
\frac{GH^2e\widetilde B}{4\pi^2}
\left(2\log\widetilde\Lambda+\gamma_E\right)>1.
$$

**Use.** The mode/Landau/proper-time calculation is a compact benchmark for quantum fields in de Sitter backgrounds.

**Why not high.** The analysis is fixed-time mean field; a constant comoving magnetic field has time-dependent physical magnitude. Backreaction, in-in evolution, and order-parameter fluctuations are not treated, and no boundary/CPS structure is present.
