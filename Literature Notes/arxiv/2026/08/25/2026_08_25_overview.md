This is the closest match to the scalar port/gluing program. 3. [[Perturbative Reconstruction of Self-Adjoint Generators from Bosonic Canonical Commutation Relations - Application to the Null-Surface Formulation (2608.21947v1)|Perturbative Reconstruction of Self-Adjoint Generators from Bosonic CCR]] — `T1-CCR`, `T1-canonical`, `T1-symplectic`, `T1-scattering`, `T2-null-surface`. The recursive BCH lift is correct for vacuum-preserving normally ordered polynomial residuals. It proves a formal algebraic generator, not a closed self-adjoint operator or a genuine infinite-dimensional Fock-space unitary.
4. [[A second rotational Killing field on gauged D=5 vector multiplet horizons (2608.22509v1)]] — `T1-horizon`, `T1-rigidity`, `T1-Killing`, `T1-supergravity`, `T1-global-extension`. The second Killing field is valid unconditionally on the nondegenerate open set and globally on the constant-moduli branch. The varying-moduli global corollary needs a $C^1$ or smooth extension hypothesis; the stated continuous-extension condition is insufficient for the proof step it uses.
