## [[Subleading Asymptotic Charges in Massless Scalar QED (2608.02368v1)]]

**Éanna É. Flanagan and Zhihan Liu — [arXiv:2608.02368v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02368v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.

**Technical relevance.** The paper allows

$$
A_A^{(0)}(u,\hat x)
\sim A_A^{\pm(0,0)}(\hat x)
+\frac{A_A^{\pm(0,1)}(\hat x)}u+\cdots,
$$

while keeping the massless scalar data compactly supported. These tails force a polyhomogeneous Maxwell expansion and split the first-subleading data into three aspects. If

$$
T_\pm
=\left.u^2\partial_u\Delta D^{\bar z}A_{\bar z}^{(0)}
\right|_{\mathscr I^+_\pm},
$$

then the two logarithmic charges probe \(T_+-T_-\) and \(T_++T_-\), while the ordinary subleading charge requires a \(Q_1^{\ln}\log\Lambda\) subtraction and finite endpoint improvements. Conservation is proved by identifying the three null aspects with fast, slow, and finite coefficients of source-free Maxwell theory on the hyperbolic blow-up of \(i^0\).

**Claim boundary.** This is a classical asymptotic-coefficient and spatial-matching theorem. The source does not derive a null presymplectic form, functional differentiability, integrability, Poisson action, charge algebra, or quantum Ward identity for the new tail-sum functional. The scalar is also assumed to decay faster than every power near \(i^0\), and a free-theory finite endpoint constant is imported into the interacting problem.

**Verification status.** xAct/Mathematica reproduces the field equations, retarded-coordinate master equation, null recursion, Appendix A endpoint algebra, hyperboloidal equations, antipodal signs, and the full displayed \(k=1\) matching chain without a nonzero residual. Exact finite sectors also check the closed hypergeometric coefficients for \(k=1\), \(L=1,\ldots,5\), and \(k=2,L=2\). This is not a general all-\((k,L)\) analytic proof: the closed resonant asymptotics, implicit higher matching coefficients, imported free endpoint normalization, global scattering compatibility, and Hamiltonianity remain explicitly blocked. The detailed note records the complete source-order ledger.

**Author signal.** Neither author is on the explicit rising-star watchlist. Placement is entirely technical.

## [[The first law of black hole mechanics in conformal Einstein-Power-Yang-Mills theory (2608.01815v1)]]

**Xiaokai He, Xiaoning Wu, and Naqing Xie — [arXiv:2608.01815v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01815v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-charge`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.

**Technical relevance.** In four dimensions, conformal invariance sets the power-Yang--Mills exponent to one, so the actual model is a nonminimally coupled scalar plus ordinary Yang--Mills. The source derives \(\Theta\), \(J_\xi=C_\xi+dQ_\xi\), and the fixed-generator surface form, then obtains

$$
\delta\mathcal E
=\Omega_H\delta\mathcal J
+\frac\kappa6\delta(\phi_H^2A)
-\int_{S_H}(A_\xi)^a\delta({*F_a}).
$$

The last term is not reduced to \(\Phi\,\delta Q\), because the non-Abelian potential and flux need not be covariantly aligned.

**Claim boundary.** The source action has gravitational normalization \(R/12\), not standard \(R/(16\pi)\) at \(G=1\). Yang--Mills falloffs and an integrable boundary ensemble are missing, \(Q_\xi^{\mathrm{YM}}\) is a gauge-dependent pure-diffeomorphism representative, and cross-sectional constancy of \(\phi_H\) is assumed. Printed Eqs. (66)--(68) have malformed mass-variation algebra, and Eqs. (81)--(82) use \(A\cdot\xi\) where the axial charge requires \(A\cdot\varphi\).

**Verification status.** xAct/Mathematica reproduces Eqs. (28)--(52), the Gaussian-null inverse, the conditional horizon cancellations, and

$$
S=\frac\pi3\int_{S_H}\phi^2dA.
$$

It independently fails Eq. (56)'s off-shell sign, the sign/measure and normalization of Eqs. (66)--(68), the unqualified non-Abelian magnitude identities (74), (76), the axial formulas (81)--(82), and the claim that Eq. (103) is already in standard \(G=1\) normalization. The downstream-safe result keeps canonical boundary variations:

$$
\delta H_t-\Omega_H\delta H_\varphi
=\frac\kappa6\delta\!\int_{S_H}\phi^2dA
-\int_{S_H}(A^a\cdot\xi)\delta({*F_a}),
$$

under fixed \(\xi\), preserved Gaussian-null gauge, and \(F_a^{rB}|_H=0\). The detailed note gives the complete residual and assumption ledger.

**Author signal.** No explicit watchlist match; technical placement only.

## [[Projecting Gravitational Fluctuations onto Near-Horizon Throats (2608.00363v1)]]

**Alejandro Cabo-Bizet, Vasil Dimitrov, Dario Martelli, and Lorenzo Ruggeri — [arXiv:2608.00363v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00363v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** A four-singularity Heun fluctuation equation is split into fixed-\(z\) outer and fixed-\(\zeta=z/t\) throat problems. Projective coordinate maps remove the interaction through the Schwarzian transformation law, while the omitted complementary region becomes an energy-dependent Robin connection ratio. The spectrum obeys

$$
R_{\mathrm{out}}
=t^{-2\kappa}
\left(1-\frac{2\kappa\Delta C_0}{t}+O(t^{-2})\right)R_{\mathrm{in}},
$$

with explicit \(\Delta C_0\). In the near-cold Kerr--(A)dS scaling, the source claims that a subset of \(m=0\) modes has

$$
\omega=-2\pi iT_hn+\cdots,
\qquad |n|\ge|s|,
$$

and leading radial profiles matching the tensor/vector Schwarzian--JT mode spaces.

**Verification failure.** The Heun degeneration, projective transformation, first-order maps, and Robin correction factor reproduce exactly. The printed near-cold lattice does not: Eqs. (4.24), (4.28), and (4.29) imply \(i\widetilde\omega/(2\pi)=\pm(\ell+p+1)\), while Eq. (4.31) states \(\pm(\ell+p)\). The printed value leaves residual one, so the frequency lattice and its downstream mode exclusions require repair.

**Use case.** A precise finite-mouth AdS\(_2\) gluing model: integrate out the exterior, keep its frequency-dependent Dirichlet-to-Neumann kernel, and solve the throat problem with that induced boundary datum.

**Why not high.** The paper explicitly realizes only the first nontrivial correction and gives a formal higher-order recursion; global all-order existence is not proved. It does not derive a Schwarzian action, a four-dimensional CPS norm, the complete determinant measure, or direct Kerr--AdS conformal-boundary conditions.

**Author signal.** None; technical placement only.

## [[Canonical quantization of the Pais-Uhlenbeck oscillator with a higher-derivative perturbation: a covariant phase space approach (2608.00108v1)]]

**Jie-qiang Wu and Jinan Zhao — [arXiv:2608.00108v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00108v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-model`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** For

$$
L=\frac12\dot x^2-\frac12\omega^2x^2
-\frac\lambda2\ddot x^2,
$$

the full exact theory has a negative-energy fast branch. The paper instead solves perturbatively on the branch regular at \(\lambda\to0\), pulls back

$$
\Omega
=(\delta\dot x+\lambda\delta\dddot x)\wedge\delta x
-\lambda\delta\ddot x\wedge\delta\dot x,
$$

and obtains \(\iota_{\mathrm{slow}}^*\Omega=-i\delta a\wedge\delta a^\dagger+O(\lambda^3)\). The pulled-back Hamiltonian and unequal-time commutator reproduce the exact slow-branch expansion.

**Use case.** A finite-mode benchmark for order reduction and branch selection before inverting a symplectic form.

**Why not high.** This is a two-dimensional slow-sector identity within a finite secular-time window. It does not remove the exact Ostrogradsky branch or establish a field-theory, boundary-flux, correlator, or Fock-equivalence result.

**Verification.** Mathematica reproduced the Euler--Lagrange boundary term, frequency series, both perturbative solution equations, cancellation of the \(O(\lambda)\) and \(O(\lambda^2)\) symplectic corrections, the Hamiltonian correction, and the slow commutator.

**Author signal.** None.

## [[Quantization of Galilean Electrodynamics: a non-trivially trivial theory (2608.01372v1)]]

**Rafael Hernández, Juan Miguel Nieto García, and Ander Urtiaga — [arXiv:2608.01372v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01372v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** Null reduction of five-dimensional Maxwell theory produces a singular Legendre map with two first-class and six second-class constraints at generic nonzero spatial momentum. The count

$$
10-2(2)-6=0
$$

removes local propagating modes, but the constrained path integral retains

$$
S_{\mathrm{red}}=V\int dt\left(p\dot q-\frac12p^2\right)
$$

for the spatial zero mode.

**Use case.** A warning for Galilean/Carrollian limits and boundary reductions: generic-momentum constraint rank, the \(k=0\) kernel, and differentiability of the gauge generator must be handled separately.

**Why not high.** The boundary conditions are deliberately restrictive, the functional determinants are not evaluated, and no asymptotic phase space or surface-charge algebra is constructed.

**Verification.** Mathematica found the generic-\(k\) second-class determinant \(-k^{14}\), confirmed rank collapse at \(k=0\), and independently showed that the printed transverse projector in Eq. (3.18) has the wrong sign. The paper's position-space claim \(1/\omega^2\mapsto t^2\) also fails: the Green function is piecewise linear, modulo prescription and homogeneous terms.

**Author signal.** None.

## Hidden Symmetry of Kerr-deSitter from Manifest Symmetry of Painlevé VI — `2608.02259v1`

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`, `A-big-name`.

**Source claim.** The radial Teukolsky mass symmetries arise from permutation/sign symmetries of the Painlevé-VI isomonodromic system that preserves the monodromy of a four-puncture Fuchsian equation.

**Main mechanism.** A subset of those transformations maps the Teukolsky equation to a scalar wave equation on a dual Lorentzian spacetime. For Kerr--dS the dual geometry is again a black hole; its conserved current and positive-energy region give a geometric route to a known partial mode-stability bound, and recover the absence of exponentially growing Schwarzschild--dS modes.

**Likely use.** A bridge among Teukolsky connection problems, Painlevé VI, hidden mass symmetries, and geometric energy estimates.

**Why not high.** The result is spectral/isomonodromic rather than a surface-charge or boundary phase-space construction, and the mode-stability conclusion remains partial in rotating Kerr--dS.

**Author signal.** Independent `A-big-name` signal from Stefan Hollands; medium placement is also technically warranted.
