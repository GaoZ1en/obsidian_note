# High-priority recommendations

## [[Infinite Symmetry Algebras in Four-Dimensional Conformal Field Theories (2607.28718v1)]]

**Elizabeth Himwich and Monica Pate — [arXiv:2607.28718v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28718v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.

**Technical relevance.** The paper begins with CFT null-infinity falloffs and conservation, classifies maximal-spin Lorentz-primary stress and current detectors $\mathcal W_m$ and $\mathcal S_m$, and uses their $P_u$ recursion to constrain coincident-angle commutators. The local stress bracket is

$$
[\mathcal W^p,\mathcal W^q]
=i\big[(p-1)\partial'-(q-1)\partial\big]
\bigl(\delta^{(2)}\mathcal W^{p+q-2}\bigr)
+\text{contacts at }\partial^{2p-1}\text{ or }\partial'^{2q-1}.
$$

Polynomial wedge smearing annihilates those contacts and leaves

$$
[\mathrm w^p_{M,\bar M},\mathrm w^q_{N,\bar N}]
=i[M(q-1)-N(p-1)]
\mathrm w^{p+q-2}_{M+N,\bar M+\bar N}.
$$

The same architecture yields a nonabelian current $S$ algebra and makes $S$ a module for the stress wedge algebra. This is directly useful for the current null-boundary/asymptotic-symmetry work because it cleanly separates the detector density, local improvements, test-function projection, and resulting algebra.

**Claim boundary.** The construction gives hard-like CFT detector operators, not full soft-plus-hard gravitational charges. The stress-algebra theorem omits identity/central terms, assumes no neutral scalar with $1\le\Delta\le2$, and relies on integrated-operator convergence. Bare generators require a global prescription for inverse $\bar z$ derivatives. Independent large-$r$ calculations reproduce both stress and current event shapes through every source-displayed order $m\le4$; the all-$m$ statements remain extrapolations. The algebra audit found four repairable local source defects—one half-trace normalization statement, one $\ell$ inequality, and two Appendix-B inverse-derivative typos—and one unrepaired Appendix-E failure: the printed $[\mathcal S_0,\mathcal S_2]$ commutator violates its stated $P_u$ Jacobi identity by a term proportional to $\partial_z[\delta^{(2)}\int du\,j_z^{(2)}]$. That term is killed by the $\mathcal S_0$ wedge smearing, so the projected wedge coefficients remain independently checked; the printed local base case does not.

**Author signal.** None of the named rising-star watchlist authors appears. Placement is entirely technical, not author-driven.

## [[From Horizon Microstates to the Black Hole Membrane (2607.28746v1)]]

**Chong-Sun Chu — [arXiv:2607.28746v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28746v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-model`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Source claim.** A fuzzy-sphere matrix model supplies finite horizon lowest-Landau-level partons; a tachyonic bifundamental link condenses in a Planck-thickness layer and couples their emergent gauge field to exterior Maxwell theory.

**Main mechanism.** Varying the finite-stiffness horizon/interface action gives

$$
\frac{1}{4\pi}F_{An}=j_A+j_A^{\rm pol},
$$

without first taking the locking stiffness to infinity. In one local response eigenchannel,

$$
\mathcal R=\frac{1-4\pi\sigma}{1+4\pi\sigma},
\qquad
\mathcal A=\frac{16\pi\operatorname{Re}\sigma}{|1+4\pi\sigma|^2}\le1.

**Use case.** This is a concrete model for separating microscopic boundary carriers, an interface condensate, finite-stiffness current transfer, low-frequency locking, and high-frequency inclusive absorption.

**Why not high.** The friction and exact $\sigma_{xx}(\omega)$ are not derived, the full unstable link sector is not minimized, and there is no gravitational CPS/surface-charge calculation.

**Author signal.** None; technical placement only.

## Excitation region of Kerr black hole quasinormal modes from Stokes geometry

**Naritaka Oshita — [arXiv:2607.28930v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28930v1)**

Reason codes: `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** The radial Teukolsky problem is put in WKB form,

$$
\psi''(z)+\omega^2V(z)^2\psi(z)=0.
$$

The anti-Stokes line connecting the relevant complex turning points intersects the real radial axis at an “excitation radius.” For Schwarzschild the condition $\operatorname{Re}z=0$ gives $r/M\simeq2.5569291$. The paper compares that radius with the convergence boundary of the QNM-plus-branch-cut reconstruction. At slow and moderate spin they agree numerically; in the near-extremal/high-damping regime the Stokes geometry develops multiple intersections and the simple identification fails.

**Central construction.** The transferable object is a geometric diagnostic of where a spectral/QNM expansion ceases to reconstruct local data, not a new QNM formula.

**Use case.** Useful as a spectral-convergence benchmark for black-hole mode reconstructions and for distinguishing ordinary damped modes from the near-extremal zero-damped sector.

**Why not high.** The result is a WKB/spectral diagnostic with no boundary symplectic form, charge algebra, or directly reusable AdS quantization formula.

**Author signal.** None.

## Schwarzian Field Theory at High Temperatures

**Ilya Losev — [arXiv:2607.29423v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29423v1)**

Reason code: `T2-spectral`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** Starting from the rigorous Schwarzian measure on $\mathrm{Diff}^1(\mathbb T)/SL(2,\mathbb R)$, the paper studies $\sigma^2\to\infty$ with $\sigma^2/\beta\to\kappa^2$. After choosing a local chart, the weak limit is a real-line process

$$
f(t)=\int_0^t e^{\xi(s)}ds,
$$

with two-sided Brownian $\xi$. Globally, gauge representatives concentrate on discontinuous configurations with at most three jumps, and that global description is representative-dependent.

**Central construction.** The useful content is the distinction between a well-defined local stochastic limit and a gauge-representative-sensitive global concentration statement.

**Use case.** A rigorous reference for high-temperature Schwarzian measures and possible spectral/path-integral limits in nearly-AdS$_2$ models.

**Why not high.** It does not supply a current AdS$_3$/CPS calculation, boundary charge, or direct determinant needed by an active project.

**Author signal.** None.

## First Law of Proto-Area Entropy from Modular Spectral Geometry

**Ling-Zheng Xia and Lixin Xu — [arXiv:2607.29432v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29432v1)**

Reason codes: `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-spectral`.

**Source claim and mechanism.** For a random interaction, the Kubo--Mori spectral factor is written

$$
\Xi=\frac12\sum_{ij}L(k_i-k_j),
\qquad
L(x)=x\coth\frac x2.
$$

Near a maximally mixed state,

$$
\Xi=d_1^2\left(1+\frac13\Delta S_a+\cdots\right),
$$

so the leading proto-area entropy shift is linear in the subsystem entropy deficit. The proposed gravitational matching retains an unfixed coefficient $\lambda$; the exact gravitational slope would require $\lambda=3$, which the model does not derive.

**Central construction.** A modular-spectral toy calculation explains why a first-law-like linear term can arise from an unstructured random coupling.

**Use case.** A compact toy model for checking which part of an area/entropy first law is kinematic and which coefficient still needs gravitational input.

**Why not high.** The GUE interaction has no spatial, gauge, or gravitational constraint structure, and the matching coefficient remains free.

**Author signal.** None.

## [[A Holographic Map from AdS3 to CFT2 (2607.29635v1)]]

**Manish Ramchander and Ronak M Soni — [arXiv:2607.29635v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29635v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.

**Source claim.** A fixed-area-network Wheeler--DeWitt quantization of pure AdS$_3$ gravity is mapped into a single CFT$_2$ Hilbert space by sending complex geodesic lengths to primary weights and pair-of-pants vertices to normalized OPE coefficients.

**Main mechanism.** The fixed-area polarization has symplectic form

$$
\Omega=\sum_{\gamma,s=\pm}\delta\ell_\gamma^s\wedge\delta\vartheta_\gamma^s+\cdots,
$$

and requires explicit Hayward/twist corner terms. Sharp fixed-area states are not mapped isometrically. Approximate inner-product matching is argued only after every length is microcanonically smeared and contracted OPE products are replaced by conjectural single-CFT averages matching the gravitational topology sum.

**Use case.** The paper makes fixed-area polarization, rigging maps, topology-changing overlaps, emergent bulk gauges, and relational interior-length reconstruction part of one explicit proposal.

**Why not high.** The map is conditional rather than proved; internal length/OPE matching, topology-sum completion, and typicality are conjectural. The source also contains an unresolved factor-of-two length/momentum mismatch and an inconsistent width-versus-variance scaling statement.

**Author signal.** None.

## [[A-type Sigma Models from Differential Poisson Geometry (2607.29668v1)]]

**Cesar Arias and Per Sundell — [arXiv:2607.29668v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29668v1)**

Reason codes: `T1-symplectic`, `T2-model`, `T3-math`.

**Source claim.** A graded Poisson tensor on $T[1]M$ produces a first-order DPSM. In the nondegenerate phase, its Jacobi identities force the parent connection $\Gamma$ to be flat, while torsion leaves the transposed connection $\widetilde\Gamma$ curved.

**Main mechanism.** Eliminating $p_a$ gives

$$
S_{\rm red}=\int_\Sigma\left[
\frac12\omega_{ab}dx^a\wedge dx^b
+\chi_a\wedge\nabla\theta^a
+\frac14\widetilde R_{ab}{}^{cd}\theta^a\theta^b
\chi_c\wedge\chi_d\right].
$$

The parent $Q$ symmetry is off-shell nilpotent and $Q$-exact; after eliminating $p_a$, closure is only on shell. A Kodaira--Thurston example realizes $R(\Gamma)=0$ but $\widetilde R_{12}{}^{33}=1$.

**Use case.** A controlled non-Kähler symplectic model for distinguishing first-order parent data, torsion-induced quartic couplings, on-shell reduction, and differential-Poisson observables.

**Why not high.** It does not yet organize a gravity or boundary dynamics problem, and the paper's degree-$-1$ bracket/shifted strict-$L_\infty$ conventions need a separate sign audit.

**Author signal.** None.
