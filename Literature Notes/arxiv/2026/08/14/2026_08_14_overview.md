### [[Edge physics and the Casimir interaction in Maxwell--Chern--Simons theory on a strip (2608.12622v1)]]

- Reusable determinant:
  \[
  E_{\mathrm{int}}(h)
  =
  \frac12
  \int\frac{d\zeta\,dk}{(2\pi)^2}
  \log\left[
  1-r_0(\zeta,k)r_h(\zeta,k)e^{-2Qh}
  \right],
  \qquad
  Q=\sqrt{\zeta^2+k^2+m^2}.
  \]
- Independent audit: Mathematica and xAct checks reproduced the boundary variation, physical-polarization reduction, compatibility factorization, reflection algebra, determinant differentiation, and limiting asymptotics. The source supplies no numerical driver, so its plots were not independently regenerated.
- Claim tier: a verified finite-cutoff one-helicity Gaussian determinant and edge algebra, not an interacting continuum gluing theorem.

### [[Horizon-regular cross-focusing and inner-horizon obstructions in spherical f(R) gravity (2608.12651v1)]]

- Exact balance for marginal endpoints:

  \int_{\lambda_o}^{\lambda_i}

  \frac{r^2\mathcal P_{\ell n}}{F}-1
