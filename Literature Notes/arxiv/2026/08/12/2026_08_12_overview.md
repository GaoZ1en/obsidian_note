## Medium-priority technical candidates

### Detailed medium notes

- **Octupolar Gravitational Radiation in de Sitter Spacetime** — [`2608.10593v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10593v1), `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`; [[Octupolar Gravitational Radiation in de Sitter Spacetime (2608.10593v1)]]. It extends the generalized-harmonic retarded solution to \(l=3\), separating direct, global-tail, and near-zone-tail pieces and translating STF moments to spherical harmonics. The universal identity
  $$
  \int d^3x\,x^L\partial_\eta^nT
  =a^{n-l-1}\prod_{k=0}^{n-1}[\partial_t-(l+1-k)H]S_L
  $$
  is the main reusable mechanism. **Checked:** cubic distance expansions and both angular structures in the simplified \(G_3\) block have zero Mathematica residual. It is not high because it supplies neither Bondi data nor charges/fluxes at \(\mathcal I^+\); the authors' mode-by-mode Einstein-equation check was not independently reproduced.
- **Casimir Force in Spacetimes with Torsion** — [`2608.10009v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10009v1), `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`; [[Casimir Force in Spacetimes with Torsion (2608.10009v1)]]. The setup is a spacelike Carroll--Field--Jackiw photon with conducting plates. **Failed:** the claimed \(O(\kappa^2)\) pressure coefficient is derived after omitting the explicit \(+\kappa^2/2\) term in the paper's own exact dispersion relation. Under the paper's provisional bulk-spectrum assumption, restoring it changes \(\Delta E/A\) from \(\kappa^2/(192a)\) to \(\kappa^2/(64a)\), but even this corrected factor is conditional on a cavity eigenproblem the paper does not finish. The statement that the CFJ density is generically a total derivative also fails the bulk Euler--Lagrange test. It remains medium because it is a useful boundary-spectral failure case; the qualitative experimental smallness survives.
- **Anomalous symmetries in Kähler geometry** — [`2608.10861v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10861v1), `T1-symplectic`, `T3-math`; [[Anomalous symmetries in Kähler geometry (2608.10861v1)]]. The structural contribution is a local classification of Abelian Kähler isometries with centrally extended moment maps into Heisenberg blocks, ordinary Kähler quotients, and generalized-Kähler gauging with twisted-chiral compensators. **Failed normalization check:** with \(\omega=\frac{i}{2}\partial\bar\partial K\), the displayed moment maps are twice those satisfying \(\iota_V\omega=d\mu_V\) in flat \(\mathbb C\); the paper's example then reports a third incompatible central factor. The classification mechanism remains plausible after a consistent rescaling, but the numerical \(c\)-normalization and the general quotient/superconnection theorem were not independently established.

### Compact medium treatments

- **Sharp Tangential NEC Minimization and Israel Surface Layers in Schwarzschild Mass Interpolations** — [`2608.09963v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09963v1), `T1-boundary`, `T2-model`. A nonlocal weighted positive-variation problem has a box-profile relaxed minimizer; smooth minimizing sequences converge to a constant-density \(p=-\rho\) bulk with two timelike Israel layers, and the negative tangential NEC concentrates on the inner layer. Useful as a sharp smooth-to-distributional boundary-layer model. It is not high because it does not construct boundary charges, CPS data, or dynamical gravitational fluxes.
- **Approximate locality, black hole complementarity and overlapping qubits** — [`2608.10085v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10085v1), `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T3-math`. Radiation and interior operators are distinct representations of one overlapping-qubit algebra rather than commuting factors; correcting entropy counting for this overlap yields a Page curve without an exact interior/exterior tensor factorization. Worth tracking for algebraic factorization questions, but it is a toy Hilbert-space model rather than a gravitational phase-space construction.
- **Holographic Complexity as a Probe of Boundary Entropy in AdS/BCFT** — [`2608.10348v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10348v1), `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`. The paper maps cobordism composition to holographic sewing and claims that Euclidean action, RT entropy, and a relative Complexity=Action renormalization isolate the same \(\log g\); in BTZ, late-time growth also depends on brane tension. Kept medium for the EOW-brane/interface dictionary, not high because the central observable is complexity rather than a boundary symplectic or charge construction.
- **Conductors and superconductors in stationary spacetimes** — [`2608.10814v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10814v1), `T2-model`. A threading/slicing decomposition of the charged-particle 3-force is combined with London phenomenology to obtain generalized Schiff--Barnhill and Meissner relations without a weak-field or low-velocity expansion. Observer dependence and gravitational constitutive terms are potentially reusable. It is not high because it is a material-response model with no direct charge/CPS deliverable.
- **Quasinormal modes in effective loop quantum gravity and consequences for isospectrality** — [`2608.11088v1`](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11088v1), `T2-spectral`, `T2-model`. High-order WKB plus Padé resummation gives axial/polar spectra of an effective AOS black hole and finds isospectrality breaking that decays toward Schwarzschild with increasing mass; the authors explicitly map where WKB fails. Useful as a spectral-method caution, but model- and approximation-specific rather than a general quantization result.
