# Top papers worth actually reading

## Subleading Asymptotic Charges in Massless Scalar QED

- arXiv: [2608.02368v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02368v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-04.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.
- Technical relevance: the week's most direct null/spatial-infinity result. Inverse-time gauge tails force polyhomogeneous data and produce three first-subleading matching functionals.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read first, especially the endpoint algebra and hyperboloidal matching chain. The tail-sum functional is not yet shown to be differentiable, integrable, or Hamiltonian on a null presymplectic phase space; that missing CPS construction is the transferable next problem.

## AKSZ Descent on Manifolds with Ordinary Corners

- arXiv: [2608.02928v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02928v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-05.
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T3-math`.
- Technical relevance: packages facewise BV-BFV defects, codimension-two incidence cancellation, factorially normalized transgression, and gluing into one ordinary-corner total complex.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read second as the week's cleanest corner-sign and descent reference. The square-face/BF core is independently checked; infinite-dimensional smoothness, gravitational reduction, and generalized-corner gluing remain assumptions or open extensions.

## Bessel-Hagen currents for linearised Weyl-squared gravity

- arXiv: [2608.02912v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02912v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-05.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T2-model`.
- Technical relevance: proves a useful failure shield—an on-shell conformal current class can exist even when no strictly gauge- and Weyl-invariant local stress-tensor representative exists.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read the current construction and no-go argument together. The parity-even identity is analytically checked and the parity-odd identity has exact component support, but the full analytic parity-odd xAct enumeration remains blocked.

## Boundary Conditions and Entanglement in Anti-de Sitter Space

- arXiv: [2608.04229v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04229v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-06.
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T2-spectral`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.
- Technical relevance: the week's best immediate bridge to alternative quantization and nonuniform regulators. Its explicit finite-`N` Robin parameter exposes noncommuting continuum and Dirichlet limits.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read the finite stiffness matrix, covariance construction, and analytic DN--DD benchmark. The exact finite-matrix checks and the `1/6` result are reusable; the full angular sum and continuum fits are not independently reproducible from the released source bundle.

## Infinite Symmetry Algebras in Four-Dimensional Conformal Field Theories

- arXiv: [2607.28718v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28718v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-03.
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.
- Technical relevance: cleanly distinguishes local light-ray density brackets, high-derivative contact terms, polynomial wedge projection, and the resulting loop-`w_{1+\infty}`-type algebra.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read after the direct charge/corner papers. The projected wedge algebra is the controlled result; it is not a gravitational CPS charge algebra, and one printed local current commutator fails its stated Jacobi identity before wedge smearing.
