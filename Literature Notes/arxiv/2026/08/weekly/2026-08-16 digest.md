# Top papers worth actually reading

## Edge physics and the Casimir interaction in Maxwell--Chern--Simons theory on a strip

- arXiv: [2608.12622v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12622v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-14.
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-charge`, `T2-model`, `T2-spectral`.
- Technical relevance: the week's closest bridge to the active finite-coupling gluing work. It connects a differentiable quadratic boundary functional, opposite edge-current levels, one physical helicity, and an exact finite-width determinant without identifying those layers.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read first. The boundary variation, polarization reduction, reflection algebra, and determinant identities were independently checked. Treat it as a verified finite-cutoff Gaussian benchmark, not an interacting continuum gluing theorem.

## Horizon flux-balance laws in the multiscale perturbation

- arXiv: [2608.10093v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10093v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-12.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symmetry`.
- Technical relevance: the week's strongest direct CPS/charge paper. It joins exact horizon evolution, two-timescale Kerr perturbation theory, an ingoing-Newman--Unti gauge map, and CFP/Wald--Zoupas-type flux laws.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read second, focusing on the separation between ambiguous local Carter data and protected global entropy and angular momentum. The proposed off-Kerr horizon energy remains prescription-dependent, and the complete finite coordinate transformation was not independently reproduced.

## Supertranslations are Soft Dressings

- Source daily overview: 2026-08-11.
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`.

- Author-signal relevance: none used.

## Aspects of Carrollian field theory from holography

- arXiv: [2608.12241v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12241v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-13.
- Reason codes: `T1-symmetry`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-charge`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`.
- Technical relevance: supplies an explicit AFS3 rigged-null-boundary response tensor, BMS3 central data, a flux-balance Ward identity, and a boundary Schwarzian/Hamiltonian proposal.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.

The metric/response and central-charge checks passed, but the printed BMS Schwarzian fails its required linearization and the stated flat-frame trace identity conflicts with the computed trace.

## Holographic Renormalization for String-Derived Lovelock--Horndeski Theory

- arXiv: [2608.13319v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13319v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-14.
- Reason codes: `T1-boundary`, `T1-charge`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`, `T2-model`.
- Technical relevance: the strongest finite-cutoff variational reference of the week, carrying an exact branch through Dirichlet completion, Fefferman--Graham recursion, anomaly, one-point functions, Ward identities, and an inverse coupling map.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read after the direct charge papers. Its main value is the explicit separation of branch existence, response invertibility, differentiability, anomaly consistency, and identifiability. The inverse is valid only on the stated regular quotient and truncation.

## A global variational calculus on Frechet manifolds and the Lagrangian structure of the Einstein evolution equations

- arXiv: [2608.08987v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08987v1).
- Source daily overview: 2026-08-11.
- Reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T1-charge`, `T3-math`.
- Technical relevance: organizes Einstein evolution globally on Frechet manifolds and exposes transport algebra, reduction, and momentum-map-like structures adjacent to CPS.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: use as a variational-organization reference. Its smooth-solution framework does not prove existence, uniqueness, or global time, and its Noether momentum map is not automatically a canonical CPS moment map.
