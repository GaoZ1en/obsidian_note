# Top papers worth actually reading

## The reduced Dirac structure of General Relativity on manifolds with corners

- arXiv: [2607.28262v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28262v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-31.
- Reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symplectic`, `T2-model`; author code: `A-big-name` for Alberto Cattaneo.
- Technical relevance: the week's closest match to the finite-boundary/corner program. It carries the Palatini--Cartan boundary potential through four corner residuals to a reduced graph-of-Poisson structure.
- Author-signal relevance: supportive and independent of placement.
- Judgment: read first, through the quotient and Eq. 58. Treat the reduced classical core as the result; do not copy the BF2V transcription until its documented sign, degree, type, and generator-label defects are repaired.

## Soft charges and zero modes at null boundaries

- arXiv: [2607.28543v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28543v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-31.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-model`.
- Technical relevance: the shortest explicit construction this week of a reduced null-boundary bracket and Regge--Teitelboim soft generator.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read second, using the corrected tangency condition `delta pi = -F eta`. It is a finite-radial zero-mode model, not yet a radiative null-infinity CPS or Wald--Zoupas balance law.

## A comparison of two constructions for dynamical corrections to Wald entropy

- arXiv: [2607.27732v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27732v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-31.
- Reason codes: `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-boundary`, `T2-dS-BH-holography`.
- Technical relevance: gives a finite triangular inverse between the linearized Wall construction and the Wald--Iyer dynamical entropy on a stationary Gaussian-null background.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read the recursion, the Riemann-squared example, and Appendix E together. The result is controlled at linear amplitude on a closed cut and does not determine a nonlinear JKM completion.

## Conserved Gravitational Charges as Curvature Fluxes

- arXiv: [2607.25646v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25646v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-29.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-boundary`, `T1-symmetry`.
- Technical relevance: supplies the week's strongest comparison between a manifest curvature-flux representative and ADM/AdS/Lorentz charges.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: use the exact P-tensor flux and the checked flat-space reductions. The Riemann-only AdS expansion omits a Lambda-dependent index-raising term under the paper's stated convention and must be repaired before use.

## Kerr Soft Dressing and the w-frame algebra at null infinity

- arXiv: [2607.27478v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27478v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-31.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T2-celestial-carrollian`.
- Technical relevance: joins Kerr multipoles, soft dressing, Ward data, and an explicit cotangent-bundle Poisson algebra.
- Author-signal relevance: none used.
- Judgment: read after the concrete charge constructions. The verified global object is the polynomial Poisson algebra on `T*S^2`; the `w`-type form appears after a local chiral reduction, not as a demonstrated global exact higher-spin symmetry.

## Mass-electric versus NUT-magnetic charges

- arXiv: [2607.22938v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22938v1)
- Source daily overview: 2026-07-28.
- Reason codes: `T1-charge`, `T2-model`; author code: `A-big-name` for Ricardo Monteiro.
- Technical relevance: provides a broad mass/NUT and electric/magnetic solution-space duality from Kerr--Schild fields through amplitudes and higher-spin curvatures.
- Author-signal relevance: supportive and independent of placement.
- Judgment: read as the framework reference for the mass/NUT line, but keep the object type explicit: these are normalized solution/source parameters, not a derived CPS charge algebra. The closed all-spin formula also remains a conjecture supported by finite-spin tests.
