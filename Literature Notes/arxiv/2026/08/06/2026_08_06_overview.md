## High-priority recommendations

### 1. Boundary Conditions and Entanglement in Anti-de Sitter Space

**[2608.04229v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04229v1)** — Konstantinos Boutivas, Dimitrios Katsinis, Georgios Pastras, Nathan Smeyers, Nikolaos Tetradis.

Reason codes: **T1-boundary, T2-spectral, T2-dS-BH-holography**. Technical relevance, not author signal.

Detailed note: [[Boundary Conditions and Entanglement in Anti-de Sitter Space (2608.04229v1)]]

The paper studies a conformal scalar in global AdS4 with \(\Delta_-=1\), \(\Delta_+=2\), and mixed boundary parameter \(A\). Its finite regulator is explicit:

$$
Q(A,N)=\frac{1-\pi A/[2(N+1)]}{1+\pi A/[2(N+1)]},
$$

which enters a tridiagonal stiffness matrix \(K_{\ell}^{(N)}\). This directly separates finite-\(N\) matrix identities from the continuum boundary condition and exposes the noncommuting Dirichlet/continuum limits. The analytic \(s\)-wave benchmark gives

$$
S_{\rm DN}-S_{\rm DD}
=\frac16\frac{R^2}{a^2}+O(R^4/a^4).
$$

Independent Mathematica/Sage checks reproduced the coordinate reduction, spectral endpoints, Robin ratio, finite-matrix positivity and covariance sanity, representative spectra, and the analytic appendix chain. The full three-dimensional angular sum and continuum fits are blocked because the source bundle has no numerical code or raw data. One harmonic-sign/dimension typo, one omitted common endpoint normalization, and two regulator qualifications were identified without changing the central \(1/6\) result.

### 2. A smooth BTZ black bounce with an extremal null throat

**[2608.04461v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04461v1)** — Farzad Milani.

Reason codes: **T1-charge, T1-Wald-CPS, T1-boundary, T2-dS-BH-holography, T2-model**. Technical relevance, not author signal.

Detailed note: [[A smooth BTZ black bounce with an extremal null throat (2608.04461v1)]]

The static metric

$$
ds^2=-Fdt^2+\frac{dr^2}{SF}+r^2d\phi^2,
\qquad
F=-M+\frac{r^2}{\ell^2},
\qquad
S=\tanh\!\frac{r-r_h}{\delta},
$$

is analytically extended with \(r=r_h+q^2\) to a second exterior. The source derives an effective stress tensor, three entropy assignments, a positive scalar potential, and an exact horizon conservation law

$$
H_0=\partial_q\psi|_{q=0}.
$$

This is high priority because it touches finite-boundary charges, Iyer--Wald language, Brown--Henneaux/Cardy normalization, and extremal horizon data. The decisive caution is that \(T^\mu{}_\nu\) is defined kinematically from the metric and has no action; no \(\theta\), \(\omega\), \(k_\xi\), integrable \(H_\xi\), or first law is constructed.

The xAct/Mathematica audit found four substantive errors:

1. the AdS2 radius is \(2\sqrt{\delta/F'(r_h)}\), not \(\sqrt{2\delta/F'(r_h)}\);
2. the scalar AdS endpoint is limit-point, not limit-circle;
3. the Wald binormal contraction is \(-4\), not \(-2\), although the final \(\mathcal A/(4G)\) value is correct;
4. the claimed SEC cannot hold when the radial NEC is negative.

The exact analytic extension, source tensor, throat invariants, scalar potential, Brown--York asymptotic mass, Cardy arithmetic, and Aretakis recursion otherwise reproduce.
