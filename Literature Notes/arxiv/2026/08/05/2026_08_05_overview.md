## High-priority recommendations

### 1. Bessel-Hagen currents for linearised Weyl-squared gravity — 2608.02912v1

**Codes:** `T1-charge`, `T1-symmetry`, `T2-model`
**Technical relevance:** high. **Author signal:** none used in the placement.

Detailed note: [[Bessel-Hagen currents for linearised Weyl-squared gravity (2608.02912v1)]]

The paper first constructs the conformal Bessel-Hagen current family
\[
J^\alpha_{\zeta,\xi,\omega}
=J^\alpha_{\zeta,0,0}
-2B^{\alpha\nu}\xi_\nu
+\partial_\beta S^{\alpha\beta},
\]
so compensating diffeomorphism/Weyl transformations change the Bach-shell current only by a superpotential. It then asks the stronger question: can every conformal current be written as \(T^\mu{}_{\nu}\zeta^\nu\) with a local, polynomial, symmetric, curvature-quadratic, dimension-four tensor that is strictly invariant under both gauge symmetries and conserved on every Bach-flat field?

Four-dimensional Weyl identities collapse every admissible even/odd candidate to
\[
T_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}(c_1C^2+c_2C{}^\star C),
\]
and Bach-flat Schwarzschild/Kerr-type fields force \(c_1=c_2=0\). This is directly useful as a charge-formalism failure shield: nonexistence of a strict bulk stress representative does not imply nonexistence of an on-shell current class, superpotential, or boundary charge. xAct reproduced the parity-even DDI analytically; Mathematica gave exact component checks of both parity identities. The parity-odd analytic xAct enumeration timed out and is reported as blocked in the detailed note.

### 2. AKSZ Descent on Manifolds with Ordinary Corners — 2608.02928v1

**Codes:** `T1-boundary`, `T1-Wald-CPS`, `T1-symplectic`, `T3-math`
**Technical relevance:** high. **Author signal:** none used in the placement.

Detailed note: [[AKSZ Descent on Manifolds with Ordinary Corners (2608.02928v1)]]

For each ordinary face \(F\), the paper assigns an AKSZ mapping space and derives
\[
\iota_{Q_F}\omega_F
=(-1)^{\dim F}\delta S_F
+\sum_{G\subset\partial F}[F:G]\iota_{GF}^*\alpha_G.
\]
Taking another differential yields the symplectic defect and the codimension-two corner square. Incidence cancellation and the mixed sign combine into a total differential \(\mathbb D\) with \(\mathbb D^2=0\). The normalized transgression
\[
\mathbb T=\sum_{k\ge0}\frac1{k!}
\sum_{F\in\mathcal F_k(M)}\mathsf T_F^k
\]
is a cochain map because the Stokes term's factor \(k\) cancels the factorial shift.

The 35-page source was read in monograph mode across all sections and appendices. The detailed note covers the face complex, transgression theorem, shifted cotangent/Poisson model, regular versus singular reduction, imported Palatini–Cartan graph, explicit four-dimensional BF square, gluing, and the still-open generalized-corner extension. Sage independently checked the square incidence complex, total-differential mixed signs, factorial normalization, shifted-Poisson master equation, and a BF/CE nilpotency model. Infinite-dimensional smoothness, gravitational reduction, and general clean gluing remain explicit assumptions rather than verified theorems.

### 2. Quantum Anomalies of Tensionless Bosonic Strings — 2608.02987v1

**Codes:** `T2-celestial-carrollian`, `T2-model`, `T3-math`
**Technical relevance:** medium. **Author signal:** none.

Detailed note: [[Quantum Anomalies of Tensionless Bosonic Strings (2608.02987v1)]]

**Source claim and mechanism.** Four theories with the same gauge-fixed matter action retain different constraint and ghost complexes. Jacobi classification precedes vacuum-dependent contractions. In the induced vacuum a symmetric \(\alpha=0\) trace prescription removes the non-cocycle mismatch; in the flipped vacuum the conformal and Carroll-Weyl models have incompatible anomaly roots, ILST gives \(D=26\), and the hybrid model gives

D(\lambda)=\frac{2(6\lambda^2+6\lambda+14)}{3\lambda^2-1}.

The ILST appendix separately shows that worldsheet BRST nilpotency need not make the target special-conformal generator BRST closed. **Use:** an anomaly ledger with precise vacuum/regulator boundaries. **Why not high:** no new boundary/CPS structure, and the strongest “no critical dimension” statement is prescription dependent.
