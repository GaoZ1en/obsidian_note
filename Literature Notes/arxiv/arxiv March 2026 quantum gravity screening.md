# arXiv March 2026 quantum gravity screening

## Screening profile

This shortlist is tuned for a theoretical physics graduate student working on quantum gravity and already comfortable with covariant phase space. I prioritize papers involving boundaries, corners, edge modes, Noether charges, gravitational constraints, null infinity, spin foams, black hole entropy, holographic microstates, and mathematically structured approaches to gravitational path integrals or cosmological wavefunctions. The final selection is dominated by `hep-th` and `gr-qc`, with `math-ph` entering mostly through representation theory, coadjoint orbits, graph/coaction methods, Grassmannian structures, and geometric gauge formulations.

## High-priority reading

1. [At the Corner of Quantum and Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21941)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Develops a two-dimensional quantum corner symmetry framework, including representations, gluing, coadjoint orbits, moment maps, coherent states, and a semiclassical derivation of the horizon area law.
   - Why it is relevant: This is the most directly connected March item for covariant phase space, extended phase space, boundary/corner charges, and the quantum interpretation of gravitational edge degrees of freedom.

1. [Horizon Edge Partition Functions in $\Lambda>0$ Quantum Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20913)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Computes codimension-2 horizon edge spectra for gravity and higher-spin fields in de Sitter and Nariai backgrounds, revealing universal shift symmetries in one-loop partition functions.
   - Why it is relevant: It sits directly at the interface of horizon edge modes, de Sitter quantum gravity, one-loop thermodynamics, and symmetry breaking in gravitational partition functions.

1. [Peeling-violating coefficients in classical gravitational scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22681)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Derives exact peeling-violating Weyl coefficients at null infinity from matching at timelike/spatial infinity and gravitational wave tail formulas.
   - Why it is relevant: The paper connects null infinity, matching conditions, celestial holography, soft physics, and asymptotic gravitational data in a way that is close to charge and radiative phase-space questions.

1. [First-principle evolution Hamiltonian operator: derivation from ADM quantum constraints and quantum reference-frame conditions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20747)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Proposes a universal construction of an evolution Hamiltonian from quantum ADM constraints and quantum reference-frame conditions in Dirac quantum gravity.
   - Why it is relevant: It addresses the relation between constraints, relational observables, reference frames, and Schrodinger evolution in generally covariant quantum systems.

1. [Causal Structure for Generalized Spinfoams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22661)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Defines causal structures on arbitrary spin-foam 2-complexes and introduces a causal vertex amplitude for generalized EPRL spin foams.
   - Why it is relevant: It is a natural loop/spinfoam counterpart to continuum causality and semiclassical boundary data, with a concrete role for graph-theoretic and algebraic structures.

1. [Quantum gravity and matter fields in a general background gauge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23332)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Computes the one-loop gauge dependence of the effective action for quantum gravity coupled to matter and uses the DeWitt-Kallosh theorem to isolate the on-shell gauge-independent content.
   - Why it is relevant: It is useful background for gauge fixing, background-field methods, one-loop gravity, and the distinction between off-shell and on-shell information.

1. [The Resolved Elliptic Genus and the D1-D5 CFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18138)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Introduces a resolved elliptic genus for the D1-D5 CFT that resolves otherwise invisible BPS sectors and improves agreement with supergravity below the black-hole threshold.
   - Why it is relevant: It gives a new index-like diagnostic for black hole microstates in a canonical holographic system.

1. [Entanglement Entropy of Massive Scalar Fields: Mass Suppression, Violation of Universal mR Scaling, and Implications for Black Hole Thermodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24158)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Studies massive scalar entanglement in a spherical shell lattice model and shows that excited-state entropy depends on multiple infrared scales rather than only on $mR$.
   - Why it is relevant: It is a useful semiclassical-gravity input for matter entropy, generalized entropy, island formula intuition, and black-hole thermodynamics.

## Secondary candidates

1. [Towards Two-to-Two Scattering of Scalars in Asymptotically Safe Quantum Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10168)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Computes graviton-mediated scalar scattering with momentum-dependent scalar-graviton vertices from the functional renormalization group and studies the UV behavior of the cross section.

1. [The Vasiliev Grassmannian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24656)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Rewrites a de Sitter Vasiliev higher-spin scalar four-point function as an orthogonal Grassmannian integral with Veneziano-like singularity structure.

1. [A Graphical Coaction for FRW Wavefunction Coefficients](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25703)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Gives a graph-theoretic coaction for FRW wavefunction coefficients, organizing their differential equations and discontinuities at arbitrary multiplicity and loop order.

1. [Variations on a theme of MacDowell-Mansouri](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21930)
   - Categories: `math.DG`, `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Studies gauge-theoretic four-dimensional functionals inspired by MacDowell-Mansouri gravity and relates their critical points to constant scalar curvature almost-Kahler geometry.

1. [Fractal universe and quantum gravity made simple](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24593)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Presents a multifractional/fractal-spacetime quantum gravity proposal claimed to be perturbatively super-renormalizable and unitary, with comments on possible black-hole and gravitational-wave tests.

1. [Perturbative unitarity of fractional field theories and gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25709)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`-adjacent.
   - One-line summary: Analyzes perturbative unitarity in fractional kinetic theories motivated by multiscale quantum-gravity geometries and discusses complex-mass virtual sectors.

1. [Suppression of Trapped Surface Formation by Quantum Gravitational Effects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24729)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Argues that quantum fluctuations of a collapsing shell keep the expectation value of a horizon-order parameter from vanishing, obstructing apparent-horizon and trapped-surface formation.

1. [Wilson network expansion for four-point contact and exchange scalar Feynman diagrams in $AdS_2$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23080)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Expands AdS two-dimensional scalar contact and exchange diagrams into Wilson-line network matrix elements and recovers conformal-block decompositions near the boundary.

1. [Consistent Gauge Conditions for Dust-Shell Dynamics in Effective Quantum Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24168)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Develops a method for choosing gauges compatible with dust shells in effective quantum-gravity black-hole models and explains failures of common global gauge choices.

1. [The scalar-Maxwell-$\Lambda(x)$ system: Wormhole spacetimes without nonlinear electrodynamics in unimodular gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.30003)
    - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
    - One-line summary: Constructs exact scalar-Maxwell wormholes in unimodular gravity using a dynamical cosmological term instead of nonlinear electrodynamics.

## Suggested reading order

1. Start with [At the Corner of Quantum and Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21941), because it is closest to covariant phase space, extended phase space, and boundary/corner charges.
1. Read [Horizon Edge Partition Functions in $\Lambda>0$ Quantum Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20913) next, then compare its edge spectra with the corner-symmetry logic of the first paper.
1. Follow with [Peeling-violating coefficients in classical gravitational scattering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22681), which moves from finite boundaries and horizons to asymptotic null-boundary data.
1. Then read [First-principle evolution Hamiltonian operator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20747) and [Causal Structure for Generalized Spinfoams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22661) as two complementary approaches to constraints, relational evolution, and discrete covariant quantum gravity.
1. Use [Quantum gravity and matter fields in a general background gauge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23332) as technical background on gauge dependence and one-loop gravity.
1. Finish the high-priority set with [The Resolved Elliptic Genus and the D1-D5 CFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18138) and [Entanglement Entropy of Massive Scalar Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24158) for the black-hole microstate and generalized-entropy tracks.
