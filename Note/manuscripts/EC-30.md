# Twistor Lines, N=2 Gauge Actions, and a Scalar Amplitude

DRAFT — bounded independent review recorded.

A projective line can organize a gauge-theory amplitude, but incidence alone is not a string action. We construct the degree-one line/moduli geometry, use a known N=4 twistor-string and twistor-action reference, and derive a genuinely N=2 weighted gauge-field action with its required nonlocal line completion. A four-scalar contour then evaluates to the same nonzero number as an independent pure-N=2 Yang–Mills tree calculation. Three uses of N must be separated. The B-model reference has worldsheet N=(2,2) before its topological twist and a chosen spacetime N=4 gauge multiplet. ## 1. The Actual Degree-One Lines

Physical spacetime keeps $\eta=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,+,+)$, $c=\hbar=1$ and the book's spinor convention. The unprimed fundamental space is the positive-gamma-five block, while its covectors transform by $A^{-T}$. Use the dual incidence of [AST-14](<AST-14.md>) and [EC-21](<EC-21.md>):

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

Thus, in ordinary column notation, $\mu=y^T\lambda=i(X^T/L_0)\lambda$. The transpose is not optional. We work on the specified split open supertwistor space $U_N\subset\mathbb{CP}^{3|N}$ with body $\lambda\ne0$. Its coordinates are dimensionless; physical odd coordinates can be divided by $\sqrt{L_0}$. The complex chiral moduli $\mathbb C^{4|2N}$ are not the full real superspace of [AST-15](<AST-15.md>). In particular four complex even moduli are not two complex moduli or four real moduli without a separately imposed real slice.

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

On the open set $\det M\ne0$ its image is exactly a line in (1), with

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

Changing the source basis sends $(M,W,\Theta)$ to $(MG,WG,\Theta G)$ and leaves these matrices unchanged. This is the actual GL(2) quotient, not merely a subtraction of dimensions. For example,

Direct substitution reconstructs both columns, and the answer is unchanged by a nonsingular source-basis transformation. This example is complex; no Lorentzian reality condition is being smuggled into it.

Here Pi marks odd parity. The two sections1,z of each summand give the actual coefficients in (2). The graph construction supplies the local family in this split setting. It is not a theorem that an arbitrary curved spacetime or affine connection has such lines.

## 3. A Known N=4 Reference Action, and Why hCS Is Not Enough

Choose a rank-r complex bundle E, an invariant trace and a gauge group as input. Let $\mathcal A$ be an End(E)-valued body $(0,1)$ form, even in total Grassmann parity, of homogeneous weight zero on $U_4$. A convenient normalization of the holomorphic Chern–Simons action is

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

Assume boundary terms vanish, or work with supported variations in the chosen open domain. Its variation gives $\bar\partial\mathcal A+\mathcal A\wedge\mathcal A=0$. Complex gauge transformations act by $\mathcal A\mapsto h^{-1}\mathcal A h+h^{-1}\bar\partial h$.

This equation gives an integrable holomorphic bundle. The [AST-24](<AST-24.md>) Ward correspondence additionally requires holomorphic triviality on the relevant lines, appropriate splitting and reality data. In the book's chiral convention it describes the restricted sector $\varphi_{AB}=0$, or $*_L F=-iF$ on complexified Lorentzian two-forms. It is not unrestricted Yang–Mills theory. A nonzero compact-real gauge curvature cannot satisfy that one-chirality condition on an unchanged real Lorentzian slice.

The known N=4 twistor-string reference supplements the open B-model sector by degree-one D1 effects. A useful field-theory formulation encodes the needed completion as a line determinant [1,3]:

$$\begin{align}
S_4=S_{\rm hCS}+\tau I_4[\mathcal A],\qquad I_4=\int_{\mathcal C}d^{4|8}(y,\vartheta) \log\frac{\det\!\left[(\bar\partial+\mathcal A|_{L_{y,\vartheta}})_{E|_L\otimes\mathcal O(-1)}\right]} {(\det\bar\partial_{\mathcal O(-1)})^r}. \tag{7}
\end{align}$$

The determinant notation means the operator on the indicated twisted bundle. Work perturbatively near a bundle trivial on each chosen line. Since $H^0(\mathcal O(-1))=H^1(\mathcal O(-1))=0$, the reference line operator has no zero mode. Its inverse is the Cauchy kernel, proportional to $1/\langle\lambda_1\lambda_2\rangle$. Jumping lines or determinant zero modes lie outside this patch.

For present purposes the logarithm is its specified perturbative series $\operatorname{Tr}\log(1+G\mathcal A)$, with the coincident-point prescription/normal ordering fixed. The coefficient at order n is $(-1)^{n+1}/n$. We do not infer a nonperturbative, globally anomaly-free determinant measure from this notation. The bosonic cycle may be taken as the Lorentzian Fourier cycle $y=iX(x)/L_0$, initially with test functions and a chosen vacuum prescription. The twistor-action equivalence to perturbative N=4 Yang–Mills is a named framework input [3]. The original string reference and its closed conformal-supergravity sector are not thereby identical to a pure-SYM quantum field theory [4]. In particular hCS alone must not be advertised as the full Yang–Mills action.

## 4. A Genuine N=2 Weighted Action

$$\begin{align}

Integrating the two omitted odd coordinates in (6) gives an actual BF action. With the measure selecting $\chi^3\chi^4$,
