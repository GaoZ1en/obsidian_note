# Eleven-Dimensional Supergravity: Flux Geometry, Charges and a Circle

DRAFT — bounded mathematical review recorded.

Eleven-dimensional supergravity supplies a known low-energy theory, not a complete construction of M-theory. We evaluate two different systems: an electric Freund–Rubin product with its actual curvature, flux equation and membrane charge; and a circle reduction with a dynamical radius, normalized modes and brane/coupling calibration. The first fixes an AdS-to-sphere radius ratio. The second explains a standard M/IIA matching under explicitly imported string and brane data. Neither a spinor count nor either construction selects a Standard Model vacuum or proves ultraviolet completion.

The curvature and form conventions extend [AST-04](<AST-04.md>) and [AST-05](<AST-05.md>). Boundary variations retain the distinctions of [AST-11](<AST-11.md>) and [AST-19](<AST-19.md>). The four-dimensional spin dictionary and the limits of representation/constraint counting in [EC-02](<EC-02.md>), [EC-21](<EC-21.md>) and [EC-24](<EC-24.md>) are prerequisites, not proofs of an eleven-dimensional local theory. No unreviewed parallel string or holographic draft is assumed.

## 1. The Selected Low-Energy Action

Let the eleven-manifold be oriented, time-oriented and spin, with physical signature $(-,+,\ldots,+)$, one negative and ten positive directions. Use $c=\hbar=1$, a torsion-free metric Levi-Civita connection and the curvature convention

$$\begin{align}
R^P{}_{QMN}=\partial_M\Gamma^P{}_{NQ}-\partial_N\Gamma^P{}_{MQ} +\Gamma^P{}_{MR}\Gamma^R{}_{NQ}-\Gamma^P{}_{NR}\Gamma^R{}_{MQ}. \tag{1}
\end{align}$$

In this convention a round sphere has positive Ricci curvature and AdS has negative Ricci curvature. The bosonic fields are the metric and a real three-form potential $C_3$, defined on a gauge patch when necessary. Normalize

$$\begin{align}
F_4=dC_3,\qquad C_3=\frac1{3!}C_{MNP}\,dx^M\wedge dx^N\wedge dx^P,\qquad 2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}=(2\pi)^8\ell_p^9. \tag{2}
\end{align}$$

The analogous component convention for F has a factor $1/4!$. The coupling here is the conventional eleven-dimensional squared kappa, not an unconverted four-dimensional kappa or Barbero–Immirzi parameter. Take $\ell_p>0$. The leading action is

$$\begin{align}
S_{\rm bos}=\frac1{2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}} \int\left(\mathcal R\,*1-\frac12F_4\wedge*F_4 -\frac16C_3\wedge F_4\wedge F_4\right). \tag{3}
\end{align}$$

This is the standard bosonic sector of the known eleven-dimensional supergravity framework. The gravitino, its Pauli couplings and higher fermion terms are omitted by setting the fermionic field to zero. We use the known consistent bosonic truncation; we do not derive the complete supersymmetry transformation laws or prove their closure. No arbitrary independent nonmetric connection is added.

Fix the Hodge operation by the metric and orientation:

$$\begin{align}
\alpha_p\wedge*\beta_p =\frac1{p!}\alpha_{M_1\cdots M_p}\beta^{M_1\cdots M_p}\,\mathrm{vol}_{11}, \qquad **\alpha_p=(-1)^{p(11-p)+1}\alpha_p=-\alpha_p. \tag{4}
\end{align}$$

All indices on the second factor are genuinely raised. The Lorentzian sign is not obtained by taking an absolute value of a flux component. In an orthonormal coframe F has component units $L^{-1}$; as a differential form F and C have units $L^3$. Thus $*F$ and $C\wedge F$ have units $L^6$, and every term integrated in the action has units $L^9$, cancelled by $\kappa_{11}^{\,2}$.

## 2. Variations, the Chern–Simons Term and Charge Forms

Metric variation of the actual kinetic action, with covariant F components held fixed, gives

$$\begin{align}
R_{MN}-\frac12g_{MN}\mathcal R =\frac1{12}\left(F_{MPQR}F_N{}^{PQR}-\frac18g_{MN}F^2\right), \qquad F^2=F_{PQRS}F^{PQRS}. \tag{5}
\end{align}$$

Its eleven-dimensional trace is $\mathcal R=F^2/144$, so equivalently

$$\begin{align}
R_{MN}=\frac1{12}\left(F_{MPQR}F_N{}^{PQR}-\frac1{12}g_{MN}F^2\right). \tag{6}
\end{align}$$

The metric Euler–Lagrange expression and the trace were checked with xAct variation and contraction, not fixed by demanding that a desired radius be real. The Chern–Simons form term is metric independent and adds no stress term to this equation.

At fixed metric suppress the common action prefactor. Since $dF=0$ and $\delta F=d\delta C$, graded integration by parts gives

$$\begin{align}
\delta(C\wedge F\wedge F) =3\delta C\wedge F\wedge F-2d(C\wedge\delta C\wedge F). \tag{7}
\end{align}$$

The three-form degree, not fermionic statistics, supplies the signs here. For $\ell_C=-F\wedge*F/2-C\wedge F\wedge F/6$, one compatible potential is

$$\begin{align}
\delta\ell_C=-\delta C\wedge\mathcal E_C+d\Theta_C,\qquad \mathcal E_C=d*F+\frac12F\wedge F,\qquad \Theta_C=-\delta C\wedge*F+\frac13C\wedge\delta C\wedge F. \tag{8}
\end{align}$$

Thus the source-free form equations are $dF=0$ and $\mathcal E_C=0$. A nontrivial exterior-algebra check used a flat eleven-dimensional metric, real nonzero a,b and coordinates expressed in one fixed length unit:

$$\begin{align}
C=a x^0\,dx^{123}+b x^4\,dx^{567},\qquad \delta C=(x^0)^2(dx^{8\,9\,10}+dx^{123}), \tag{9}
\end{align}$$

where $dx^{ijk}$ denotes the ordered wedge. Here $F\wedge F=2ab\,dx^{01234567}$ is nonzero, the bare Chern–Simons density variation is $-2ab(x^0)^2\,dx^{0\cdots10}$, and the full variation identity has zero residual. Omitting the Chern–Simons bulk term leaves the nonzero residual $-ab(x^0)^2\,dx^{0\cdots10}$. This is an off-shell variational control, not an additional supergravity solution.

The displayed potential concerns C variation at fixed metric. A complete covariant phase-space calculation also requires the gravitational potential, allowed variations, boundary/corner prescriptions and gauge conditions. In particular this local potential does not by itself prove charge integrability, a positive physical Hilbert space or a boundary duality.

On a source-free patch define the leading seven-form

$$\begin{align}
P_7=*F_4+\frac12C_3\wedge F_4,\qquad dP_7=\mathcal E_C\quad(dF=0). \tag{10}
\end{align}$$

The Maxwell form $*F$ is gauge invariant but generally not closed on shell, because $d*F=-F\wedge F/2$. The Page form is closed on shell in the stated patch but depends on the potential. For a small transformation with globally defined parameter $\Lambda_2$, its change is $d(\Lambda_2\wedge F/2)$, so its integral on a closed seven-cycle is unchanged. For a closed shift $C\mapsto C+\Lambda_3$,

$$\begin{align}
\Delta P_7=\frac12\Lambda_3\wedge F_4. \tag{11}
\end{align}$$

A globally nonexact shift can change a Page integral. The local formula does not prove an unqualified integer change or solve the global differential-cohomology problem. Magnetic sources, gravitational corrections and the patching of C require additional terms/data. We will not identify these three notions—local equations, gauge-invariant Maxwell flux and quantized Page charge—as interchangeable.

## 3. First Evaluated Construction: an Electric Freund–Rubin Product

Take the universal cover of AdS4 with radius $L_A>0$ and a round S7 of radius $R_S>0$. Orient the product by

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{vol}_{11}=\mathrm{vol}_A\wedge\mathrm{vol}_S,\qquad F_4=f\,\mathrm{vol}_A,\qquad f\in\mathbb R\ \text{constant}. \tag{12}
\end{align}$$

The actual factor metrics used for the curvature check are a Poincare patch and stereographic sphere coordinates:

$$\begin{align}
ds_A^2=\frac{L_A^2}{z^2}(-dt^2+dx_1^2+dx_2^2+dz^2),\quad z>0, \qquad ds_S^2=\frac{4R_S^2}{(1+|u|^2)^2}\sum_{i=1}^7du_i^2. \tag{13}
\end{align}$$

The second metric is the round sphere with one coordinate pole omitted. Direct xAct curvature gives $R_{\mu\nu}=-3g_{\mu\nu}/L_A^2$ and $R_{mn}=6g_{mn}/R_S^2$, with scalar curvatures $-12/L_A^2$ and $42/R_S^2$. The global sphere Ricci residual has49 zero component entries; the external Ricci residual has16.

An explicit antisymmetric flux tensor gives

$$\begin{align}
F^2=-24f^2,\qquad F_{\mu PQR}F_\nu{}^{PQR}=-6f^2g_{\mu\nu},\qquad F_{mPQR}=0. \tag{14}
\end{align}$$

The metric equations then reduce to

$$\begin{align}
-\frac3{L_A^2}=-\frac{f^2}3,\qquad \frac6{R_S^2}=\frac{f^2}6, \qquad R_S=2L_A,\quad |f|=6/R_S. \tag{15}
\end{align}$$

The mixed equations vanish by the direct-product structure. Substituting the solved radii and the signed choice below gives16 external and49 internal zero residual entries in the field equations. There is no adjustable sign branch in the already specified action.

The Lorentzian Hodge definition fixes

$$\begin{align}
*\mathrm{vol}_A=-\mathrm{vol}_S,\qquad *F_4=-f\,\mathrm{vol}_S. \tag{16}
\end{align}$$

A coordinate potential is

$$\begin{align}
C_3=\frac{fL_A^4}{3z^3}\,dt\wedge dx_1\wedge dx_2, \qquad F_4=\frac{fL_A^4}{z^4}\,dt\wedge dx_1\wedge dx_2\wedge dz. \tag{17}
\end{align}$$

Taking its exterior derivative gives exactly the stated F, including the sign from moving dz through three factors. With the physical product metric the actual Hodge calculation gives $*F=-f\mathrm{vol}_S$, $dF=d*F=0$ and $F\wedge F=0$. Also $C\wedge F=0$ in this patch. The Bianchi and form equations are therefore verified, not inferred from incorrectly treating all coordinate components as constants.

### The Signed Membrane Charge and the Radius

Use the standard membrane and fivebrane tensions as quantum-framework inputs:

$$\begin{align}
T_{M2}=\frac1{(2\pi)^2\ell_p^3},\qquad T_{M5}=\frac1{(2\pi)^5\ell_p^6},\qquad 2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}T_{M2}=(2\pi\ell_p)^6,\qquad 2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}T_{M5}=(2\pi\ell_p)^3. \tag{18}
\end{align}$$

They obey $2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}T_{M2}T_{M5}=2\pi$, compatible with the standard unit Dirac charge normalization. This algebra does not derive that quantization law or the existence of either brane. Choose the electric orientation of a membrane coupling $+T_{M2}\int C_3$ and define its leading Page number on the positively oriented sphere by

$$\begin{align}
N=\frac1{2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}T_{M2}}\int_{S^7}P_7. \tag{19}
\end{align}$$

Here Page and Maxwell integrals agree: the pullback of F to S7 is zero. For N positive choose $f=-6/R_S$, not an absolute value inserted into the Hodge operation. The actual angular integral is

$$\begin{align}
\operatorname{Vol}(S^7_{\rm unit}) =2\pi\prod_{k=1}^6\int_0^\pi\sin^k\theta\,d\theta=\frac{\pi^4}3. \tag{20}
\end{align}$$

Consequently

$$\begin{align}
N=\frac{(-f)R_S^7(\pi^4/3)}{(2\pi\ell_p)^6} =\frac{R_S^6}{32\pi^2\ell_p^6},\qquad R_S^6=32\pi^2N\ell_p^6,\qquad L_A=R_S/2. \tag{21}
\end{align}$$

The local classical field equations allowed continuous f. Treating this Page number as the chosen integer membrane charge is an additional quantum/global input. The formula is the leading two-derivative radius calibration for this smooth background, not a computation of every finite-N correction.

For comparison, the known shifted magnetic-flux rule on a spin background has the form

$$\begin{align}
\left[\frac{F_4}{(2\pi\ell_p)^3}\right]-\frac{p_1(TM)}4
 \ \in H^4(M,\mathbb Z) \tag{22}
\end{align}$$

in the standard integral Pontryagin-class normalization. This condition concerns the real image of an integral class; torsion and differential-cohomology data are additional. It is not a statement that every unshifted four-period is an integer. Higher-derivative terms including a gravitational $C_3\wedge X_8$ term also modify equations and induced electric charges. Their coefficient and corrected charge prescription are not evaluated here. The bare $P_7$ above is not asserted as the universal quantum charge form on every compactification.

### What Large N Controls

With $N\gg1$, $R_S/\ell_p=(32\pi^2N)^{1/6}$ is large. This supplies a weak-curvature regime for the leading eleven-dimensional geometry; it does not remove all quantum, higher-derivative or global questions. It does not give an AdS/KK scale separation either, since $L_A/R_S=1/2$ stays fixed.

A diagnostic minimally coupled massless scalar—not an additional elementary field of eleven-dimensional supergravity—makes the latter point explicit. On the round sphere the polar operator on a regular function is $R_S^{-2}\sin^{-6}\chi\,\partial_\chi(\sin^6\chi\,\partial_\chi)$. The checked functions $\cos\chi$ and $\cos^2\chi-1/8$ have eigenvalues $7/R_S^2$ and $16/R_S^2$ for the positive operator $-\Delta_{S^7}$. Their separated probe masses give $m^2L_A^2=7/4,4$, independent of N. These are not the full coupled metric/three-form Kaluza–Klein spectrum. No universal graviton mass tower, finite AdS on-shell action, holographic free energy or boundary quantum theory is calculated by this probe.

In the known full fermionic theory the standard round product preserves32 real supercharges on the universal cover with the standard spin structure. That is an imported Killing-spinor theorem, not a consequence of our bosonic equations or a multiplication of spinor dimensions. We do not solve the supercovariant Killing-spinor equation here.

## 4. Second Evaluated Construction: Reducing a Circle with a Radius Field

Use a separate background, not a quotient of the preceding sphere. Let $y\sim y+2\pi R_{11}$, with $R_{11}>0$ the asymptotic proper circle radius. Choose a periodic spatial spin structure for the conventional supersymmetric interpretation, no twist, y-independent fields, and set the KK vector and $C_3$ to zero in the checked sector. Write

$$\begin{align}
ds_{11}^2=e^{-2\varphi/3}g^{(s)}_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu +e^{4\varphi/3}dy^2,\qquad \varphi_\infty=0. \tag{23}
\end{align}$$

The ten-dimensional metric $g^{(s)}$ and the eleven-dimensional base metric agree asymptotically. This fixes the length and energy calibration used below. The symbol $g_s$ will instead denote a string coupling, not this metric. The local proper radius is $R_{11}e^{2\varphi/3}$.

The warped-product and conformal curvature identities give

$$\begin{align}
\sqrt{-g_{11}}=e^{-8\varphi/3}\sqrt{-g^{(s)}},\qquad \mathcal R_{11}=e^{2\varphi/3} \left(\mathcal R_s+\frac{14}3\Box_s\varphi -\frac{16}3(\nabla_s\varphi)^2\right). \tag{24}
\end{align}$$

For example first remove the conformal factor: the remaining circle warp has scalar curvature $\mathcal R_s-2\Box_s\varphi-2(\nabla_s\varphi)^2$ and scalar Laplacian $\widetilde\Box\varphi=\Box_s\varphi+(\nabla_s\varphi)^2$. Applying the eleven-dimensional conformal formula with exponent $-\varphi/3$ yields the coefficients14/3 and minus16/3 above.

Dedicated xAct computations on a flat ten-dimensional base with an arbitrary spacelike profile $\varphi(x)$ and separately an arbitrary timelike profile $\varphi(t)$ give exactly these two signs. The actual scalar, volume and density boundary-term residuals vanish in each test. These are off-shell reduction identities, not a claim that arbitrary dilaton profiles solve eleven-dimensional Einstein equations. For constant dilaton the actual121 Ricci entries vanish, giving the source-free flat product vacuum.

The crucial integration by parts retains its surface term:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sqrt{-g_{11}}\mathcal R_{11}
={}&\sqrt{-g^{(s)}}e^{-2\varphi}
       \left(\mathcal R_s+4(\nabla_s\varphi)^2\right)\\
&+\frac{14}3\partial_\mu
 \left(\sqrt{-g^{(s)}}e^{-2\varphi}\nabla_s^\mu\varphi\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{25}
$$

Only under compact support/falloff or an explicitly matched boundary prescription may the displayed divergence be omitted. Keeping the bulk term and integrating the actual circle gives

$$\begin{align}
S_{10,\rm grav}=\frac1{2\kappa_{10,\rm phys}^{\,2}} \int d^{10}x\sqrt{-g^{(s)}}e^{-2\varphi} (\mathcal R_s+4(\nabla_s\varphi)^2),\qquad 2\kappa_{10,\rm phys}^{\,2}=\frac{2\kappa_{11}^{\,2}}{2\pi R_{11}}. \tag{26}

This is a metric/dilaton sector, not a derivation of the complete IIA action with all RR/NS fields and fermions. Nor is the string-frame plus4 coefficient a scalar ghost: using $g^{(E)}=e^{-\varphi/2}g^{(s)}$ gives the Einstein-frame bulk terms $\mathcal R_E-(\nabla_E\varphi)^2/2$, with its own total derivative kept in a matched boundary treatment. The coefficient minus one half was explicitly checked.

The circle modes, in the asymptotic flat vacuum, have

$$\begin{align}
f_n(y)=\frac{e^{iny/R_{11}}}{\sqrt{2\pi R_{11}}},\qquad -\partial_y^2f_n=\frac{n^2}{R_{11}^2}f_n,\qquad n\in\mathbb Z. \tag{27}
\end{align}$$

The actual Gram matrix for n from minus2 through2 is the identity. A positive-energy n=2 rest profile $e^{-2it/R_{11}}f_2(y)$ satisfies the flat massless wave equation. Thus a free massless separated profile has ten-dimensional mass $|n|/R_{11}$. This checks a mode equation and normalization, not the full constrained gravity multiplet. For a real field the coefficients of opposite n are conjugate; these Fourier labels are not independent generations.

## 6. Deriving the Eleven-Dimensional Spin Convention

The eleven-dimensional Clifford choice must not be copied from the four-dimensional negative-Clifford book matrices. We use

$$\begin{align}
\{\Gamma^a,\Gamma^b\}=2\eta^{ab}I_{32},\qquad \eta=\operatorname{diag}(-,+,\ldots,+). \tag{32}
\end{align}$$

This plus-Clifford convention admits an explicit real32 Majorana representation. To construct it, for j=1 through5 let

$$\begin{align}
s_{2j-1}=\sigma_3^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes\sigma_1\otimes I^{\otimes(5-j)},\qquad s_{2j}=\sigma_3^{\otimes(j-1)}\otimes\sigma_2\otimes I^{\otimes(5-j)}, \qquad \Gamma^i=s_i,\quad \Gamma^0=s_1\cdots s_{10}. \tag{33}
\end{align}$$

The ten spatial matrices square to plus one and anticommute; their product squares to minus one. In this initial complex basis define

$$\begin{align}
B=s_1s_3s_5s_7s_9,\qquad J=BK,\qquad B\overline B=I,\quad B\overline{\Gamma^a}B^{-1}=\Gamma^a. \tag{34}
\end{align}$$

K is entrywise conjugation. Here B is a real symmetric involution. Put $P_{B,\pm}=(I\pm B)/2$ and $U=P_{B,+}+iP_{B,-}$. Then $U^\dagger\Gamma^aU$ are real matrices, and J becomes K in that basis. A Majorana spinor is a fixed vector with32 real components, not a complex dimension multiplied by two after an unrelated projection.

In the real basis the time gamma is antisymmetric and the ten spatial gammas symmetric. With the spinor matrix $\mathsf C=\Gamma^0$, $\mathsf C^T=-\mathsf C$ and every $\mathsf C\Gamma^a$ is symmetric. The complete plus-Clifford test has123904 zero matrix entries; the real-structure, basis, transpose and charge-conjugation residuals vanish. The odd-dimensional volume is

$$\begin{align}
\Gamma^0\Gamma^1\cdots\Gamma^{10}=-I_{32}. \tag{35}
\end{align}$$

It is central, not an independent chirality projector.

To bridge to the book, restrict to a four-dimensional Lorentz subspace and set

$$\begin{align}
\gamma_{\rm book}^{\mu}=-i\Gamma^{\mu},\qquad \{\gamma_{\rm book}^{\mu},\gamma_{\rm book}^{\nu}\}=-2\eta^{\mu\nu},\qquad \gamma_{5,\rm book}=i\Gamma^0\Gamma^1\Gamma^2\Gamma^3. \tag{36}
\end{align}$$

The complex restricted module is eight Dirac copies; a constant basis change recovers the book's diagonal gamma-five and Lorentz action. The fundamental unprimed block remains physical S-plus, not silently a left block. In the current real11D basis the book gammas are purely imaginary, and K flips the sign of the four-dimensional gamma-five. The checked chirality square is one and its trace zero. Majorana reality therefore relates the opposite four-dimensional chirality sectors; the multiplicity is not a family count. The same phase conversion sends the book's spin-connection expression $-\omega_{ab}\gamma_{\rm book}^{ab}/4$ to $+\omega_{ab}\Gamma^{ab}/4$. One cannot declare all negative-Clifford eleven-dimensional matrices to have the same entrywise reality while ignoring this change.

A separate little-group check for free massless fields uses Spin9. ## 7. What Is and Is Not Established

The first construction solves the stated leading bosonic equations with a signed Lorentzian electric flux and calculates its leading membrane charge/radius relation. The second derives and checks a circle metric/dilaton action, its boundary divergence, normalized mode profiles and consistent coupling/tension calibration after choosing the IIA interpretation. Their regimes and extra inputs are different; $R_S$ and $R_{11}$ are not the same radius.

The eleven-dimensional action is a nonrenormalizable low-energy effective description. Its higher-derivative and loop terms, global flux/charge refinements, anomalies and quantum measures are not supplied by local tensor identities. Large N controls weak curvature of the chosen flux background, not an all-energy theorem. A classically consistent truncation, a parametrically heavy KK tower, a supersymmetric vacuum and a complete quantum duality are also separate assertions. In particular the locked AdS/sphere radii alone do not prove a four-dimensional scale-separated effective model or any nonlinear consistent-truncation theorem.

No full interacting IIA reduction, all supercovariant/brane Killing-spinor projectors, exact finite-N background, universal U-duality charge spectrum, complete gravity KK spectrum or holographic observable has been derived here. The standard brane tensions, shifted flux rule, M/IIA identifications and stated supersymmetry theorem are framework inputs. Dimensions, matching ratios and gamma-matrix squares alone are not proofs of any of them.

Eleven-dimensional geometry does not specify a unique internal compactification, Standard Model gauge representations, chiral zero modes, three generations, moduli stabilization or their couplings. Those would need additional geometry, flux/source and global/quantum data with their own checks. These explicit effective-theory calculations are useful controls within known supergravity; they are not a complete construction of M-theory.
