# String Theory Reference

## Core Setup

The Polyakov action organizes the bosonic string as a two-dimensional worldsheet field theory with constraints. In conformal gauge, the residual constraints become Virasoro constraints.

For the bosonic string, quantum consistency requires

$$\begin{align}
D=26.
\end{align}$$

Equivalently, the matter central charge cancels the $bc$ ghost central charge:

$$\begin{align}
c_X+c_{bc}=D-26=0.
\end{align}$$

## Physical-State Logic

Covariant quantization keeps Lorentz covariance manifest but introduces negative-norm time-like excitations. Physical states are selected by Virasoro constraints or BRST cohomology.

Light-cone quantization removes unphysical degrees of freedom explicitly but hides Lorentz covariance.

DDF operators bridge the two descriptions: they are covariant operators that commute with the Virasoro constraints and reproduce the transverse light-cone oscillator algebra.

## Worldsheet And Spectrum Data

The worldsheet description of a string spectrum involves:

- worldsheet coordinates, periodicity, and boundary conditions;
- mode expansions with left/right convention;
- canonical brackets or oscillator algebra;
- zero-point energy in each sector;
- Virasoro, super-Virasoro, or BRST physical-state conditions;
- GSO or Chan-Paton projections;
- spacetime little-group representations.

## CFT Ingredients

Standard CFT ingredients:

- primary fields and descendants;
- OPE-to-mode-algebra conversion;
- state-operator correspondence;
- Virasoro and super-Virasoro algebras;
- free boson and free fermion OPEs;
- bosonization dictionaries;
- $bc$ and $\beta\gamma$ ghost systems.

## Ghost Systems And DDF Operators

Covariant quantization contains:

- matter central charge;
- the appropriate ghost system;
- BRST charge and normal ordering;
- nilpotency and physical cohomology;
- DDF operators as a bridge to light-cone states.

## T-Duality And Branes

For a closed string on a circle, momenta and winding are exchanged under T-duality:

$$\begin{align}
R\longleftrightarrow \frac{\alpha'}{R}.
\end{align}$$

For open strings, T-duality changes Neumann and Dirichlet boundary conditions and gives the D-brane interpretation.

The compactified theory also contains momentum number, winding number, left/right momenta, and level matching.

## Five Ten-Dimensional Superstring Theories

The standard consistent ten-dimensional superstring theories are:

- Type IIA;
- Type IIB;
- Type I;
- heterotic $SO(32)$;
- heterotic $E_8\times E_8$.

The RNS construction organizes the NS/R sectors and GSO projections; the Green-Schwarz formalism makes spacetime supersymmetry more direct.
