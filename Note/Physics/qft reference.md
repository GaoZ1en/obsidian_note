# QFT Reference

## Conventions

Metric signature, Fourier transform, propagator $i\epsilon$, and vertex momentum-flow conventions determine the signs of many QFT formulas.

## Core Structures

Durable structures:

- canonical scalar quantization and propagator definitions;
- LSZ reduction logic;
- Dirac spinor sums and gamma-matrix trace technology;
- QED, scalar QED, and vector QED Feynman rules;
- Mandelstam kinematics for $2\to2$ scattering;
- Feynman parameterization and Wick rotation;
- dimensional-regularization master integrals;
- Ward-identity sanity checks.

## Field Dimensions

In four dimensions with $\hbar=c=1$,

$$\begin{align}
[\phi]=1,\qquad [\psi]=\frac32,\qquad [A_\mu]=1,\qquad [\partial_\mu]=1.
\end{align}$$

This agrees with the gauge-theory power-counting note.

## Loop Integrals And Traces

One-loop expressions are organized by:

- Feynman parameterization of denominators;
- loop-momentum shifts compatible with the regulator;
- Wick rotation with fixed metric convention;
- tensor reduction to scalar integrals;
- divergent and finite pieces in dimensional regularization;
- local counterterms matching the divergent part.

Spin sums and traces are organized by:

- amplitudes before spin sums;
- final-state sums and initial-state averages;
- spinor-chain conversion to traces;
- Ward identities from replacing polarization vectors by momenta.

## Sign-Sensitive Items

The following structures are convention-sensitive:

- Wick rotation signs;
- fermion propagator signs;
- gamma-trace identities tied to metric signature;
- scalar-QED and vector-QED vertex signs;
- CPT tables for bilinears;
- all formulae involving epsilon tensors.
