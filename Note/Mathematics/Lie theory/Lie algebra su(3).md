# combining two $\displaystyle{\mathfrak{sl}(2)}$-algebras

the generators of the isospin $\displaystyle{\mathfrak{sl}(2)}$-algebra will be denoted by $\displaystyle{E_{\pm}^{1}}$ and $\displaystyle{H^{1}}$ so that

$$\tag{2.1}
\begin{align}
[H^{1},E^{1}_{\pm}] & =\pm2E^{1}_{\pm}, & [E^{1}_{+},E^{1}_{-}]=H^{1}
\end{align}
$$
