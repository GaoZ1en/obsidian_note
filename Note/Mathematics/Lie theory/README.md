# Lie Theory

This directory is being rebuilt as an understanding-first Lie theory workspace. The older notes in this folder are preserved as legacy source material; they were mostly copied from *Symmetries, Lie Algebras and Representations* by Fuchs and Schweigert and should not be treated as the main learning path.

The new notes emphasize:

1. concrete structures before abstractions;
1. conventions before formulas;
1. structural meaning before theorem lists.

## Current v2 Notes

- `SU2 SO3 and rotations.md`: the entry point through rotations, the double cover, weights, spin, and the $A_1$ root system.
- `SU3 roots weights and representations.md`: the first rank-two model, with roots, weights, Dynkin labels, dimensions, and basic tensor products.
- `Cartan-Weyl structure.md`: the relation between Cartan subalgebras, roots, coroots, positive systems, and highest weights.
- `Root systems and representations.md`: recognition tables for $A,B,C,D$ and exceptional types, plus Weyl dimensions and tensor-product data.

## Knowledge Order

1. `SU2 SO3 and rotations.md`: global groups, local Lie algebras, spin, and the $A_1$ root system.
1. `SU3 roots weights and representations.md`: rank-two geometry and the first nontrivial tensor products.
1. `Cartan-Weyl structure.md`: the general language behind roots, coroots, Cartan matrices, and highest weights.
1. `Root systems and representations.md`: classification data and representation dimensions.

## Legacy Notes

- `formalization.md`: basic definitions of algebraic structures and Lie algebras.
- `Cartan-Weyl basis.md`: copied exposition on roots, Killing form, Cartan-Weyl bases, and root strings.
- `simple and affine Lie algebras.md`: copied classification notes.
- `basic examples.md`: older physics examples.
- `Lie algebra su(3).md`: incomplete old SU(3) scratch note.

Use the legacy notes as raw references, not as polished explanations.
