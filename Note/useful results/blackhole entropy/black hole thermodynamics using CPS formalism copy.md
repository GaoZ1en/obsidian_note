# general discussion

consider a Lagrangian $\displaystyle{n}$-form $\displaystyle{\mathbf{L}[\phi]}$. take a variation

$$\tag{1.1}\begin{align}
\delta \mathbf{L}[\phi] & =\mathbf{E}[\phi]\delta \phi+\mathrm{d}\Theta[\phi,\delta \phi]
\end{align}$$

the symplectic form is defined as

$$\tag{1.2}\begin{align}
\omega & =\int _{\Sigma}\delta \Theta
\end{align}$$

which satisfies

$$\tag{1.3}\begin{align}
\omega|_{\Sigma _{f}} & =\omega|_{\Sigma _{i}} \\
\delta \omega & =0
\end{align}$$

since

$$\tag{1.4}\begin{align}
\omega|_{\Sigma _{f}}-\omega _{\Sigma _{i}} & =\delta \int _{\partial M}\Theta \\
 & =\int _{M}\delta ^{2} \mathbf{L}-\delta\mathbf{E}\wedge\delta \phi \\
 & \approx 0 \\
\delta \omega & =\int _{\Sigma}\delta ^{2}\Theta=0
\end{align}$$

consider a diffeomorphism generated by a vector $\displaystyle{\xi}$

$$\tag{1.5}\begin{align}
X_{\xi} & =\int \varepsilon \mathcal{L}_{\xi}\phi \frac{\delta}{\delta \phi}
\end{align}$$

act on the Lagrangian

$$\tag{1.6}\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \mathbf{L} & =\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})
\end{align}$$

(here we assume that the Lagrangian $\displaystyle{L}$ is covariant) then we have the Noether charge

$$\tag{1.7}\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =\int _{\Sigma}(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})
\end{align}$$

we have the Noether theorem

$$\tag{1.8}\begin{align}
\delta H_{\xi} & \approx-X_{\xi}\cdot \omega
\end{align}$$

since

$$\tag{1.9}\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\delta\mathbf{L} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \delta ^{2} \mathbf{L}+\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \mathbf{L}) \\
 & =\mathrm{d}(\delta(\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})) \\
 & =\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\left(\mathbf{E}\delta \phi+\mathrm{d}\Theta\right) \\
 & =X_{\xi}\cdot (\delta E\wedge \delta \phi)+\delta(E(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \phi))+\mathrm{d}(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta+\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta)) \\
 & =(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta E)\delta \phi+\mathrm{d}(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta)+\mathrm{d}(\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta)) \\
\implies \delta H_{\xi} & =\delta \int _{\Sigma}(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L}) \\
 & \approx -X_{\xi}\int _{\Sigma}\delta \Theta=-X_{\xi}\cdot \omega
\end{align}$$

we have

$$\tag{1.10}\begin{align}
H_{\xi}|_{\Sigma _{f}} & =H_{\xi}|_{\Sigma _{i}}
\end{align}$$

then we can write

$$\tag{1.11}\begin{align}
J_{\xi} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L} \\
 & =\mathrm{d}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi}+\mathbf{C}_{\xi}
\end{align}$$

then

$$\tag{1.12}\begin{align}
\delta H_{\xi} & =\delta\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi}+\mathbf{C}_{\xi} \\
 & \approx\int _{\Sigma}\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L}) \\
 & =\int _{\Sigma}\left(\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\Theta-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta-\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-\xi \cdot \delta \mathbf{L}\right) \\
 & =\int _{\Sigma}\left(\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\Theta+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta-\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}-(\xi \cdot \mathbf{E})\delta \phi-\xi \cdot \mathrm{d}\Theta\right) \\
 & \approx\int _{\Sigma}\left(-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta+\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \Theta)+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta-\delta \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}\right) \\
 & =\int _{\Sigma}\left(-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta+\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \Theta)+\mathrm{d}\mathbf{Q}_{\delta \xi}+\mathbf{C}_{\delta \xi}\right) \\
\implies \int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\mathbf{Q}_{\delta \xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta) & \approx \int _{\Sigma}\left(-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta-\delta \mathbf{C}_{\xi}+\mathbf{C}_{\delta \xi}\right)
\end{align}$$

here we use

$$\tag{1.13}\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\vartheta & =\mathcal{L}_{\xi}\vartheta+X_{\delta \xi}\cdot \Theta
\end{align}$$

for one-form on the configuration space. assume that $\displaystyle{\xi}$ does not depend on $\displaystyle{\phi}$, we got the usual formula

$$\begin{align}
\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right) & \approx \int _{\Sigma}\left(-X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \Theta-\delta \mathbf{C}_{\xi}\right) \\
\implies \delta H_{\xi} & \approx \int _{\partial \Sigma}\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta+\int _{\Sigma}\delta \mathbf{C}_{\xi}
\end{align}$$

if we choose $\displaystyle{\xi ^{a}=t^{a}+\Omega _{H}\varphi ^{a}}$ as the horizon Killing vector, where $\displaystyle{t^{a}}$ is the timelike Killing field, $\displaystyle{\varphi ^{a}}$ is the axial Killing field, and $\displaystyle{\Omega _{H}}$ is the angular velocity of the horizon.

$$\begin{align}

Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}(\nabla ^{\mu}\xi ^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}\xi ^{\mu}) \\

$$\begin{align}
\int _{B}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right)& =\int _{\infty}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right)
\end{align}$$

this is the first law of black hole thermodynamics. the black hole entropy is defined as Noether charge corresponding to the horizon Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi}$

$$\begin{align}
S=\frac{2\pi}{\kappa}\int _{\Sigma}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi}
\end{align}$$

## BTZ thermodynamics using covariant phase space formalism

for BTZ black hole, the metric is

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =-N^{2}\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}r^{2}}{N^{2}}+r^{2}(\mathrm{d}\phi+N_{\phi}\mathrm{d}t)^{2} \\
N^{2} & =-M+\frac{r^{2}}{\ell ^{2}}+\frac{J^{2}}{4r^{2}} \\
N_{\phi} & =-\frac{J}{2r^{2}}
\end{align}$$

the horizon Killing vector is

$$\begin{align}
\xi & =t^{a}+\Omega _{H}\varphi ^{a} \\
\Omega _{H} & =\frac{J}{2r_{+}^{2}}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{r_{+}}$ is the outer horizon radius.

### Noether charge calculation

from the general formula, the Noether current density is

$$\begin{align}
J^{\mu}_{\xi} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \theta ^{\mu}-\xi ^{\mu}\mathcal{L} \\
 & =\nabla _{\nu}Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu}+C_{\xi}^{\mu}
\end{align}$$

where the Noether charge density is

$$\begin{align}
Q_{\xi}^{\mu \nu} & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}(\nabla ^{\mu}\xi ^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}\xi ^{\mu})
\end{align}$$

for the horizon Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi ^{a}=t^{a}+\Omega _{H}\varphi ^{a}}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
\nabla ^{\mu}\xi ^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}\xi ^{\mu} & =\nabla ^{\mu}t^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}t^{\mu}+\Omega _{H}(\nabla ^{\mu}\varphi ^{\nu}-\nabla ^{\nu}\varphi ^{\mu})
\end{align}$$

### Mass and angular momentum

the mass is calculated as the Noether charge associated with time translation $\displaystyle{t^{a}}$

$$\begin{align}
M & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}\oint _{S^{1}}(\nabla ^{r}t^{\phi}-\nabla ^{\phi}t^{r})\sqrt{ \gamma }\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}\oint _{S^{1}}\nabla ^{r}t^{\phi}r\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{1}{8G}\int _{0}^{2\pi}\frac{1}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r^{2}N_{\phi})\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{1}{8G}\int _{0}^{2\pi}\frac{1}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( -\frac{J}{2} \right)\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{r_{+}^{2}+r_{-}^{2}}{8G\ell ^{2}}
\end{align}$$

the angular momentum is

$$\begin{align}
J & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}\oint _{S^{1}}(\nabla ^{r}\varphi ^{\phi}-\nabla ^{\phi}\varphi ^{r})\sqrt{ \gamma }\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{1}{16\pi G}\oint _{S^{1}}\nabla ^{r}\varphi ^{\phi}r\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{1}{8G}\int _{0}^{2\pi}\frac{1}{r^{2}}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(r^{2})\mathrm{d}\phi \\
 & =\frac{r_{+}r_{-}}{4G\ell}
\end{align}$$

### Entropy calculation

the entropy is calculated using the Noether charge corresponding to the horizon Killing vector at the horizon

$$\begin{align}
S & =\frac{2\pi}{\kappa}\int _{\text{horizon}}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi} \\
 & =\frac{2\pi}{\kappa}\frac{1}{16\pi G}\oint _{S^{1}}(\nabla ^{r}\xi ^{\phi}-\nabla ^{\phi}\xi ^{r})\sqrt{ \gamma }\mathrm{d}\phi
\end{align}$$

at the horizon, $\displaystyle{\xi ^{r}=0}$ and $\displaystyle{\xi ^{\phi}=\Omega _{H}}$, so

$$\begin{align}
\nabla ^{r}\xi ^{\phi} & =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ -g }}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(\sqrt{ -g }g^{r\mu}\xi _{\mu}) \\
 & =\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(rg^{r\phi}\xi _{\phi}) \\
 & =\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( r\frac{N_{\phi}}{N^{2}}\Omega _{H}r^{2} \right) \\
 & =\frac{\Omega _{H}}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( \frac{r^{3}N_{\phi}}{N^{2}} \right)
\end{align}$$

at the horizon where $\displaystyle{N^{2}=0}$, this calculation becomes delicate. we use the fact that $\displaystyle{\xi}$ is the horizon generator

$$\begin{align}
S & =\frac{2\pi r_{+}}{4G} \\
 & =\frac{\pi r_{+}}{2G}
\end{align}$$

### Temperature calculation

the surface gravity is

$$\begin{align}
\kappa & =\frac{1}{2}\frac{\mathrm{d}N^{2}}{\mathrm{d}r}\bigg|_{r=r_{+}} \\
 & =\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{2r}{\ell ^{2}}-\frac{J^{2}}{2r^{3}} \right)\bigg|_{r=r_{+}} \\
 & =\frac{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}{2\ell ^{2}r_{+}}
\end{align}$$

the Hawking temperature is

$$\begin{align}
T_{H} & =\frac{\kappa}{2\pi} \\
 & =\frac{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}{4\pi\ell ^{2}r_{+}}
\end{align}$$

### First law derivation

now we apply the covariant phase space formalism. for the horizon Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi ^{a}=t^{a}+\Omega _{H}\varphi ^{a}}$, we have $\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}_{\xi}g_{\mu \nu}=0}$ on the background, so

$$\begin{align}
0 & =\delta H_{\xi} \\
 & =\int _{B}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right)-\int _{\infty}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right)
\end{align}$$

at spatial infinity, we identify

$$\begin{align}
\int _{\infty}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right) & =\delta M-\Omega _{H}\delta J
\end{align}$$

at the black hole horizon, using the fact that $\displaystyle{\xi}$ is tangent to the horizon

$$\begin{align}
\int _{B}\left(\delta \mathbf{Q}_{\xi}-\xi \cdot \Theta\right) & =\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\delta S
\end{align}$$

where we used the relation

$$\begin{align}
\delta S & =\frac{2\pi}{\kappa}\delta\int _{\text{horizon}}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi}
\end{align}$$

therefore, we obtain the first law of black hole thermodynamics

$$\begin{align}
\boxed{\delta M=T_{H}\delta S+\Omega _{H}\delta J}
\end{align}$$

### Verification

we can verify this explicitly. from the relations

$$\begin{align}
r_{+}^{2} & =\frac{M\ell ^{2}}{2}\left( 1+\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} } \right) \\
r_{-}^{2} & =\frac{M\ell ^{2}}{2}\left( 1-\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} } \right)
\end{align}$$

we have

$$\begin{align}
\delta r_{+} & =\frac{\ell ^{2}}{2r_{+}}\left( 1+\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} }} \right)\delta M-\frac{J}{2Mr_{+}\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} }}\delta J \\
\delta r_{-} & =\frac{\ell ^{2}}{2r_{-}}\left( 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} }} \right)\delta M+\frac{J}{2Mr_{-}\sqrt{ 1-\frac{J^{2}}{M^{2}\ell ^{2}} }}\delta J
\end{align}$$

then

$$\begin{align}
\delta S & =\frac{\pi}{2G}\delta r_{+} \\
T_{H}\delta S & =\frac{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}{4\pi\ell ^{2}r_{+}}\frac{\pi}{2G}\delta r_{+} \\
 & =\frac{r_{+}^{2}-r_{-}^{2}}{8G\ell ^{2}}\delta r_{+}
\end{align}$$

and

$$\begin{align}
\Omega _{H}\delta J & =\frac{J}{2r_{+}^{2}}\frac{1}{4G\ell}(\delta r_{+}r_{-}+r_{+}\delta r_{-}) \\
 & =\frac{J}{8G\ell r_{+}^{2}}(r_{-}\delta r_{+}+r_{+}\delta r_{-})
\end{align}$$

by direct computation using the expressions for $\displaystyle{\delta r_{\pm}}$, one can verify that

$$\begin{align}
\delta M & = T_{H}\delta S+\Omega _{H}\delta J
\end{align}$$

### Physical interpretation

the first law expresses the fact that the change in mass equals the sum of:
1. thermal contribution $\displaystyle{T_{H}\delta S}$ from entropy change
2. rotational contribution $\displaystyle{\Omega _{H}\delta J}$ from angular momentum change

this is a direct consequence of the covariant phase space formalism applied to the BTZ black hole geometry.
