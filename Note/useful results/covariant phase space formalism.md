### Yang-Mills

$$\begin{align}
S & =-\frac{1}{4}\int _{M} \mathrm{d}^{4}x\sqrt{ -g }F^{a}_{\mu \nu}F_{a}^{\mu \nu}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{F^{a}_{\mu \nu}=\nabla _{\mu}A^{a}_{\nu}-\nabla _{\nu}A^{a}_{\mu}+f^{a}_{~bc}A^{b}_{\mu}A^{c}_{\nu}}$, and $\displaystyle{f^{a}_{~bc}}$ is the structure constant of the gauge group. the Lie algebra indices are raised and lowered with the Killing metric $\displaystyle{g_{ab}=-\frac{1}{2}f^{c}_{~ad}f^{d}_{~bc}}$. take a variation of the action

$$\begin{align}
\delta S & =-\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{4}x\sqrt{ -g }F^{\mu \nu}_{a}(\nabla _{\mu}\delta A^{a}_{\nu}+f^{a}_{~bc}A^{b}_{\mu}\delta A^{c}_{\nu}) \\
 & =\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{4}x\sqrt{ -g }(\nabla _{\mu}F^{\mu \nu}_{a}-F^{\mu \nu}_{c}f^{c}_{~})\delta A^{a}_{\nu}
\end{align}$$
