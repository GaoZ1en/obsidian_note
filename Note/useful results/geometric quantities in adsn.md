# general discuttion

for $\displaystyle{\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}}$, as a maximal symmetric space, the Riemann tensor, Ricci tensor and Ricci scalar satisfies

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
R_{\mu \nu \rho \sigma} & =\frac{1}{L^{2}}(g_{\mu \rho}g_{\nu \sigma}-g_{\mu \sigma}g_{\nu \rho}) \\
R_{\mu \nu} & =-\frac{d}{L^{2}}g_{\mu \nu} \\
R & = -\frac{d(d+1)}{L^{2}}
\end{align}
$$

by Einstein equation, we solves the cosmological constant

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
\Lambda & =-\frac{d(d-1)}{2L^{2}}
\end{align}
$$

# global coordinates

$$\tag{2.1}
\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =-(1+\rho ^{2})\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}\rho ^{2}}{1+\rho ^{2}}+\rho^{2}\mathrm{d}\Omega_{d-1}^{2}
\end{align}
$$

the Christoffel symbols are given by

$$\tag{2.2}
\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{t}_{~t\rho} & =\frac{\rho}{1+\rho ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{~tt} & =\rho(1+\rho ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{~\rho \rho} & =-\frac{\rho}{1+\rho ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{ij} & =-\rho (1+\rho ^{2})\tilde{g}_{ij} \\
\Gamma ^{k}_{~ij} & =\tilde{\Gamma}^{k}_{~ij} \\
\Gamma ^{i}_{~\rho j} & =\frac{1}{\rho}\delta ^{i}_{~j}
\end{align}
$$

in the case of $\displaystyle{d=3}$, we have

$$\tag{2.3}
\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{t}_{~t\rho} & =\frac{\rho}{1+\rho ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{~tt} & =\rho(1+\rho ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{~\rho \rho} & =-\frac{\rho}{1+\rho ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{\rho}_{~\theta \theta} & =-\rho (1+\rho ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\rho \theta} & =\frac{1}{\rho}
\end{align}
$$
