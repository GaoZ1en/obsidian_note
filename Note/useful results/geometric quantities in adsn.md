# general discuttion

for $\displaystyle{\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}}$, as a maximal symmetric space, the Riemann tensor, Ricci tensor and Ricci scalar satisfies

$$\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
R_{\mu \nu \rho \sigma} & =\frac{1}{L^{2}}(g_{\mu \rho}g_{\nu \sigma}-g_{\mu \sigma}g_{\nu \rho}) \\
R_{\mu \nu} & =-\frac{d}{L^{2}}g_{\mu \nu} \\
R & = -\frac{d(d+1)}{L^{2}}
\end{align}
$$

by Einstein equation, we solves the cosmological constant

$$\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
\Lambda & =-\frac{d(d-1)}{2L^{2}}
\end{align}
$$

# global coordinates

$$\tag{2.1}
\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2} & =-(1+r ^{2})\mathrm{d}t^{2}+\frac{\mathrm{d}r ^{2}}{1+r ^{2}}+r^{2}\mathrm{d}\Omega_{d-1}^{2}
\end{align}
$$

the Christoffel symbols are given by

$$\tag{2.2}
\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{t}_{~tr} & =\frac{r}{1+r ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{~tt} & =r(1+r ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{~r \rho} & =-\frac{r}{1+r ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{ij} & =-r (1+r ^{2})\tilde{g}_{ij} \\
\Gamma ^{k}_{~ij} & =\tilde{\Gamma}^{k}_{~ij} \\
\Gamma ^{i}_{~r j} & =\frac{1}{r}\delta ^{i}_{~j}
\end{align}
$$

in the case of $\displaystyle{d=3}$, we have

$$\tag{2.3}
\begin{align}
2\Gamma ^{t}_{~tr} & =\frac{r}{1+r ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{~tt} & =r(1+r ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{~r r} & =-\frac{r}{1+r ^{2}} \\
\Gamma ^{r}_{~\phi \phi} & =-r (1+r ^{2}) \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~r \phi} & =\frac{1}{r}
\end{align}
$$
