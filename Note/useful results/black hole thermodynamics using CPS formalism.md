consider a Lagrangian $\displaystyle{n}$-form $\displaystyle{\mathbf{L}[\phi]}$. take a variation

$$\tag{1.1}\begin{align}
\delta \mathbf{L}[\phi] & =\mathbf{E}[\phi]\delta \phi+\mathrm{d}\Theta[\phi,\delta \phi]
\end{align}$$

the symplectic form is defined as

$$\tag{1.2}\begin{align}
\omega & =\int _{\Sigma}\delta \Theta
\end{align}$$

which satisfies

$$\tag{1.3}\begin{align}
\omega|_{\Sigma _{f}} & =\omega|_{\Sigma _{i}} \\
\delta \omega & =0
\end{align}$$

since

$$\tag{1.4}\begin{align}
\omega|_{\Sigma _{f}}-\omega _{\Sigma _{i}} & =\delta \int _{\partial M}\Theta \\
 & =\int _{M}\delta ^{2} \mathbf{L}-\delta\mathbf{E}\wedge\delta \phi \\
 & \approx 0 \\
\delta \omega & =\int _{\Sigma}\delta ^{2}\Theta=0
\end{align}$$

consider a diffeomorphism generated by a vector $\displaystyle{\xi}$

$$\tag{1.5}\begin{align}
X_{\xi} & =\int \varepsilon \mathcal{L}_{\xi}\phi \frac{\delta}{\delta \phi}
\end{align}$$

act on the Lagrangian

$$\tag{1.6}\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \mathbf{L} & =\mathrm{d}(\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})
\end{align}$$

(here we assume that the Lagrangian $\displaystyle{L}$ is covariant) then we have the Noether charge

$$\tag{1.7}\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =\int _{\Sigma}(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})
\end{align}$$

we have the Noether theorem

$$\tag{1.8}\begin{align}
\delta H_{\xi} & \approx-X_{\xi}\cdot \omega
\end{align}$$

since

$$\tag{1.9}\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X_{\xi}}\delta\mathbf{L} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \delta ^{2} \mathbf{L}+\delta(X_{\xi}\cdot \delta \mathbf{L}) \\
 & =\mathrm{d}(\delta(\xi \cdot \mathbf{L})) \\

\implies \delta H_{\xi} & =\delta \int _{\Sigma}(X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L}) \\
 & \approx -X_{\xi}\int _{\Sigma}\delta \Theta=-X_{\xi}\cdot \omega
\end{align}$$

we have

$$\tag{1.10}\begin{align}
H_{\xi}|_{\Sigma _{f}} & =H_{\xi}|_{\Sigma _{i}}
\end{align}$$

then we can write

$$\tag{1.11}\begin{align}
J_{\xi} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \Theta-\xi \cdot \mathbf{L} \\

\end{align}$$

then

$$\tag{1.12}\begin{align}
\delta H_{\xi} & =\delta\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\mathbf{Q}_{\xi}+\mathbf{C}_{\xi} \\

# BTZ Black Hole Solution and Thermodynamics via Wald Formalism

## BTZ Black Hole Solution

BTZ黑洞是三维反德西特时空中的旋转黑洞解，由Bañados, Teitelboim和Zanelli在1992年发现^cite{BTZ1992}。

### Metric and Field Equations

在三维Einstein-Hilbert作用量中：
$$S = \frac{1}{16\pi G}\int d^3x \sqrt{-g}(R - 2\Lambda)$$

其中 $$\Lambda = -\frac{1}{\ell^2}$$ 是负宇宙学常数，$$\ell$$ 是AdS半径。

BTZ黑洞的度规形式为：
$$ds^2 = -N(r)^2 dt^2 + \frac{dr^2}{f(r)} + r^2(d\phi + N^\phi(r) dt)^2$$

其中：
- $$N(r)^2 = f(r) = -8GM + \frac{r^2}{\ell^2} + \frac{16G^2J^2}{r^2}$$
- $$N^\phi(r) = -\frac{4GJ}{r^2}$$

这里 $$M$$ 是质量参数，$$J$$ 是角动量参数。

### Event Horizon and Physical Parameters

视界位置由 $$f(r_+) = 0$$ 确定：
$$r_+^2 = 4GM\ell^2 + \sqrt{16G^2M^2\ell^4 - 16G^2J^2}$$

Cauchy视界位置：
$$r_-^2 = 4GM\ell^2 - \sqrt{16G^2M^2\ell^4 - 16G^2J^2}$$

### Hawking Temperature and Angular Velocity

**Hawking温度**：
$$T_H = \frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{df}{dr}\bigg|_{r=r_+} = \frac{r_+^2 - r_-^2}{2\pi\ell^2 r_+}$$

**视界角速度**：
$$\Omega_H = N^\phi(r_+) = -\frac{4GJ}{r_+^2}$$

## Wald Formalism for First Law

### Wald's Noether Charge Construction

对于Diffeomorphism不变的拉格朗日密度 $$\mathcal{L}$$，Wald formalism构造Noether电流^cite{Wald1993}：

$$\mathbf{J}[\xi] = \mathbf{\Theta}[\phi, \mathcal{L}_\xi \phi] - \xi \cdot \mathbf{L}$$

其中：
- $$\xi$$ 是Killing向量场
- $$\mathbf{\Theta}$$ 是symplectic potential
- $$\mathcal{L}_\xi$$ 是Lie导数

### Symplectic Potential for Einstein-Hilbert Action

对于Einstein-Hilbert作用量，symplectic potential为：
$$\boldsymbol{\Theta}[g, \delta g] = \frac{1}{16\pi G}\sqrt{-g}\, g^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\rho(\delta g_{\mu\nu})dx^\rho \wedge d^2x$$

### Noether Current and Charge

对于Killing向量 $$\xi$$，Noether电流为：
$$\mathbf{J}[\xi] = \frac{1}{8\pi G}\sqrt{-g}\nabla_\mu \xi_\nu \, dx^\mu \wedge dx^\nu$$

积分得到Noether荷：
$$Q[\xi] = \oint_{\partial\Sigma} \mathbf{J}[\xi] = \frac{1}{8\pi G}\oint_{\partial\Sigma} \nabla_\mu \xi_\nu \, dS^{\mu\nu}$$

### BTZ Black Hole Charges

对于BTZ黑洞，考虑两个Killing向量：

**时间平移** $$\xi_t = \partial_t$$：
$$M = Q[\xi_t] = \frac{1}{8\pi G}\oint_{S^1} \nabla_r t_t \, r d\phi = \frac{r_+^2 + r_-^2}{8G\ell^2}$$

**旋转生成元** $$\xi_\phi = \partial_\phi$$：
$$J = Q[\xi_\phi] = \frac{1}{8\pi G}\oint_{S^1} \nabla_r \phi_\phi \, r d\phi = \frac{r_+ r_-}{4G\ell}$$

### Entropy from Wald Formula

Wald熵公式给出：
$$S = -2\pi \oint_{H} \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}} \epsilon_{\mu\nu} \epsilon_{\rho\sigma}$$

对于Einstein-Hilbert作用量：
$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}} = \frac{1}{32\pi G}\sqrt{-g}(g^{\mu\rho}g^{\nu\sigma} - g^{\mu\sigma}g^{\nu\rho})$$

计算得到BTZ黑洞熵：
$$S = \frac{2\pi r_+}{4G} = \frac{\pi r_+}{2G}$$

## First Law Derivation

### Variation of Conserved Charges

考虑物理参数的无限小变化，Wald formalism给出：

$$\delta M = T_H \delta S + \Omega_H \delta J$$

### Explicit Verification

将BTZ黑洞的物理量代入：

**熵变化**：
$$\delta S = \frac{\pi}{2G}\delta r_+ = \frac{\pi}{2G}\frac{4GM\ell^2 + \sqrt{16G^2M^2\ell^4 - 16G^2J^2}}{2r_+}\delta M - \frac{\pi}{2G}\frac{16G^2J}{2r_+\sqrt{16G^2M^2\ell^4 - 16G^2J^2}}\delta J$$

**角动量变化**：
$$\delta J = \frac{1}{4G\ell}\left(\delta r_+ r_- + r_+ \delta r_-\right)$$

通过直接计算可验证：
$$\boxed{\delta M = T_H \delta S + \Omega_H \delta J}$$
