consider the following chain complex

```tikz
\usepackage{tikz-cd,amsmath,amssymb}
\begin{document}\begin{tikzcd}
\dots \arrow{r} & 0 \arrow{r} & R[w] \arrow{r}{\partial _{2}} & R[y_{1}]\oplus R[y_{2}] \arrow{r}{\partial _{1}} & R[p_{1}]\oplus R[p_{2}] \arrow{r} & 0
\end{tikzcd}\end{document}
```

with $\displaystyle{\partial _{2}w=2y_{1},\partial _{1}y_{1}=p_{1}-p_{2}, \partial _{1}y_{2}=p_{2}-p_{1}}$. then the $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Ker}\partial _{1}}$ is generated by $\displaystyle{y_{1}+y_{2}}$, while the $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Im}\partial _{2}=2y_{1}}$. so we have

$$
\tag{1.1}
\begin{align}
H_{1}(\Delta,R) & = \frac{R[y_{1}+y_{2}]}{R[2y_{1}]}=\mathbb{Z}_{2}\oplus R
\end{align}
$$

$\displaystyle{\mathrm{Ker}\partial _{2}=0}$, so $\displaystyle{H_{2}(\Delta,R)=0}$. $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Ker}\partial _{0}=R[p_{1}]\oplus R[p_{2}]}$ and $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Im}\partial _{1}=R[p_{1}-p_{2}]}$, we have

$$
\tag{1.2}
\begin{align}
H_{2}(\Delta,R) & =\frac{R[p_{1}]\oplus R[p_{2}]}{R[p_{1}-p_{2}]}=R
\end{align}
$$

as a conclusion, we have

$$
\tag{1.3}
\begin{align}
H_{n}(\Delta,R) & =\begin{cases}
R & n=0 \\
\mathbb{Z}_{2}\oplus R & n=1 \\
0 & \text{else}
\end{cases}
\end{align}
$$

$$
\tag{1.5}
\begin{align}
H_{0}(S^{1},R) & =\frac{R[p_{1}]\oplus R[p_{2}]}{R[p_{1}-p_{2}]}=R \\
H_{1}(S^{1},R) & = \frac{R[y_{1}]\oplus R[y_{2}]}{R[y_{1}+y_{2}]}=R \\
H_{n}(S^{1},R) & =0\quad n\geqslant 2
\end{align}
$$

## $\displaystyle{S^{n}}$

we can make it iteratively. start from $\displaystyle{S^{0}=\left\{-1,1\right\}}$, which we call $\displaystyle{\sigma _{0}}$ and $\displaystyle{T\sigma _{0}}$, then glue them by $\displaystyle{\sigma _{1}}$ and $\displaystyle{T\sigma _{1}}$ with $\displaystyle{\partial _{1}\sigma _{1}=\sigma _{0}-T\sigma _{0}, \partial _{1}T\sigma _{1}=T\sigma _{0}-\sigma _{0}}$. then glue on 2-cells by $\displaystyle{\sigma _{2}}$ and $\displaystyle{T\sigma _{2}}$, with $\displaystyle{\partial _{2}\sigma _{2}=\sigma _{1}+T\sigma _{1}=-\partial _{2}T\sigma _{2}}$. on and on. finally we have $\displaystyle{\partial _{k}\sigma _{k}=\sigma _{k-1}+(-1)^{k}T\sigma _{k-1}}$. so we have

$$
\tag{1.7}
\begin{align}
H_{m}(S^{n},R) & =\begin{cases}
R & m=0,n \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{align}
$$

## $\displaystyle{\mathbb{RP}^{n}}$

$\displaystyle{\mathbb{RP}^{n}}$ is the space of lines through the origin in $\displaystyle{\mathbb{R}^{n+1}}$. we can make similar construction as $\displaystyle{S^{n}}$ but with $\displaystyle{\partial _{n}\sigma _{n}=(1+(-1)^{i})\sigma _{i-1}}$, which gives rise to

$$
\tag{1.8}
\begin{align}
H_{m}(\mathbb{RP}^{n},R)=\begin{cases}
R & i=0 \text{ or } n \text{ with } n \text{ odd} \\
\mathbb{Z}_{2} & i \text{ odd, <n} \\
0 & \text{else}
\end{cases}
\end{align}
$$
