# arXiv December 2025 quantum gravity screening

## Screening profile

This shortlist is tuned for a theoretical physics graduate student working on quantum gravity and already comfortable with covariant phase space. I prioritize papers involving boundary and asymptotic symmetries, Noether charges, first-order gravity, horizon thermodynamics, semiclassical black hole radiation, gravitational constraints, holographic quantum mechanics, and mathematically structured higher-gauge or geometric formulations. The final selection is dominated by `hep-th` and `gr-qc`; the `math-ph` contribution enters through black hole mechanics, higher gauge fields, topological quantization, anomalies, and geometric formulations of general relativity.

## High-priority reading

1. [GGI lectures on boundary and asymptotic symmetries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16810)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Gives a pedagogical account of Noether theorem, covariant phase space, boundary and asymptotic symmetries, null hypersurface flux laws, BMS charges, and an integral Hamiltonian generator on a null hypersurface.
   - Why it is relevant: This is the most directly connected December item for covariant phase space, boundary charges, null infinity, BMS symmetry, and flux-balance laws.

1. [Thermodynamics of Black Holes, far from Equilibrium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11659)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`, `math-ph`.
   - One-line summary: Extends black hole mechanics to finite physical processes far from equilibrium, with a quantitative second law relating dynamical-horizon area change to infalling energy flux.
   - Why it is relevant: It connects black hole mechanics, dynamical horizons, and flux laws in a form that is naturally comparable with covariant phase-space and quasi-local charge ideas.

1. [Black hole thermodynamics is gauge independent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11196)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Shows directly that the first law of black hole thermodynamics in first-order gravity is independent of the gauge choice associated with torsion and internal frame freedom.
   - Why it is relevant: The paper is tightly related to first-order formulations, horizon charges, gauge ambiguities, and the robustness of black hole thermodynamics as a quantum-gravity guide.

1. [Dynamical Black Hole Emission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16480)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Computes the time-dependent renormalized stress tensor for a four-dimensional black hole formed by a null shock wave and shows Hawking flux dynamically emerging from the near-horizon region.
   - Why it is relevant: It is a concrete semiclassical-gravity study of Hawking radiation, null infinity flux, horizon formation, and possible back-reaction.

1. [Squeezed States in Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20601)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Develops a general framework for producing squeezed quantum states of the linearized gravitational field from time-dependent quadratic couplings to background curvature or matter.
   - Why it is relevant: It gives a controlled perturbative quantum-gravity setting for asking how classical spacetimes and matter sources create nontrivial graviton states.

1. [Complete Topological Quantization of Higher Gauge Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12431)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `math-ph`, `math.AT`, `quant-ph`, `cond-mat.str-el`.
   - One-line summary: Surveys topological light-front quantization of globally completed higher gauge fields, including 5D Maxwell-Chern-Simons theory and the higher gauge sector of 11D supergravity.
   - Why it is relevant: It is a mathematically serious bridge between higher gauge fields, flux quantization, supergravity, M-theory topological sectors, and nonperturbative observables.

1. [Pfaffian Systems, Cartan Connections, and the Null Surface Formulation of General Relativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14501)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Reviews differential forms, Pfaffian systems, Cartan conformal connections, and the null surface formulation in which spacetime geometry is reconstructed from a scalar whose level sets are null.
   - Why it is relevant: It is useful for the geometric side of null hypersurfaces, conformal structure, light-cone data, and alternative variables for gravitational dynamics.

1. [Scaling limits of complex Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev models and holographic geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05294)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `cond-mat.str-el`.
   - One-line summary: Compares large-$N$, large-$p$, and double-scaling limits of complex SYK models and matches the results to JT gravity with an additional $U(1)$ gauge field.
   - Why it is relevant: It gives a sharp quantum-mechanical holographic laboratory for nearly-$AdS_2$ gravity, charged JT dynamics, and low-dimensional black hole physics.

## Secondary candidates

1. [Introduction to black hole thermodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24929)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Provides lecture notes on black hole mechanics, Hawking radiation, Euclidean quantum gravity methods, and AdS black holes.

1. [Quantum Black Holes and Gauge/Gravity Duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18845)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Reviews AdS2 black hole information loss, generalized entropy, quantum extremal surfaces, islands, replica wormholes, and Page curve physics.

1. [Near-horizon gravitational perturbations of rotating black holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07937)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Constructs a nonsingular source term for near-horizon Kerr perturbations and computes horizon deformation, quasinormal excitation, and inspiral energy flux.

1. [On extremal black holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01898)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`, `astro-ph.CO`.
   - One-line summary: Reassesses whether electrically, magnetically, or rotationally extremal black holes can persist in realistic low-energy physics.

1. [Charged Regular Black Holes From Quasi-topological Gravities in $D\ge 5$](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.04604)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Constructs higher-dimensional charged black holes in an infinite higher-curvature quasi-topological gravity tower and shows how the singularity can be smoothed in the infinite-order limit.

1. [Signatures of Quantum-Corrected Black Holes in Gravitational Waves from Periodic Orbits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24036)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Studies gravitational waves from zoom-whirl orbits around a loop-quantum-gravity-inspired Schwarzschild black hole and identifies phase and spectral shifts due to holonomy corrections.

1. [Anomalies on ALE spaces and phases of gauge theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11970)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `hep-ph`, `cond-mat.str-el`.
   - One-line summary: Shows that placing QFTs on Eguchi-Hanson and related ALE backgrounds can reveal 't Hooft anomalies invisible on standard closed four-manifolds.

1. [Exploring the nature of gravity with quantum information methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20429)
   - Categories: `quant-ph`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Introduces gravitationally induced entanglement and nonclassical causal structures as quantum-information probes of whether gravity must be quantized.

1. [Novel Phenomena in Noncommutative Field Theory: Emergent Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18855)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Gives a pedagogical overview of noncommutative field theory, UV/IR mixing, quantized spacetime, and emergent geometry as quantum-gravity-motivated structures.

1. [Exploring the twisted sector of $\mathbb{Z}_{L}$ orbifolds: Matching $\alpha'$-corrections to localisation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16984)
    - Categories: `hep-th`.
    - One-line summary: Matches strong-coupling localization data in $\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5/\mathbb{Z}_{L}$ orbifolds to twisted-sector string amplitudes and explains failures of naive low-energy reduction.

1. [TASI Lectures on de Sitter Vacua](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17095)
    - Categories: `hep-th`.
    - One-line summary: Provides extensive graduate-level lecture notes on type IIB flux compactifications, moduli stabilization, quantum corrections, and candidate de Sitter vacua.

## Suggested reading order

1. Start with [GGI lectures on boundary and asymptotic symmetries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16810), because it directly reviews covariant phase space, Noether charges, null hypersurfaces, and BMS fluxes.
1. Read [Thermodynamics of Black Holes, far from Equilibrium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11659) and [Black hole thermodynamics is gauge independent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.11196) next as the December black-hole-mechanics core.
1. Follow with [Dynamical Black Hole Emission](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16480) and [Squeezed States in Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20601) for semiclassical and perturbative quantum-gravity dynamics.
1. Then read [Complete Topological Quantization of Higher Gauge Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12431) and [Pfaffian Systems, Cartan Connections, and the Null Surface Formulation of General Relativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14501) as the mathematical-structure track.
1. Use [Scaling limits of complex Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev models and holographic geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05294), [Quantum Black Holes and Gauge/Gravity Duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18845), and [Introduction to black hole thermodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24929) for the low-dimensional holography and black-hole-entropy track.
1. Keep the remaining secondary candidates as specialized follow-ups: near-horizon perturbation theory, extremal black hole viability, higher-curvature regular black holes, phenomenological loop-inspired signatures, ALE anomaly probes, noncommutative geometry, orbifold localization, and de Sitter compactifications.
