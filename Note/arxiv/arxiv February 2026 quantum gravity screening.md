# arXiv February 2026 quantum gravity screening

## Screening profile

This shortlist is tuned for a theoretical physics graduate student working on quantum gravity and already comfortable with covariant phase space. I prioritize papers involving null boundaries, Noether charges, gravitational path integrals, black hole entropy and interiors, holographic reconstruction/locality, matrix models, and symmetry structures. The final selection is dominated by `hep-th` and `gr-qc`; the `math-ph` contribution is mostly through adjacent mathematical structures such as categorical symmetry, momentum maps, matrix integrals, and geometric actions.

## High-priority reading

1. [Duality symmetry and dynamics on finite null boundaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21017)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Derives boost-weighted metric functionals, near-horizon symmetry transformations, and subleading Noether charges for finite null boundaries in Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity.
   - Why it is relevant: This is the most directly connected February paper for covariant phase space, null-boundary dynamics, Holst charges, and near-horizon symmetry.

2. [Kosmann derivative and momentum maps from a duality covariant framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11267)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Reformulates generalized Kosmann derivatives in Double Field Theory using generalized fluxes and momentum maps, with implications for conserved currents and Noether charges.
   - Why it is relevant: Momentum maps and symmetry-covariant diffeomorphisms are close to the geometric side of charge construction, especially for higher-derivative and duality-covariant gravity.

3. [Area terms and entanglement entropy in the $c=1$ string theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02683)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Studies whether generalized entropy in two-dimensional string theory should contain a dilaton-dependent gravitational area term in addition to matter entanglement.
   - Why it is relevant: It is a clean low-dimensional setting for the relation between target-space gravity, matrix models, and gravitational entropy terms.

4. [Consistent Evaluation of the No-Boundary Proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02682)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `astro-ph.CO`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Re-evaluates the Hartle-Hawking no-boundary proposal by including both gravitational path-integral amplitudes and their norms.
   - Why it is relevant: The paper is about defining probabilities in gravitational path integrals, which is conceptually close to Hilbert-space and state issues in quantum gravity.

5. [Bulk Phase Shift and Singularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06558)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Uses holographic thermal correlators to extract bulk time delay and angular deflection even when black-hole scattering probes small impact parameters near singular behavior.
   - Why it is relevant: It connects black hole singularities, eikonal scattering, and CFT correlator singularities, making it useful for holographic probes of interior physics.

6. [Localization of the BFSS matrix model and three-point amplitude in M-theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11667)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Applies localization to a BFSS matrix-model boundary condition related to eleven-dimensional graviton scattering and recovers the expected three-point amplitude.
   - Why it is relevant: BFSS remains one of the sharpest nonperturbative quantum-gravity laboratories, and this paper gives an exact computation tied to M-theory scattering.

7. [Null fluid/gravity correspondence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20268)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Constructs asymptotically AdS pp-wave spacetimes from long-wavelength expansions and interprets them as a null fluid limit of holographic hydrodynamics.
   - Why it is relevant: It extends fluid/gravity ideas into null states and pp-wave geometries, a useful bridge between boundary stress tensor dynamics and bulk geometry.

8. [Renormalized pseudoentropy in dS/CFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17989)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Defines a finite holographic pseudoentropy for subregions in dS/CFT using conformal gravity on-shell actions and replica methods.
   - Why it is relevant: It gives a concrete entropy-like observable in de Sitter holography, with replica and conformal-gravity ingredients that overlap with gravitational entropy methods.

## Secondary candidates

1. [Boundary conformal field theory, holography and bulk locality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04223)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Tests bulk locality in AdS effective field theory with an end-of-the-world brane by comparing loop Witten diagrams with BCFT constraints.

2. [General Actions of Extended Objects and Volume-Preserving Diffeomorphism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23443)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Classifies generalized worldsheet and worldvolume actions and shows when volume-preserving diffeomorphism symmetry is as restrictive as full diffeomorphism symmetry.

3. [Asymptotic bootstrap for unitary matrix integrals at complex coupling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18559)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Develops an asymptotic bootstrap method for nonperturbative unitary matrix integrals, including instanton-sensitive observables and complex-coupling phase structure.

4. [Asymmetric orbifolds with vanishing one-loop vacuum energy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07113)
   - Categories: `hep-th`.
   - One-line summary: Classifies non-supersymmetric type II asymmetric orbifolds whose one-loop vacuum energy cancels sector by sector.

5. [On the $(\text{Fib} \boxtimes \text{Fib}) \rtimes S_2$ fusion category](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06117)
   - Categories: `hep-th`, mathematical CFT adjacent.
   - One-line summary: Computes categorical data needed for modular bootstrap of non-rational Virasoro CFTs with a non-invertible categorical symmetry.

6. [Scale Invariance, Variety and Central Configurations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11225)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Studies scale-invariant relational $N$-body dynamics on shape space and argues that variety can generate cosmic-web-like structure and a gravitational arrow of time.

7. [Reissner Nordstrom black holes with integrable singularity interiors supported by string distributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03050)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Constructs RN exterior geometries from string-supported interiors with integrable singularities and no inner horizon.

8. [Effects of nonlinear interactions on the superradiant instability of charged black holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05268)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Evolves an axion-electromagnetic system around charged black holes in a cavity and studies nonlinear endpoints of superradiant growth.

9. [Inspiral tests of general relativity and waveform geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17524)
   - Categories: `gr-qc`.
   - One-line summary: Uses waveform-manifold geometry and bias analysis to understand when parameterized inspiral tests detect or miss deviations from general relativity.

10. [Are black hole spins truly near-zero?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12859)
    - Categories: `gr-qc`.
    - One-line summary: Argues that the apparent near-zero spin distribution in GWTC-4 binary black holes may be strongly prior-driven, affecting GR tests and black-hole population inference.

## Suggested reading order

1. Start with [Duality symmetry and dynamics on finite null boundaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21017), because it directly touches null-boundary phase space and Noether charges.
2. Read [Kosmann derivative and momentum maps from a duality covariant framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11267) next to compare charge and momentum-map language across first-order, doubled, and duality-covariant frameworks.
3. Then read [Area terms and entanglement entropy in the $c=1$ string theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02683) and [Consistent Evaluation of the No-Boundary Proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02682) as two complementary windows into gravitational entropy and gravitational path integrals.
4. Follow with [Bulk Phase Shift and Singularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06558), [Boundary conformal field theory, holography and bulk locality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04223), and [Renormalized pseudoentropy in dS/CFT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17989) for holographic diagnostics.
5. Keep [Localization of the BFSS matrix model and three-point amplitude in M-theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11667), [Null fluid/gravity correspondence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20268), and [General Actions of Extended Objects and Volume-Preserving Diffeomorphism](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23443) as the broader string/M-theory and geometric-action track.
