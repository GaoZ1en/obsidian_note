# arXiv January 2026 quantum gravity screening

## Screening profile

This shortlist is biased toward topics close to quantum gravity and covariant phase space:

- Covariant phase space, constraints, boundary charges, gauge symmetry, and conserved quantities.
- Black hole thermodynamics, horizon dynamics, singularity resolution, and semiclassical or quantum gravitational collapse.
- AdS/CFT, holographic RG, de Sitter quantum gravity, celestial symmetry, soft theorems, higher spin theory, and three-dimensional gravity.
- Mathematical-physics-adjacent structures such as Chern-Simons theory, BF theory, twistor geometry, and $p$-adic or ultrametric spectra.

## High-priority reading

### Revisiting Spherically Symmetric Spacetime I: Geometro-Hydrodynamics

- arXiv: [2601.03077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03077)
- Categories: `gr-qc`, `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The paper reformulates spherically symmetric Einstein and Lovelock dynamics using radius-adapted frames and a geometro-hydrodynamic picture, explicitly positioning the series as a foundation for covariant phase space and spacetime thermodynamics.
- Why it is relevant: This is the closest match to covariant phase space, horizon thermodynamics, and quasi-local gravitational dynamics.

### Intertwiners for D=3 Gauge Theories

- arXiv: [2601.18490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18490)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The authors construct intertwiner operators for BF theory, Chern-Simons theory, and three-dimensional gravity, comparing canonical and holomorphic quantization while preserving covariance on the Cauchy surface.
- Why it is relevant: Useful for 3d gravity, Chern-Simons methods, constraints, Wilson loop observables, and the relation between canonical and covariant quantization.

### Quantum gravitational stellar evolution beyond shell-crossing singularities

- arXiv: [2601.18618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18618)
- Categories: `gr-qc`
- One-sentence summary: The paper extends loop-quantum-gravity-inspired collapsing stellar models beyond shell-crossing singularities by using a Hamiltonian treatment of Darmois-Israel junction conditions and obtains an inter-universal wormhole evolution.
- Why it is relevant: A concrete model of quantum-gravity-inspired singularity resolution with an explicitly Hamiltonian matching construction.

### Probing Black Hole Thermal Effects in the Dual CFT via Wave Packets

- arXiv: [2601.04647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04647)
- Categories: `hep-th`, `gr-qc`
- One-sentence summary: The work studies wave packets falling into an AdS black hole and computes boundary CFT three-point functions to show how black hole thermal effects appear as damping and finite-temperature signatures.
- Why it is relevant: A compact AdS/CFT probe of black hole physics from the CFT side.

### All loop soft photon theorems and higher spin currents on the celestial sphere

- arXiv: [2601.03361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03361)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The paper interprets all-loop soft photon theorems as celestial Ward identities requiring additional higher-spin currents on the celestial sphere.
- Why it is relevant: Although about photons rather than gravitons, it is structurally close to asymptotic symmetries, celestial holography, and loop-corrected soft theorems.

### $SO(1, d + 1)$ symmetry of the Exact RG equation

- arXiv: [2601.02056](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02056)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The authors show that Polchinski exact RG evolution has an $SO(1,d+1)$ symmetry for arbitrary cutoff functions, generalizing the construction of holographic bulk dual actions in Euclidean AdS.
- Why it is relevant: Relevant to holographic RG and the emergence of AdS isometries from boundary RG flow.

### Energy-Energy Correlator from the AdS Virasoro-Shapiro Amplitude

- arXiv: [2601.05312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05312)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The paper relates worldsheet integrals of the AdS Virasoro-Shapiro amplitude to energy-energy correlators in strongly coupled $\mathcal{N}=4$ SYM, extracting flat-space and curvature-correction contributions.
- Why it is relevant: A stringy AdS/CFT calculation connecting worldsheet amplitudes, curvature corrections, and boundary collider observables.

### Quantum Cosmology as a Hydrogen atom: Discrete $\Lambda$ and cyclic Universes from Wheeler-DeWitt quantization

- arXiv: [2601.20286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20286)
- Categories: `gr-qc`, `astro-ph.CO`, `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: In a flat FLRW dust model with negative cosmological constant, Wheeler-DeWitt quantization maps the system to a hydrogen-like bound-state problem with a discrete $\Lambda$ spectrum and cyclic nonsingular wave-packet evolution.
- Why it is relevant: A solvable quantum cosmology model with singularity resolution and a discrete cosmological constant sector.

## Secondary candidates

### The extended phase space thermodynamics and Ehrenfest scheme for the Kerr-Sen AdS black holes

- arXiv: [2601.01814](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01814)
- Categories: `gr-qc`
- One-sentence summary: The paper studies horizon structure, extended thermodynamic phase transitions, Ehrenfest relations, and boundary CFT thermodynamic quantities for Kerr-Sen-AdS black holes.

### Finite-resolution measurement induces topological curvature defects in spacetime

- arXiv: [2601.13491](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13491)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The authors argue that Gaussian finite-resolution probing of $(2+1)$-dimensional Minkowski spacetime induces an effective curved metric with a universal topological curvature defect.

### Extended symmetry of the Maxwell theory with a gauge coupling constant as a conserved charge

- arXiv: [2601.15592](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15592)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The work treats a gauge coupling as a conserved charge via auxiliary fields, analyzes the resulting Maxwell constraints, and restores local symmetry using the Batalin-Fradkin-Tyutin formalism.

### The complete action for $\mathcal{N}=2$ de Sitter pure supergravity

- arXiv: [2601.16891](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16891)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The paper constructs the complete four-dimensional $\mathcal{N}=2$ pure de Sitter supergravity action and discusses its non-unitarity in relation to Euclidean approaches to de Sitter quantum gravity.

### Twisting Twistor space

- arXiv: [2601.19983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19983)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The authors build twisted noncommutative gauge theories on twistor space and relate noncommutative BF and holomorphic Chern-Simons theories to four-dimensional Yang-Mills systems.

### Irregular higher-spin generating equations and chiral perturbation theory

- arXiv: [2601.10680](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10680)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The paper proposes irregular generating equations for minimally nonlocal four-dimensional higher-spin interactions beyond the standard Vasiliev setup.

### A glimpse into the Ultrametric spectrum

- arXiv: [2601.03738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03738)
- Categories: `hep-th`
- One-sentence summary: The work studies $p$-adic and ultrametric spectra whose energy and degeneracy growth can reproduce Hardy-Ramanujan-like high-temperature scaling with log-periodic modulation.

1. [2601.03077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03077) for covariant phase space and thermodynamic motivation.
2. [2601.18490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18490) for 3d gravity, Chern-Simons, BF theory, and quantization.
3. [2601.04647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04647) and [2601.05312](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05312) for complementary AdS/CFT black hole and stringy observable calculations.
4. [2601.03361](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03361) if the immediate interest is soft/celestial symmetry structures.
5. [2601.18618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18618) and [2601.20286](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20286) for quantum-gravity-inspired collapse and quantum cosmology.
