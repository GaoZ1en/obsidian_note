1. 推导OLS，以$\displaystyle{y_{i}=a+bX_{i}+c\ln X_{i}+e_{i}}$
	1. 写出残差平方和 $\displaystyle{S(a,b,c)=\sum^{n}_{i=1}(y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})^{2}}$
	2. 最小化$\displaystyle{S(a,b,c)}$，对$\displaystyle{a,b,c}$求偏导:$$\tag{2022.4.1}\begin{align}\frac{\partial S}{\partial a} & =-2\sum^{n}_{i=1}(y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})=0 \\\frac{\partial S}{\partial b} & =-2\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}(y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})=0 \\\frac{\partial S}{\partial c} & =-2\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln X_{i}(y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})=0\end{align}$$得到$$\tag{2022.4.2}\begin{align}\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i} & =an+b\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}+c\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln X_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i}X_{i} & =a\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}+b\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}^{2}+c\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}\ln X_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i}\ln X_{i} & =a\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln X_{i}+b\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}\ln X_{i}+c\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln^{2}X_{i}\end{align}$$定义 $$\tag{2022.4.3}\begin{align}B=\begin{pmatrix}\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i}X_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}y_{i}\ln X_{i}\end{pmatrix}A=\begin{pmatrix}n & \sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i} & \sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln X_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i} & \sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}^{2} & \sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}\ln X_{i} \\\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln X_{i} & \sum^{n}_{i=1}X_{i}l\ln X_{i} & \sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln^{2}X_{i}\end{pmatrix}\end{align}$$那么$\displaystyle{\hat{a},\hat{b},\hat{c}}$的OLS估计量为$\displaystyle{\begin{pmatrix}\hat{a}\\\hat{b}\\\hat{c}\end{pmatrix}=A^{-1}B}$
2. 检验是否存在非线性关系，也就是检验是否$\displaystyle{c=0}$
	1. 原假设，备择假设
	2. 给出$\displaystyle{t}$统计量$\displaystyle{t=\frac{\hat{c}}{SE(\hat{c})}}$，其中$\displaystyle{SE(\hat{c})}$是$\displaystyle{\hat{c}}$的标准误，和$\displaystyle{\hat{c}}$都从全模型中给出
	3. 确定自由度$\displaystyle{dof=m-k=n-3}$，其中$\displaystyle{k}$是估计的参数数量
	4. 最后做出决策
		1. 若$\displaystyle{|t|>t_{\alpha/2}(dof)}$，那么拒绝原假设，认为非线性项显著
		2. 否则不拒绝原假设

对于线性回归模型，最大似然估计等价于OLS，但是我们还是给出最大似然估计的流程：
1. 定义似然函数$\displaystyle{L(\theta)=\prod^{n}_{i=1}p(X_{i}|\theta)}$，取对数得到$\displaystyle{\ell(\theta)=\sum^{n}_{i=1}\ln p(X_{i}|\theta)}$
2. 对$\displaystyle{\theta}$求导，得到最大似然估计量$\displaystyle{\hat{\theta}}$

在OLS的情况下，设$\displaystyle{Y_{i}=a+bX_{i}+c\sin X_{i}+e_{i}}$，$\displaystyle{e_{i}}$服从正态分布$\displaystyle{e_{i}=Y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\sin X_{i}\sim N(0,d^{2})}$
构造似然函数
$$\tag{2021.4.1}
\begin{align}
L(a,b,c) & =\prod^{n}_{i=1} \frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }d}\exp\left(-\frac{(Y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})^{2}}{2d^{2}}\right) \\
\ln \ell(a,b,c) & =-n\ln \sqrt{ 2\pi}d-\frac{1}{2d^{2}}\sum^{n}_{i=1}(Y_{i}-a-bX_{i}-c\ln X_{i})^{2}
\end{align}
$$
因此最大似然=最小二乘残差。
然后用之前的方法就可以求出$\displaystyle{\hat{a},\hat{b},\hat{c}}$的估计

\mathbf{X}=\begin{pmatrix}1 & X_{1} & \sin X_{1} \\1 & X_{2} & \sin X_{2} \\\vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\1 & X_{n} & \sin X_{n}\end{pmatrix},
\mathbf{Y}=\begin{pmatrix}Y_{1} \\Y_{2} \\\vdots \\Y_{n}\end{pmatrix}\end{align}
