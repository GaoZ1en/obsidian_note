### 邀请信

**Template**

Dear ..., 

(the event) will be held at (place) on (time). In view of the prestige/achievements in some fields, we are very glad/honored to invite you to ...

(the event) is targeted to (some people) / aims to (some topic). (对于竞技类) Prizes will be awarded against three criteria .../(对于参与类) The event will be in three stages .... Please find more details in the attached file(用于合理地凑字数).

You are so welcomed to .... Please confirm your availability on or before (date) by replying to this email. Your consideration of this invitation will be highly appreciated.

Kind regards!

**Example**
*Write an email to a professor at a British university, inviting him/her to organize a team for the international innovation contest to be held at your university*.

Dear Prof. Bruce

An international innovation contest will be held at our university from September 21st-25th, and we are very glad to invite you to organize a team for the event

The innovation contest is targeted to talented youth exploring the sea of innovative ideas. And prizes will be awarded against three criteria: innovation degree, professional level and practical value. Please find more details in the attached file.

You are so welcomed to lead to a team in the innovation contest. Please confirm your availability on or before August 1st by replying to this email. Your consideration of this invitation will be highly appreciated!

Kind regards!

Li Ming

### 感谢信&建议信

通常对对方表达感谢并给出建议

**Template**

Dear ...,

I am writing to you to extend my thanks and gratefulness for ... / Thank you for your kind gesture of doing ... It means a lot to be.

As for ... here is a bit of some suggestions according to my personal experience/opinions. First, ... is a cost-effective way of doing .... The more ..., the more .... Second, ... requires ... a good knowledge/command of ... to convert ... into ....

There is no universal recipe for ..., but I am sure the advice above can help you improve upon ... If you need further clarifications, please do not hesitate to contact me at any time

Yours sincerely

LiMing

**Example**
*Suppose you won a translation contest and your friend, Jack, wrote an email to congratulate you and ask for advice on translation, Write him a reply to thank him and give your advice.*

Dear Jack,

Thank a lot of your kind gesture of congratulating me on my achievement. It means a lot to me.

As for advice on translation, here is a bit of my personal experience. First, reading widely is a cost-effective way of developing your language sense. The wider your reading habits, the better for boosting your language skills. Second, translation requires a very good knowledge of the local culture to convert the underlying meaning in one language into the other language.

There is no universal recipe for good translation, but I am sure the advice above can help you improve upon translation skills. If you need further clarifications, do not hesitate to contact me at any time

Yours Truly,

LiMing

### 图表

按照以下三步走即可

1. 描述图表
	1. 综述要义
		1. The column chart/bar chart/pie chart/line graph/table ...
			1. invites our attention to a holistic picture/view/pattern of ...
			2. offers us an informative glimpse into the outcomes of a survey conducted on ... regarding ...
	2. 具体内容
		1. As can be seen/it is clear that/To be specific/According to the data ...
			1. 对于静态图
				1. 高值
					1. The number/percentage of ... stands at an impressive ...%
					2. ... constitute/make up/account for a massive/an overwhelming ...%
				2. 中值
					1. ... followed (closely) by ...
					2. This is followed by ...
					3. Following this, ...
				3. 低值
					1. However, only ...
					2. while even fewer ...
					3. By contrast, only a small minority/mere (...%) prefer to ...
			2. 对于动态图
				1. 趋势
					1. an upward/downward trend is visible in ...
					2. a slowdown/upturn in ... has occurred across (all age groups/the income spectrum)
				2. 数据
					1. in ..., the number/percentage (of...) stands at...
						1. which jumps to/falls to/rises slightly to/drops dramatically to...
						2. almost doubled/tripled/quadrupled ...
						3. which increases about fivefold/sixfold ...
						4. an all-time high/low
		2. 双图或双动态趋势
			1. When shifting/narrowing the focus to ..., there is also a strong growth/ a slowdown ... (during the same X-year period)
2. 原因分析(穿插数据分析)
	1. 承上启下
		1. in an up-close view/analysis, (three) factors
			1. have determined
			2. could lead us to more clarity into ...
	2. 罗列原因
		1. the convenience(or other reasons) of ... allows ...
			1. which could power the number of ...
			2. which contributes directly to/corresponds to the overwhelming/growing number of those doing sth.
		2. the availability/access of .. has become less a problem for ..., thanks to ...
			1. thus enabling somebody to do something
		3. ... is associated with/causally linked to ...
			1. which is a big reason why ...
		4. this would translate into ...
			1. that helps explain why ...
3. 总结收尾
	1. As a final observation/In the final analysis/In conclusion
		1. 趋势（三选二即可）
			1. we can probably expect to see a persistent growth of/decline in/focus on...
			2. It is a trend/phenomenon/choice/pattern that is likely to introduce/carry ...
		2. 影响
			1. Such improvements could make ... thereby/enriching ...
			2. The impact of ... will be felt in the foreseeable future/immediate future/coming years
			3. And it may hold a particular promise of ...
		4. 建议
			1. It is advisable for the government/agencies/institutions to promote/invest in/focus on/develop/encourage ..., which will better prepare ... for ...
			2. Meanwhile, it would be wise/beneficial/preferable for individuals/people to consider/make/participate in ..., which can ...

**Example**

The bar chart invites our attention to the gains students made from a practice course in a certain university. To be specific, the percentage stands at an impressive 91.30% for students who acquired knowledge. This is followed by 84.80% of students reporting an improvement in their hands-on abilities. 3. How/Why/What ...
