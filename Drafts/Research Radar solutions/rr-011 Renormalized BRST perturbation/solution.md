# Interacting reduction and BRST cohomology: an exact mechanical benchmark

## Result and remaining requirement

For the compact Abelian constrained model below, physical reduction and ghost-number-zero BRST cohomology give unitarily equivalent interacting dynamics, exactly in the coupling. The constraint has a nonzero first-order deformation. The homological perturbation inclusion and the first-order energy shifts can both be computed explicitly.

This closes the finite mechanical gate in rr-011. The independent continuum requirement is addressed separately in [the continuum benchmark](continuum.md), using an explicit Abelian Stueckelberg model, local counterterms, and a support-preserving comparison of quantum observables and relative evolution. Its proof does not follow from this mechanical similarity transformation. Sewing-specific comparison maps are deferred by the user's exclusion.

## Action and classical reduction

Take a gauge angle $\vartheta\in S^1$, a matter coordinate $x\in\mathbb R$, and canonical momenta $p_\vartheta,p_x$. Set $\hbar=1$, oscillator frequency $\omega>0$, and coupling $g\geq0$. Define

$$
F=x^2\sin\vartheta,\qquad
C_g=p_\vartheta+g x^2\cos\vartheta,
$$

$$
H_g=\frac12(p_x+2g x\sin\vartheta)^2+\frac{\omega^2x^2}{2}+g x^4,
\qquad
S=\int dt\,[p_x\dot x+p_\vartheta\dot\vartheta-H_g-aC_g].
$$

The momenta $P_x=p_x+g\partial_xF$ and $P_\vartheta=p_\vartheta+g\partial_\vartheta F$ are related to the original ones by a canonical transformation: the canonical potentials differ by $g\delta F$. The function $F$ is periodic, so the transformation respects the compact gauge angle globally. In these variables,

$$
C_g=P_\vartheta,\qquad H_g=\frac{P_x^2}{2}+\frac{\omega^2x^2}{2}+g x^4.
$$

The off-shell gauge symmetry generated by $C_g$ acts freely on the angle. Variation of $a$ imposes $C_g=0$. Choosing the global section $\vartheta=0$ gives $p_\vartheta=-gx^2$, while the reduced potential is $p_x\delta x$. Thus

$$
\Omega_{\rm red}=\delta p_x\wedge\delta x,\qquad
H_{\rm red}=\frac{p_x^2}{2}+\frac{\omega^2x^2}{2}+g x^4.
$$

There is no Gribov ambiguity in this example: each angle-translation orbit meets the point $\vartheta=0$ once as a point of $S^1$.

## Quantization and BRST complex

Use $L^2(S^1,d\vartheta/2\pi)\otimes L^2(\mathbb R,dx)$ and $p=-i\partial$. The operator ordering is fixed by the globally defined unitary multiplication operator

$$
U_g=e^{-igF},\qquad C_g=U_gp_\vartheta U_g^{-1},\qquad
H_g=U_g(H_0+g x^4)U_g^{-1},
$$

where $H_0=(p_x^2+\omega^2x^2)/2$. This fixes the square of $p_x+2gx\sin\vartheta$ as an operator product, including the derivative of its coefficient.

The common smooth core is $C^\infty(S^1;\mathcal S(\mathbb R))$, with uniform Schwartz bounds for every angle derivative. Multiplication by $U_g$ preserves this space. The self-adjoint operators are defined by unitary transport of the usual angle momentum and the positive quartic oscillator. In particular, $H_g$ and $C_g$ strongly commute. The transported domains, not merely their differential expressions, are part of the definition.

Introduce one Grassmann ghost $c$ of ghost number one and use the two-term minimal complex

$$
\mathcal K^0\xrightarrow{\ Q_g\ }\mathcal K^1=c\mathcal K^0,
\qquad Q_g\psi=cC_g\psi,\qquad Q_g(c\psi)=0.
$$

Consequently $Q_g^2=0$ and $[Q_g,H_g]=0$. Physical states are taken at ghost number zero:

$$
H^0(Q_g)=\ker C_g
=\{U_g\psi(x):\psi\in L^2(\mathbb R)}.
$$

The minimal complex also has degree-one cohomology from the constant-angle sector. It is not erased by assertion; selecting ghost number zero is the physical prescription. A nonminimal gauge-fixing quartet can be added as an acyclic factor without changing this cohomology.

The map $I_g\psi=U_g\psi$, with $\psi$ constant in the angle, is unitary onto the physical Hilbert space and obeys

$$
\boxed{H_g I_g=I_g H_{\rm red}.}
$$

This proves equality of self-adjoint physical dynamics at every $g\geq0$. For an observable $O$ on the matter space, $U_g(1\otimes O)U_g^{-1}$ commutes with the constraint and induces $O$ on cohomology. On common operator domains this correspondence preserves products and adjoints. Every bounded physical operator can be obtained this way; no claim about a continuum local observable algebra is inferred.

## Homological perturbation at first order

Let $\Pi$ be the average over the angle, applied in both ghost degrees. Let $i$ include the constant-angle sector. On Fourier modes with nonzero integer momentum, define

$$
h(c\psi)=p_\vartheta^{-1}(1-\Pi)\psi,\qquad h(\psi)=0.
$$

Then $Q_0h+hQ_0=1-i\Pi$. The first deformation is $Q_1=c x^2\cos\vartheta$. For a physical degree-zero vector,

$$
\Pi Q_1i=0,\qquad
i_g^{\rm HPT}=i-g hQ_1i+O(g^2)
=\bigl(1-igx^2\sin\vartheta\bigr)i+O(g^2).
$$

This is exactly the expansion of $I_g$. The sign follows from $p_\vartheta(i\sin\vartheta)=\cos\vartheta$ and the contraction identity above.

Expanding the ordered Hamiltonian gives

$$
H_g=H_0+gH_1+O(g^2),\qquad
H_1=\sin\vartheta\{p_x,x\}+x^4.
$$

The gauge-dependent piece has zero angle average. The compatibility equation is

$$
[p_\vartheta,H_1]+[x^2\cos\vartheta,H_0]=0,
$$

and the induced effective Hamiltonian is $H_0+g\Pi H_1i=H_0+gx^4$. The exact unitary map proves the result beyond this perturbative comparison. The normalized HPT inclusion need not coincide with $I_g$ at higher orders: its condition $\Pi i_g^{\rm HPT}=1$ is a coordinate choice, whereas $\Pi I_g$ is multiplication by $J_0(gx^2)$. An inverse of the latter is only formal near $g=0$ and cannot be asserted globally through its zeros. This does not affect the exact unitary reduction.

## First-order physical spectrum

For the harmonic-oscillator eigenstate $|n\rangle$,

$$
E_n(g)=\omega\left(n+\frac12\right)
+\frac{3g}{4\omega^2}(2n^2+2n+1)+O(g^2).
$$

To obtain the coefficient, use $x=(a+a^\dagger)/\sqrt{2\omega}$ inside the already reduced oscillator. Only the number-preserving part of $(a+a^\dagger)^4$ contributes: $6n(n-1)+12n+3$. This oscillator calculation is an evaluation of the physical Hamiltonian, not the definition of BRST reduction. Both routes therefore give the same coefficient for every $n$.

**Verified:** Mathematica returns zero for the exact constraint and Hamiltonian intertwiners on arbitrary test functions, the exact constraint--Hamiltonian commutator, the first-order compatibility equation, the first HPT correction, and the projected first-order interaction. The energy coefficient was additionally checked for levels 0--8 in an oscillator matrix large enough to retain every intermediate transition contributing to $x^4$.

**Assumptions:** periodic gauge angle; positive oscillator frequency; $g\geq0$ for the stated positive self-adjoint quartic Hamiltonian; the explicit unitary ordering and transported domains; ghost-number-zero physical prescription. Perturbative statements use formal expansions on the common smooth core.

**Not verified by this mechanical calculation:** renormalized continuum BRST generators, admissible local counterterms, causal support of continuum chain maps, or a pAQFT/BV comparison theorem. Those are treated independently, with different assumptions, in [continuum.md](continuum.md). The scalar determinant calculation in rr-005 does not by itself establish such operator and product identities.
