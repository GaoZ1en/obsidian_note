## Why the remaining Kerr metric problem is a stronger continuation

Earlier progress notes kept a full, convergent infinite-angular daughter metric as a gate because the selected source/current normalization was not yet established. The new construction changes the logical dependency.

The selected coefficient is now defined directly by a convergent radial source functional and its quadratic observable completion. The equation-exact source identity proves its gauge cancellation. The adjoint metric-source identity supplies its EH/CPS normalization, with the source current and signature factor retained. Any compatible sourced reconstruction gives this coefficient, but the definition and invariance proof do not assume a pointwise infinite daughter-metric sum.

Thus a complete infinite-angular metric existence theorem is not needed to define the selected observable requested by the card. It remains an unproved stronger result. This audit neither claims that theorem nor treats finite angular refinement as its proof.

Nonzero-Wronskian and simple-angular-branch hypotheses are explicit. At the displayed numerical Kerr parameters they have numerical evidence, not certified interval enclosures. The public waveform comparison is a numerical comparison with refinement, not a new time-domain simulation.
