# Warped AdS3 TMG: the retained negative answer

**Not proven by the current equations or literature evidence inspected here:** a complete propagating-mode representation decomposition suitable for CPS quantization on the homogeneous global spacelike-warped vacuum. The evidence supports the negative feasibility verdict already recorded on the killed Radar card.

The current local note is [the warped-AdS3 feasibility audit](../../../Articles/Quantization%20in%20AdS/linearized%20gravity/massive%20gravity/warped%20ads_3%20TMG.md). Its sections 6--14 distinguish three different requirements: a local solution of the linearized equation, a globally admissible solution on the unquotiented background, and a solution belonging to a specified finite-flux CPS. They cannot be interchanged.

The answer separates two boundary-condition choices:

| Choice | What is established by the inspected evidence | Consequence for the requested construction |
|---|---|---|
| Standard stretched-warped Compère--Detournay conditions | The propagating negative-energy modes are excluded; the perturbative spectrum contains boundary excitations. | There is no retained local massive-graviton sector to normalize and quantize in this phase space. |
| Relaxed asymptotically warped conditions | Explicit propagating solutions can be retained on warped black-hole quotients, with finite integrable generators. | This does not establish a complete global-vacuum basis, its CPS norm, or its full representation decomposition. |

The standard-boundary statement is explicitly supported by the abstract of [Anninos--Esole--Guica](https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.2612). The relaxed construction is [Henneaux--Martínez--Troncoso](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.2841). Neither the presence of an isometry algebra nor the construction of a highest-weight subclass establishes completeness. The local audit also treats the zero-fibre-momentum gauge separately, rather than extrapolating a gauge fixing that divides by that momentum.

The precise conclusion is therefore a failed route under the documented phase spaces, not a theorem that every possible warped-gravity quantization is impossible. A future reopening would require one of the original card's concrete new inputs: a complete gauge-invariant master equation, a finite-flux and nonnegative-energy phase space retaining the graviton, or a resolution of the general-mode and zero-momentum completeness problems. No such new input is supplied by this audit.
