# Verification results and limits

The scalar current comparison holds on the common analytic contour, not on an arbitrary real hyperboloid or the whole regularity-QNM Hilbert space. The gravitational result is a **selected, nonresonant, gauge-completed direct–direct (DD) waveform coefficient** with a normalized metric-CPS source representation on the declared analytic domain. Neither result requires completeness of QNMs, and the selected coefficient does not establish existence of the full untruncated sourced metric. See [the main argument](solution.md) and [the selected observable](invariant-selected-coupling.md).

## Scalar contour equality and its finite-cutoff counterexample

- Conventions: subextremal Kerr, massless scalar, signature $(-+++)$, phase $e^{-i\omega t+im\varphi}$, $B_\Sigma(u,v)=\Omega_\Sigma(\mathcal Ju,v)$, with the *same* complex-linear spacetime reflection $(t,\varphi)\mapsto(-t,-\varphi)$ on both slices. No conjugation. The simple angular branch has $\int S^2\sin\theta\,d\theta=1$; the radial Frobenius series has $a_0=1$ (not unit full horizon amplitude). The $2\pi$ azimuthal factor stays. For the diagonal, the two densities coincide pointwise, giving $2\pi i\int R^2\partial_\omega V\,dr$, with the angular-eigenvalue derivative included; transforming to a regular field does not make this density integrable on the real axis. For equal $m$ and $d=\omega_1-\omega_2\ne0$, $B_h-B_t=2\pi[(e^{idh}-1)W]_{\partial C}/(id)$, with $W=\Delta(R_1'R_2-R_1R_2')\int S_1S_2\sin\theta\,d\theta$. Both $W$ and $e^{idh}W$ must decay on the same branches; unequal $m$ projects out. The scalar Green, frequency derivative, Kerr density/determinant, and height-pullback checks returned zero. Evidence: `solution.md:9–39,63–201,252–260`; `scalar-benchmark.md:11–31`; `verification/pairing-identities.wl:1–12`, `verification/kerr-slice-comparison.wl:1–16`, `verification/hyperboloidal-leaver-identities.wl:1–15`; independent covariant xAct divergence check, **ignored** `verification/scalar-current-xact-request.json:2–12` and `verification/scalar-current-xact-result.json:22–33` (exact zero).
- At $M=1,a=0.3,\ell=m=1$, the independently derived recurrence and angular solve gave $\omega_0=0.3201264576179286-0.0966913168558801i$ and $\omega_1=0.2962126664971207-0.3003246791131332i$. On the unwrapped horizon spiral, real bridge and vertical outgoing end, the degree-800 norms were $134.54519944519555+13.94280516969829i$ and $-57.68321991189984+1655.63251210859482i$. The hyperboloidal current used the transformed metric and mode derivatives rather than imposing the endpoint formula. Off-diagonal residuals were $O(10^{-20})$ on the long contour. An independent real-axis Tricomi-$U$ continuation differed by about $4.2\times10^{-13}$ absolute for the overtone at degree 320; the supported accuracy claim is ten significant digits. These are finite results on a continuously tracked logarithmic branch, not a Hankel contour with an unstated monodromy factor or a certified infinite-series bound. Evidence: `scalar-benchmark.md:33–101,150–249,274–293`; `verification/leaver-derive.wl:1–12`, `verification/scalar-kerr-roots-stable.wl:1–21`, `verification/scalar-kerr-modes.wl:24–42`, `verification/scalar-kerr-contour-pairing.wl:29–64`, `verification/scalar-kerr-tricomi-norm.wl:35–50`; **ignored** `verification/scalar-kerr-contour-convergence-{request,result}.json`, `verification/scalar-kerr-tricomi-{long,refined}-{request,result}.json`.

## What the gravitational evidence proves about a metric and a coupling

- **Chiral source, not source-free.** The arbitrary-jet null-block identities in adapted Kerr coordinates imply $F_{ab}n^b=F_{ab}m^b=0$ and zero trace for $F=-\tfrac12 D^2G[h,h]$ in the single-parameter family. The mixed derivative for distinct perturbations has no $1/2$. An on-shell parent gives a conserved source. This permits a zero GHZ corrector with zero homogeneous corrector data and gives zero quadratic $\Psi_4$ extraction in the compatible tetrad. It does **not** set $F$ or the source Green current $j_S$ to zero. Independent coordinate-tensor tests at $a=0.3,r=3,z=1/3$ found a nonzero on-shell quadratic source (maximum $187.61$ in the unnormalized test) and working-precision zeros for the predicted projection, trace and quadratic extreme extraction. An off-shell polynomial example also has nonzero quadratic terms with zero projected terms; conservation still needs the on-shell hypothesis. Evidence: `chiral-source-identity.md:79–200`; `verification/null-block-einstein-identity.wl:19–29`, `verification/null-block-curvature-identity.wl:22–33`, `verification/kerr-null-coordinates.wl:1–8`, `verification/chiral-kerr-einstein-point.wl:37–43`, `verification/chiral-kerr-offshell-point.wl:39–45`, `verification/chiral-kerr-weyl-point.wl:43–49`.
- **Outgoing Green and Hertz problems are different inverses.** The separated radial Green proof uses nonzero $W_\ell$, the strict horizon spiral inequality and the fixed vertical outgoing sector. At infinity the product with the incoming test branch is $O(r^{-3})$ and integrable, not exponentially damped; the tail is $O(r^{-2})$. This yields $E_\ell W_\ell=\int_\mathcal C u_\ell\Delta^{-2}Q_\ell\,dr$, not conservation of a sourced bare pairing. The Green integral itself was not independently numerically integrated. Separately, the Hertz transport inverse uses $(qU)''''=32 ZD$ with no incoming polynomial in $qU$. Its outgoing factor is $2E/\Omega^4$; use $\psi^{(2)}=\bar\Gamma^4\Psi_4^{(2)}$, not twice that, and translate signatures by negating every metric order, not just the background. A factor 16 and a signature mismatch in the initial harness gave a relative Einstein residual about $1.5$; the scaling diagnostic is not an independent fitted normalization. The corrected jets use the exact factor 32 (the early corrected result's `saved` text still labels the old target, but its `Put` target is the corrected file). Evidence: `separated-source-green.md:55–168`; `hertz-metric-reconstruction.md:9–123,181–189`; `verification/kerr-source-green-identity.wl:1–13`, `verification/hertz-inverse-identities.wl:1–9`, `verification/kerr-curvature-normalization.wl:43–52`, `verification/kerr-hertz-inversion-initial.wl:19–24`, `verification/kerr-hertz-inversion-corrected.wl:19–24`.
- **Independent all-component tests, and a conditional untruncated implication.** Computing the full coordinate Einstein tensor from reconstructed metric jets is independent of setting the separated Teukolsky residual to zero. At $(r,z)=(3,1/3)$ and $(2.5,-1/5)$, the relative max-component residuals $\max|E_1k+\tfrac12D^2G[h,h]|/\max|\tfrac12D^2G[h,h]|$ fall from $(1.88\times10^{-4},1.16\times10^{-3})$ at $\ell_{\max}=6$ to $(7.26\times10^{-19},3.34\times10^{-17})$ at 18 (radial cutoff 800, angular eigenbasis cutoff 20). Quadrature-order changes in the independent Hertz jets were below $5\times10^{-68}$ and $7\times10^{-64}$; these are differences, not certified accuracy. The analytic outgoing kernel argument is separate: source transport kills the residual's $C$ component since its homogeneous solutions carry incoming phase; conservation then reduces $B$ and $A$ to analogous algebraic kernels, excluded at the same outgoing end. This proves the all-component Einstein implication *if* a sufficiently regular curvature response in that class exists; not convergence or existence of its full angular reconstruction. The initial transport run shadowed spin $a$ by a summation index; only the corrected exact zero supports the factorization. Evidence: `hertz-metric-reconstruction.md:125–189`; `chiral-reconstruction-proof.md:43–145`; `verification/kerr-hertz-inversion-l18.wl:10–24`, `verification/kerr-hertz-inversion-second-point.wl:10–24`, `verification/kerr-metric-residual-convergence.wl:55–65`, `verification/kerr-metric-residual-second-point.wl:55–65`, `verification/chiral-source-transport.wl:14–27`, `verification/chiral-residual-transport.wl:9–17`, `verification/chiral-transport-kernels.wl:1–9`.
- **Normalized source current and completed observable.** In signature $(+---)$ with the stated curvature convention, $O'(\bar\Gamma^{-4}YS)=-L_{\Omega,\ell}Y\,S/(\Sigma\bar\Gamma^4)$, with opposite Fourier phase and the adjoint test $\eta=-\bar\Gamma^4\Delta^{-2}u_\ell S$ times that phase, $H=S'^\dagger\eta$, $\int S^2 d\Omega_{\rm ang}=1$. The full covariant operator (not only its principal part) and the corrected generic tensor adjoint current have exact zero residuals. The initial multiline current harness omitted parentheses and did not implement the full current. In $j_E=j_{O'}-j_{S'}+dH_{\rm sup}$, the source-current radial endpoint vanishes by analytic power estimates, including the incoming branch, not by assuming $j_{S'}=0$ in the interior. A one-dimensional checked example gives $j_E-j_O=-uF\ne0$ generically. The matched numerator is $E_\ell W_\ell=\int\Sigma H^{ab}F_{ab}\,dr\,d\Omega_{\rm ang}$. For the same EH action, $\kappa_{\rm EH}$ changes from $+1/(16\pi G)$ in $(-+++)$ to $-1/(16\pi G)$ in $(+---)$; the outward flux multiplier is $-\kappa_{\rm EH}^{-1}$, and the simple-pole bilinear form is $-i\kappa_{\rm EH}N_\ell$ *when the frequency-derivative endpoints vanish on the common domain*. The off-pole coefficient is defined instead by $c_\Omega\Pi_\ell(F)+\mathcal W_{2,\ell}$ divided by the fixed parent strain squared. Under an admissible proper gauge change $F\mapsto F+EB$, the Green boundary identity gives $c_\Omega\Pi_\ell(EB)=\mathcal W_{1,\ell}(B)$, canceled by the change of quadratic extraction. The four-dimensional mixed-order test used a nonzero mixed source and returned zero identity residual, rather than testing a trivial zero. Invariance is for equivalent parents, fixed extraction and boundary data, and admitted reconstructions/slices/contours; a change in physical radiation, Kerr parameters or frame is different input. Evidence: `gravitational-response.md:36–208`; `metric-cps-normalization.md:15–291`; `invariant-selected-coupling.md:11–117,147–159`; `verification/adjoint-source-current.wl:1–10`, `verification/mixed-einstein-gauge.wl:13–17`, `verification/kerr-teukolsky-normalization.wl:1–31`, `verification/tensor-source-current-corrected.wl:1–11`, `verification/metric-pairing-algebra.wl:1–8`.

## Limits that survive the positive results

The finite-matrix residue conjugation check confirms the algebra, not invertibility between the *actual* Kerr operator domains (the frequency-dependent multiplier may be unbounded). A positive damped Hilbert norm cannot be the conserved scalar bilinear form. A raw, nonresonant modal coefficient is not invariant: $C'_{jkl}-C_{jkl}=(\lambda_k+\lambda_l-\lambda_j)B_{jkl}$ under a quadratic change of coordinates, so it can even be set to zero. This does not eliminate the fixed waveform observable. Axial covariance enforces $m_Q=m_b+m_c$ with signed conjugate labels; gravitational reflection uses physical parity of the completed observable (spin-weighted strain is exchanged with its conjugate). Do not infer a rotating spherical triangle rule or transplant the scalar even-$\ell$ rule. See `solution.md:27–39,205–248,252–262`, `gravitational-response.md:210–259`, and `invariant-selected-coupling.md:149–159`.

Still open: certified resonance/Wronskian bounds, independent numerical integration of the rotating separated Green numerator or the metric-source numerator, existence and uniform estimates for the full infinite daughter metric, extension beyond the declared analytic Hilbert/source domains, independent solution of opposite-chirality or DM channels, and a new nonlinear time-domain/NR simulation. A finite local source-product bound is not a Green-operator or infinite-angular estimate. The older supplements' “not verified here” lists describe their historical scope; the later normalization and selected observable settle the narrower criterion only. See `analytic-source-domain.md:59–84`, `separated-source-green.md:156–168`, `metric-cps-normalization.md:283–291`, and `invariant-selected-coupling.md:149–159`.
