the usual Polyakov action is given by

$$\begin{align}
S & =-\frac{1}{4\pi \alpha'}\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{2}\xi \sqrt{ -\gamma }\gamma ^{ab}\partial _{a}X^{\mu}\partial _{b}X_{\mu}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{\xi ^{a},a=1,2}$ is the worldsheet coordinates, $\displaystyle{\gamma _{ab}}$ is the worldsheet metric, and $\displaystyle{X^{\mu}}$ are the embedding functions into the target spacetime.

now take a variation with respect to $\displaystyle{X^{\mu}}$

$$\begin{align}
\delta S & =-\frac{1}{2\pi \alpha'}\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{2}\xi \sqrt{ -\gamma }\gamma ^{ab}\partial _{a}X^{\mu}\partial _{b}\delta X^{\nu} \\
 & =\frac{1}{2\pi \alpha'}\int _{M}\mathrm{d}^{2}\xi \sqrt{ -\gamma }\partial ^{2}X^{\mu}\delta X^{\nu}+\frac{1}{2\pi \alpha'}\int _{\Sigma _{f}-\Sigma _{i}}\mathrm{d}\xi \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}\partial _{a}X^{\mu}\delta X_{\mu}
\end{align}$$
