## 4. Where the evidence stops

The positive results use different domains: the complete Lorentzian geometric vacuum benchmark, four-dimensional regular time/orientation/branch and regional data, and complete Euclidean $L^2$ Gaussian/Hodge realizations. No theorem here supplies a characterization by maximal GH alone, general timelike Einstein IBVP, arbitrary CS framing/modular phases, a physical coupling calibration, or a nonlinear Hilbert-space/measure equivalence. The Cotler–Jensen v3 review checks historical source claims in their version and page scope; its later positivity repair is explicitly a supplied proof, not something attributed to the paper or promoted to all-orders localization (`reviews/Independent reviews.md:146–152`).

The immediate mathematical gap is simultaneous renormalization in the low-wheel comparison: the nonlocal quadratic/cubic kernels and quotient insertion are not evaluated, the nonzero compact-test tadpole has no continuation of the required kind, and a freely specified subtraction is not yet a transported quantum source. A common spatial prescription, source generator, full graded domain, removed geometric ghost factor, metric auxiliary transport, and a retained BV-Stokes regulator would have to satisfy **both** residuals of (46), not merely remove a divergent coefficient (`21 Low-wheel heat traces and an AdS gravitational tadpole.md:639–704`). Neither an unavoidable anomaly nor its cancellation has been shown. This archival consolidation does not resume the paused research.
