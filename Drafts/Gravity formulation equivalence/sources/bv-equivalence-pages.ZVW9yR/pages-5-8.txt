    In this section we will prove that there is a strong equivalence between the BV
theories FBF and FGR . In order to do this we will adapt to three dimensions the
strong equivalnce between the Palatini–Cartan BV formulation of gravity presented
in [CS17b] and the version that was suggested by Piguet [Piguet]. The main differ-
ence between the two BV theories is that the latter involves non-covariant expres-
sions and non-global fields, but they are essentially equivalent up to (symplectic)
field redefinitions.
