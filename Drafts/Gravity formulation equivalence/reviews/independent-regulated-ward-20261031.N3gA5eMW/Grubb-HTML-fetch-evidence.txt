###### Abstract

Classical pseudodifferential operators on closed manifolds are considered. It is shown that the basic properties of the canonical trace AA introduced by Kontsevich and Vishik are easily proved by identifying it with the leading nonlocal coefficient TRA\operatorname{TR}A in the trace expansion of C0(A,P)C_{0}(A,P) (with an auxiliary elliptic operator A(P−λ)−NA(P-\lambda)^{-N}), as determined in a joint work with Seeley 1995. The definition of PP is extended from the cases of noninteger order, or integer order and even-even parity on odd-dimensional manifolds, to the case of even-odd parity on even-dimensional manifolds.TRA\operatorname{TR}A

For the generalized zeta function, extended meromorphically to ζ(A,P,s)=Tr(AP−s)\zeta(A,P,s)=\operatorname{Tr}(AP^{-s}),ℂ\mathbb{C} equals the coefficient of C0(A,P)C_{0}(A,P) in the Laurent expansion at s0s^{0} when s=0s=0 is invertible. In the mentioned parity cases,PP is regular at all integer points. The higher Laurent coefficients ζ(A,P,s)\zeta(A,P,s) at Cj(A,P)C_{j}(A,P) are described as leading nonlocal coefficients s=0s=0 in trace expansions of resolvent expressions C0(B,P)C_{0}(B,P), with B(P−λ)−NB(P-\lambda)^{-N}log-polyhomogeneous as defined by Lesch (here BB gives the zeta-determinant). −C1(I,P)=C0(logP,P)-C_{1}(I,P)=C_{0}(\log P,P) is shown to be a quasi-trace in general, a canonical trace C0(B,P)C_{0}(B,P) in restricted cases, and the formula of Lesch for TRB\operatorname{TR}B in terms of a finite part integral of the symbol is extended to the parity cases.TRB\operatorname{TR}B
