The audit requested the following two scope clarifications.

### 1. Inherited boundary domains and zero-mode normalization

The earlier wording “common zero-mode/gauge-volume normalization” should be replaced by:

> a zero-mode/gauge-volume normalization fixed within this metric heat-kernel prescription

unless a separate regulated identification with the orbit normalization has actually been proved.

The proposed additional qualification was:

> The complete-manifold normalizable metric/proper-ghost domains and absence of additional collective-coordinate factors are adopted here as part of the prescription; these kernels do not by themselves prove equivalence to every Brown–Henneaux boundary-domain completion or to an orbit measure. Large Brown–Henneaux transformations are not included in the ghost quotient.

This was the key inherited-domain limitation, not a correction to the local operators or evaluated determinant.

### 2. The complete finite prescription behind “AC”

The “AC” convention should explicitly mean all three of:

1. analytic continuation of the proper-time integral;
2. the stated geodesic-cutoff renormalized volume;
3. no additional finite local term.

The “omit” convention should explicitly mean dropping the identity image before the proper-time integration.

Analytic continuation alone does not choose the finite local/coupling matching condition. The subsequent discussion around equation (5.6) was otherwise appropriately careful: it did not claim that a fixed measured central charge is arbitrarily scheme-dependent.
