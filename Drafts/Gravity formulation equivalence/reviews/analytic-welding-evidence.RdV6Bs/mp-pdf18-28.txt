3.4   Equivalent descriptions of locality

Generally, the sewing isomorphisms of an R+ -extension can depend on the boundary
parametrizations of the respective surfaces in any way — as long as associativity holds.
Following the example of the real determinant line bundle in Section 1.4 (to which we
also return in Section 3.7), it makes sense to assume that the sewing isomorphisms only
depend on the boundary parametrizations that are involved in the sewing. In other words,
if a boundary parametrization that is external to the sewing operation Σ1 j ∞k Σ2 is
reparametrized, say, by a diffeomorphism in Diff an  + (S ) acting on Σ1 at l ̸= j, then the
                                                          1

sewing isomorphism should be somehow invariant under this reparametrization. We call
such R+ -extensions “local,” for in the case of the real determinant line bundle, this follows
from the locality of the conformal anomaly formula (1.15).
    In Proposition 3.9, we characterize this property in several equivalent ways, and it
serves as the definition of locality. Equation (3.63) in Proposition 3.9 is perhaps the most
instructive realization of locality, as it emphasizes the aforementioned reparametrization
invariance in terms of cocycles. Such cocycles are always relative to a trivialization Z,
which we call “reparametrization invariant” if they realize locality.
