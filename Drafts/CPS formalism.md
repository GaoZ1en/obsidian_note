consider a $\displaystyle{(p,q)}$-form $\displaystyle{\Xi}$, which means $\displaystyle{\Xi}$ is a $\displaystyle{p}$-form in spacetime and a $\displaystyle{q}$-form in configuration space. consider a local transformation, i.e., a vector in configuration space

$$\begin{align}
X & =\int \mathrm{d}\mu \delta _{X}\phi ^{a} \frac{\delta}{\delta \phi ^{a}}
\end{align}$$

for simplicity, we first pick $\displaystyle{p=q=1}$.

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X}d\Xi & =\mathrm{d}\mathcal{L}_{X}\Xi
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X}\mathrm{d}\Xi & =X\cdot \delta(\mathrm{d}\Xi)+\delta(X\cdot \mathrm{d}\Xi) \\
 & =\mathrm{d}(X\cdot \delta \Xi)
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
X\cdot \mathrm{d}\omega & =\mathrm{d}(X\cdot \omega)
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
X\cdot \int _{M} \mathrm{d}\omega & =X\cdot \int _{\partial M}\omega \\
 & =\int _{\partial M}X\cdot \omega \\
 & =\int _{M}\mathrm{d}(X\cdot \omega)
\end{align}$$
