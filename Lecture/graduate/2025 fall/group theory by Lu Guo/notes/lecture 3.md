finite group. the number of elements in a group is called the **order** of the group.

$\displaystyle{\forall a\in G}$, the **order** of $a$ is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $a^n = e$, where $e$ is the identity element of the group. if no such $n$ exists, we say that $a$ has infinite order.

$\displaystyle{\left\{a^{k}|k\in \mathbb{Z}\right\}}$ is a subgroup of $G$, called the **cyclic subgroup** generated by $a$, denoted $\langle a \rangle$.

the generators of a group are the elements from which every element of the group can be obtained by applying the group operation a finite number of times. the set of all generators of a group is called a generating set. and the number of generators is called the **rank** of the group. cyclic groups are groups that can be generated by a single element.

point group. a group that describes the symmetries of one single point in space. in 3d, there are 32 point groups, can be classified into 7 crystal systems (use $\displaystyle{C_{n}}$ symbols)

| Orthorhombic   | $C_{2v}$, $D_2$, $D_{2h}$                                      | Orthorhombic crystals |
| Tetragonal     | $C_4$, $S_4$, $C_{4h}$, $D_4$, $D_{2d}$, $D_{4h}$              | Tetragonal crystals   |
