# basic idea of localization

we start from the 1-dim Gauss integral

$$\tag{.}\begin{align}
1 & =\frac{1}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }}\int _{-\infty}^{\infty}\mathrm{d}xe^{-x^{2}/2}
\end{align}$$

let $\displaystyle{x\to \frac{S[\phi]}{\sqrt{ \hbar }}}$

$$\tag{.}\begin{align}
Z & =\int _{-\infty}^{\infty} \frac{\mathrm{d}\phi}{\sqrt{ 2\pi \hbar }}S'[\phi] e^{-S^{2}[\phi]/2\hbar} \\
 & =\sum _{S[\phi _{i}]=0}\mathrm{sgn}( S'[\phi _{i}] )
\end{align}$$
