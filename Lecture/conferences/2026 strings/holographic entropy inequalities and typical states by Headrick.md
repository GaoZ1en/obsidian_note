RT formula

universal properties of holographic entropies.
- subadditivity: $\displaystyle{S_{A}+S_{B}\geqslant S_{AB}}$
	- from the triangle inequality for minimal surfaces.
- strong subadditivity: $\displaystyle{S_{AB}+S_{BC}\geqslant S_{B}+S_{ABC}}$

the proofs do not use any bulk physics.

- MMI: $\displaystyle{S_{AB}+S_{BC}+S_{AC}\geqslant S_{A}+S_{B}+S_{C}+S_{ABC}}$

in terms of mutual information

$$\begin{align}
I_{A:B}:= S_{A}+S_{B}-S_{AB}
\end{align}$$

then

- SA: $\displaystyle{I_{A:B}\geqslant 0}$
- SSA: $\displaystyle{I_{AB:C}\geqslant I_{A:C}}$
- MMI: $\displaystyle{I_{A:BC}+I_{B:C}\geqslant I_{AB:C}}$

we can use graph theory to systematically find new entropy inequalities.

holographic entropy cone:
- a set of allowed entropy vector $\displaystyle{(S_{A},S_{B},S_{AB},\dots )\in \mathbb{R}^{2^{n}-1}}$ for $\displaystyle{n}$-regions $\displaystyle{A,B,\dots}$
- convex and polyhedral cone

not all the inequalities are created equal.
- SA and SSA are true for general quantum states
- higher inequalities are not true for general quantum states
	- but are obeyed by any state with RT-like entropies
