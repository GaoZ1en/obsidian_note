swampland and landscape

- **Landscape**: low-energy effective theories or vacua that really admit a consistent quantum-gravity UV completion, for example some string compactification vacua.
- **Swampland**: low-energy theories that look consistent as EFTs but violate universal quantum-gravity constraints, so they cannot be UV completed into consistent quantum gravity.
