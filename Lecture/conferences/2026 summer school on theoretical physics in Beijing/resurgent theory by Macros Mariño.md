[[Lecture/conferences/2026 strings/an overview of resurgence by Mariño|an overview of resurgence by Mariño]]

---

Borel resummation. For a asymptotic series

$$\begin{align}
\varphi(z) & =\sum _{n}a_{n}z^{n}
\end{align}$$

we take the Borel transformation

$$\begin{align}
\hat{\varphi}(\zeta) & =\sum _{n} \dfrac{a_{n}}{n!}\zeta^{n}
\end{align}$$

in general we have several cases
- log singularity at $\displaystyle{\zeta=\zeta _{\omega}}$, where $\displaystyle{\zeta _{\omega}}$ is the Stokes' constant

$$\begin{align}
\hat{\varphi}(\zeta=\xi+\zeta _{\omega}) & =-\dfrac{S_{\omega}}{2\pi}\log \xi \sum _{n\geqslant 0} \hat{c}_{n}\xi ^{n}+\text{regular}
\end{align}$$

- branch cut

$$\begin{align}
\hat{\varphi}(\zeta=\zeta _{\omega}+\zeta) & = \dfrac{S_{\omega}}{\sin nb}(-\xi)^{-b}\sum _{n\geqslant 0} \hat{c}_{n}\xi ^{n}+\text{regular}
\end{align}$$

then

$$\begin{align}
\varphi _{\omega}(z) & =z^{-b}\sum _{n\geqslant 0}\Gamma(n+1-b) \hat{c}_{n}z^{n}
\end{align}$$

$\displaystyle{b=0}$ is the logarithmic case.
