$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
\partial _{\mu}j ^{\mu} & =0,\quad \mathrm{d}\star j=0
\end{align}$$

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
Q & =\int _{\Sigma _{d-1}}\star j
\end{align}$$

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
U_{\alpha} & =e^{i\alpha Q}
\end{align}$$

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
\text{ symmetry }\leftrightarrow \text{ topological operators }
