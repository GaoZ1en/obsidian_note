## numerical differentiation

中心差分

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
f'(x) & =\frac{f(x+h)-f(x-h)}{2h}+\mathcal{O}(h^{2}) \\
f''(x) & =\frac{f(x+h)-2f(x)+f(x-h)}{h^{2}}+\mathcal{O}(h^{2})
\end{align}
$$

问题在于端点处的导数怎么做? Lagrange插值

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
f(x) & \approx P(x)=\sum ^{n}_{k=0}L_{nk}(x)f(x_{k}) \\
L_{nk}(x) & =\prod ^{n}_{i=0,i\neq k}\frac{x-x_{i}}{x_{k}-x_{i}}
\end{align}
$$

iterately use the Rolle's theorem, we have

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
f(x)-P(x)=\frac{f^{(n+1)}(\xi)}{(n+1)!}\prod ^{n}_{k=0}(x-x_{k})
\end{align}
$$

那么就有

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
f'(x_{0}) & =\frac{f(x_{0})+4f(x_{1})-3f(x_{2})}{2h}+\mathcal{O}(h^{2}) \\
f'(x_{2}) & =\frac{-3f(x_{0})+4f(x_{1})-f(x_{2})}{2h}+\mathcal{O}(h^{2})
\end{align}
$$

and some 4-point generalization

$$\tag{.}
\begin{align}
f'(x_{0}) & =\frac{-f(x_{0})+8f(x_{1})-8f(x_{2})+f(x_{3})}{12h}+\mathcal{O}(h^{4}) \\
f'(x_{3}) & =\frac{f(x_{0})-8f(x_{1})+8f(x_{2})-f(x_{3})}{12h}+\mathcal{O}(h^{4})
\end{align}
$$
