when we think of terms ending in -ism, we typically imagine ideologies or some philosophies, for example, capitalism or deconstructism. But today, I would like to talk about morphism, which is not a philosophical concept, but also changed my perspective on our world.

So what is a morphism? Morphism is a mathematical concept, which is a structure-preserving mapping between objects, like algebras, topologies, and so on. Or in a simpler way, it is a bridge that connects different objects while maintaining their essential properties. It is abstract and scary, but category theory, which is based on objects and morphisms, has become the building brick of frontier math topics, unifying almost every field of math. Morphism appears even in computer science. For example, the most-loved programming language Python is directed by the functional programming paradigm, which is inspired by morphism.

Morphism emphasizes the importance of connection between objects. So in our atomized society, where we're increasingly isolated in our digital bubbles and ideological echo chambers, I believe we need more "human morphisms" - people who can build meaningful bridges while preserving what matters. When I encounter someone with vastly different beliefs, I try to act as a morphism - finding the essential structures we share as humans while respecting our differences.
