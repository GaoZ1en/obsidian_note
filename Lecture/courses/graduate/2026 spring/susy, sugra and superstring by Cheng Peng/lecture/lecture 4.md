$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

by Weyl transformation, the world sheet diffeomorphsim can be written as two branches

$$\begin{align}
\varepsilon ^{+}(\sigma ^{+}), \varepsilon ^{-}(\sigma ^{-})
\end{align}$$

then the corresponding Noether current $\displaystyle{T^{ab}}$ satisfies the conserved equation

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{a}T^{ab}\implies \partial _{+}T_{\mp}+\partial _{-}T_{++} & =0 \\
\implies \partial _{-}T_{+-}+\partial _{+}T_{--} & =0
\end{align}$$

the Weyl invariance implies

$$\begin{align}
0 & =\frac{\delta S}{\delta g^{ab}}\delta g^{ab}\propto \Lambda T_{ab}g^{ab}\implies g^{ab}T_{ab}=0 \\
\implies  & T_{+-}=T_{-+}=0
\end{align}$$

plug back, we have

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{+}T_{--} & =0, \partial _{-}T_{++}=0 \\
\implies T_{\pm\pm} & =T_{\pm\pm}(\sigma ^{\pm})
\end{align}$$
