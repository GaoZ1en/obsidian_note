supersymmetry

---

point particle. the most natural action is

$$\begin{align}
S & =-m\int \mathrm{d}s \\
 & =-m\int \sqrt{ -G_{\mu \nu}\frac{\mathrm{d}X^{\mu}}{\mathrm{d}\lambda} \frac{\mathrm{d}X^{\nu}}{\mathrm{d}\lambda} }\mathrm{d}\lambda
\end{align}$$

this action has a reparameterization symmetry, and a gauge constraint.

by fixing the gauge $\displaystyle{\tau=X^{0}}$ and taking non-relativistic limit, we have

$$\begin{align}
S & =\int \mathrm{d}t\left( -m+\frac{1}{2}mv^{2}+\dots \right)
\end{align}$$

which is exactly the non-relativistic action for point particle in flat spacetime.
