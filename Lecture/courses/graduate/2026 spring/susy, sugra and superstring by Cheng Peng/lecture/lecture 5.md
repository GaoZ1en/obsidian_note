for the closed string, the general solution can be written as

$$\begin{align}
X_{R,L}^{\mu} & =\frac{x^{\mu}}{2}+\frac{\pi \alpha'}{\ell}p^{\mu}(\tau\mp \sigma)+i\sqrt{ \frac{\alpha'}{2} }\sum _{n\neq 0} \frac{\tilde{\alpha}_{n}}{n}e^{-i2n\pi(\tau\mp \sigma)/\ell}
\end{align}$$

since $\displaystyle{X^{\mu}}$ is real, we have

$$\begin{align}
x^{\mu} & \in \mathbb{R} \\
p^{\mu} & \in \mathbb{R} \\
\alpha _{n}^{\mu} & =\alpha _{-n}^{\mu \dagger} \\
\tilde{\alpha}_{n}^{\mu} & =\tilde{\alpha}_{-n}^{\mu \dagger}
\end{align}$$

for future convenience, we introduce the "zero" mode $\alpha _{0}^{\mu}$ and $\tilde{\alpha}_{0}^{\mu}$ as

$$\begin{align}
\alpha _{0}^{\mu} & =\tilde{\alpha}_{0}^{\mu}=\sqrt{ \frac{\alpha'}{2} }p^{\mu}
\end{align}$$

the target space Poincaré symmetry is generated by the momentum operator $P^{\mu}$ and the angular momentum operator $J^{\mu \nu}$. the former is given by

$$\begin{align}
\Pi^{\mu} & =T\partial _{\tau}X^{\mu} \\
 & =\sqrt{ \frac{\alpha'}{2} }\frac{\pi}{\ell}(\alpha _{0}^{\mu}+\tilde{\alpha}_{0}^{\mu})+\sqrt{ \frac{\alpha'}{2} }\sum _{n\neq 0}\frac{\alpha _{n}}{} \frac{2\pi}{\ell}e^{-in\pi(\tau-\sigma)/\ell} \\
 & =\frac{2\pi}{\ell}\sqrt{ \frac{\alpha'}{2} }\sum _{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\left(\alpha _{n}e^{-2in\pi(\tau-\sigma)/\ell}+\tilde{\alpha}_{n}e^{-2in\pi(\tau+\sigma)/\ell}\right)
\end{align}$$

for the NN open string, the general solution can be written as

$$\begin{align}
X^{\mu} & =x^{\mu}+2\alpha'p^{\mu}\tau+i\sqrt{2\alpha'}\sum _{n\neq 0}\frac{\alpha _{n}^{\mu}}{n}e^{-i2n\pi \tau/\ell}\cos \left(\frac{2n\pi \sigma}{\ell}\right)
\end{align}$$

defined the zero mode $\alpha _{0}^{\mu}$ as

$$\begin{align}
\alpha _{0}^{\mu} & =\sqrt{2\alpha'}p^{\mu}
\end{align}$$

the momentum operator is given by

$$\begin{align}
\Pi^{\mu} & =T\partial _{\tau}X^{\mu} \\
 & =\frac{1}{\ell}\sqrt{2\alpha'}\sum _{n=-\infty}^{\infty}\alpha _{n}^{\mu}e^{-i2n\pi \tau/\ell}\cos \left(\frac{2n\pi \sigma}{\ell}\right)
\end{align}$$

via quantization, we impose the canonical commutation relation

$$\begin{align}
[X^{\mu}(\tau,\sigma),\Pi^{\nu}(\tau,\sigma')] & =i\eta ^{\mu \nu}\delta (\sigma-\sigma') \\
\implies [\alpha _{m}^{\mu},\alpha _{n}^{\nu}] & =m\delta _{m+n,0}\eta ^{\mu \nu} \\
[\tilde{\alpha}_{m}^{\mu},\tilde{\alpha}_{n}^{\nu}] & =m\delta _{m+n,0}\eta ^{\mu \nu} \\
[\alpha _{m}^{\mu},\tilde{\alpha}_{n}^{\nu}] & =0 \\
[x^{\mu},p^{\nu}] & =i\eta ^{\mu \nu}
\end{align}$$

the ground state $\displaystyle{\ket{\Omega,p}}$ is chosen to be annihilated by all positive modes

$$\begin{align}
\alpha _{n}^{\mu}\ket{\Omega,p} & =0 \\
\tilde{\alpha}_{n}^{\mu}\ket{\Omega,p} & =0
\end{align}$$

but a nontrivial eigenstate of the momentum operator

$$\begin{align}
p^{\mu}\ket{\Omega,p} & =p^{\mu}\ket{\Omega,p}
\end{align}$$

and the excited states are generated by acting negative modes on the ground state

$$\begin{align}
\ket{\{N_{n}\},\{\tilde{N}_{n}\};p} & =\prod _{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(\alpha _{-n}^{\mu})^{N_{n}}}{\sqrt{N_{n}!}}\frac{(\tilde{\alpha}_{-n}^{\mu})^{\tilde{N}_{n}}}{\sqrt{\tilde{N}_{n}!}}\ket{\Omega;p}
\end{align}$$

problems may arise when we have negative norm states, which can be seen from the commutation relation of the modes. for example, we have

$$\begin{align}
[\alpha _{m}^{0},\alpha _{n}^{0}] & =-m\delta _{m+n,0} \\
\implies \bra{\Omega,p}\alpha _{m}^{0}\alpha _{-m}^{0}\ket{\Omega,p} & =-m\braket{\Omega,p|\Omega,p} \\
 & <0
\end{align}$$

to avoid this problem, we need to impose the so-called Virasoro constraints. the worldsheet energy-momentum tensor is given by

$$\begin{align}
T_{\pm\pm} & =\frac{T}{2}\eta _{\mu \nu}\partial _{\pm}X^{\mu}\partial _{\pm}X^{\nu} \\
 & =\frac{1}{\alpha'}\sum _{n=-\infty}^{\infty}(L_{n}e^{-i2n\pi(\tau\mp \sigma)/\ell}+\tilde{L}_{n}e^{-2in\pi(\tau\mp \sigma)/\ell}) \\
L_{n} & =\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum _{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\alpha _{n-m}\cdot \alpha _{m} \\
\tilde{L}_{n} & =\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum _{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\tilde{\alpha}_{n-m}\cdot \tilde{\alpha}_{m} \\
T_{\pm\mp} & =0
\end{align}$$

for open string, we have

$$\begin{align}
T_{\pm\pm} & =\frac{1}{\alpha'}\sum _{n=-\infty}^{\infty}L_{n}e^{-i2n\pi \tau/\ell}\cos \frac{2\pi n\sigma}{\ell} \\
L_{n} & =\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum _{m=-\infty}^{\infty}\alpha _{n-m}\cdot \alpha _{m} \\
T_{\pm\mp} & =0
\end{align}$$

the Virasoro constraints are given by

$$\begin{align}
L_{n}\ket{\text{phys}} & =0, \quad n>0 \\
(L_{0}-a)\ket{\text{phys}} & =0
\end{align}$$

where the $\displaystyle{a}$ is the normal ordering constant, which is given by

$$\begin{align}
a & =\frac{D-2}{24}
\end{align}$$
