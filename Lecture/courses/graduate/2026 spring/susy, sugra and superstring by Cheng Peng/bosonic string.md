the Polyakov action for bosonic string is

$$\begin{align}
S & =-\frac{T}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }G_{\mu \nu}(X)D _{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}g^{ab}
\end{align}$$

here $\displaystyle{D_{a}}$ is the covariant derivative compatible with the worldsheet metric $\displaystyle{g^{ab}}$. take a variation of the action with respect to $\displaystyle{X^{\mu}}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
\delta S & =-\frac{T}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\partial _{\rho}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D _{b}X^{\nu}g^{ab}\delta X^{\rho}+2G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D _{b}\delta X^{\nu}g^{ab} \\
 & =T\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }G_{\mu \nu}\left(D ^{2}X^{\mu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho \sigma}D_{a}X^{\rho}D^{a}X^{\sigma}\right)\delta X^{\nu} \\
 & +T\int _{\Sigma _{f}-\Sigma _{i}} \mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}\delta X^{\nu}
\end{align}$$

here $\displaystyle{\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho \sigma}}$ is the Christoffel symbol of the target space metric $\displaystyle{G_{\mu \nu}}$, $\displaystyle{\Sigma _{f}}$ and $\displaystyle{\Sigma _{i}}$ are the final and initial boundaries of the worldsheet, $\displaystyle{\sigma}$ is the determinant of the induced metric on the boundary, $\displaystyle{\tau ^{a}}$ is the unit normal vector to the boundary. the equation of motion for $\displaystyle{X^{\mu}}$ is

$$\begin{align}
D^{2}X^{\mu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho \sigma}D_{a}X^{\rho}D^{a}X^{\sigma} & =0
\end{align}$$

and the symplectic potential is

$$\begin{align}
\theta & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}\delta X^{\nu}
\end{align}$$

take a variation of the symplectic potential, we obtain the symplectic form

$$\begin{align}
\omega & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}(G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}\delta X^{\mu}\wedge \delta X^{\nu}+\partial _{\rho}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}\delta X^{\rho}\wedge \delta X^{\nu}) \\
 & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}\left(G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}\delta X^{\mu}\wedge \delta X^{\nu}+(\Gamma ^{\lambda}_{~\rho \mu}G_{\lambda \nu}+\Gamma ^{\lambda}_{~\rho \nu}G_{\mu \lambda})D_{a}X^{\mu}\delta X^{\rho}\wedge \delta X^{\nu}\right) \\
 & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}G_{\mu \nu}(D_{a}\delta X^{\mu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho \sigma}D_{a}X^{\rho}\delta X^{\sigma})\wedge \delta X^{\nu} \\
 & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}G_{\mu \nu}\nabla _{a}\delta X^{\mu}\wedge \delta X^{\nu}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{\nabla _{a}\delta X^{\mu}=D_{a}\delta X^{\mu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho \sigma}D_{a}X^{\rho}\delta X^{\sigma}}$ is the pullback of the covariant derivative on the target space to the worldsheet.

suppose the target space $\displaystyle{\mathcal{M}}$ has an isometry that generated by a Killing vector $\displaystyle{\xi}$, then we can write an vector of the configuration space as

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi} & =\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\xi ^{\mu} \frac{\delta}{\delta X^{\mu}}
\end{align}$$

act on the action with the vector $\displaystyle{X_{\xi}}$, we have

$$\begin{align}
X_{\xi}\cdot \delta S & =-\frac{T}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\left(\partial _{\rho}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}g^{ab}\xi ^{\rho}+2G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}\xi ^{\nu}g^{ab}\right) \\
 & =-\frac{T}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\left(\xi ^{\rho}\partial _{\rho}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}g^{ab}+2G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\rho}\partial _{\rho}\xi ^{\nu}g^{ab}\right) \\
 & =-\frac{T}{2}\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\mathcal{L}_{\xi}G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}g^{ab} \\
 & =0
\end{align}$$

then the corresponding Noether charge is then

$$\begin{align}
H_{\xi} & =X_{\xi}\cdot \theta \\
 & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}\xi _{\mu}D_{a}X^{\mu}
\end{align}$$

for worldsheet diffeomorphism generated by a vector $\displaystyle{\zeta ^{a}}$, we can write the corresponding vector on the configuration space as

$$\begin{align}
X_{\zeta} & =\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\mathcal{L}_{\zeta}X^{\mu} \frac{\delta}{\delta X^{\mu}} \\
 & =\int \mathrm{d}^{2}\tau \sqrt{ -g }\zeta ^{a}D_{a}X^{\mu} \frac{\delta}{\delta X^{\mu}}
\end{align}$$

act on the action, we have

$$\begin{align}
X_{\zeta}\cdot \delta S & =-\int _{\Sigma _{f}-\Sigma _{i}}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{a}\zeta ^{a}\mathcal{L}
\end{align}$$

then the corresponding Noether charge is given by

$$\begin{align}
H_{\zeta} & =X_{\zeta}\cdot \theta - \int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau _{a}\zeta ^{a}\mathcal{L} \\
 & =T\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}\zeta ^{b}\left(G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}-\frac{1}{2}g_{ab}G_{\mu \nu}D_{c}X^{\mu}D^{c}X^{\nu}\right) \\
 & =\int _{\Sigma}\mathrm{d}\tau \sqrt{ \sigma }\tau ^{a}\zeta ^{b}T_{ab}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{T_{ab}=T\left( G_{\mu \nu}D_{a}X^{\mu}D_{b}X^{\nu}-\frac{1}{2}g_{ab}G_{\mu \nu}D_{c}X^{\mu}D^{c}X^{\nu} \right)}$ is the worldsheet energy-momentum tensor, whose trace is trivially zero.
