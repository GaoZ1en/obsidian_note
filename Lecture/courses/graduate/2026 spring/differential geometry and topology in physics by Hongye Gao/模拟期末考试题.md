### 题 1（15 分）— Hausdorff 判据与闭图像

设 $X$ 为拓扑空间，对角集为

$$\begin{align}
\Delta_X=\{(x,x)\mid x\in X\}\subset X\times X.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明：若 $\Delta_X$ 在 $X\times X$ 中闭，则 $X$ 为 Hausdorff 空间。
sol. since $\displaystyle{\Delta _{X}}$ is closed in $\displaystyle{X\times X}$, we have $\displaystyle{X\times X-\Delta _{X}}$ is open, then $\displaystyle{\forall (a,b)\in X\times X-\Delta _{X},\exists W_{a}\times W_{b}\subset X\times X-\Delta _{X}\text{ s.t. } (a,b)\in W_{a}\times W_{b} \subset X\times X-\Delta_{X}}$, in this case $\displaystyle{a\neq b}$. thus $\displaystyle{a\in W_{a},b\in W_{b},W_{a}\cap W_{b}=\varnothing}$, which means $\displaystyle{X}$ is Hausdoordd.
2. 设 $Y$ 为 Hausdorff 空间，$f,g:X\to Y$ 连续。证明 $\{x\in X\mid f(x)=g(x)\}$ 是闭集。
sol. define $\displaystyle{h :X\to Y\times Y, h(x)=(f(x),g(x))}$, since $\displaystyle{f,g}$ are continuous, then $\displaystyle{h}$ is continuous. since $\displaystyle{Y}$ is Hausdorff, the diagonal $\displaystyle{\Delta _{Y}=\left\{(y,y)|y \in Y\right\}}$ is closed, then $\displaystyle{h^{-1}(\Delta _{Y})=\left\{x \in X|f(x)=g(x)\right\}}$ is closed for $\displaystyle{h}$ is continuous.
3. 令 $f:X\to X$ 为连续自映射。说明当 $X$ Hausdorff 时，不动点集 $\mathcal F(f)$ 是闭集。
sol. consider a function $\displaystyle{h:X\to X\times X, h(x)\to(x,f(x))}$. since $\displaystyle{X}$ is Hausdorff, then the diagonal $\displaystyle{\Delta _{X}}$ is closed, then $\displaystyle{\mathcal{F}(f)=h^{-1}(\Delta _{X})}$ is closed.

### 题 2（15 分）— Lie 导数

设 $g=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu\otimes dx^\nu$ 为度量张量，$V=V^\mu\partial_\mu$ 为向量场。

1. 推导 $(\mathcal L_Vg)_{\mu\nu}$ 的坐标表达式。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{L}_{V}g)_{\mu \nu} & =V^{\rho}\partial _{\rho}g_{\mu \nu}+\partial _{\mu}V^{\rho}g_{\rho \nu}+\partial _{\nu}V^{\rho}g_{\mu \rho}
\end{align}$$

2. 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 上取 Euclid 度量和向量场 $V=x\partial_x-y\partial_y$，计算 $\mathcal L_V g$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{L}_{V}g)_{\mu \nu} & =2\partial _{x}V^{x}g_{xx}\delta _{\mu}^{x}\delta _{\nu}^{x}+2\partial _{y}V^{y}g_{yy}\delta _{\mu}^{y}\delta _{\nu}^{y} \\
 & =2(\delta _{\mu}^{x}\delta _{\nu}^{x}+\delta _{\mu}^{y}\delta _{\nu}^{y})
\end{align}$$

3. 判断 $V$ 是否为 Killing 向量场。
sol. since $\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}_{V}g\neq 0}$, $\displaystyle{V}$ is not a Killing vector

### 题 3（20 分）— 曲面乘积的同调

设 $\Sigma_g,\Sigma_h$ 为亏格分别为 $g,h$ 的闭定向曲面。

1. 写出 $H_k(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\Sigma _{g};\mathbb{Z}) & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb{Z} & ,k=0,2 \\
\mathbb{Z}^{2g} & ,k=1 \\
0 & ,\text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

2. 用 Künneth 公式计算 $H_k(\Sigma_g\times\Sigma_h;\mathbb Z)$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) & = \bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_{i}(\Sigma _{g};\mathbb{Z})\times H_{j}(\Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =\begin{cases}
 \mathbb{Z} & k=0,4 \\
 \mathbb{Z}^{2g+2h} & k=1,3 \\
 \mathbb{Z}^{4gh+2} & k=2 \\
 0 & \text{otherwise}
 \end{cases}
\end{align}$$

3. 计算 $\chi(\Sigma_g\times\Sigma_h)$，并说明它与 $\chi(\Sigma_g)\chi(\Sigma_h)$ 的关系。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\chi(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h}) & =\sum _{k}(-)^{k}\mathrm{rank}H_{k}(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =-4g-4h+4gh+4 \\
 & =(2-2g)(2-2h) \\
 & =\chi(\Sigma _{g})\chi(\Sigma _{h})
\end{align}$$

### 题 4（25 分）— 环面度量与曲率

考虑环面坐标 $(\theta,\phi)$ 上的度量

$$\begin{align}
ds^2=r^2d\theta^2+(R+r\cos\theta)^2d\phi^2,\qquad R>r>0.
\end{align}$$

1. 计算非零 Christoffel 符号。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi \phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\sin \theta}{r} \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\theta \phi} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}
\end{align}$$

2. 按约定

$$\begin{align}
R^\kappa{}_{\lambda\mu\nu} =\partial_\mu\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\nu\lambda} -\partial_\nu\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\mu\lambda} +\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\mu\rho}\Gamma^\rho{}_{\nu\lambda} -\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\nu\rho}\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\lambda}
\end{align}$$

   计算独立非零曲率分量。
sol. by some theorem, in 2d there is only one independent component

$$\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\sin \theta}{r}\mathrm{d}\phi \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\theta} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}\mathrm{d}\phi \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\phi} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}\mathrm{d}\theta \\
\implies \Omega ^{\mu}_{~\nu} & =\dfrac{1}{2!}R^{\mu}_{~\nu \rho \sigma}\mathrm{d}x^{\rho}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\sigma} \\
 & =\mathrm{d}\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\nu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho}\wedge \Gamma ^{\rho}_{~\nu} \\
\Omega ^{\theta}_{~\phi} & =\mathrm{d}\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi}+\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi}\wedge \Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\phi} \\
 & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\cos \theta}{r}\mathrm{d}\theta \wedge \mathrm{d}\phi \\
\implies R^{\theta}_{~\phi \theta \phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\cos \theta}{r} \\
\implies R_{\theta \phi \theta \phi} & =(R+r\cos \theta)r\cos \theta
\end{align}$$

3. 求标曲率 $\mathcal R=g^{\nu\rho}R^\mu{}_{\nu\mu\rho}$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{R} & =g^{\mu \rho}g^{\nu \sigma}R_{\mu \nu \rho \sigma} \\
 & =2g^{\theta \theta}g^{\phi \phi}R_{\theta \phi \theta \phi} \\
 & =\dfrac{1}{r^{2}(R+r\cos \theta)^{2}} (R+r\cos \theta)r\cos \theta \\
 & =\dfrac{\cos \theta}{r(R+r\cos \theta)}
\end{align}$$

4. 计算 $\int_{T^2}\sqrt{\det g}\,\mathcal R\,d\theta\,d\phi$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\int _{T^{2}}\sqrt{ \det g }\mathcal{R}\mathrm{d}\theta \mathrm{d}\phi & =\int _{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta \int _{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\phi r(R+r\cos \theta) \dfrac{\cos \theta}{r(R+r\cos \theta)}=0
\end{align}$$

remark. we may use moving frame method

### 题 5（25 分）— $\mathbb{CP}^n$ 与 Fubini-Study 度量

设 $\mathbb{CP}^n=(\mathbb C^{n+1}\setminus\{0\})/\mathbb C^\times$。

1. 给出标准图册 $U_i=\{z_i\ne0\}$ 和非齐次坐标，并写出 $U_i\cap U_j$ 上的转移函数。
sol. we have $\displaystyle{\phi _{i}:U_{i}\to \mathbb{C}^{n}, \phi _{i}:(z_{0},z_{1},\dots,z_{n})\mapsto (\zeta_{0},\dots,\zeta _{i-1},\zeta _{i+1},\dots,\zeta _{n})=\left( \dfrac{z_{0}}{z_{i}},\dots,\dfrac{z_{i-1}}{z_{i}},\dfrac{z_{i+1}}{z_{i}},\dots,\dfrac{z_{n}}{z_{i}} \right)}$
the transition function $\displaystyle{\phi _{j}^{-1}\circ\phi _{i}:U_{i}\cap U_{j}\to U_{i}\cap U_{j}, \phi _{j}^{-1}\circ\phi _{i}:(z_{0},z_{1},\dots,z_{n})\mapsto \left(z_{0} \dfrac{z_{j}}{z_{i}}, \dots, \dfrac{z^{2}_{j}}{z_{i}},\dots, z_{i-1} \dfrac{z_{j}}{z_{i}}, z_{i+1} \dfrac{z_{j}}{z_{i}}, \dots, z_{n} \dfrac{z_{j}}{z_{i}} \right)}$
2. 证明 $\mathbb{CP}^n=S^{2n+1}/U(1)$，并由 Hopf 纤维化求 $\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)$ 和 $\pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)$。
sol. Since $\mathbb{CP}^n$ is the space of complex lines in $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}$, every line has unit representatives in $S^{2n+1}$, and two unit representatives differ by a phase in $U(1)$. Hence $\mathbb{CP}^n=S^{2n+1}/U(1)$. we have the following short exact sequence

$$\begin{align}
0\to U(1)\to S^{2n+1}\to \mathbb{CP}^{n}\to 0
\end{align}$$

which induces the following long exact sequence

$$\begin{align}
\dots\to \pi _{k}(U(1))\to \pi _{k}(S^{2n+1})\to \pi _{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{n})\to \pi _{k-1}(U(1))\dots
\end{align}$$

For $n\ge 1$, $S^{2n+1}$ is simply connected. The low-degree part is

$$\begin{align}
\pi_2(S^{2n+1}) \to \pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)\to \pi_1(U(1)) \to \pi_1(S^{2n+1})\to \pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)\to \pi_0(U(1)).
\end{align}$$

Since $\pi_2(S^{2n+1})=0$, $\pi_1(U(1))=\mathbb{Z}$, $\pi_1(S^{2n+1})=0$, and $\pi_0(U(1))=0$, exactness gives

$$\begin{align}
\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)=0,\qquad \pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong \mathbb{Z}.
\end{align}$$

For $k\ge 3$, $\pi_k(U(1))=\pi_{k-1}(U(1))=0$, hence exactness gives $\pi_k(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong \pi_k(S^{2n+1})$.

3. 解释齐次坐标表达式

$$\begin{align}
   h_z=\frac{|z|^2dz\cdot d\bar z-(\bar z\cdot dz)(z\cdot d\bar z)}{|z|^4}
\end{align}$$

   为什么在 $\mathbb C^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}$ 上退化。
sol. consider $\displaystyle{V=z^{\alpha} \dfrac{\partial}{\partial z^{\alpha}}}$, we have $\displaystyle{h_{z}(V,\cdot)=0}$. thus $\displaystyle{h_{z}}$ is degenerate on $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}$.
4. 在局部截面 $z=(1,\zeta)$ 上推出 Fubini-Study 度量，并验证其 Kähler 势为 $K=\log(1+|\zeta|^2)$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2}_{\mathrm{FS}} & = \dfrac{(1+|\zeta|^{2})\mathrm{d}\zeta \cdot \mathrm{d}\bar{z}-(\bar{\zeta}\cdot \mathrm{d}\zeta)(\zeta \cdot \mathrm{d}\bar{\zeta})}{(1+|\zeta|^{2})^{2}}
\end{align}$$

the complement-countable topology is defined as $\displaystyle{\tau _{\text{cocount}}=\left\{U\subseteq \mathbb{R}|\mathbb{R}-U \text{ is countable}\right\}}$

### 题 4（20 分）— 胞腔同调

1. 写出 $\mathbb{RP}^2$ 的 CW 结构和链复形。

sol. The standard CW structure of $\mathbb{RP}^2$ has one cell in each dimension $0,1,2$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{RP}^{2}=e^{0}\cup e^{1}\cup e^{2}.
\end{align}$$

The $1$-skeleton is $\mathbb{RP}^{1}\cong S^{1}$. The attaching map of the $2$-cell is the double covering $S^{1}\to \mathbb{RP}^{1}$, so the cellular chain complex is

$$\begin{align}
0\to C_{2}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{2}=2}C_{1}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{1}=0}C_{0}\cong\mathbb Z\to 0.
\end{align}$$

2. 计算 $H_k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z)$。

sol. From the chain complex,

$$\begin{align}
H_{0}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z,\\
H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,\\
H_{2}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&=0.
\end{align}$$

3. 写出 $\mathbb{RP}^3$ 的链复形，并计算其同调群。

sol. The standard CW structure has one cell in each dimension $0,1,2,3$. The cellular boundary maps alternate:

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{k}&=1+(-1)^{k}.
\end{align}$$

Thus the chain complex is

$$\begin{align}
0\to C_{3}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{3}=0}C_{2}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{2}=2}C_{1}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{1}=0}C_{0}\cong\mathbb Z\to 0.
\end{align}$$

Therefore

$$\begin{align}
H_{0}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z,\\
H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,\\
H_{2}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb Z)&=0,\\
H_{3}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

4. 比较 $\mathbb{RP}^{2m}$ 与 $\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1}$ 的顶维同调差异。

sol. In both cases the CW complex has one cell in every dimension. The top cellular boundary is multiplication by $1+(-1)^{n}$ for $\mathbb{RP}^{n}$.

For $n=2m$, the top boundary is multiplication by $2$, so no nonzero top cycle survives:

$$\begin{align}
H_{2m}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m};\mathbb Z)=0.
\end{align}$$

For $n=2m+1$, the top boundary is zero and there is no higher boundary, so the top cell gives the orientation class:

$$\begin{align}
H_{2m+1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1};\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

### 题 6（20 分）— $\mathbb{CP}^2$

1. 用 CW 结构计算 $H_k(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)$。

sol. The standard CW structure of $\mathbb{CP}^{2}$ has one cell in dimensions $0,2,4$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{CP}^{2}=e^{0}\cup e^{2}\cup e^{4}.
\end{align}$$

Thus $C_{0}\cong C_{2}\cong C_{4}\cong\mathbb Z$ and the other cellular chain groups vanish. Since there are no odd-dimensional cellular chain groups, all cellular boundary maps are zero. Hence

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2};\mathbb{Z}) & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,2,4, \\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

2. 写出 $\mathbb{CP}^2$ 的 Euler characteristic。
sol. From the CW structure or from the ranks of homology,

$$\begin{align}
\chi(\mathbb{CP}^{2})&=1-0+1-0+1=3.
\end{align}$$

1. 由 Hopf 纤维化 $U(1)\to S^5\to\mathbb{CP}^2$ 计算 $\pi_1,\pi_2$。
sol. The long exact sequence of the fibration $U(1)\to S^{5}\to\mathbb{CP}^{2}$ gives

$$\begin{align}
\pi_{2}(S^{5})\to\pi_{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})\to\pi_{1}(U(1))\to\pi_{1}(S^{5})\to\pi_{1}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})\to\pi_{0}(U(1)).
\end{align}$$

Using $\pi_{2}(S^{5})=0$, $\pi_{1}(U(1))\cong\mathbb Z$, $\pi_{1}(S^{5})=0$, and $\pi_{0}(U(1))=0$, exactness gives

$$\begin{align}
\pi_{1}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})&=0,\\
\pi_{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})&\cong \mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

1. 比较 $\mathbb{CP}^2$ 和 $S^2\times S^2$ 的同调群；是否可仅由同调判断它们同胚？
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(S^{2}\times S^{2};\mathbb{Z}) & =\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_{i}(S^{2};\mathbb{Z})\otimes H_{j}(S^{2};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,4, \\
\mathbb Z^{2}, & k=2, \\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

Since $H_{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ but $H_{2}(S^{2}\times S^{2};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{2}$, the two spaces cannot be homeomorphic. In this example homology is already enough to distinguish them.

### 题 5（20 分）— $\mathbb{RP}^n$ 与 $\mathbb{CP}^n$

1. 用 CW 结构写出 $\mathbb{RP}^n$ 的链复形。

sol. The standard CW structure of $\mathbb{RP}^{n}$ has one cell in each dimension $0,1,\dots,n$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{RP}^{n}=e^{0}\cup e^{1}\cup\cdots\cup e^{n}.
\end{align}$$

The cellular chain group is $C_{k}\cong\mathbb Z$ for $0\le k\le n$, and the boundary map is multiplication by

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{k}=1+(-1)^{k}.
\end{align}$$

Equivalently,

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{k}=\begin{cases}
0, & k \text{ odd},\\
2, & k \text{ even}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

1. 分别列出 $n$ 偶和 $n$ 奇时 $\mathbb{RP}^n$ 的整数同调群。

sol. For $n=2m$,

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m};\mathbb Z)&\cong \begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,\\
\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z, & k \text{ odd},\ 0<k<2m,\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

For $n=2m+1$,

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1};\mathbb Z)&\cong \begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,2m+1,\\
\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z, & k \text{ odd},\ 0<k<2m+1,\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

1. 写出 $\mathbb{CP}^n$ 的 CW 结构和整数同调群。

sol. The standard CW structure has one cell in every even real dimension:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{CP}^{n}=e^{0}\cup e^{2}\cup e^{4}\cup\cdots\cup e^{2n}.
\end{align}$$

Thus $C_{2j}\cong\mathbb Z$ for $0\le j\le n$, and all odd cellular chain groups vanish. Therefore all cellular boundary maps are zero, and

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{n};\mathbb Z)&\cong \begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,2,4,\dots,2n,\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

1. 比较 $\mathbb{RP}^2$、$\mathbb{CP}^1$、$S^2$ 的 $\pi_1$ 与 $H_2$。

sol. The relevant groups are

$$\begin{align}
\pi_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2})&\cong\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z,& H_{2}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&=0,\\
\pi_{1}(\mathbb{CP}^{1})&=0,& H_{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{1};\mathbb Z)&\cong\mathbb Z,\\
\pi_{1}(S^{2})&=0,& H_{2}(S^{2};\mathbb Z)&\cong\mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

Thus $\mathbb{CP}^{1}$ and $S^{2}$ have the same entries here, consistent with $\mathbb{CP}^{1}\cong S^{2}$, while $\mathbb{RP}^{2}$ is distinguished by its nontrivial fundamental group and vanishing top homology over $\mathbb Z$.
