### 题 1（15 分）— Hausdorff 判据与闭图像

设 $X$ 为拓扑空间，对角集为

$$\begin{align}
\Delta_X=\{(x,x)\mid x\in X\}\subset X\times X.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明：若 $\Delta_X$ 在 $X\times X$ 中闭，则 $X$ 为 Hausdorff 空间。
sol. since $\displaystyle{\Delta _{X}}$ is closed in $\displaystyle{X\times X}$, we have $\displaystyle{X\times X-\Delta _{X}}$ is open, then $\displaystyle{\forall (a,b)\in X\times X-\Delta _{X},\exists W_{a}\times W_{b}\subset X\times X-\Delta _{X}\text{ s.t. } (a,b)\in W_{a}\times W_{b} \subset X\times X-\Delta_{X}}$, in this case $\displaystyle{a\neq b}$. thus $\displaystyle{a\in W_{a},b\in W_{b},W_{a}\cap W_{b}=\varnothing}$, which means $\displaystyle{X}$ is Hausdorff.
2. 设 $Y$ 为 Hausdorff 空间，$f,g:X\to Y$ 连续。证明 $\{x\in X\mid f(x)=g(x)\}$ 是闭集。
sol. define $\displaystyle{h :X\to Y\times Y, h(x)=(f(x),g(x))}$, since $\displaystyle{f,g}$ are continuous, then $\displaystyle{h}$ is continuous. since $\displaystyle{Y}$ is Hausdorff, the diagonal $\displaystyle{\Delta _{Y}=\left\{(y,y)|y \in Y\right\}}$ is closed, then $\displaystyle{h^{-1}(\Delta _{Y})=\left\{x \in X|f(x)=g(x)\right\}}$ is closed for $\displaystyle{h}$ is continuous.
3. 令 $f:X\to X$ 为连续自映射。说明当 $X$ Hausdorff 时，不动点集 $\mathcal F(f)$ 是闭集。
sol. consider a function $\displaystyle{h:X\to X\times X, h(x)\to(x,f(x))}$. since $\displaystyle{X}$ is Hausdorff, then the diagonal $\displaystyle{\Delta _{X}}$ is closed, then $\displaystyle{\mathcal{F}(f)=h^{-1}(\Delta _{X})}$ is closed.

### 题 2（15 分）— Lie 导数

设 $g=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu\otimes dx^\nu$ 为度量张量，$V=V^\mu\partial_\mu$ 为向量场。

1. 推导 $(\mathcal L_Vg)_{\mu\nu}$ 的坐标表达式。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{L}_{V}g)_{\mu \nu} & =V^{\rho}\partial _{\rho}g_{\mu \nu}+\partial _{\mu}V^{\rho}g_{\rho \nu}+\partial _{\nu}V^{\rho}g_{\mu \rho}
\end{align}$$

2. 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 上取 Euclid 度量和向量场 $V=x\partial_x-y\partial_y$，计算 $\mathcal L_V g$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
(\mathcal{L}_{V}g)_{\mu \nu} & =2\partial _{x}V^{x}g_{xx}\delta _{\mu}^{x}\delta _{\nu}^{x}+2\partial _{y}V^{y}g_{yy}\delta _{\mu}^{y}\delta _{\nu}^{y} \\
 & =2\delta _{\mu}^{x}\delta _{\nu}^{x}-2\delta _{\mu}^{y}\delta _{\nu}^{y}
\end{align}$$

Equivalently,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{V}g & =2\,\mathrm{d}x\otimes \mathrm{d}x-2\,\mathrm{d}y\otimes \mathrm{d}y.
\end{align}$$

3. 判断 $V$ 是否为 Killing 向量场。
sol. since $\displaystyle{\mathcal{L}_{V}g\neq 0}$, $\displaystyle{V}$ is not a Killing vector

### 题 3（20 分）— 曲面乘积的同调

设 $\Sigma_g,\Sigma_h$ 为亏格分别为 $g,h$ 的闭定向曲面。

1. 写出 $H_k(\Sigma_g;\mathbb Z)$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\Sigma _{g};\mathbb{Z}) & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb{Z} & ,k=0,2 \\
\mathbb{Z}^{2g} & ,k=1 \\
0 & ,\text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

2. 用 Künneth 公式计算 $H_k(\Sigma_g\times\Sigma_h;\mathbb Z)$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) & = \bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_{i}(\Sigma _{g};\mathbb{Z})\otimes H_{j}(\Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =\begin{cases}
 \mathbb{Z} & k=0,4 \\
 \mathbb{Z}^{2g+2h} & k=1,3 \\
 \mathbb{Z}^{4gh+2} & k=2 \\
 0 & \text{otherwise}
 \end{cases}
\end{align}$$

3. 计算 $\chi(\Sigma_g\times\Sigma_h)$，并说明它与 $\chi(\Sigma_g)\chi(\Sigma_h)$ 的关系。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\chi(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h}) & =\sum _{k}(-)^{k}\mathrm{rank}H_{k}(\Sigma _{g}\times \Sigma _{h};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =-4g-4h+4gh+4 \\
 & =(2-2g)(2-2h) \\
 & =\chi(\Sigma _{g})\chi(\Sigma _{h})
\end{align}$$

### 题 4（25 分）— 环面度量与曲率

考虑环面坐标 $(\theta,\phi)$ 上的度量

$$\begin{align}
ds^2=r^2d\theta^2+(R+r\cos\theta)^2d\phi^2,\qquad R>r>0.
\end{align}$$

1. 计算非零 Christoffel 符号。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi \phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\sin \theta}{r} \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\theta \phi} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}
\end{align}$$

2. 按约定

$$\begin{align}
R^\kappa{}_{\lambda\mu\nu} =\partial_\mu\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\nu\lambda} -\partial_\nu\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\mu\lambda} +\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\mu\rho}\Gamma^\rho{}_{\nu\lambda} -\Gamma^\kappa{}_{\nu\rho}\Gamma^\rho{}_{\mu\lambda}
\end{align}$$

   计算独立非零曲率分量。
sol. by some theorem, in 2d there is only one independent component

$$\begin{align}
\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\sin \theta}{r}\mathrm{d}\phi \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\theta} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}\mathrm{d}\phi \\
\Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\phi} & =-\dfrac{r\sin \theta}{R+r\cos \theta}\mathrm{d}\theta \\
\implies \Omega ^{\mu}_{~\nu} & =\dfrac{1}{2!}R^{\mu}_{~\nu \rho \sigma}\mathrm{d}x^{\rho}\wedge \mathrm{d}x^{\sigma} \\
 & =\mathrm{d}\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\nu}+\Gamma ^{\mu}_{~\rho}\wedge \Gamma ^{\rho}_{~\nu} \\
\Omega ^{\theta}_{~\phi} & =\mathrm{d}\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi}+\Gamma ^{\theta}_{~\phi}\wedge \Gamma ^{\phi}_{~\phi} \\
 & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\cos \theta}{r}\mathrm{d}\theta \wedge \mathrm{d}\phi \\
\implies R^{\theta}_{~\phi \theta \phi} & =\dfrac{(R+r\cos \theta)\cos \theta}{r} \\
\implies R_{\theta \phi \theta \phi} & =(R+r\cos \theta)r\cos \theta
\end{align}$$

3. 求标曲率 $\mathcal R=g^{\nu\rho}R^\mu{}_{\nu\mu\rho}$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{R} & =g^{\mu \rho}g^{\nu \sigma}R_{\mu \nu \rho \sigma} \\
 & =2g^{\theta \theta}g^{\phi \phi}R_{\theta \phi \theta \phi} \\
 & =\dfrac{2}{r^{2}(R+r\cos \theta)^{2}} (R+r\cos \theta)r\cos \theta \\
 & =\dfrac{2\cos \theta}{r(R+r\cos \theta)}
\end{align}$$

4. 计算 $\int_{T^2}\sqrt{\det g}\,\mathcal R\,d\theta\,d\phi$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\int _{T^{2}}\sqrt{ \det g }\mathcal{R}\mathrm{d}\theta \mathrm{d}\phi & =\int _{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta \int _{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\phi r(R+r\cos \theta) \dfrac{2\cos \theta}{r(R+r\cos \theta)}=0
\end{align}$$

remark. we may use moving frame method

### 题 5（25 分）— $\mathbb{CP}^n$ 与 Fubini-Study 度量

设 $\mathbb{CP}^n=(\mathbb C^{n+1}\setminus\{0\})/\mathbb C^\times$。

1. 给出标准图册 $U_i=\{z_i\ne0\}$ 和非齐次坐标，并写出 $U_i\cap U_j$ 上的转移函数。
sol. On $U_i$, use the inhomogeneous coordinates

$$\begin{align}
\zeta_a=\frac{z_a}{z_i},\qquad a\ne i.
\end{align}$$

On $U_i\cap U_j$, we have $\zeta_j\ne0$. If $\eta_a=z_a/z_j$ are the coordinates on $U_j$, then the transition map is

$$\begin{align}
\eta_i&=\frac{1}{\zeta_j},\\
\eta_a&=\frac{\zeta_a}{\zeta_j},\qquad a\ne i,j.
\end{align}$$

This is holomorphic on the overlap $\zeta_j\ne0$.
2. 证明 $\mathbb{CP}^n=S^{2n+1}/U(1)$，并由 Hopf 纤维化求 $\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)$ 和 $\pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)$。
sol. Since $\mathbb{CP}^n$ is the space of complex lines in $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}$, every line has unit representatives in $S^{2n+1}$, and two unit representatives differ by a phase in $U(1)$. Hence $\mathbb{CP}^n=S^{2n+1}/U(1)$. This gives the Hopf fibration

$$\begin{align}
U(1)\to S^{2n+1}\to \mathbb{CP}^{n},
\end{align}$$

which induces the following long exact sequence

$$\begin{align}
\dots\to \pi _{k}(U(1))\to \pi _{k}(S^{2n+1})\to \pi _{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{n})\to \pi _{k-1}(U(1))\dots
\end{align}$$

For $n\ge 1$, $S^{2n+1}$ is simply connected. The low-degree part is

$$\begin{align}
\pi_2(S^{2n+1}) \to \pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)\to \pi_1(U(1)) \to \pi_1(S^{2n+1})\to \pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)\to \pi_0(U(1)).
\end{align}$$

Since $\pi_2(S^{2n+1})=0$, $\pi_1(U(1))=\mathbb{Z}$, $\pi_1(S^{2n+1})=0$, and $\pi_0(U(1))=0$, exactness gives

$$\begin{align}
\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)=0,\qquad \pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong \mathbb{Z}.
\end{align}$$

For $k\ge 3$, $\pi_k(U(1))=\pi_{k-1}(U(1))=0$, hence exactness gives $\pi_k(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong \pi_k(S^{2n+1})$.

3. 解释齐次坐标表达式

$$\begin{align}
   h_z=\frac{|z|^2dz\cdot d\bar z-(\bar z\cdot dz)(z\cdot d\bar z)}{|z|^4}
\end{align}$$

   为什么在 $\mathbb C^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}$ 上退化。
sol. consider $\displaystyle{V=z^{\alpha} \dfrac{\partial}{\partial z^{\alpha}}}$, we have $\displaystyle{h_{z}(V,\cdot)=0}$. thus $\displaystyle{h_{z}}$ is degenerate on $\mathbb{C}^{n+1}\setminus\{0\}$.
4. 在局部截面 $z=(1,\zeta)$ 上推出 Fubini-Study 度量，并验证其 Kähler 势为 $K=\log(1+|\zeta|^2)$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}s^{2}_{\mathrm{FS}} & = \dfrac{(1+|\zeta|^{2})\mathrm{d}\zeta \cdot \mathrm{d}\bar{\zeta}-(\bar{\zeta}\cdot \mathrm{d}\zeta)(\zeta \cdot \mathrm{d}\bar{\zeta})}{(1+|\zeta|^{2})^{2}},\\
g_{a\bar b}&=\frac{(1+|\zeta|^{2})\delta_{a\bar b}-\bar{\zeta}^{\bar a}\zeta^b}{(1+|\zeta|^{2})^{2}}.
\end{align}$$

Indeed, for

$$\begin{align}
K=\log(1+|\zeta|^2),
\end{align}$$

one has

$$\begin{align}
\partial_a\partial_{\bar b}K &=\partial_a\left(\frac{\zeta^b}{1+|\zeta|^2}\right)\\
&=\frac{(1+|\zeta|^2)\delta_{a\bar b}-\bar{\zeta}^{\bar a}\zeta^b}{(1+|\zeta|^2)^2}.
\end{align}$$

### 题 1（15 分）— 拓扑比较与连续性

设 $X$ 为集合，$\tau_1,\tau_2$ 为 $X$ 上的两个拓扑。
1. 证明 $\operatorname{Id}_X:X_{\tau_1}\to X_{\tau_2}$ 连续当且仅当 $\tau_1$ 不弱于 $\tau_2$。
sol. sufficiency: for $\displaystyle{U_{2}\in \tau _{2}\leqslant\tau_{1}}$, then $\displaystyle{\operatorname{Id}_{X}^{-1}U_{2}=U_{2}\in \tau_{1}}$, thus the $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Id}_{X}}$is continuous.
necessity: since $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Id}_{X}}$ is continuous, for $\displaystyle{\forall U\in \tau _{2}}$, we have $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Id}_{X}^{-1}U=U\in \tau _{1}}$, i.e. $\displaystyle{\tau_{1}\geqslant \tau_{2}}$
2. 在 $\mathbb R$ 上比较通常拓扑、余有限拓扑和余可数拓扑。
sol. the complement-finite topology is defined as $\displaystyle{\tau _{\text{cofinite}}=\left\{U\subseteq \mathbb{R}\mid \mathbb{R}-U \text{ is finite}\right\}\cup\{\varnothing\}}$
the complement-countable topology is defined as $\displaystyle{\tau _{\text{cocount}}=\left\{U\subseteq \mathbb{R}\mid \mathbb{R}-U \text{ is countable}\right\}\cup\{\varnothing\}}$
Thus $\tau _{\text{cofinite}}\subset \tau _{\text{cocount}}$. Also $\tau _{\text{cofinite}}\subset \tau_{\text{usual}}$, since finite subsets of $\mathbb R$ are closed in the usual topology. The usual topology and the cocountable topology are not comparable: $(0,1)$ is usual-open but not cocountable-open, while $\mathbb R-\mathbb Q$ is cocountable-open but not usual-open.
3. 判断 $\operatorname{Id}_{\mathbb R}:\mathbb R_{\mathrm{usual}}\to\mathbb R_{\mathrm{cofinite}}$ 是否连续，并说明理由。
sol. $\displaystyle{\mathrm{Id}_{\mathbb{R}}}$ is continuous. Indeed, every cofinite-open subset of $\mathbb R$ has finite complement, hence is open in the usual topology.

### 题 2（15 分）— 球面图册

1. 写出 $S^n$ 的北极、南极球极投影图册。
sol. Let $N=(0,\dots,0,1)$ and $S=(0,\dots,0,-1)$. The two stereographic charts are

$$\begin{align}
U_N&=S^n\setminus\{N\},& \phi_N(x_1,\dots,x_{n+1})&=\frac{(x_1,\dots,x_n)}{1-x_{n+1}},\\
U_S&=S^n\setminus\{S\},& \phi_S(x_1,\dots,x_{n+1})&=\frac{(x_1,\dots,x_n)}{1+x_{n+1}}.
\end{align}$$

2. 计算两个坐标卡重叠区域上的转移函数。
sol. On the overlap, the coordinate transition $\phi_S\circ\phi_N^{-1}:\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}\to \mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}$ is

$$\begin{align}
v=\frac{u}{\|u\|^2}.
\end{align}$$

The inverse transition $\phi_N\circ\phi_S^{-1}$ has the same formula.
3. 说明为什么该转移函数光滑，从而 $S^n$ 是 $n$ 维光滑流形。
sol. Each component $v_i=u_i/\|u\|^2$ is smooth on $\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}$, so the transition maps are smooth. Thus $S^n$ is an $n$-dimensional smooth manifold.

### 题 3（15 分）— Cartan 公式

设 $M=\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}$，$X=-y\partial_x+x\partial_y$，

$$\begin{align}
\omega=\frac{x\,dy-y\,dx}{x^2+y^2}.
\end{align}$$

1. 计算 $i_X\omega$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
i_{X}\omega & =\braket{ -y\partial _{x}+x\partial _{y},\dfrac{x\mathrm{d}y-y\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}+y^{2}} }  \\
 & = 1
\end{align}$$

1. 计算 $d\omega$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\mathrm{d}\omega & =0
\end{align}$$

1. 用 Cartan 公式计算 $\mathcal L_X\omega$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal{L}_{X}\omega & =i_{X}\mathrm{d}\omega+\mathrm{d}(i_{X}\omega) \\
 & =0
\end{align}$$

1. 判断 $\omega$ 是否为恰当形式，并用闭曲线积分说明。
sol. $\displaystyle{\omega}$ is not an exact form. consider

$$\begin{align}
\oint _{x^{2}+y^{2}=1}\omega & =2\pi.
\end{align}$$

More generally, for a closed loop with winding number $n$ around the origin,

$$\begin{align}
\oint \omega & =2\pi n,\qquad n\in \mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

### 题 4（20 分）— 胞腔同调

1. 写出 $\mathbb{RP}^2$ 的 CW 结构和链复形。
sol. The standard CW structure of $\mathbb{RP}^2$ has one cell in each dimension $0,1,2$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{RP}^{2}=e^{0}\cup e^{1}\cup e^{2}.
\end{align}$$

The $1$-skeleton is $\mathbb{RP}^{1}\cong S^{1}$. The attaching map of the $2$-cell is the double covering $S^{1}\to \mathbb{RP}^{1}$, so the cellular chain complex is

$$\begin{align}
0\to C_{2}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{2}=2}C_{1}\cong\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{\partial _{1}=0}C_{0}\cong\mathbb Z\to 0.
\end{align}$$

2. 计算 $H_k(\mathbb{RP}^2;\mathbb Z)$。

sol. From the chain complex,

$$\begin{align}
H_{0}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z,\\
H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb{Z}_{2},\\
H_{2}(\mathbb{RP}^{2};\mathbb Z)&=0.
\end{align}$$

3. 写出 $\mathbb{RP}^3$ 的链复形，并计算其同调群。

sol. The standard CW structure has one cell in each dimension $0,1,2,3$.

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{RP}^{3} & =e^{0}\cup e^{1}\cup e^{2}\cup e^{3}
\end{align}$$

The cellular boundary maps alternate:

$$\begin{align}
\partial _{k}&=1+(-1)^{k}.
\end{align}$$

Thus the chain complex is

$$\begin{align}
0\to C_{3}\cong\mathbb{Z} \xrightarrow{\partial _{3}=0}C_{2}\cong\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{\partial _{2}=2}C_{1}\cong\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{\partial _{1}=0}C_{0}\cong\mathbb{Z}\to0
\end{align}$$

Therefore

$$\begin{align}
H_{0}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb{Z}) & =\mathbb{Z} \\
H_{1}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb{Z}) & =\mathbb{Z}_{2} \\
H_{2}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb{Z}) & =0 \\
H_{3}(\mathbb{RP}^{3};\mathbb{Z}) & =\mathbb{Z}
\end{align}$$

4. 比较 $\mathbb{RP}^{2m}$ 与 $\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1}$ 的顶维同调差异。
sol. In both cases the CW complex has one cell in every dimension. The top cellular boundary is multiplication by $1+(-1)^{n}$ for $\mathbb{RP}^{n}$.

For $n=2m$, the top boundary is multiplication by $2$, so no nonzero top cycle survives:

$$\begin{align}
H_{2m}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m};\mathbb Z)=0.
\end{align}$$

For $n=2m+1$, the top boundary is zero and there is no higher boundary, so the top cell gives the orientation class:

$$\begin{align}
H_{2m+1}(\mathbb{RP}^{2m+1};\mathbb Z)\cong \mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

### 题 5（15 分）— Hodge 对偶

在三维 Euclid 空间中取标准坐标 $(x,y,z)$。
1. 计算 $*dx,*dy,*dz$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\star \mathrm{d}x & =\mathrm{d}y\wedge \mathrm{d}z \\
\star \mathrm{d}y & =\mathrm{d}z\wedge \mathrm{d}x \\
\star \mathrm{d}z & =\mathrm{d}x\wedge \mathrm{d}y
\end{align}$$

2. 计算 $*(dx\wedge dy)$、$*(dy\wedge dz)$、$*(dz\wedge dx)$。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\star(\mathrm{d}x\wedge \mathrm{d}y) & =\mathrm{d}z \\
\star(\mathrm{d}y\wedge \mathrm{d}z) & =\mathrm{d}x \\
\star(\mathrm{d}z\wedge \mathrm{d}x) & =\mathrm{d}y
\end{align}$$

3. 验证对任意 1-形式 $\alpha$，有 $**\alpha=\alpha$。
sol. define $\displaystyle{\alpha=f\mathrm{d}x+g\mathrm{d}y+h\mathrm{d}z}$, then

$$\begin{align}
\star \star \alpha & =\star(f\mathrm{d}y\wedge \mathrm{d}z+g\mathrm{d}z\wedge \mathrm{d}x+h\mathrm{d}x\wedge \mathrm{d}y) \\
 & =f\mathrm{d}x+g\mathrm{d}y+h\mathrm{d}z \\
 & =\alpha
\end{align}$$

### 题 6（20 分）— $\mathbb{CP}^2$

1. 用 CW 结构计算 $H_k(\mathbb{CP}^2;\mathbb Z)$。
sol. the standard CW structure of $\displaystyle{\mathbb{CP}^{2}}$ has one cell in dimensions $\displaystyle{0,2,4}$:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{CP}^{2} & =e^{0}\cup e^{2}\cup e^{4}
\end{align}$$

thus we have

$$\begin{align}
0\to C_{4}\cong\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{\partial _{4}}C_{3}\cong0\xrightarrow{\partial _{3}}C_{2}\cong\mathbb{Z}\xrightarrow{\partial _{2}}C_{1}\cong0\xrightarrow{\partial _{1}}C_{0}\cong\mathbb{Z}\to0
\end{align}$$

therefore we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2};\mathbb{Z}) & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb{Z}, & k=0,2,4 \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

2. 写出 $\mathbb{CP}^2$ 的 Euler characteristic。
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
\chi(\mathbb{CP}^{2}) & =\sum _{k}(-)^{k}\dim H_{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =3
\end{align}$$

3. 由 Hopf 纤维化 $U(1)\to S^5\to\mathbb{CP}^2$ 计算 $\pi_1,\pi_2$。
sol. the fibration induces the long exact sequence:

$$\begin{align}
\dots\to \pi _{k}(U(1))\to \pi _{k}(S^{5})\to \pi _{k}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})\to \pi _{k-1}(U(1))\to\dots
\end{align}$$

then we have

$$\begin{align}
\pi _{2}(U(1))\to \pi _{2}(S^{5})\cong 0\to \pi _{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})\to \pi _{1}(U(1))\cong \mathbb{Z}\to \pi _{1}(S^{5})\cong 0\to \pi _{1}(\mathbb{CP}^{2})\to \pi _{0}(U(1))
\end{align}$$

thus

$$\begin{align}
\pi _{1}(\mathbb{CP}^{2}) & =0 \\
\pi _{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2}) & =\mathbb{Z}
\end{align}$$

4. 比较 $\mathbb{CP}^2$ 和 $S^2\times S^2$ 的同调群；是否可仅由同调判断它们同胚？
sol. we have

$$\begin{align}
H_{k}(S^{2}\times S^{2};\mathbb{Z}) & =\bigoplus_{i+j=k}H_{i}(S^{2};\mathbb{Z})\otimes H_{j}(S^{2};\mathbb{Z}) \\
 & =\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,4, \\
\mathbb Z^{2}, & k=2, \\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

Since $H_{2}(\mathbb{CP}^{2};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ but $H_{2}(S^{2}\times S^{2};\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^{2}$, the two spaces cannot be homeomorphic. In this example homology is already enough to distinguish them.

### 题 1（15 分）— 度量的有界化

设 $(X,d)$ 为度量空间，定义

$$\begin{align}
d_1(x,y)=\frac{d(x,y)}{1+d(x,y)},\qquad d_2(x,y)=\min\{d(x,y),1\}.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $d_1$ 满足度量公理。
sol. positivity and reflectivity are easy to see. $\displaystyle{\forall x,y,z\in X}$, then

$$\begin{align}

1. 判断余有限拓扑是否为拓扑。

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcap_{i=1}^{n}U_{i} & =\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}(X-U_{i}),
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{X-U_{i}}$ is finite. then $\displaystyle{\bigcup ^{n}_{i=1}(X-U_{i})}$ is finite. for the arbitrary union:

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcup_{\alpha}U_{\alpha} & =\bigcap_{\alpha}(X-U_{\alpha})
\end{align}$$

If every $U_\alpha$ is empty, then the union is empty and hence open. Otherwise some $U_{\alpha_0}$ is nonempty cofinite, and

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcup_\alpha U_\alpha \subset X-U_{\alpha_0},
\end{align}$$

so the complement is finite. Thus arbitrary unions are open, and it is a topology.
2. 判断 $\tau=\{U\subset X\mid U^c\text{ 为无限集或空集}\}$ 是否为拓扑。
sol. In general this is not a topology. Take $X=\mathbb N$ and let

$$\begin{align}
A&=\{2,4,6,\dots\},\\
B&=\{1,3,5,\dots\}\cup\{2\}.
\end{align}$$

Both $A$ and $B$ are infinite, so $U=X-A$ and $V=X-B$ belong to $\tau$. But

$$\begin{align}
(U\cup V)^c=A\cap B=\{2\},
\end{align}$$

which is finite and nonempty. Hence $U\cup V\notin\tau$, so $\tau$ is not closed under finite unions and is not a topology.

$$\begin{align}
X-(U\cap V)=(X-U)\cup(X-V)
\end{align}$$

is countable. Since $X$ is uncountable, $U\cap V\ne\varnothing$. Thus no two nonempty open sets are disjoint, so two distinct points cannot be separated by disjoint neighborhoods.
4. 在余可数拓扑下求可数子集 $A\subset X$ 的闭包。
sol. In the cocountable topology, the closed sets are $X$ and all countable subsets of $X$. Therefore a countable subset $A$ is already closed, and

\overline{A}=A.

$$\begin{align}

### 题 4（15 分）— 广义 Kronecker $\delta$

定义

$$\begin{align}
\delta^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_s}_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_s} =\det(\delta^{\nu_j}_{\mu_i})_{1\le i,j\le s}.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $s>m$ 时该符号为零。
sol. The indices take values in $\{1,\dots,m\}$. If $s>m$, then among $\nu_1,\dots,\nu_s$ at least two indices are equal, so two columns of the determinant are equal. Hence

$$\begin{align}
\delta^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_s}_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_s}=0.
\end{align}$$

The same conclusion also follows if two lower indices coincide, since then two rows coincide.
2. 证明 $\epsilon_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_m}\epsilon^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_m}=\delta^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_m}_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_m}$。
sol. Both sides are completely antisymmetric in the lower indices and in the upper indices. If any indices repeat, both sides vanish. If the lower and upper multi-indices are permutations of $(1,\dots,m)$, both sides equal the sign of the relative permutation. Therefore

$$\begin{align}
\epsilon_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_m}\epsilon^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_m} =\det(\delta^{\nu_j}_{\mu_i}) =\delta^{\nu_1\cdots\nu_m}_{\mu_1\cdots\mu_m}.
\end{align}$$

3. 推导

$$\begin{align}
   \epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{pqk}=\delta^i_p\delta^j_q-\delta^i_q\delta^j_p.
\end{align}$$

sol. Contracting one index in the three-dimensional identity gives

$$\begin{align}
\epsilon_{ijk}\epsilon_{pqk} &=\delta^{ij}_{pq}\\
&=\det\begin{pmatrix}
\delta^i_p & \delta^j_p\\
\delta^i_q & \delta^j_q
\end{pmatrix}\\
&=\delta^i_p\delta^j_q-\delta^i_q\delta^j_p.
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\mathring{\mathbb Q}=\varnothing.

$$\begin{align}

sol.

$$\begin{align}
\partial\mathbb Q =\overline{\mathbb Q}-\mathring{\mathbb Q} =\mathbb R.
\end{align}$$

sol. In the cocountable topology on the uncountable set $\mathbb R$, the closed sets are $\mathbb R$ and all countable subsets. Hence $\mathbb Q$ is closed, so

$$\begin{align}
\overline{\mathbb Q}=\mathbb Q.
\end{align}$$

No nonempty cocountable open set can be contained in $\mathbb Q$, since every nonempty cocountable open set is uncountable. Therefore

$$\begin{align}
\mathring{\mathbb Q}=\varnothing,\qquad \partial\mathbb Q=\mathbb Q.
\end{align}$$

### 题 3（20 分）— 微分形式恒等式

设 $\omega\in\Omega^q(M)$，$\eta\in\Omega^r(M)$，$X\in\mathfrak X(M)$。

1. 证明 $i_X(\omega\wedge\eta)=i_X\omega\wedge\eta+(-1)^q\omega\wedge i_X\eta$。
sol. Evaluating both sides on $q+r-1$ vector fields and expanding the wedge product, the vector $X$ can be inserted either into the $\omega$ slot or into the $\eta$ slot. Moving $X$ past the $q$ arguments of $\omega$ in the second case gives the sign $(-1)^q$. Thus

$$\begin{align}
i_X(\omega\wedge\eta)=i_X\omega\wedge\eta+(-1)^q\omega\wedge i_X\eta.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $d(\omega\wedge\eta)=d\omega\wedge\eta+(-1)^q\omega\wedge d\eta$。
sol. This is the graded Leibniz rule for the exterior derivative. In local coordinates, write

$$\begin{align}
\omega=\sum_I\omega_I dx^I,\qquad \eta=\sum_J\eta_J dx^J.
\end{align}$$

Then $d$ differentiates the coefficient of $\omega$ with no sign, while differentiating the coefficient of $\eta$ requires moving the new $dx^\mu$ past the $q$ one-forms in $dx^I$, giving

$$\begin{align}
d(\omega\wedge\eta)=d\omega\wedge\eta+(-1)^q\omega\wedge d\eta.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $\mathcal L_X(\omega\wedge\eta)=\mathcal L_X\omega\wedge\eta+\omega\wedge\mathcal L_X\eta$。
sol. Using Cartan's formula and the two graded Leibniz rules above,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_X(\omega\wedge\eta) &=i_Xd(\omega\wedge\eta)+d\,i_X(\omega\wedge\eta)\\
&=\mathcal L_X\omega\wedge\eta+\omega\wedge\mathcal L_X\eta.
\end{align}$$

The intermediate signs cancel because $\mathcal L_X$ has degree $0$.
1. 说明这些公式中的符号来源。
sol. The sign comes from the Koszul sign rule. The operators $i_X$ and $d$ have odd degrees $-1$ and $+1$, so moving them past a $q$-form produces $(-1)^q$. The Lie derivative has degree $0$, so its Leibniz rule has no extra sign.

### 题 1（15 分）— Cauchy 与 Minkowski

1. 证明 Cauchy 不等式。
sol. For $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$, consider

$$\begin{align}
0\le \|x-ty\|^2=\|x\|^2-2t(x\cdot y)+t^2\|y\|^2
\end{align}$$

as a quadratic polynomial in $t$. Its discriminant is nonpositive, hence

$$\begin{align}
(x\cdot y)^2\le \|x\|^2\|y\|^2.
\end{align}$$

This is the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality.
1. 由 Cauchy 不等式证明 Minkowski 不等式。
sol. Using Cauchy-Schwarz,

$$\begin{align}
\|x+y\|^2 &=\|x\|^2+2x\cdot y+\|y\|^2\\
&\le \|x\|^2+2\|x\|\|y\|+\|y\|^2\\
&=(\|x\|+\|y\|)^2.
\end{align}$$

Taking square roots gives

$$\begin{align}
\|x+y\|\le \|x\|+\|y\|.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $d(x,y)=\|x-y\|$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 上的度量。
sol. Positivity, symmetry, and $d(x,y)=0$ iff $x=y$ are immediate from the norm. The triangle inequality follows from Minkowski:

$$\begin{align}
d(x,z)=\|x-z\|=\|(x-y)+(y-z)\|\le \|x-y\|+\|y-z\|.
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_{fX}Y &=[fX,Y]\\

1. 推导 $\mathcal L_X(fY)=f\mathcal L_XY+X[f]Y$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_X(fY) &=[X,fY]\\

\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}

\Gamma^\phi{}_{\rho\phi} &=\Gamma^\phi{}_{\phi\rho} =\frac{f'(\rho)}{f(\rho)}.
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
R^\rho{}_{\phi\rho\phi} &=\partial_\rho\Gamma^\rho{}_{\phi\phi} -\Gamma^\rho{}_{\phi\phi}\Gamma^\phi{}_{\rho\phi}\\

\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}

\mathcal R=0.
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal R=2.
\end{align}$$

### 题 1（15 分）— 拓扑构造

设 $\{A_j\}_{j\in J}$ 为集合 $X$ 的子集族。

1. 证明 de Morgan 公式。
sol. For any $x\in X$,

$$\begin{align}
x\in X-\bigcup_{j\in J}A_j &\Longleftrightarrow x\notin A_j\ \text{for all }j\\
&\Longleftrightarrow x\in \bigcap_{j\in J}(X-A_j).
\end{align}$$

Thus

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcup_{j\in J}A_j=\bigcap_{j\in J}(X-A_j).
\end{align}$$

Similarly,

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j=\bigcup_{j\in J}(X-A_j).
\end{align}$$

1. 用 de Morgan 公式证明余有限拓扑的任意并封闭和有限交封闭。
sol. In the cofinite topology, $U$ is open if $U=\varnothing$ or $X-U$ is finite. For an arbitrary union, if all $U_\alpha$ are empty, the union is empty. Otherwise choose a nonempty $U_{\alpha_0}$. Then

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcup_\alpha U_\alpha =\bigcap_\alpha(X-U_\alpha)
\subset X-U_{\alpha_0},
\end{align}$$

which is finite. Hence the union is open. For a finite intersection,

$$\begin{align}
X-\bigcap_{i=1}^{N}U_i =\bigcup_{i=1}^{N}(X-U_i),
\end{align}$$

which is finite if every $U_i$ is nonempty cofinite; if some $U_i=\varnothing$, the intersection is empty and is open.
1. 给出一个子集族不是拓扑的例子，并指出失败的公理。
sol. Let $X=\{a,b,c\}$ and

$$\begin{align}
\tau=\{\varnothing,X,\{a\},\{b\}\}.
\end{align}$$

Then $\{a\},\{b\}\in\tau$, but

$$\begin{align}
\{a\}\cup\{b\}=\{a,b\}\notin\tau.
\end{align}$$

Thus $\tau$ is not closed under arbitrary unions, so it is not a topology.

sol. For differential forms,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_X\omega=i_Xd\omega+d(i_X\omega).
\end{align}$$

Equivalently,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_X=[i_X,d]_{\mathrm{gr}}=i_Xd+di_X,
\end{align}$$

because $i_X$ has degree $-1$ and $d$ has degree $+1$.
1. 证明 $[\mathcal L_X,d]=0$。
sol. Using Cartan's formula and $d^2=0$,

$$\begin{align}
\mathcal L_Xd &=(i_Xd+di_X)d\\
&=di_Xd,\\
d\mathcal L_X &=d(i_Xd+di_X)\\
&=di_Xd.
\end{align}$$

Therefore

$$\begin{align}
[\mathcal L_X,d]=0.
\end{align}$$

1. 证明 $[\mathcal L_X,i_Y]=i_{[X,Y]}$。
sol. Both sides are degree $-1$ derivations of the exterior algebra, so it is enough to check them on functions and $1$-forms. On functions both sides vanish. For a $1$-form $\alpha$,

$$\begin{align}
([\mathcal L_X,i_Y]\alpha) &=\mathcal L_X(\alpha(Y))-i_Y(\mathcal L_X\alpha)\\
&=X[\alpha(Y)]-(\mathcal L_X\alpha)(Y)\\
&=\alpha([X,Y])\\
&=i_{[X,Y]}\alpha.
\end{align}$$

Hence $[\mathcal L_X,i_Y]=i_{[X,Y]}$ on all forms.
1. 证明 $[\mathcal L_X,\mathcal L_Y]=\mathcal L_{[X,Y]}$。
sol. Since Lie derivatives are degree $0$ derivations, it is enough to check functions and $1$-forms. On functions,

$$\begin{align}
[\mathcal L_X,\mathcal L_Y]f =X[Y[f]]-Y[X[f]] =[X,Y][f] =\mathcal L_{[X,Y]}f.
\end{align}$$

Compatibility with contractions and the identity $[\mathcal L_X,i_Y]=i_{[X,Y]}$ give the same action on $1$-forms, hence on all forms:

$$\begin{align}
[\mathcal L_X,\mathcal L_Y]=\mathcal L_{[X,Y]}.
\end{align}$$

### 题 4（20 分）— 常见空间同调速算

计算以下空间的整数同调群，并给出 Euler characteristic：

1. $S^4$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
H_k(S^4;\mathbb Z)&\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,4,\\
0, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
& \chi(S^4)&=2.
\end{align}$$

1. $T^4$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
H_k(T^4;\mathbb Z)&\cong \mathbb Z^{\binom{4}{k}},\qquad 0\le k\le4,
\end{align}$$

and zero otherwise. Explicitly the ranks are $1,4,6,4,1$, so

$$\begin{align}
\chi(T^4)=1-4+6-4+1=0.
\end{align}$$

1. $\mathbb{CP}^3$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
H_k(\mathbb{CP}^3;\mathbb Z)&\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,2,4,6,\\
0, & \text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
& \chi(\mathbb{CP}^3)&=4.
\end{align}$$

1. Klein 瓶 $K$。
sol.

$$\begin{align}
H_k(K;\mathbb Z)&\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,\\
\mathbb Z\oplus\mathbb Z_2, & k=1,\\
0, & k\ge2,
\end{cases}
& \chi(K)&=0.
\end{align}$$

### 题 5（15 分）— 曲率与 Gauss-Bonnet

设 $M$ 为紧致二维定向 Riemann 流形。

1. 写出二维 Gauss-Bonnet 公式中标曲率形式的表达。
sol. In two dimensions, the scalar curvature is $\mathcal R=2K$, where $K$ is the Gaussian curvature. Therefore Gauss-Bonnet can be written as

$$\begin{align}
\int_M \sqrt{g}\,\mathcal R\,d^2x=4\pi\chi(M).
\end{align}$$

1. 对标准二维球面 $S^2$，说明积分 $\int_{S^2}\sqrt g\,\mathcal R\,d^2x$ 的值。
sol. For $S^2$, $\chi(S^2)=2$. Hence

$$\begin{align}
\int_{S^2}\sqrt g\,\mathcal R\,d^2x=4\pi\cdot2=8\pi.
\end{align}$$

1. 对环面 $T^2$，说明为什么该积分为零。
sol. Since $\chi(T^2)=0$, Gauss-Bonnet gives

$$\begin{align}
\int_{T^2}\sqrt g\,\mathcal R\,d^2x=0.
\end{align}$$

This holds for any smooth Riemannian metric on the compact oriented torus.
1. 解释局部曲率可以非零但总积分为零的原因。
sol. Gauss-Bonnet fixes only the total curvature integral, not the pointwise curvature. On a torus, positive curvature regions and negative curvature regions can cancel in the integral, so the scalar curvature may be locally nonzero even though the total integral is zero.

### 题 6（15 分）— 期末压轴：$\mathbb{CP}^n$ 全流程

完整回答以下问题：

1. $\mathbb{CP}^n$ 的定义、图册和维数。
sol. By definition,

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{CP}^n=(\mathbb C^{n+1}-\{0\})/\mathbb C^\times,
\end{align}$$

where $z\sim \lambda z$ for $\lambda\in\mathbb C^\times$. On

$$\begin{align}
U_i=\{[z_0:\cdots:z_n]\mid z_i\ne0\},
\end{align}$$

use inhomogeneous coordinates $\zeta_a=z_a/z_i$ for $a\ne i$. On overlaps the transition functions are

$$\begin{align}
\eta_i=\frac{1}{\zeta_j},\qquad \eta_a=\frac{\zeta_a}{\zeta_j}\quad(a\ne i,j).
\end{align}$$

They are holomorphic, so $\mathbb{CP}^n$ is a complex $n$-manifold, hence a real $2n$-manifold.
1. Hopf 纤维化与 $\pi_1,\pi_2$。
sol. Every complex line has unit representatives in $S^{2n+1}$, and the unit representatives differ by a phase. Thus

$$\begin{align}
U(1)\to S^{2n+1}\to\mathbb{CP}^n
\end{align}$$

is the Hopf fibration. Its long exact homotopy sequence gives, for $n\ge1$,

$$\begin{align}
\pi_1(\mathbb{CP}^n)=0,\qquad \pi_2(\mathbb{CP}^n)\cong\mathbb Z.
\end{align}$$

1. CW 结构与整数同调群。
sol. The standard CW structure has one cell in each even real dimension:

$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{CP}^n=e^0\cup e^2\cup\cdots\cup e^{2n}.
\end{align}$$

All cellular differentials vanish for degree reasons, so

$$\begin{align}
H_k(\mathbb{CP}^n;\mathbb Z)&\cong
\begin{cases}
\mathbb Z, & k=0,2,4,\dots,2n,\\
0, & \text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{align}$$

1. Fubini-Study 度量的齐次表达、退化方向、局部表达和 Kähler 势。
sol. On $\mathbb C^{n+1}-\{0\}$, the homogeneous expression is

$$\begin{align}
h_z =\frac{|z|^2dz\cdot d\bar z-(\bar z\cdot dz)(z\cdot d\bar z)}{|z|^4}.
\end{align}$$

It is degenerate along the complex scaling direction

$$\begin{align}
z^\alpha\frac{\partial}{\partial z^\alpha},
\end{align}$$

which is tangent to the $\mathbb C^\times$ orbit. On the local section $z=(1,\zeta)$, it descends to

$$\begin{align}
ds^2_{\mathrm{FS}} =\frac{(1+|\zeta|^2)d\zeta\cdot d\bar\zeta -(\bar\zeta\cdot d\zeta)(\zeta\cdot d\bar\zeta)} {(1+|\zeta|^2)^2}.
\end{align}$$

Equivalently,

$$\begin{align}
g_{a\bar b} =\frac{(1+|\zeta|^2)\delta_{a\bar b} -\bar\zeta^{\bar a}\zeta^b}{(1+|\zeta|^2)^2} =\partial_a\partial_{\bar b}\log(1+|\zeta|^2).
\end{align}$$

Thus a Kähler potential is

$$\begin{align}
K=\log(1+|\zeta|^2).
\end{align}$$
