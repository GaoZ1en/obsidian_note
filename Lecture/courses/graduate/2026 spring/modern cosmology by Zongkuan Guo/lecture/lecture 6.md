type Ia supernovae, standard candles, standard rulers, standard sirens

luminosity distance, defined as

$$\begin{align}
D_{L} & =\sqrt{\frac{L}{4\pi F}}
\end{align}$$

where $\displaystyle{L}$ is the intrinsic luminosity of the source, and $\displaystyle{F}$ is the observed flux. since for type Ia supernovae, the intrinsic luminosity is known, we can use them as standard candles to measure the luminosity distance.
