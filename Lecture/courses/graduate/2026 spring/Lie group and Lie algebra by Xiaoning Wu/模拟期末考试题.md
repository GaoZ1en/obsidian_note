$$\begin{align}
V & =\bigoplus_{i}V_{i}
\end{align}$$

$$\begin{align}

$$\begin{align}
R(t) & =I+tX+\mathcal{O}(t^{2})

## 4. 旋量表示（20 分）

1. 写出 $SO(N)$ 旋量表示的定义。
sol. in dim-$\displaystyle{N}$ Euclidean space, the Clifford algebra is generated by the gamma matrices

$$\begin{align}
\left\{\gamma _{a},\gamma _{b}\right\} & =2\delta _{ab},\quad a,b=1,\dots,N
\end{align}$$

the spin rep of $\displaystyle{SO(N)}$ is defined as the natural action of $\displaystyle{\mathrm{SO}(N)}$ on the Clifford module.
2. 设 Clifford 代数满足

$$\begin{align}
\{\gamma_a,\gamma_b\}=2\delta_{ab}.
\end{align}$$

证明

$$\begin{align}
I_{ab}=\frac{1}{4}[\gamma_a,\gamma_b]
\end{align}$$

是旋量表示中 $\mathfrak{so}(N)$ 的生成元。
sol. it is easy to see

$$\begin{align}
I_{ab} & =-I_{ba}
\end{align}$$

and

$$\begin{align}
[I_{ab},I_{cd}] & =\dfrac{1}{16}[[\gamma _{a},\gamma _{b}],[\gamma _{c},\gamma _{d}]] \\
 & =\delta _{bc}I_{ad}-\delta _{ac}I_{bd}-\delta _{bd}I_{ac}+\delta _{ad}I_{bc}
\end{align}$$

which is exactly the commutation relation between the $\displaystyle{\mathfrak{so}(N)}$ generators.

## 5. 不变张量恒等式（20 分）

设 $SU(N)$ 基本表示生成元满足

$$\begin{align}
\operatorname{tr}(T_AT_B)=\frac{1}{2}\delta_{AB}.
\end{align}$$

证明

$$\begin{align}
\sum_{A=1}^{N^2-1}(T_A)_a^{\ c}(T_A)_b^{\ d} =\frac{1}{2}\delta_a^d\delta_b^c-\frac{1}{2N}\delta_a^c\delta_b^d.
\end{align}$$

sol. Define

$$\begin{align}
S_{ab}^{\ \ cd} =\sum_{A=1}^{N^{2}-1}(T_A)_a^{\ c}(T_A)_b^{\ d}.
\end{align}$$

This tensor is $SU(N)$-invariant. The only invariant tensors with these indices are $\delta_a^d\delta_b^c$ and $\delta_a^c\delta_b^d$, so

$$\begin{align}
S_{ab}^{\ \ cd} =a\delta_a^d\delta_b^c+b\delta_a^c\delta_b^d .
\end{align}$$

First contract $c=a$. Since each $T_A$ is traceless,

$$\begin{align}
0 =S_{ab}^{\ \ ad} =a\delta_a^d\delta_b^a+b\delta_a^a\delta_b^d =(a+Nb)\delta_b^d.
\end{align}$$

Thus

$$\begin{align}
a+Nb=0.
\end{align}$$

Next contract $b=c$. Then

$$\begin{align}
S_{ac}^{\ \ cd} =\sum_A (T_A T_A)_a^{\ d}.
\end{align}$$

The operator $\sum_A T_A T_A$ commutes with $SU(N)$, hence by Schur's lemma it is a scalar on the fundamental representation:

$$\begin{align}
\sum_A T_A T_A=C_F I.
\end{align}$$

Taking the trace gives

$$\begin{align}
NC_F =\sum_A \operatorname{tr}(T_A T_A) =\frac{1}{2}(N^2-1), \qquad C_F=\frac{N^2-1}{2N}.
\end{align}$$

On the other hand,

$$\begin{align}
S_{ac}^{\ \ cd} =a\delta_a^d\delta_c^c+b\delta_a^c\delta_c^d =(Na+b)\delta_a^d.
\end{align}$$

Therefore

$$\begin{align}
Na+b=\frac{N^2-1}{2N},\qquad a+Nb=0.
\end{align}$$

Solving these two equations gives

$$\begin{align}
a=\frac{1}{2},\qquad b=-\frac{1}{2N}.
\end{align}$$

Hence

$$\begin{align}
\sum_{A=1}^{N^2-1}(T_A)_a^{\ c}(T_A)_b^{\ d} =\frac{1}{2}\delta_a^d\delta_b^c-\frac{1}{2N}\delta_a^c\delta_b^d.
\end{align}$$

1. 定义 Cartan 子代数、根空间、根向量。

$$\begin{align}

\end{align}$$

If $\alpha\ne0$ and $\mathfrak g_{\alpha}\ne0$, then $\alpha$ is a root. A nonzero element $E_{\alpha}\in\mathfrak g_{\alpha}$ is a root vector.
2. 写出半单李代数的根空间分解。
sol. The Cartan-Weyl decomposition is

$$\begin{align}
\mathfrak g =\mathfrak h\oplus \bigoplus_{\alpha\in\Phi}\mathfrak g_{\alpha}.
\end{align}$$

For a complex semisimple Lie algebra, each root space $\mathfrak g_{\alpha}$ is one-dimensional.
3. 说明 Chevalley 基中 $H_i,E_{\alpha},E_{-\alpha}$ 的基本对易关系。
sol. For simple roots $\alpha_i$, write $H_i=\alpha_i^\vee$ and choose $E_i=E_{\alpha_i}$, $F_i=E_{-\alpha_i}$. The basic Chevalley relations are

$$\begin{align}

[H_i,E_j]&=A_{ij}E_j,\qquad [H_i,F_j]=-A_{ij}F_j,\\
[E_i,F_j]&=\delta_{ij}H_i.
\end{align}$$

More generally,

$$\begin{align}
[H,E_{\alpha}]&=\alpha(H)E_{\alpha},\\
[E_{\alpha},E_{-\alpha}]&=H_{\alpha},\\
[E_{\alpha},E_{\beta}]&=N_{\alpha,\beta}E_{\alpha+\beta}
\end{align}$$
