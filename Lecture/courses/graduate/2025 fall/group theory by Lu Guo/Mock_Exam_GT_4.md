1.  **[Perturbation Splitting]**
    An atom is placed in a crystal field with $D_3$ symmetry.
    The original $d$-orbitals ($l=2$, dim 5) span a representation of $SO(3)$.
    1.  Determine the character of the $l=2$ representation for rotations corresponding to classes of $D_3$ ($E, 2C_3, 3C_2$).
        *(Hint: $\chi^{(l)}(\alpha) = \frac{\sin((l+1/2)\alpha)}{\sin(\alpha/2)}$)*
    1.  Decompose the $d$-orbital level into irreps of $D_3$.
    1.  How many distinct energy levels will the $d$-state split into?
*sol*.
a) the character table for $\displaystyle{D_{3}}$ is given by (irreps and the induced rep from $\displaystyle{l=2}$):

| $\displaystyle{D_{3}}$  | $\displaystyle{E}$ | $\displaystyle{2C_{3}}$ | $\displaystyle{3C_{2}}$ |
| ----------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| A1                      | 1                  | 1                       | 1                       |
| A2                      | 1                  | 1                       | -1                      |
| D                       | 2                  | -1                      | 0                       |
| $\displaystyle{\ell=2}$ | 5                  | -1                      | 1                       |
b) we have

$$\begin{align}
n_{A_{1}} & =\frac{1}{6}\left(1\times1\times 5- 2\times 1\times1+3\times 1\times 1\right) \\
 & =1 \\
n_{A_{2}} & =\frac{1}{6}\left(1\times 1\times 5+2\times1\times(-1)+3\times(-1)\times(1)\right) \\
 & =0 \\
n_{D} & =\frac{1}{6}\left(1\times 2\times 5+2\times(-1)\times(-1)\right) \\
 & =2
\end{align}$$

so we have

$$\begin{align}
D^{\ell=2} & =A_{1}\oplus D\oplus D
\end{align}$$

c) there will be 2 distinct energy levels: one non-degenerate (A1) and one doubly-degenerate (D).
