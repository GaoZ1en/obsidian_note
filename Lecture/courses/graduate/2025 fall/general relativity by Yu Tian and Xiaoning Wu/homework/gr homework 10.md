# problem

derive the Newtonian version of Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff equation

$$\begin{align}
\frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}r} & =-\frac{G}{r^{2}}(\rho+p)(m+4\pi r^{3}p)\left( 1-\frac{2Gm}{r} \right)^{-1}
\end{align}$$

and compare with the original form

*sol*.

consider a spherical shell at radius $\displaystyle{r}$ with thickness $\displaystyle{\mathrm{d}r}$. the outward pressure difference $\displaystyle{\mathrm{d}p}$ exactly balances the Newtonian gravitational forces

$$\begin{align}
\frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}r} & =-\frac{Gm\rho}{r^{2}}
\end{align}$$

we can also get it by taking the Newtonian limit $\displaystyle{p\ll \rho, \frac{Gm}{r}\ll 1}$.
